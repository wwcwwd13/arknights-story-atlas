==The Second Summit==

===Our Unblemished Paradise===
[[File:61 i01 2.png|thumb|left|The halos and wings of the Sankta in the choir suddenly shatter, and their light is extinguished.]]
The year is 1101. It is just an ordinary day. Suddenly, the wings and halos of [[Sankta]] outside of [[Laterano]] blacken and shatter. It affects the pious nuns of [[Bolívar]], Father [[Agenir]] in [[Siracusa]], [[Woodrow Bianchi|Woodrow]] and [["Clip" Cliff|Cliff]] in [[Columbia]], and even [[Enforcer|Ezell]] in [[Kazdel]]. All of this points to a sign: something terrible has happened to the Holy City.<ref>[[MT-ST-1]]</ref>

Yet within Laterano, nothing seems to have changed. In fact, everyone seems to be more vigorous than before. Laterano is hosting the Second Summit of Nations to promote a new Lateran order in face of an impending prophesied calamity on Terra. Even Kazdel and [[Ægir]] have sent delegations to the Holy City, willing to band together to face the prophesied calamity that will befall [[Terra]]. The future of Laterano has never been brighter.<ref name=1Bef>[[MT-1/Story#Before operation|MT-1 Before]]</ref>

With everyone in Penguin Logistics out doing their own thing, Exusiai decides that now is the perfect time to visit her hometown in Laterano. As she is filling out her application for leave, she is visited by a couple of Rhodes Island Operators, who present her with classified files from the landship. Exusiai learns that something strange has happened in Laterano, and decides to see the situation with her own two eyes.<ref name=3Bef>[[MT-3/Story#Before operation|MT-3 Before]]</ref>

In the past several years since the first Summit of Nations, Laterano has been undergoing massive reformations. The Curia has been enacting extensive arms control policies, cracking down on unsanctioned gunshots and seizing explosive materials in order to keep things safe and secure. In efforts to standardize weapons productions, they have also been closing down all firearms workshops across the country, which place more importance on customization rather than efficiency. Furthermore, plans are being drafted to allow non-Sankta to wield their own patron firearms. To some, this may seem like progress; to Exusiai, it is erasing everything that made Laterano unique, and it seems that nobody thinks that these regulations are strange.<ref name=2Aft>[[MT-2/Story#After operation|MT-2 After]]</ref>

===Let's Blow Things Up===
[[File:61 i03 2.png|thumb|"If there's a problem, find a solution! If there's no problem, make a problem!"]]
Exusiai concludes that the anomalies plaguing Laterano stem from the majestic [[Shrine of Nurtury]], which seems to have appeared out of the blue. The shrine is planning to host the Nativity–a special birth ceremony–inviting all Lateran citizens and international delegates to witness a "miracle." She travels all over the city collecting an assortment of homemade materials to construct a makeshift bomb in order to blow up the massive shrine. She tests the bomb by detonating it in a small alleyway at Pagus Stevonus, but it barely damages a single pillar. What's more, her actions draw the attention of the Seventh Tribunal, who are on high alert with the ongoing Summit of Nations. The bombing is quickly labelled as a terrorist attack, and Gun-Knight [[Sankta Miksaparato|Patrizion]] assigns newly appointed Cardinal [[Lemuen]] and her aide [[Amos]] to investigate the case.<ref name=1Bef/><ref name=3Bef/>

Exusiai enters a mobile confessional codenamed ELCARO-001. After discussing the results with the "electronic confessor," Exusiai decides to visit a firearms workshop, hoping that one of the gunsmiths might be willing to help. She goes to [[Paganini]]'s workshop at Via Borgos, Pagus Faber, where she once selected her patron firearm. However, Paganini refuses to help as he had just signed over the workshop to the Curia, rendering any further workshop operations illegal. Nevertheless, Exusiai convinces Paganini's apprentice and her childhood friend, [[Vannini]], as the latter opines that it is not illegal for her since she is not officially a gunsmith yet. With her help, they create several bombs of adequate strength and the two head towards the Shrine of Nurtury. Exusiai leaves Vannini stationed in the plaza while she disguises herself as a refugee and blends in with the masses.<ref name=3Bef/>

Just beyond Laterano's borders, the infamous fugitive [[Arturia]] has been serving time amidst the golden wheat fields of [[Laterano/Branches#Landen Monastery|Landen Monastery]]. The monastery, which has long been neglected by the Lateran government, has recently regained its standing with the Curia and is assigned with monitoring her.<ref name=1Aft>[[MT-1/Story#After operation|MT-1 After]]</ref> Despite the watchful supervision of [[Archetto|Hildegard]] and the other nuns, Arturia manages to escape by using her musical [[Arts]] to trick them. [[Executor the Ex Foedere|Federico]] arrives on the scene just a little too late, and he follows her trail to the Holy City with Hildegard. They learn from a Kuranta refugee that Arturia has specifically asked if any pregnant woman has disappeared after entering Laterano recently.<ref name=3Bef/><ref name=3Aft>[[MT-3/Story#After operation|MT-3 After]]</ref>

Federico deduces Arturia's whereabouts and finds her on the roof of an unremarkable building in front of the Shrine of Nurtury. As there are no recent reports of missing pregnant persons in Notarial Hall records, Federico asks Arturia to explain. Arturia replies that she accidentally "touched" a pregnant mother's emotions while playing in the wheat fields and followed her trail here: the Shrine of Nurtury.<ref name=4Bef>[[MT-4/Story#Before operation|MT-4 Before]]</ref><ref name=4Aft>[[MT-4/Story#After operation|MT-4 After]]</ref>

==Holy Revelation==
===Shrine of Nurtury===
[[File:61 i06.png|thumb|The Matriarch prays for the health of everyone in the Holy City.]]
Laterano has also been receiving nomadic abbeys with refugees of many different races. The latest abbey includes a pregnant [[Sarkaz]] named Extra who is hostile and distrustful towards the Sankta's help. A blind Sankta with Sarkaz horns, [[The First Saint|Keph, the Matriarch]] of the Shrine of Nurtury, arrives at the scene. She uses her powerful empathy to calm and convince Extra to stay in the shrine for the birth. She then teaches Extra how to pray and seek comfort in the Lateran faith. The Matriarch shares with Patrizion a vision of Laterano's order for all on Terra, reiterating the importance to ensure that the Nativity proceeds without issues.<ref name=2Bef>[[MT-2/Story#Before operation|MT-2 Before]]</ref><ref name=2Aft/>

[[Andoain]], who previously caused chaos in the Holy City [[Guide Ahead/Synopsis|during the first Summit of Nations]], is chosen to be the nurturer in charge of child delivery at the Nativity.<ref name=1Bef/> He is almost abducted by intruders who are foiled and captured by the Shrine Patrol when the comfort bells ring in a loud alarm. Upon learning of the incident, Lemuen visits the Shrine to investigate and offer the Seventh Tribunal's assistance in the Shrine's security, but the offer is rejected by [[Velliv]]—now a key staff at the Shrine—as well as the Matriarch.<ref name=3Bef/><ref name=3Aft/> Patrizion and Velliv express concern over Lemuen's suspicious attitude towards the Matriarch and decide to closely monitor her.<ref name=4Bef/>

[[File:61 i07.png|thumb|left|Exusiai stands across her sister, who is no longer bound in her wheelchair.]]
Exusiai manages to infiltrate the Shrine and starts planting the bombs. However, she is shocked to find that the Shrine is unexpectedly populated with children and refugees, contrary to the public information that most of its facilities were still empty. Just as Exusiai decides to dismantle the bombs, she is confronted by Lemuen, who already suspects her involvement in the recent bombing in Pagus Stevonus. Exusiai uses their private password to signal Lemuen that it is a secret, and the two sisters share a quick emotional reunion before Exusiai runs off and leaves the disarming of the bombs to Lemuen.<ref name=4Bef/>

Meanwhile, outside the Shrine, a worried Vannini attempts to create a distraction for Exusiai by detonating an improvised bomb but is caught red-handed by Patrizion. Just as Patrizion is about to arrest Vannini, Paganini arrives and lies that he has instructed his apprentice on the bombing as a protest against the recent closure of many Firearms Workshops, including his. Patrizion lets them off, deducing that their accomplice is still inside the Shrine and orders increased security. Patrizion encounters Amos, who reports in his findings that the suspect in the recent bombing is Lemuen's sister.<ref name=4Aft/>

Andoain visits an Ecclesia Requietum to make funeral preparations for the mother who will give birth at the Nativity ceremony. He is ambushed by [[Patia]] and the [[Pathfinders]], but he does not recognize his former comrades, which infuriates Patia. Andoain is rescued by [[Mostima]] and [[Fiammetta]], who had been asked to return to Laterano for the Nativity. Fiametta is still angry with Andoain for injuring Lemuen and then returning to Laterano as if nothing has happened. Andoain explains that the Matriarch convinced him that his answer still lies in Laterano and promised a miracle that will give rise to many more miracles, so he must hurry back to the Shrine for the Nativity. The birth is imminent.<ref name=4Aft/>

===The Nativity===
Outside the Shrine, [[Insider|Richele]]'s team arrives on special orders jointly issued by all seven tribunals of the Curia, to supervise Arturia's arrest and even arrest Federico if there is suspicious behaviour. Federico pretends to comply, before firing at a building nearby to knock out Richele's team and Hildegard while Arturia uses her cello music to drown out the gunshot sound. He uses Richele's annual pass for the automated confessionals to deliver the injured personnel to a hospital and Hildegard back to Landen Monastery. Federico and Arturia team up to resolve the dangers Laterano is facing.<ref name=ST2>[[MT-ST-2]]</ref>

Exusiai is still attempting to disrupt the ceremony by blowing up the path to the nursery, when she is surprised to run into Extra, whom she has thought to be giving birth at the Nativity. Patrizion arrives with other guards to foil the bombing and apprehend Exusiai, later instructing Lemuen to detain Exusiai at the Seventh Tribunal. All the bombs have been disarmed and the Nativity continues without a hitch.<ref name=ST2/>

[[File:61 i08.png|thumb|"This child was born amidst our prayers. He is a miracle that you have made together."]]
[[File:61 i09.png|thumb|"Henceforth, we are born equal and one—as Sankta."]]
The Matriarch welcomes all attendees to the Shrine of Nurtury and begins the ceremony by asking all participants to prepare to listen to the heartbeat of new life.<ref name=4Aft/> All attendees, Sanktas and non-Sanktas alike, experience a powerful, collective emotional surge and a shared heartbeat through the comfort bells, conveying the mother's struggle during childbirth. Everyone in the hall waits in bated breath as each thump gets quicker and quicker, then slower and slower, until it eventually goes completely silent. A short while later, Andoain steps into the hall, his clothes covered in blood, holding the child in his hands. It is not a Sarkaz, or even a humanoid being; it is a baby arborbeast.<ref name=ST2/>

The Matriarch cradles the fledgling in her arms, yet the baby does not make a single sound. Everyone wails and laments over the stillborn who will never know life. As though sensing the audience's emotions, a miracle happens. The baby arborbeast cries out and a golden halo manifests above its head. The first time a halo has ever been granted to a non-Sankta. This is the revelation that [[The Law]] has granted them, on how to remove the barriers between all nations and build lasting peace. Laterano will share its halo with all races, uniting them all as Sankta.<ref name=ST2/><ref name=5Aft>[[MT-5/Story#After operation|MT-5 After]]</ref>

From outside the Shrine, Federico and Arturia look on with shock. The anomalies they witnessed in Laterano, in addition to the "miracle" in the shrine, contradict the very foundation of The Law. Both are certain that, in an incomprehensible way, the entire Laterano populace is sharing the same hallucination. They awaken from the dream and witness the true, calamitous state of Laterano: The pristine white city warps with dream-like colours and strange, unfamiliar, halo-adorned creatures emerge from portals extending from the Shrine onto the silent streets.<ref name=ST2/> The duo decide to seek out the one person who might be able to provide answers: Pope [[Yvangelista XI]].<ref name=5Bef>[[MT-5/Story#Before operation|MT-5 Before]]</ref>

==Rebirth of Laterano==
===Dissonant Chords===
[[File:61 i10.png|thumb|left|The Executor must answer the questions of those exiles who resent the Holy City.]]
Federico and Arturia arrive at the Pope's home only to discover that he has gone missing. From his house they observe that more and more halo-adorned creatures emerge to drag any awakened Laterans into strangely-coloured projections tied to the Shrine of Nurtury before vanishing together. Any Sanktas that try to shoot them will instantly "Fall," but they do not grow Sarkaz horns or tail. Curiously, they only become errari, but that nonetheless signifies that The Law judges these nonsentient creatures as fellow "Sankta." They decide to seek the Doctor's cooperation in overcoming this crisis, with Arturia finding the Doctor with her music. Their discussion is interrupted by a sudden communication from a former citizen, [[Aurela]], requesting permission to enter the city for a group of errari Sankta and war refugees.<ref name=6Aft>[[MT-6/Story#After operation|MT-6 After]]</ref>

Outside the city, Aurela's group becomes increasingly agitated by the silence to their request. Aurela, Amos's wife and a former Gun-Knight who fell during a mission where she killed her colleague and was exiled from Laterano, has been protecting [[Gracebearer|Nueda]], a [[K'uk'ulkan]] nun, and the refugee children under her care since their evacuation from Bolívar. While Aurela returns for revenge, Nueda and her children visit for Lateran faith. The group also includes Fallen Sanktas from abroad, who seek answers for their predicament as they have not committed any offence against the Law.<ref name=5Bef/>

Just as the group loses their patience and intends to enter the city, Federico arrives and stops them, explaining that the city is suffering from anomalies beyond comprehension. Arturia also arrives, on the Doctor's instructions, and confirms that Laterano is caught up in some kind of dream where the anomalies are being reflected in reality. The group of outsiders can be of help, and Arturia can use her music to keep the group sane when they enter the calamity-ridden city.<ref name=7Aft>[[MT-7/Story#After operation|MT-7 After]]</ref>

===Shatter the Dream===
[[File:61 i23.png|thumb|"Automated Confessional, 'ELCARO-001', in position!"]]
Lemuen brings Exusiai to the Seventh Tribunal for an official interrogation. They have a warm conversation but know that Exusiai will be handed over elsewhere if she still offers no information. Suddenly, the Seventh Tribunal is attacked by the confessional ELCARO-001, which is actually the Doctor in disguise, accompanied by Vannini. With their help, Exusiai escapes to the roofs where she is confronted by Lemuen again. Nevertheless, Lemuen ultimately allows Exusiai to escape as she trusts her sister and shares the same doubts about the current state of Laterano.<ref name=6Bef>[[MT-5/Story#Before operation|MT-6 Before]]</ref>

Lemuen is later suspended by the Curia for allowing Exusiai to escape multiple times, with Amos promoted to acting cardinal in her place. After becoming the acting cardinal, Amos approves his own application to join the Legati so that he can go to Bolívar to find Aurela. However, the moment he clicks the button, the application vanishes, as if it never existed. He visits Lemuen who is under house detention to share his suspicions about the city's reality and informs her about an even bigger Nativity that will be held at the Shrine that day. The two decide to work together to stop the ceremony.<ref name=7Aft/>

[[Apostolic Knights]] are sent out to bring all Liberi in Laterano to the Shrine of Nurtury, including Paganini and Hildegard. Hildegard finds out that thousands of Liberi have been gathered at the nursery, with other nurseries similarly gathering other races, even Fallen Sankta. A nurturer explains that they will be sharing the halo with all races, starting with the Liberi. Only then can they understand one another, eliminate conflicts and barriers, and "become a truly inseparable part of Laterano." Hildegard is unconvinced and unwilling to accept a halo. In a different part of the nursery, Paganini also refuses and lashes out at Patrizion. Nevertheless, the unwilling Liberi are forcefully included in the ceremony.<ref name=7Bef>[[MT-7/Story#Before operation|MT-7 Before]]</ref>

[[File:MT-"Lemuen the Silent".png|thumb|left|"Keph, Matriarch of the Shrine of Nurtury... Hit."]]
In the Hall of Enlightenment, the Matriarch has started the ceremony to bestow halos on non-Sanktas, calling it "the rebirth of Laterano." From a rooftop, Lemuen fires her sniper rifle and the bullet hits the Matriarch square in the head, causing the ceremony to fall into disarray. As Lemuen retreats, Amos covers her, but is ultimately shot dead by the pursuing soldiers.<ref name=8Bef>[[MT-8/Story#Before operation|MT-8 Before]]</ref>

Elsewhere in the shrine, Andoain conducts the funeral of the arborbeast mother who dies after giving birth at the Nativity. The arborbeast fledgling is initially sad, but soon returns to its joyful and playful state, even wanting to dance with Andoain. Through the power of empathy, Andoain realises that he too is no longer sad, and hysterically questions if being Sankta means only the instinct to avoid pain. He regains his memories of the Pathfinders and the funeral of [[Cecelia]]'s mother.<ref name=7Aft/>

[[File:61 i13.png|thumb|"If this is a dream, then I shall wake. If this is obsession, then I shall shatter it."]]
Awakened from the dream, Andoain heads towards the first nursery, where he and the Matriarch placed their faith and the shrine's point of origin. He is almost apprehended by a nurturer, but is saved by Mostima and Fiametta, who had escaped from joining the Nativity. Andoain says he will do something about the ceremony and asks Fiammetta and Mostima to evacuate the people in the ensuing chaos. Mostima hands him one of Exusiai's bombs before they separate for their own tasks. Andoain goes into the first nursery and detonates the bomb.<ref name=8Bef>[[MT-8/Story#Before operation|MT-8 Before]]</ref>

The destruction of the original nursery triggers the collapse of the Shrine. The Matriarch, despite having been shot in the head, stands back up again. As she orders an evacuation, Exusiai crashes into the Hall of Enlightenment and pulls her out of the boundary separating illusion from reality.<ref name=8Bef/>

==Awaken From Dream==
===Where It All Began===
[[File:61 i24 1.png|thumb|left|The faith of the masses has condensed into a vast sea. Not even ''The Saints' Travels'' wrote of a sight so bizarre.]]
Exusiai finds herself cradled on the palm of the Matriarch, now in the form of a gigantic monster with a halo. The Matriarch says she is what historical accounts call the "Saint," and her current form is a snippet of code given form. The Saint begins a grand procession of Sanktas, newly haloed refugees, and a host of haloed creatures. Under the overwhelming power of her empathy, all become inclined to follow. Projections follow where the procession goes and halos begin to appear on inorganic objects across the city.<ref name=8Aft>[[MT-8/Story#After operation|MT-8 After]]</ref>

The Saint uses her empathy to reveal to the masses a hidden history: the very first "Sankta" originated from desperate Teekaz warriors who discovered The Law hidden beneath the earth. It responded to their primal desire for survival, thus granting them halos and wings, boundless vitality, and the ability to touch each other's emotions. To clear her mind, Exusiai fires at the Saint to become an errari, thereby cutting off the Saint's empathetic influence.<ref name=8Aft/>

[[File:61 i27.png|thumb|"The countdown has begun, Doctor."]]
Meanwhile, the Doctor and [[Mon3tr]] continue moving through the city, making sure to avoid the haloed monsters. The Doctor and Mon3tr know that the processions is heading towards the Law, so Mon3tr attempts to use Kal'tsit's permissions to hack into the machine through a backdoor access port. However, her actions are detected and the duo become trapped in a stasis-like projection of Laterano. It is a space where all colour and life has been drained, frozen for all eternity. They are then confronted by the true mastermind behind the Laterano Calamity: Priestess. She fully admits to hacking and recalibrating the Law—officially known as the Personality and Cognitive Synchronization (PCS) system—in order to convert all races into Sankta.<ref name=8Aft/><ref name=10Bef/>

Her true goal is not to achieve some kind of interracial harmony on Terra, but something far more nefarious: to simplify biological information so that it will be easier to assimilate into Originium.<ref name=10Bef/>

===The Fallen Unite===
Observing the procession from afar is Federico's group from outside the city, now joined by the Pathfinders who have rescued Andoain from the collapsed Shrine. Andoain deduces that their destination must be the Basilica, where the Law is hidden. In order to stop them, Federico, Arturia and Andoain head to the Basilica. Patia leads the Pathfinders and errari away from the Saint to avoid being influenced by her powerful empathy, whilst Nueda finds shelter for the scared children.<ref name=8Aft/> Mostima convinces the resentful errari and fallen Sankta to help in the defence against the procession, while Lemuen returns to the Seventh Tribunal to get administrative permissions for the roadside facilities.<ref name=9Bef>[[MT-9/Story#Before operation|MT-9 Before]]</ref><ref name=9Aft>[[MT-9/Story#After operation|MT-9 After]]</ref>

[[File:61 i20.png|thumb|left|"You don't exist anymore. You're just a ghost! A string of code I can shoot!"]]
Upon seeing Patrizion amongst the procession, Aurela leaves Nueda's group, seeking revenge against the one who exiled her.<ref name=9Bef/> She contacts him with her communicator and fires at him, but Paganini takes the bullet for his old friend. As the Liberi bleeds out, he urges the latter to value real connections over false promise of a better Laterano. Patrizion returns to the procession, finding Vannini and regretfully informs the girl about her adoptive father. The emotional distress causes Vannini to start being overwhelmed by the Saint's empathetic influence, but Patrizion relieves her by making her shoot himself to become errari.<ref name=10Bef>[[MT-10/Story#Before operation|MT-10 Before]]</ref><ref name=10Aft>[[MT-10/Story#After operation|MT-10 After]]</ref>

As the procession approaches the Basilica, Mostima's group begins to struggle but is reinforced by Hildegard and the Pathfinders. As the resistance group runs out of ammunition, Fiammetta decides to use time bombs to cut off the path to the Basilica. Lemuen soon arrives to hold off the approaching hostilities. With the nomadic plate's lock mechanisms disabled by Lemuen, Fiammetta's bombs destroy the plaza in front of the Basilica, creating a massive rift that cuts off the procession's path. The Saint attempts to climb down the rift regardless, but Exusiai bombards the Saint with custom-made programmatic bullets and explosives that erase the Saint's data. The physical manifestation of the astray Law is thus defeated.<ref name=10Aft>[[MT-10/Story#After operation|MT-10 After]]</ref>

===A Simple Wish===
[[File:61 i25.png|thumb|What does it truly mean to be an "Angel"?]]
Meanwhile, in the Basilica, Andoain guides Federico and Arturia into a secret underground area. However, what was supposed to be one-way passage has turned into a bizarre, impassable maze, which is further protected by the Shrine Patrol. Andoain decides to use his luminescent Arts to their fullest extent in order to locate the real path, causing irreparable optic nerve damage to himself in the process. With the path revealed, the trio make their way to the final door, where the Law rests. Upon seeing that the Law is a machine, Arturia becomes disinterested, preferring to lure away the hostile guards in the maze instead.<ref name=9Bef/><ref name=9Aft/>

Federico and Andoain enter the room where the Law resides and find Pope Yvangelista XI in a state of aphasia and neurodegeneration. Since the beginning of the calamity, he has been desperately trying to persuade it, but to no avail. The Law disconnects from the Pope and requests the duo, deemed suitable "samples," to assist in the Transfiguration.<ref name=9Aft/> Through their halos, the duo synchronise with the Law for information transfer. After a lengthy interactive simulation, akin to a discourse with the Law, Federico and Andoain conclude that the Law itself is a paradox which must be corrected. The Law attempts to resolve the paradox but determines that its existing commands are incompatible. To find a solution, it requires an additional input, unintentionally provided as "refuse" by Andoain.<ref name=10Bef/><ref name=10Aft/>

The Law's solution is to eliminate itself, which Federico and Andoain accept. Together, they destroy the Law, the foundation of the Holy City and their faith. In an instant, the Lateran empathetic connections are severed, and all Sankta instantly become disconnected. As panic and unease grow in the city, Nueda comforts the children by singing hymns. Arturia uses her musical Arts to help her people to fill the void and 'communicate' without reservation, purely and honestly.<ref name=10Aft/>

==Fly, Broken Wings==
===The End of the Beginning===
Within the static dimension, Doctor, Mon3tr, and Priestess witness the destruction of the Law, even though the machine should have been capable of withstanding Federico and Andoain's attacks. Mon3tr is worried that Priestess might try to stop it, but the Doctor surmises that Priestess is actually incapable of directly affecting things in reality. The Doctor believes that Priestess has lost more control over Originium since the incident at the Rhodes Island landship, and the latter affirms their guess. Still, Priestess remains assured that the Doctor will eventually return to her side. Before leaving, Priestess states she looks forward for how the rest of Terra will live up to her expectations. The Doctor and Mon3tr then return to reality.<ref name=ST4>[[MT-ST-4]]</ref>

[[File:61 i22 1.png|thumb|"In the end, though, so many people chose to stay in these ruins... our halos still shine. Life goes on, right?"]]
Three months after the incident, halos forced upon non-Sanktas have been removed. Despite the Law's supposed "shutdown," Sankta halos damaged during the incident are recovering. Empathy still exists, between Sanktas only, but the connective strength now depends on the bond between the individuals. Many are unable to accept the truth behind their god and their ancestry, resulting in soaring suicide rates and a mass exodus of citizens. The Pope believes that the Law has left a blank state for the Laterans to communicate with it; he repurposes the underground structure of the Basilica as a public space so that every Sankta will have their say in the future of Laterano. <ref name=ST3>[[MT-ST-3]]</ref>

Exusiai plans to open up a new logistics company called Apple Pie Logistics to help the Curia handle Laterano's affairs. Lemuen returns Amos' personal effects to Aurela. Fiametta decides to invite Patrizion to vacation. Paganini recovers from his injuries and lets Vannini take over his firearms workshop's operations. Despite his contributions to save Laterano, Andoain is judged by the Tribunals and exiled from Laterano. Thereafter, Andoain dissolves the Pathfinders, letting them choose their own path in life. In a nomadic abbey far away from Laterano, Extra's baby is born with a halo despite both parents being Sarkaz. Andoain believes that the Sarkaz has learnt to connect with the Law through prayers, and a new Law has been born.<ref name=ST3/>

Within the ruins of the Law, Priestess attempts to restart the machine, but it remains unresponsive. Suddenly, one of the mechanical cubes that form the Law detach from the ring, giving birth to a "newborn angel". It soars into the sky, becoming a shooting star that will never fall.<ref name=ST4/>

===The Beginning of the End===
Somewhere in the woods, Priestess resurrects the Presence using Originium. In the vast wilds, Hierda and her followers continue granting "salvation" to the Infected. In the frigid tundra of Ursus, rumours spread that their Originium mines have suddenly dried up. Along the coastline, a Seaborn carelessly knocks a piece of Originium into the ocean, and the shiny rock is swallowed by the waves.<ref name=ST4/>

The calamity that befell Laterano is only the beginning of the worldwide apocalypse that will consume Terra.
