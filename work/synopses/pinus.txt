==Home==
Amidst a [[Kazimierz Major]] tournament, Sona meets a fellow [[Zalak]] knight. Being [[Infected]] herself, Sona shares her thoughts on how Infected knights are treated. The indifferent knight introduces herself as [[Ashlock|Greynuty Kaliska]], and the two team up for the melee match. Some time later, Greynuty reflects on their first meeting and how Sona stole her points near the end. Sona decides to form a Knightclub for the Infected and names it "Pinus Sylvestris" after the pine trees that once grew around her hometown in southern Kazimierz, causing Greynuty to realize that Sona was one of the Zalaks abandoned by the Kaliskas during a [[Catastrophe]].

Later, Pinus Sylvestris recruits other Infected knights, including [[Wild Mane|Iwona Krukowska]] and Jamie, into its ranks. Greynuty and Sona watch a match featuring [[Fartooth|Justyna Valentine]], whom they later recruit. Pinus Sylvestris's rising popularity draws the attention of the Adeptus Sprawiedliwi. Iwona, who hails from a family of campaign knights, expresses that the campaign knights are more trustworthy than the [[Kazimierz General Chamber of Commerce]], leading Pinus Sylvestris to consider the offer so that all of its Infected members can obtain legal identities and continue living in Kazimierz.

However, Pinus Sylvestris is also being preyed upon by the [[Armorless Union]], which requests that Pinus Sylvestris hand over the illegal Infected. Sona and the knights fight against the assassins, who are led by [[Platinum]], though she shows little interest in the assignment and hands it off to her subordinates. The Armorless Union's persecution becomes increasingly stressful, and Pinus Sylvestris eventually considers the offer from the Adeptus Sprawiedliwi. Sona meets Demian, the Vice President of the Knights Association, who wants to enlist their efforts to put Kawalerielki to sleep for a great separation. Sona explains the plan to the others and, although doubtful, resolves to find a place for the Infected.

==Dropping Grey==
Some time later, Greynuty is pitted against [[Olmer Ingra]], the Brassrust Knight, known for previously fighting [[Blemishine|Maria Nearl]] to a draw. Ingra, who despises the Infected, relentlessly attacks Greynuty, but she manages to guard against his attacks while also countering them with her cannonlance. Although she is taunted by Ingra, Greynuty remains focused and silent throughout the back-and-forth fight, further annoying him. 

When Greynuty is running low on shells for her cannon, the Brassrust Knight charges with the help of a thruster. She throws her cannon down and charges at Ingra with her shield, then finishes reloading before launching another barrage. As Ingra grows enraged and commits to a final charge, Greynuty uses her shelling to sail past him and fires a point-blank shot into his back. Knocked down and severely injured, Greynuty points her cannon at Olmer, seemingly to finish him off. Instead, Greynuty fires her last shot into the sky, calling it an offering to the audience and declaring that the Brassrust Knight does not deserve it. Olmer then falls unconscious, and the match ends with Greynuty's victory.

In the aftermath, Greynuty remembers her past training with her father after her grandmother passed away. The other members of Pinus Sylvestris bring her out to celebrate her victory, and they forget the ruse they were planning for a moment.

==Let Us Ring a Ding Ding==
Iwona "Wild Mane" Krukowska makes the news when she is involved in a violent altercation with other knights, facing possible suspension from the Major. Sona shows interest in recruiting Iwona to Pinus Sylvestris, and Greynuty advises her to think it through before taking such a risky gamble. A few days later, Iwona is released from custody and dodges the paparazzi. She encounters Greynuty and Sona, who invite her to join Pinus Sylvestris. Iwona agrees to join on the condition that they provide her with financial support for food and weapon maintenance, having become penniless after paying the K.G.C.C. her bail money.

The next day, Iwona visits an abandoned building with all her luggage. Although she is doubtful about the place, she enters and is welcomed by an Infected knight, who eventually informs her that Greynuty and Sona went to an Infected settlement to scatter its residents before they are rounded up. Iwona heads to the area and arrives just as Sona hides a child from hunters. Guessing that their true aim is to improve conditions for the Infected as a whole, Iwona expresses admiration and joins the fight.

Some time later, Iwona returns to the knightclub and declares that she never had a detailed plan for life beyond becoming a campaign knight, and agrees to do something for the Infected, like the [[Dikaiopolis|Blood Knight]] did. She then asks Sona whether she knows someone who could repair her pet, a [[Raythean]] robot she picked up from the roadside. Sona directs her to a knight-engineer in the area, who fully repairs the Raythean model, and Iwona decides to call it [[Justice Knight]].

==A Lone Soul==
Justyna helps [[Szewczyk]] the Plastic Knight, who was reported to have been hospitalized due to illness, hide from the Armorless Union. Although Szewczyk does not hold the Infected in high regard and intends to make the assassins pay, Justyna reminds him of his injuries. He agrees to cooperate with Justyna to survive, though not without musing on Kazimierz's current state.

Ten days prior, Justyna meets Greynuty and Sona when she is ambushed on the streets. She tags along with them for her safety, leading to her absence from the [[MN-8/Story|doubles match]] with Maria Nearl. Greynuty leaves to assist Iwona, and Justyna has a chat with Sona, explaining that an Infected knight has no future. She learns from Sona that she was attacked on the K.G.C.C.'s orders. Justyna explains that she became Infected on her way to Kawalerielki and wandered for six weeks until the Infected were allowed to register. By that point, she had lost her savings, so she entered the black market ring to earn money for the registration fees. After learning that Sona is thinking of forming a knightclub for Infected knights, Justyna agrees to join so that she has a place to return to.

Back in the present, Justyna takes Szewczyk to safety and reveals that she dragged him out of the hospital of her own accord despite their mutual distaste for each other. She reassures him that Sona is ensuring the safety of his son. Justyna monologues that, as an Infected, she is no longer welcome in her hometown and hopes the wind only carries the sound of her flute back, so that Justyna can remain as a simple girl dreaming of knighthood.

==Way of the Knights==
Following [[MN-ST-3|the incident in the Major preliminaries]], [[Nearl|Margaret]] and [[Młynar]] have a sword duel, with the latter vowing to make her give up. However, the duel ends with Margaret winning despite the fact that Młynar refuses to use his Arts. Although he acknowledges her improvement, he warns her that she is trampling on his father's will. A phone call interrupts their talk, and Młynar reminds her not to impose her fantasies on others, prompting Margaret to say that she might not end up like him. Maria and [[Whislash|Zofia]] approach Margaret, while Młynar answers the phone call and then points out [[Toland Cash|Toland]]'s presence, who had snuck inside the manor. Maria proposes to give her points to her sister, an idea that Margaret opposes until Maria expresses that she does not want to become a knight. Zofia states she will check on Młynar and reminds Margaret to be careful. Somewhere else, the Armorless Union assassins report the Nearls' duel to Platinum.

After Młynar's phone call, Zofia approaches him. She states that Margaret has become stronger ever since her exile and her time with [[Followers|her Sarkaz companions]], then requests him to swallow his pride and talk to the sisters. Młynar points out her confidence in ordering him around, causing Zofia to express her disappointment in him. She wishes him good luck with his work and leaves the room. Meanwhile, [[Shining]] and [[Nightingale]] roam the city, and they lament the state of the Infected. Margaret regains her title and encounters the newly promoted [[Malkiewicz]], who welcomes her back to Kazimierz. She returns to the manor and talks with Maria about their disillusioned uncle, and promises to tell Maria about the things she went through.

[[Nearl the Radiant Knight|The Radiant Knight]] returns to the arena. However, she is about to face her greatest challenge.

==Somebody==
The story centers on Malkiewicz, who has been recently promoted, replacing [[Czarny]] after the former spokesman's exile. Malkiewicz undergoes a change in appearance through his new white attire and neatly combed hair. Still, he is unused to his new role, appearing nervous and keeping his assistant waiting before a forum. He accepts a phone call and reassures the caller that his assistant did not offend him in any way. They arrive at the venue, and his assistant tells him that he became spokesman because his abilities were recognized. Malkiewicz denies it, but his assistant then brings up that they worked together in a past project where they failed to get knights like the Flametail Knight and the Fartooth Knight to join their knightclub. Malkiewicz thanks the assistant for remembering him and muses that he still feels as though he is in a surreal dream, his life having improved all thanks to a phone call. His assistant expresses envy for his luck and advises him to refrain from sharing his feelings with less fortunate people.

Malkiewicz is asked by another employee about his past entrepreneurship, although Malkiewicz denies ever having much success. When the employee brings Malkiewicz a cup of wine, he accidentally spills it and apologizes for staining his coat. Although the employee persuades him to have it taken off for cleaning, Malkiewicz refuses and laments that his own thoughts do not matter, as he will be stuck wearing these suits even if he does not think of himself as an important person.

==Drifting in the Wind==
The story focuses on the past of Centaurea, the current Platinum of the Armorless Union. As a fresh graduate, Centaurea becomes an independent knight under the Rose Paper Union but grows tired of the numerous arrangements and empty promises. Some time after the Armorless Union interrogated the knights of her company, she receives a phone call from the previous Platinum and is given a test to assassinate her last opponent, an ill-tempered knight with a prestigious background. She accomplishes the mission skillfully and enters the Armorless Union as Platinum's disciple. Yet, although the assassins hold a knight's life and death in their hands, they still have to satisfy the K.G.C.C.'s demands.

On a rainy night, [[Roy]] the Lazurite approaches her and asks about her master's whereabouts. In turn, she responds that he is on a mission, but has sensed that her master had doubts. Roy admires her honesty and reveals that the previous Platinum has died as a traitor. Shocked, Centaurea ponders that she knew he had fallen in love with his target, but did not expect him to die after betraying the Armorless Union. Roy then asks whether Centaurea would take up the position as the new Platinum, but she is unable to answer. Taking her silence as an answer, Roy places the Platinum badge in her hand and tells her to do well in her new role.

After a few setbacks, Centaurea manages to become a competent Platinum and finds herself stuck with the tiring job. At one point, she even receives a mission to assassinate a K.G.C.C. director, and accomplishes it after a quick confirmation with the Lazurites. Understanding that there is no escape from the K.G.C.C.'s schemes, no matter how high one's position is, Platinum yearns for a vacation.
