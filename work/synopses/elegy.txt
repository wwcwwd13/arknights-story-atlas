==Synopsis==
A writer from [[Tara]] sits inside a dilapidated pub, retelling a story he wrote based on the dramatic events which took place in the Taran capital of [[Na Saoirsí]]. His listener, a mysterious cloaked woman, is impressed by his tale. The writer admits that although he has made countless drafts, he has been unable to come up with a proper ending. The woman offers up her own perspective on the story, starting from the beginning...

===Chapter 1: Tara, the Red Dragon's Lair===
[[File:59 i01.png|thumb|left|After a long struggle through the wilderness, Loughshinny and her fellows arrive at the fabled promised land.]]
[[Reed the Flame Shadow|Loughshinny]], the noble red dragon, leads her group through the dreary Dark Woods surrounding Tara's hills. Upon reaching the other side, the group unexpectedly run into [[The Brigadier]], one of [[Dublinn]]'s commanders. He forbids her from bringing her group of Taran refugees into the city due to security concerns but has brought a mediator named [[Nemos]] to provide a solution. She invites them to join her group–the wakemen–who are ascetics known for holding traditional Taran funeral rites. Although the other wakemen are resistant to the notion, [[Moran]] convinces them to let them stay for a couple of days.<ref>[[EA-ST-1]]</ref>

Loughshinny then leaves with the Brigadier and enters Na Saoirsí. She walks through the streets and sees people donating their metal wares to [[Culann]], a local hero and humble blacksmith. He is creating a huge sculpture of a red dragon to celebrate Samhain, a traditional Taran festival held after the autumn harvest. As the first major festival since Tara gained independence, the residents are filled with hope and vigor. Still, Loughsinny notices that something is not right with their food supplies, as their meals seem to consist of solely potatoes.<ref name=1BEF>[[EA-1/Story#Before operation|EA-1 Before]]</ref>

[[File:59 i02.png|thumb|Should the living endure and the dead rest in peace? Or should all of Tara become a kingdom of death?]]
The Brigadier then brings Loughshinny to the Na Saoirsí citadel, where Eblana is. Loughshinny relays her concerns about the citizen's livelihoods to her sister Eblana, but the latter remains as contemptuous as ever. Eblana then reveals a horrifying truth: all the residents have been tainted by her flames of death, which will eventually turn them into walking undead. Eblana taunts her sister, stating that the only way to free them is to kill her. Only then will the flames be dispelled, allowing Loughshinny to become the one and only red dragon of Tara.<ref name=1BEF/> Unwilling to accept this, Loughshinny attempts to remove the purple flames from one townsperson, but the fire is rooted too deeply into their soul and she is forced to abandon the attempt.<ref name=1AFT>[[EA-1/Story#After operation|EA-1 After]]</ref>

===Chapter 2: Fear, Hunger, and Madness===
As days pass, food supplies in the city begin to dwindle, and soon famine begins to creep in. Food carts that were supposed to bring grain to the city mysteriously disappear and rumors quickly spread that the agricultural plate was set on fire by Victorian spies. The city's curfew, meant to protect the Tarans, was instead oppressing and starving them. Amidst the chaos, Culann's apprentice, Theo, suddenly vanishes. Nemos scours the streets in search of him yet to no avail, so she enlists help from Loughshinny and a nomad named [[Brigid]].<ref name=2BEF>[[EA-1/Story#Before operation|EA-2 Before]]</ref>

While the rest of the city slowly starves, the Dublinn officials host a lavish party to celebrate their victory. Their leader Eblana soon arrives, and says that they may take whatever is in the room as reward for their hard work. At her command, all the treasures in the room suddenly come to life and run out into the night.<ref name=2AFT>[[EA-2/Story#After operation|EA-2 After]]</ref> The Dublinn officers scramble to catch whatever they can find, while Eblana mocks their pitiful avarice from afar. The treasures lead the Dublinners into the Dark Woods, where they are consumed by the black mist, never to be seen again.<ref name=3BEF>[[EA-3/Story#Before operation|EA-3 Before]]</ref>

Meanwhile, Loughshinny and Brigid continue their investigation. Following Brigid's clues, the two head to the house of the grain shop owner, Emma, and discover that she has fallen ill. In her half-conscious, delirious state, Emma groans about how a high-ranking Dublinn officer had been forcing her to smuggle grain out of the city. She also confessed about how she left Theo to die simply because he was a Victorian.<ref name=3BEF/><ref name=3AFT>[[EA-3/Story#After operation|EA-3 After]]</ref>

===Chapter 3: Hero or Monster===
[[File:59 i04.png|thumb|left|The metal dragon is dragged into the town square with a chain leash to be executed.]]
Having uncovered the intel they needed, Loughshinny informs the Brigadier about the location of the missing food. That same night, the Brigadier heads into the Dark Woods and disappears in the darkness, while the food carts mysteriously reappear at the agricultural plate. Although grain stores are quickly resupplied, this does little to stave off the public's rage with people brawling violently on the streets for food. Believing that [[Victoria]]n spies are behind all this, residents force each other to prove their identity by burning their hands in their Red Dragon's purple flames.<ref name=3BEF/><ref name=4BEF>[[EA-4/Story#Before operation|EA-4 Before]]</ref>

Brigid informs Culann of Theo's fate and the blacksmith is horrified that the people he once thought were friends could be so cruel. He vents out his frustration at the dragon sculpture in his workshop and realises that creating it has wasted too much of the town's valuable resources, contributing to the madness outside. He drags the massive statue to the square all by himself and loudly proclaims that he will destroy this symbol of greed. The entire crowd is spurred into a frenzy, and Loughshinny and Nemos could only watch as Culann is slain by the townspeople.<ref name=4BEF/>

The mysterious woman is quite impressed with the writer's retelling so far, complimenting his heroic depiction of Culann's death. The writer finds that his listener is strangely eager to help him create an ending, but ultimately drops his suspicions. The woman then suggests they move on to discussing the second part of the story.<ref name=4BEF/>

===Chapter 4: Into the Dark Woods===
[[File:59 i05.png|thumb|Eblana has long waited for a moment when her sister would stand as her equal, a partner to dance with.]]
Eblana arrives and uses her Arts to revive Culann and animate the dragon, making it fly onto the podium by itself. Afterwards, she tells the townspeople to leave so that she can talk to Loughshinny alone. Once again, she taunts her to kill her, but Loughshinny refuses, determined to find a "cure" for her purple flames.<ref name=4AFT>[[EA-4/Story#After operation|EA-4 After]]</ref>

After witnessing Culann's tragic demise, Nemos loses faith in Eblana's vision and decides to side with Loughshinny instead, though Nemos becomes tainted by the purple flames as well. Loughshinny ventures into the Dark Woods alone, hoping to request help from the wakemen, who might know how to cure the purple flames. However, the forest seems to twist and turn around her, and the black mist lingers cautiously around her. Just then, she meets a strange Draco who seems to be made of purple flames. He helps her see through the fae's illusions and escape the woods.<ref name=5BEF>[[EA-5/Story#Before operation|EA-5 Before]]</ref>

Loughshinny reunites with Moran and others, who are relieved to see her. The Draco requests help from the wakemen, but they refuse to interfere with matters of the city. Seeing their unwillingness, Loughshinny tells them about Culann's death and asks them to give him a proper funeral.<ref name=5BEF/> The wakemen agree, and later that night, everyone gathers around for the funeral. Loughshinny reads a short eulogy written by Nemos as they gather around the bonfire, and later sing a song to bid the hero farewell.<ref>[[EA-5/Story#After operation|EA-5 After]]</ref>

===Chapter 5: Slaying the Dragon===
[[File:59 i08.png|thumb|left|Eblana gracefully falls into Loughshinny's arms, like the last step of a beautiful dance.]]
Ultimately, Loughshinny is unable to convince the wakemen to aid the city. Loughshinny decides to return to Na Saoirsí when she runs into the strange Draco from earlier. The Draco reveals himself as [[Labhréathach]], the Exiled King who died six hundred years ago, now resurrected by Eblana. He shows visions of Loughshinny’s past to herself and shares his own backstory, arguing that the history of red dragons is always marred by tragedy. He explains that there is no other way to stop the purple flames unless she kills Eblana. Despite this, Loughshinny remains resolute in her belief that she is the only one who can stand up to her sister and that she must return the fire to their source.<ref>[[EA-6/Story#Before operation|EA-6 Before]]</ref>

The writer stops at this point, regretfully stating that he has lost sight of the ending. The mysterious woman accompanying him casually states that this is because he has taken too many creative liberties in his writing, adding fantastical elements that muddy the true history. She then offers to supplement the story with her own observations of what really happened that night. The writer realizes that this woman is not some random nobody and starts feeling threatened, but is forced to continue.<ref name=6AFT>[[EA-6/Story#After operation|EA-6 After]]</ref>

Na Saoirsí has fallen into chaos, with people looting homes and hysterically attacking each other.<ref name=6AFT/> Loughshinny confronts Eblana alone, who has been waiting for her in the town’s citadel. The two reminisce about their past, and although Loughshinny wishes that there was still hope for reconciliation, Eblana tells her to face reality. Loughshinny then pierces Eblana’s body with her spear, finally killing the greedy red dragon. However, purple flames suddenly burst out of Eblana’s wound and soars towards the streets. Loughshinny watches in shock as the purple fire spreads in the blink of an eye, engulfing the entire city with the flames of death.<ref>[[EA-ST-2]]</ref>

===Chapter 6: Through the Fire and Flames===
[[File:59 i09.png|thumb|Tell those wood-brains this: "It's been six hundred years. Nobody's blaming you anymore. Go do your jobs."]]
Loughshinny and the others know they have little time to act. At Loughshinny’s instructions, Nemos drags a coffin around the city, proclaiming the death of their red dragon. The aggrieved citizens harass her over their leader’s killer, so Nemos lies by saying that Eblana had committed suicide so that the people would no longer seek vengeance. Meanwhile, Brigid rushes to get help from the wakemen when she senses Labhréathach’s presence. Labhréathach tells her that Brigid should leave and never turn back, but Brigid thinks that Labhréathach actually does care about what happens to the city. Irritated, the undead king then orders her to relay an important message to the wakemen.<ref name=7AFT>[[EA-7/Story#After operation|EA-7 After]]</ref>

Loughshinny stands guard with her spear and confronts the black mist head-on as it spills into the streets. The mist is furious and reprimands her, but Loughshinny says that as long as Tara still has a red dragon, they shall not interfere. Fortunately, the wakemen soon arrive at the city upon receiving Brigid’s message. They guide the people to conduct the fire-returning ceremony, using the dragon’s statue as their bonfire. They pass their purple flames into the sculpture as they cry out their sorrows and sing their ancient ballads.<ref name=7AFT/><ref name=8BEF>[[EA-8/Story#Before operation|EA-8 Before]]</ref>

[[File:59 i11.png|thumb|left|From death comes new life and an oath for a peaceful future.]]
At the ceremony, Loughshinny once again attempts to replace the flames with her own, but finds that they still refuse to yield. The massive dragon before her suddenly comes to life and Loughshinny brandishes her spear as the two dragons fall into a deadly dance. She pushes her Arts to the absolute limit, and eventually life overpowers death; the fire’s purple hue is gradually replaced by bright gold. As Loughshinny steps out of the blaze, she greets the townspeople and declares herself as Tara’s one and only true Leader, promising to lead them to a bright future of hope.<ref name=8AFT>[[EA-8/Story#After operation|EA-8 After]]</ref>

This is the "gift" that Eblana has bestowed upon her: Loughshinny will no longer be her shadow, but it comes at the price of officially becoming Tara’s ruler. Other than Nemos, none of the other citizens of Na Saoirsí were aware that Loughshinny had replaced their leader, leaving Eblana's name to be buried in the dust. As part of her duties, she must now liaise with the other Victorian dukes, who are less than pleased at this turn of events.<ref name=ST3>[[EA-ST-3]]</ref>

===Chapter 7: A Happy Ending?===
[[File:59 i12.png|thumb|The "Leader of Dublinn" is quite pleased with how the story has turned out. Her story.]]
The writer finally finishes his story and is elated that his manuscript is now complete. He is revealed to be "The Singer", a Dublinn officer who had survived the black mist and escaped Na Saoirsí after the celebration party. He tells the mysterious woman that she can do anything with the manuscript, whether to publish it or seal it away. The woman takes off her hood, and—much to The Singer's horror—reveals herself not as Loughshinny, but as the supposedly deceased Eblana Dublinn.<ref name=ST3/>

Eblana explains everything. She had used the black mist, which was the Writer's analogy for the Duke of Wellington's blockade, to intentionally starve the city. After Loughshinny entered the city, the duke stepped up the blockade to prevent any news leaking about the existence of two red dragons. When the food mysteriously vanished, The Brigadier had gone into the Dark Woods to request for more supplies. As he was a direct subordinate of Wellington, the duke easily acquiesced.<ref name=ST3/>

After Loughshinny struck down Eblana, her body mysteriously vanished shortly afterwards. The coffin Nemos had dragged around the city was, in truth, empty. In the end, everyone had returned the purple flames back to their master, and Eblana has completely replenished her power. She congratulates The Singer for creating a happy ending for his story, but the man is mortified beyond belief. He breaks down and tries to erase his work, suffering a mild stroke and losing his voice in the process. Eblana leaves with a smile of satisfaction on her face, bidding farewell to the last Dublinner.<ref name=ST3/>

Some time later, Eblana enlists as an official [[Operator]] of [[Rhodes Island]] unbeknownst to Loughshinny and tells this "fairy tale" to the [[Doctor]]. As for why, she claims that she feels naturally drawn to Rhodes Island, which has always been surrounded by the scent of death, and around the Doctor in particular. In the past, she was once the revered Leader of Dublinn. On Rhodes Island though, she is simply the shadow of Tara's leader, Necrass, nothing more.<ref name=ST3/>
