==Synopsis==
===Let the Curtains Rise Again===
[[File:62 i08.png|thumb|Lucian plans to end the legacy of the Crimson Troupe once more.]]
[[Operator]] [[Phantom|Lucian]] and [[Miss.Christine]] leave the [[Rhodes Island]] [[Rhodes Island (landship)|landship]] in search of the nefarious [[Crimson Troupe]], an elusive criminal organization which treats death as the ultimate art performance. When he was young, he had been taken in by the troupe and raised into a prodigy assassin known as [[Lucian, Blood Diamond|Blood Diamond]]. Eventually, he rebelled against the troupe, killing most of their members and destroying it. However, the troupe had managed to survive, and Lucian himself was nearly brainwashed again by them [[Phantom & Crimson Solitaire/Synopsis|a couple of years ago in Victoria]]. Determined to put an end to the troupe, he has wandered across [[Terra]] researching the history of the [[Tragodia (NPC)|Troupe Leader]]–his former mentor–seeking a way to kill him for good.<ref name=8Bef>[[AD-8/Story#Before operation|AD-8 Before]]</ref><ref name=8Aft>[[AD-8/Story#After operation|AD-8 After]]</ref><ref>[[Prelude Suite: Blood Diamond]]</ref>

Lucian's investigation brings him to the star-studded city of [[Wrankwood]], the entertainment capital of Terra. He attends an auction where people bid for anonymously-submitted screenplays, eager for an opportunity to make it big. His cool demeanour attracts the attention of producer [[Greta Stone]], who shows him the movie script for ''The Death of Lady Dupont'', which she had just purchased at the auction. As Lucian reads the script, he discovers the twin-faced emblem of the Crimson Troupe engraved in the gaps between the pages. Believing that he has found the right place, he accepts her proposal to join the film crew and follows her to the mobile filming plate where the studio is located.<ref name=1Aft>[[AD-1/Story#After operation|AD-1 After]]</ref>

''The Death of Lady Dupont'' is a historical tragedy movie set in the early days of [[Columbia]], a dramatic tale about a marriage between two noble families. The protagonist is played by actress [[Melanie Rutherford]], who is joined by actors [[Michael]] and Dalton. Also on the crew is [[Abner]] the director of photography, [[Marion]] the costume designer, [[Shelly]] the production coordinator, and [[Tippi]] the background painter. With the rest of the crew on board, the filming plate departs from the core city. Roughly two months pass as the crew shoot scene after scene, and the final week of filming arrives before they know it. Little did anyone realize, a horrific chain of events is about to occur that will completely derail the movie's course.

===One and Two===
[[File:62 i01 2.png|thumb|left|The two men performed their scenes perfectly, but perhaps too realistically...]]
Lucian finally accepts Greta's proposal to act in her movie and gets casted to play the minor role of a Messenger. Seeing that Lucian is ready, Director [[Steven Quay]] calls for him and Dalton to enter the set. Dalton arrives late at the studio, terribly drunk. He does not seem to care much about the movie, but upon seeing his counterpart's intense focus, Dalton instantly sobers up and becomes completely immersed in his role. Lucian and Dalton have a brief yet intense exchange as they fight over a prop knife. The scene ends with Lucian taking the knife and slashing it across Dalton's chest, causing the latter to dramatically fall to the ground as blood sprays out of him. The excellent scene earns the applause of the entire crew.<ref name=ST1>[[AD-ST-1]]</ref>

However, Dalton does not stand back up. Shelly walks over to check on him, and realizes a horrific fact: it is ''real blood'' that is pouring out of his body.<ref name=ST1/>

A short investigation concluded that Dalton's death was an accident, and the prop master in charge was promptly fired as a result. Their assistant, [[Moira]], is promoted to take their place. The film crew remain uneasy about the loss of an actor, but Greta reiterates that the studio's lease will expire soon, and that they cannot afford to extend it. The crew continue filming, moving onto a scene with Melanie and Michael on a wire rig. In the middle of the shoot, Shelly starts hallucinating and lets out a scream. In her terror, she fails to notice a dangling container about to fall onto her, but Lucian whisks her away before the worst could happen.<ref name=1Aft/><ref name=2Bef>[[AD-2/Story#Before operation|AD-2 Before]]</ref>

[[File:62 i13.png|thumb|Dalton's death reminded Shelly of what happened to that girl from five years ago.]]
Shelly is kicked out of the studio for causing a commotion, where she is approached by Lucian. Shelly confesses that she's had hallucinations for years, and tells Lucian a dark secret. Five years ago, a promising young actress named Laura died during the production of one of Greta's movies. Dalton was one of the actors in that movie, and as a big-name veteran, he was annoyed at Laura's overeagerness. He wanted to put Laura in her place and added extra scenes that left her hanging on a wire rig for hours on end, until the wires snapped and she fell to her death. No investigation was ever conducted. The accident left deep scars on Shelly, both physically and mentally.<ref name=2Bef/>

Shelly also tells Lucian about a strange document on Greta's desk, prompting him to investigate the producer's office. Later that same night, Shelly receives a text message while watching a horror movie with Tippi and Moira. It came from an impossible sender—Laura. Shaken, Shelly excuses herself and follows the message's instructions to head to the studio warehouse. Inside, she discovers a roll of frayed wires with dry blood on them. Shelly starts hallucinating Laura again, and in her maddened state, confesses that she had deliberately overlooked the aged wires on Laura's rig. She had hated Laura's selfishness and how they had to constantly adjust the schedule to fit her "unreasonable demands."<ref name=2Bef/><ref name=2Aft>[[AD-2/Story#After operation|AD-2 After]]</ref>

In that moment, someone shoves Shelly into the warehouse's cold storage and locks the door. By next morning, her corpse had become as stiff as ice.<ref name=2Aft/>

===Three and Four===
Everyone seems to believe that Shelly had committed suicide due to stress, but Tippi refuses to accept it. She finds an eyewitness who saw Shelly running into Michael at the studio last night and confronts the actor about it.  His girlfriend, Marion, also presses him for answers. Michael confesses that he did bump into Shelly, but that he was there to meet Moira. Much to Tippi's surprise, Moira corroborates his story, saying that Michael wanted to know if there was any way to sneak off the filming plate.<ref name=3Bef>[[AD-3/Story#Before operation|AD-3 Before]]</ref>

While everyone else is distracted by Shelly's death, Lucian reveals a shocking secret to Abner's camera assistant. The assistant publicly confronts Greta about this and presents some shredded documents that Lucian obtained from Greta's office: a [[Catastrophe]] forecast report and a storyboard. Abner realizes that Greta intends to film the Catastrophe for the movie and smashes his equipment in anger, saying that she's putting everyone's lives in danger. Greta does not deny the accusation but claims she has already taken all the necessary precautions to ensure the safety of the crew. Still, the crew remains on edge of the impending disaster.<ref name=3Bef/><ref name=3Aft>[[AD-3/Story#After operation|AD-3 After]]</ref>

[[File:62 i03 a.png|thumb|The two lovers sway eerily in the air, fear and sorrow plastered on their faces.]]
Later that night, Michael tries to convince Marion to leave the plate together, having come to the realization that the murderer is targeting everyone involved in Laura's accident five years ago.<ref name=4Bef>[[AD-4/Story#Before operation|AD-4 Before]]</ref> However, Marion refuses to leave, and even pressures Michael to stay so he can finally be a big shot actor and earn lots of money after the movie's release.<ref name=4Aft>[[AD-4/Story#After operation|AD-4 After]]</ref> In the middle of their argument, Michael notices someone holding a fire axe approaching. By pure instinct, he shoves Marion out of the way, and the axe lodges itself in his neck instead. Marion tries to retaliate, but the murderer overpowers her and pierces her skull with a large pair of scissors.<ref name=8Bef/>

The next morning, an unlucky stagehand enters the studio... and finds both bodies strung up together on pulleys, weapons still embedded inside them. The sheer brutality of the crime leaves no room for doubt: there is a serial killer on the filming plate.<ref name=4Aft/>

===The Killer's Identity===
With four victims dead, the entire film crew is beginning to melt down. Worse still, the director Steven still hasn't figured out how to write the ending for the movie. Greta decides to gather everyone in the studio for an emergency meeting. She sends everyone their wages ahead of the scheduled time and officially disbands the crew. She proclaims that anyone who feels that their life is in danger is allowed to flee, while those who are willing can stay behind and continue filming. Greta explains her determination to finish the movie, no matter what happens next.<ref>[[AD-ST-2]]</ref> Melanie is still banking on the movie to be her big re-debut, so she requests Tippi's assistance in creating the best background for the final scene. Meanwhile, Moira heads to the underground section of the filming plate in search of an alternate escape route.<ref name=5Bef>[[AD-5/Story#Before operation|AD-5 Before]]</ref>

Later that night, Steven desperately scrawls out a bunch of ideas for the ending, but remains dissatisfied with all of them. As he reaches  his wit's end, he is suddenly approached by an unfamiliar man; a tall [[Feline]] with flashy pink hair who looked like some kind of deity. The "god of inspiration" gives Steven some pointers on how to write the ending, shifting his attention to the Messenger character played by Lucian, a seemingly insignificant character with an important role in the story. Steven realizes that he should make Lucian into the star of the final scene and fervently starts writing again.<ref name=5Bef/><ref name=5Aft>[[AD-5/Story#After operation|AD-5 After]]</ref>

[[File:62 i04 2.png|thumb|left|The perpetrator behind the hideous crimes on the studio is none other than...]]
Around the same time, Greta visits Lucian in his room, who has been waiting for her. With Michael deceased, Greta offers Lucian to become the next lead for the rest of the film. However, she also understands that Lucian is likely also here for a different reason, related to Laura's death from five years ago. Greta reveals that she had bribed the police and media to suppress the incident, and even gave a large sum of hush money to Laura's family. In exchange for revealing this information, Greta wants Lucian to find the truth behind the serial killings.<ref name=5Aft/>

Lucian sets up a trap for the killer in Greta's room, predicting that the killer would target her next. As expected, the cloaked killer enters the room. Thinking that Greta was asleep, the killer stabs the bulge on the bed, only to discover that it's Miss.Christine, who is completely unharmed. Lucian turns on the lights and reveals the true identity of the killer: Moira, the younger sister of Laura.<ref name=6Bef>[[AD-6/Story#Before operation|AD-6 Before]]</ref>

===Derisive Performers===
[[File:62 i05.png|thumb|"I don't regret avenging her. I don't regret shouldering all these sins."]]
Moira confesses the truth behind her crimes. Everything she had done was to avenge her mistreated sister and kill the people who caused her death and took advantage of it. However, Greta points out that she had reached out to every single member of Laura's extended family after the accident, and there was no one named Moira on the family tree. Lucian plainly states that Moira's core truth is nothing but a fabrication. Just then, an explosion rocks the filming plate, and Moira escapes. In reality, Moira had gone to the underground layer to sabotage the cockpit's controls. Now, the filming plate is on a full speed course towards the Catastrophe, and there is no way to stop it.<ref name=6Aft>[[AD-6/Story#After operation|AD-6 After]]</ref>

In the chaos, Moira locks herself away in the prop room. After looking back on her memories together with Laura, she realises that they cut off at some point, and that Lucian had told the truth. A gentle Feline had approached her when she was at her lowest point and "inserted" memories of Laura into Moira's head, imbuing her with hopes, dreams, and a motive for vengeance. This man was none other than the elusive Troupe Leader that Lucian has been searching for. Lucian enters the prop room to confront Moira, who has now devolved into insanity. She uses her Originium Arts to attack Lucian with living mannequins, forcing the latter to use his long forgotten Blood Diamond's song in order to incapacitate her.<ref name=7Bef>[[AD-7/Story#Before operation|AD-7 Before]]</ref><ref name=7Aft>[[AD-7/Story#After operation|AD-7 After]]</ref>

[[File:62 i07.png|thumb|left|"Who says Lady Dupont is meant to die?! She shall not perish, because this storm is the chariot that will lead her to freedom!"]]
As the Catastrophe tears through the plate, Abner's camera assistant broadcasts the truth about the serial killer's identity over the comms. Tippi was still talking with Melanie when she hears about this, and the young Liberi is utterly devastated. She suddenly remembers an obscure movie trivia about a second control cabin located at the bottom engine layer of Wrankwood's filming plates and rushes to salvage the situation.<ref name=7Bef/> However, her path is blocked by angry staff members who accuse her of covering for Moira. Fortunately, their intense anger attracts the attention of Miss.Christine, who quite literally devours their emotions, causing them to forget what they were doing. Tippi is able to pass them safely and pulls a lever to turn the mobile plate's treads.<ref name=7Aft/>

The filming plate starts to turn, although half of its surface area remain engulfed by the storm. Melanie is awestruck by the sheer force of nature and cannot help but approach it. She releases herself from fear and invites Abner to help her finish the film with this climatic scene. On the other side of the plate, Greta desperately searches for Steven amidst the rubble and discovers that he's trapped inside his studio. Steven laments that even after receiving advice from his "God", he still cannot write a satisfactory ending. Greta admonishes the director and tells him that she liked the script not because of the death element, but because she believed in Lady Dupont's potential as a protagonist. Steven then decides to reject his god's teachings and strikes "death" from the title.<ref name=8Aft/>

===To Be or Not to Be===
[[File:62 i09 2.png|thumb|The Troupe Leader and his favourite pupil finally meet again.]]
Lucian heads outside, where he sees a magnificent stage standing beneath the dark thunderclouds. Standing on the carpeted stairs leading to the stage is a familiar figure—Dalton. Lucian immediately knows he is not the real deal. The false Dalton tears their disguise and reveals their true form as [[Tragodia (NPC)|Tragodia]], exalted leader of the Crimson Troupe, and the true mastermind behind the Wrankwood serial killings.<ref name=7Aft/>

Lucian confronts Tragodia, criticizing his aesthetics and obsession with death. Lucian's investigation has revealed that Tragodia is in fact an Ageless who has orchestrated countless incidents throughout Terra's history, stirring people to commit bloodshed. Lucian uses his singing Arts in reverse to protect everyone from the Catastrophe before bravely leaping into battle. With a magnificent flourish, Lucian pierces the immortal one's heart with his own scepter. In awe of his "performance", Tragodia bestows his immortality upon his favourite student, before breaking apart into countless broken masks. Lucian notices that his outfit has changed and realizes that his former mentor has passed the torch to him, making him the new Tragodia.<ref name=8Aft/>

[[File:62 i11 4.png|thumb|left|"If our previous attempts at escape or revenge were just scripts for us to follow, then now, we can finally step into the future."]]
Two months pass after the serial killings that shocked Wrankwood. The final day of Moira's trial is soon, and it seemed clear cut that she would be receiving the death penalty for her crimes. Tippi visits Moira in prison one last time and tells her about the fate of the movie. The production crew had worked tirelessly to edit the footage and the movie was submitted to the Wrankwood Avant-Garde Film Festival. ''Lady Dupont's'' bizarre cinematography and thought-provoking plot made it stand out against the other submissions, making it a hot topic amongst attendees. While it's difficult to say how the film will perform in the box office, the entire crew—especially Greta, Steven, and Melanie—all feel satisfied with their hard work.<ref name=ST3>[[AD-ST-3]]</ref>

As the movie finishes screening, Lucian remains inside the theater with his old compatriots from the Crimson Troupe, [[Shalem]] and [[Playwright]]. Lucian tells them he will continue walking the path laid out before him, as until he is certain that what he creates can match his joy and pain. The new [[Tragodia]] welcomes both of them to follow him: to create a new "Crimson Troupe", one that will be free from its absurd, wretched fate.<ref name=ST3/>
