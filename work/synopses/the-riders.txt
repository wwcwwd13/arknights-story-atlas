==Synopsis==
===Vecchi Rancori===
It has been one year since [[Volsinii]] became Nuova Volsinii, and the city has seen many changes since then. Acting Mayor [[Vigil|Leontuzzo]] has been hard at work with the new Supreme Justice [[Penance|Lavinia]] to create a [[Siracusa]]n city that was truly free of the [[Siracusa/Branches#Mafia Famiglie|Dodici Famiglie]]'s influence. A new town hall and courthouse district was constructed, together with the assembly of a proper police force. Their most significant action was the creation of the New Urban Management Act, designed to constrain the mafia's illegal activities within the city.<ref name=":ST1">[[PV-ST-1]]</ref> The Famiglie have been forced to comply with the rules, though that hasn't stopped them from abusing its loopholes to get what they want.

[[File:56 i18.png|thumb|Preparations for Carnevale are in full swing.]]
As an acknowledgement of Leontuzzo's efforts, [[Signora Sicilia]] has lifted the ban on a traditional festive event known as Carnevale. Originally known for the infamous bloodshed it causes every year, Leontuzzo promises that this year's event is set to celebrate a new era of change for Siracusa.<ref name=":ST1"/> However, behind the scenes, there are those that seek to overturn Nuova Volsinii's new order. [[Antonio Venezia]] of Famiglia Venezia is disgusted by this so-called peace and plans to launch a hostile takeover of the city during Carnevale. He has been using his transport company, Venezia Trucking, to secretly smuggle weapons into the city, and has coerced several drivers of the local Truckers' Union to do secret deliveries for him.<ref name=":1Af">[[PV-1/Story#After operation|PV-1 After]]</ref> All the while, he has been careful not to outright violate any of the laws.

Even the [[Signori dei Lupi]] have begun to bring their game into Nuova Volsinii. [[Caesar]], a gentle yet manipulative wolf, has been quietly urging [[Projekt Red]] in her dreams to resume the game. As a result, she escapes [[Rhodes Island]] and goes to the city in order to kill one of the last remaining Fangs, [[Lunacub]].<ref name=":5Af">[[PV-5/Story#After operation|PV-5 After]]</ref> Another Signore dei Lupi, [[Vargo]], sensing that he is about to lose, travels to [[Higashi]] and makes a deal with a former assassin of Famiglia Venezia named [[Vulpisfoglia|Ingrid]]. In exchange for protecting his Fang, a tailor named [[Umberto de Montano]], Vargo promises to tell Ingrid the identity of the one who inflicted her daughter [[Suzuran]] with [[Oripathy]].<ref name=":3Be">[[PV-3/Story#Before operation|PV-3 Before]]</ref>

===Eventi Sfortunati===
[[File:56 i29.png|thumb|Who could have foreseen that a single car accident would involve so many different factions?]]
The date is now November 5th, 1100. Leontuzzo arranges for some of the Trucker Union's drivers to help with the shooting of his ad campaign. One of them, [[Sommer]], comes up with an excuse to not go so he can take care of Venezia Trucking's secret shipment. However, sensing that something is wrong, [[Crownslayer|Lyudmila]] follows him to the truck and decides to accompany him for the delivery. Sommer tells her that they're just delivering car parts, but what he doesn't realise is that all the items he's been transporting also contain illegal weapons hidden inside them.<ref name=":6Be">[[PV-6/Story#Before operation|PV-6 Before]]</ref>

During the drive, Lyudmila sees Projekt Red in the distance and instantly recognises her. While fighting Projekt Red, one of Lunacub's arrows accidentally punctures the tires of Sommer's truck. Leontuzzo, who was driving to a restaurant after finishing the ad shoot, also had his tires pierced by a different arrow. The two vehicles inevitably crash at the crossroads, leaving Leon's car in flames.<ref name=":6Be"/>

[[Figurino|Luchino]], Umberto's grandson, was on his way to deliver some clothes to the Saluzzos when he witnesses the crash. While trying to help Leon, he notices the contraband weapons which had been hidden inside Sommer's cargo. [[Capone]] and [[Gambino]], who now work for Famiglia Venezia, arrive at the scene and quickly subdue him to prevent any witnesses.<ref name=":3Be"/>They don't see Lyudmila tumble out on the other side as she chases after Projekt Red.<ref name=":6Be"/> Sommer, panicked that the police might discover his smuggling, hastily drives the truck away. He hides the damaged truck at the Nuova Volsinii Port and disguises it as one of the parade floats being kept there. [[Lappland]], who had witnessed everything from a rooftop, calls the police about the accident.<ref name=":7Be">[[PV-7/Story#Before operation|PV-7 Before]]</ref>

===Nella Tana del Lupo===
[[File:56 i30.png|thumb|left|"In the end, he is still my ''fratello.''"]]
Leontuzzo was quickly rescued by paramedics and brought to hospital, but his injuries were severe and he remains in a coma. Lavinia suppresses the information about his involvement in the accident to prevent panic from spreading. She suspects that one of the famiglie must be involved and starts by questioning [[Demetri Certaldo|Demetri]], who Leontuzzo was supposed to meet at a restaurant that night. Demetri admits that they have a difference in opinion, but still considers Leon as his sworn brother and denies any involvement.<ref name=":3Af">[[PV-3/Story#After operation|PV-3 After]]</ref>

The head of the Truckers' Union, [[Eirene Lavazza]], finds Sommer, who confesses that he has been smuggling goods for Famiglia Venezia, and that he was the driver that crashed into Leontuzzo. Eirene makes him hand over a list of everyone involved before knocking him out and locking him in a warehouse.<ref name=":1Af"/> She originally intended to request help from Judge Lavinia, but after realising that the law may not be able to help her, she instead turns to the Saluzzos. In exchange for the truck, which can be used as blackmail material against the Venezias, [[Alberto Saluzzo|Alberto]] wants the Truckers' Union to do some favors for his wine business, Saluzzo Vino.<ref name=":2Be">[[PV-2/Story#Before operation|PV-2 Before]]</ref>

===Lasciare la Famiglia===
[[File:56 i06.png|thumb|There's no room for her old famiglia in her heart.]]
Judge Lavinia receives a report that a [[Vulpo]] woman was caught on camera at the time of the crash. Lavinia recognises the woman as Ingrid, whom she had met last night during her investigation. Realising that Ingrid could pose a threat to public safety, she sets up a trap at a nearby Rhodes Island branch office, predicting that she might go there due to the company's connection with Suzuran. As expected, she finds Ingrid trying to capture Lunacub there. Lavinia attempts to put charges on her when Antonio appears and smoothly bails Ingrid out, promising to compensate for her damages. Lavinia is forced to let Ingrid go.<ref name=":4Be">[[PV-4/Story#Before operation|PV-4 Before]]</ref>

After Ingrid refused Antonio's request to rejoin the famiglia, Lappland suddenly appears and tells her that Luchino is being kept hostage at Famiglia Venezia's estate. Ingrid informs Umberto of his grandson's whereabouts and accompanies him there. Umberto meets with the famiglia don, [[Fabrizio Venezia|Fabrizio]], and reveals a shocking truth to Luchino; that he was a former assassin of the Venezias. Umberto expresses his earnest wishes that Fabrizio would let Luchino go to live a life away from the mafiosi. Fabrizio allows the two to leave unharmed as a show of "decency," much to Antonio's displeasure.<ref name=":4Af">[[PV-4/Story#After operation|PV-4 After]]</ref> Afterwards, Ingrid decides to hand over her famiglia crest, officially leaving the Venezias for good.<ref name=":ST2">[[PV-ST-2]]</ref>

===Dalla Verita alle Ceneri===
[[Texas the Omertosa|Texas]], who had only recently arrived at Nuova Volsinii, receives a phone call from Lappland. The two meet on the street just outside the hospital as it starts to rain. Lappland says that she's been acting as a taxi driver for a while and brings Texas to the port for "a show."<ref name=":7Af"/>

[[File:56 i03.png|thumb|left|Fire burns everything, both the good and the bad.]]
Lyudmila rushes to Nuova Volsinii port in a bid to find Sommer's truck before the others do. She manages to find it hidden under a tarp next to the parade floats. Looking inside, she realises that Sommer has been unknowingly transporting contraband weapons. Lyudmila knows that the Truckers' Union will be in serious trouble, and with the cops scouring the port, she ultimately decides to burn the truck in order to destroy all the evidence.<ref name=":6Af">[[PV-6/Story#After operation|PV-6 After]]</ref> Eirene, who had intended to hand the truck over to Alberto Saluzzo that afternoon, was only greeted by the sight of a flaming wolf float and police surrounding them.<ref name=":2Af">[[PV-2/Story#After operation|PV-2 After]]</ref>

With the incident happening so close to Carnevale, the new government quickly set up a trial to resolve the matter swiftly. They place Eirene on trial under suspicion of arson. Fortunately–or unfortunately–two witnesses step up to testify: Alberto Saluzzo and Antonio Venezia. Both of them vouch for Eirene's innocence in order to curry favour with the Truckers' Union and prevent her from revealing any more than necessary. Both famiglie were essentially vying for control over the union. The trial ends with Eirene's acquittal, but the matter is far from over.<ref name=":7Af">[[PV-7/Story#After operation|PV-7 After]]</ref>

===Carnevale Sanguinoso===
[[File:56 i19.png|thumb|Only one mob can stop another.]]
Upon hearing about the outcome of the trial from Texas and Lappland, Lavinia decides to crack down on the famiglie. She instructs the police to temporarily arrest Alberto Saluzzo for corporate qualification issues and probe into Venezia Trucking's factories. Antonio, wisely sensing that his time was running out, decides to forcibly usurp Famiglia Venezia from Fabrizio. He uses his newfound authority to make an emergency dispatch order to a nearby Venezia landship, which will bring dozens of soldiers to Nuova Volsinii.<ref name=":8Af">[[PV-8/Story#After operation|PV-8 After]]</ref>

Lyudmila searches for Sommer after he suddenly leaves the Trucker Camp, but all she could find was his lighter, in the hands of mafia gangsters. Assuming the worst, Lyudmila hurries to pick up Eirene at the courthouse and informs her about Sommer's death.<ref name=":8Be">[[PV-8/Story#Before operation|PV-8 Before]]</ref> Eirene also hears about what happened to Saluzzo Vino and Venezia Trucking from her. Eirene fears that Lavinia's actions have seriously agitated the famiglie, and that they may be planning to assassinate her during Carnevale. As a precaution she calls on all union employees to protect her.<ref name=":8Af"/>

===La Violenzia Tornera===
[[File:56 i12 a.png|thumb|left|"Unleash your inner beasts! Just like how in old Siracusa!"]]
The night of the highly anticipated Carnevale has arrived. Everyone in Nuova Volsinii is prepared for the festivities, in their own way. From his window, Antonio orders his men to seize the courthouse, town hall, and the entire administrative district. As Venezia mobsters rush to the courthouse, a brigade of union trucks suddenly appear and form a blockade.<ref name=":9Be"/> Eirene enters the courthouse and hands Lavinia a list of evidence implicating the Dodici Famiglie and asks her to initiate emergency judicial procedures. Her signature is all it would take to officially make the mafia outlaws and legalise killing them. Yet in spite of the rapidly escalating situation, Lavinia refuses stating, that it can only be used as a last resort.<ref name=":9Af">[[PV-9/Story#After operation|PV-9 After]]</ref>

As if things weren't bad enough, Lappland suddenly appears, driving the landship she had stolen from the Venezias into Court Square. [[Lappland the Decadenza|Dressed in fanciful black and red]], she has decorated the landship into a magnificent stage. She kicks crates full of the Venezia's weaponry into the crowd below, tempting the hungry wolves to make their first move. The Truckers' Union and the famiglie take the weapons, both sides now locked in a deadly standoff. Texas appears to confront Lappland, but the latter is undeterred. Lappland challenges Texas to a bet: that by the time the parade procession arrives in fifteen minutes, the whole plaza will be turned into a sea of blood. Even the Signori dei Lupi have gathered here at Zaaro's behest, eager to witness a most spectacular show.<ref name=":9Af"/>

===Chiamata alla Ribalta===
[[File:56 i15.png|thumb|A toast to those idealists who have chosen to keep the city streets clean of blood.]]
Antonio, infuriated by Lappland's meddling, steps outside his house to take care of the situation personally. The moment he does, he is met by Capone, who sold him out to Ingrid. Antonio finally confesses that he was the culprit who caused Suzuran's Oripathy to agitate Ingrid and run her out of the famiglia. Antonio tries to kill her first, but Ingrid's blade fatally pierces his stomach. Ingrid leaves him to bleed out in the alley.<ref name=":10Be">[[PV-10/Story#Before operation|PV-10 Before]]</ref> As Antonio tries to crawl toward the square, one of his former subordinates appears and shoots him in the head on the escaped Fabrizio's orders.<ref name=":10Af">[[PV-10/Story#After operation|PV-10 After]]</ref>

The surprise gunshot rattles everyone in Court Square. Eirene, snapping back to reality, convinces the mafia to unanimously agree to lay down their weapons and clean everything up before the parade arrives. Leontuzzo, who had woken up from his coma, arrives at Court Square with Demetri and the rest of the procession. The crowd, which had been prepared to slaughter each other moments before, mix in with the rest of the parade and welcome the acting mayor with cheers. Leontuzzo steps onto the stage and makes his inaugural speech, ushering in a new era for Nuova Volsinii.<ref name=":10Af"/>

===Nuova Era===
Amidst the bright lights of Carnevale, Projekt Red infiltrates Umberto's shop, intending to kill him. However, she is bewildered by how unafraid he seems, and only watches silently as the tailor passes away in his sleep.<ref name=":10Be"/> Shortly after, she finds Lunacub and strikes her down. Caesar appears before Projekt Red, congratulating her for winning the game… and then abandons her.<ref name=":10Af"/> Lyudmila saves Lunacub and tracks Projekt Red down, intending to kill her nemesis. Instead, all she finds is a broken Lupo aimlessly wandering the alleyways. The Signori dei Lupi's game has ended, but there is no satisfaction for the winning Fang.<ref name=":ST4">[[PV-ST-4]]</ref>

[[File:56 i17 1.png|thumb|left|The real winner of the game is not any Lupi, but Lappland, the new Alpha!]]
Following Carnevale's conclusion, Eirene had intended to resign for her negligence, but Leontuzzo reassures her that while the Truckers' Union will face penalties, City Hall will not be pursuing the smuggling or Carnevale incident. In fact, he is considering integrating the union into a government department to avoid such a problem from occurring again. Lyudmila is grateful for all the union has done, though warns Eirene that if she ever stepped on the wrong path, she would personally stop her. Lyudmila then leaves for Rhodes Island, dragging back Projekt Red with her in preparation for facing Kal'tsit.<ref name=":ST4"/>

Fabrizio cuts ties with Venezia Trucking and hands over everyone involved with Antonio to the local police. Despite being slapped with an arrest warrant, Ingrid leaves Nuova Volsinii with a smile on her face. The Bellones will be auditing all of Saluzzo Vino's business operations, meaning that Alberto's temporary stay in prison might be much longer, much to Lappland's glee. Having made an unforgettable impression at Carnevale, Lappland becomes the "alpha" of the Signori dei Lupi.<ref name=":ST4"/> The other [[Beast Lord|Beast Aristocrats]] have arrived in the city to discuss "important matters," and they will undoubtedly be astonished when they meet the wolves' representative.<ref name=":9Be">[[PV-9/Story#Before operation|PV-9 Before]]</ref>
