==Welcome to the Jungle!==

===A Rather (Un)friendly Welcome===
[[File:Wake Up, Doctor.png|thumb|"Wake the hell up, Doctor. We have a jungle to conquer."]]
After being unconscious for some time, the [[Doctor]] wakes up in the middle of a [[Sargon]]ian dune with [[Gavial]] and [[Ceobe]] at their side. Gavial tells them that while en route to her homeland, the [[Rhodes Island]] aircraft they were on was shot down with most of R.I. personnel accompanying them (including [[Blaze]], [[Croissant]], and [[Utage]]) landing separately. When returning to the crash site, several [[Archosauria]]ns surround [[Lancet-2]], who happens to know Gavial; not long after, [[Tomimi]], a childhood friend of Gavial, appears and is overjoyed upon seeing her. Tomimi leads them into the jungles of [[Acahualla]] &ndash; their homeland. Meanwhile, Ceobe accidentally eats hallucinogenic [[Magnificent Mushroom|shrooms]] that cause her to be lost in [[Ceobe's Fungimist|a hallucination]].

===Thick or Thin?===
While en route to the deeper parts of Acahualla, Tomimi briefs Gavial on the jungle's situation while she was away. She explains there are four dominant Tiacauh tribes: their own Wilderness Will tribe (which has since renamed to "Gavial's Will"), [[Eunectes|Zumama]]'s tribe, the Flint tribe, and the [[Inam]] Committee. Following an "accident" that befell the previous Great Chief Juan, the leader of all Tiacauh, a Mahuizzotia festival to elect a new Great Chief is to be held. They also witness a humorous argument between two Archosaurian Tiacauhs on whether thick or thin tails are the best.

[[File:The Big Ugly Thing.png|thumb|"Let's try this 'Big Bad Ugly Thing'!"]]
At the temple where the Mahuizzotia festival is to be held, Gavial and the gang are surprised to see loudspeakers playing music from [[Alive Until Sunset]]. Tomimi explains that the band stayed with them for some time and gifted them the speakers and signed records. The festival itself goes smoothly, despite a disturbance caused by Ceobe, as the Doctor learns more about Gavial while watching the battles between the Great Chief contestants. The festival culminates in a duel between Zumama and Gavial after the [[Phidia]] easily beats down the other contestants. Towards the end of the duel, [[The Big Ugly Thing|a massive walker]], dubbed the "Big Ugly Thing", emerges from the forest and defeats Gavial before destroying the temple with its cannon, as ordered by Zumama. She claims the Great Chief mantle for herself and vows to usher in a new era to the Tiacauhs.

Gavial holds no grudge towards Zumama and accepts her loss, but after learning that she had her Eunectes tribesman go into an [[Originium]]-infested iron mine, exposing them to [[Oripathy]], Gavial changes her mind and goes to speak with Zumama. Croissant is located safe and sound in the Inam Committee's village, and Utage also turns up. Meanwhile, Zumama uses stolen parts from the fallen R.I. plane to improve the Big Ugly Thing and sends [[Flint|Kemar]] of the Flint tribe to stop Gavial. En route, Kemar runs into Blaze and, upon being defeated by her, the [[Liberi]] fighter begs to be trained by the elite warrior. Blaze agrees, and takes Kemar with her as she reunites with Gavial and co.

Behind the scenes it's revealed that Tomimi had planned to "persuade" Gavial to stay in Acahualla and become the next Great Chief after dealing with Zumama and her walker. Before coming to Rhodes Island, Gaviel had been the best fighter and many Tiacauhs were still in agreement that she should be the next Great Chief.

==Might Makes Right==
[[File:SPLASHY TIME.png|thumb|"It's still holiday, isn't it?"]]
Gavial and co. spend some time enjoying the scenic beauty of a waterfall in Acahualla before Ceobe, still in her drug-induced craze, runs off to the Eunectes tribe's village, prompting the operators to cut their breaks short to go after the [[Perro]]. They arrive just as Zumama and the [[High Priest]] successfully install the R.I. aircraft engine into Zumama's Big Ugly Thing.

After Gavial smacks Ceobe back to her senses, the Archosauria confronts Zumama over her actions which endangered her people and Zumama reveals that she has been suffering from Oripathy for a while too. Gavial then asks her to return the R.I. aircraft engine, but the Phidia refuses, only conceding that R.I. can recover the engine if Gavial could best the Big Ugly. Before the fight, Zumama recalls her and Gavial's childhood memory of seeing a Sargonian [[nomadic city]] which inspired Zumama to study mechanics and ultimately led to her building the Big Ugly Thing after meeting the High Priest. Amidst the fight, Big Ugly suddenly goes haywire and begins attacking everything in sight, forcing Zumama and Gavial to join forces to stop the machine.

[[File:Gavial and Zumama.png|thumb|"Do you remember the day when we see civilization?"|alt=]]
With their combined forces, Gavial and Zumama are able to destroy Big Ugly and recover the R.I. aircraft engine. Gavial convinces Zumama to come back to Rhodes Island with her so she can treat her Oripathy. As they are about to leave, Tomimi stops Gavial, insisting that she must stay. Tomimi explains that Gavial had saved her life when they were younger (the event when Gavial herself contracted Oripathy) and says that Gavial is the true next Great Chief and cannot be allowed to leave. She unleashes the Tiacauh warriors of Gavial's Will to stop them by force and even take Lancet-2 and the pilot Dylan hostage, but they are defeated soundly and humorously.  In the end, the Doctor reveals that Tomimi is the one who shot down the R.I. aircraft as part of her plan to trap Gavial in Acahualla, and it ends with a funny scene where Gavial spanks Tomimi for all the trouble she caused and leaves.

The R.I. team eventually returns, but they also take in newcomers. Zumama is finally persuaded to come along out of her amaze for Lancet-2 and wishes to learn technologies from the outside world, and Kemar follows suits to follow Blaze's footsteps. Gavial settles her tribal matter by allowing Tomimi to come with her and appoints Inam as the new Great Chief, hoping to kick off a new era for the Tiacuah: an era where the leaders are not just the physically strongest, but diplomatic and rational.

Upon returning to R.I., [[Kal'tsit]] is none too pleased with the destruction of the aircraft and cuts the Doctor and Blaze's salary to cover the repair costs as a punishment. Nevertheless she tells the Doctor that their (mis)adventure in Acahualla is valuable to broaden their knowledge about the world of [[Terra]].
