==Synopsis==
===Conferre Gentibus===
[[File:Welcome to Laterano.png|thumb|The First Summit of Nation being held at Laterano's Basilica]]
15 March 1099, three days before the Summit of Nations. The [[Laterano|Lateran City]] is ready for this moment in history. Since last year, Laterano has been busy sending out invitations and bringing envoys from countries all over the world, from [[Victoria]] to [[Kjerag]]. This conference will be held at the Pope's Basilica where, for the first time ever, nations of [[Terra]] will discuss worldwide security issues and establish stronger diplomatic relations. Even though only a handful of envoys and representatives arrive at the Summit, the current Pope, [[Yvangelista XI]], is grateful to see their willingness to participate.<ref name="ST1">[[GA-ST-1]]</ref>

Down at Pagus Stevonus, [[Enforcer|Ezell Pastore]], a trainee executor of the Notarial Hall, is on an ordinary mission to reclaim the patron firearm of a recently deceased woman named Feoria La Porta. Upon completing his task, he sees a young Sankta girl suddenly fainting on the street.<ref name="ST1"/> Ezell quickly brings her to the hospital but discovers something incredibly unusual with her. For a brief moment, Cecelia’s halo and wings become fragmented, similar to those belonging to [[Sankta#Fallen|Fallen Sankta]]. After hearing that Cecelia’s mother was taken away by people in gowns, Ezell promises Cecelia to protect her and find her mother.<ref name="1Bef">[[GA-1/Story#Before operation|GA-1 Before]]</ref>

In another ward, [[Fiametta]] and [[Mostima]] are paying a visit to [[Exusiai|Lemuel]]'s sister, [[Lemuen]], who is currently undergoing physical therapy. Aware of their affiliation with the Lateran Curia, one of the doctors shows them an unusual physical checkup report–belonging to Cecelia–something so concerning that Mostima makes a direct call to the pope. Realizing the severity of the situation, Fiametta rushes to find Cecelia, only to find a group of fighters led by her old Pontifica Cohors comrade, a [[Liberi]] named [[Patia]]. They had attempted to take Cecelia but were foiled by Ezell, who then escaped with her.<ref name="1Aft">[[GA-1/Story#After operation|GA-1 After]]</ref>

===Angels and Demons===
[[File:Rise and Shine alt.png|thumb|left|Could this girl really hold a secret that could overturn Laterano?]]
Sensing that something is amiss, Ezell brings Cecelia back to her house. Upon inspecting the address, he realizes that Cecelia’s mother was actually Feoria, the woman who had passed away earlier that morning. As he tries to explain this to the young child, a [[Laterano/Overview#Legatus|Legatus]] named [[Oren Argiolas]] suddenly appears, intending to bring Cecelia back to the Basilica. He reveals a shocking truth to Ezell: that despite being a Sankta, Cecelia is a mixed-blood child, something that should be fundamentally impossible. What’s worse, Cecelia’s father is actually a Sarkaz–the mortal enemy of the Sankta.<ref name="2Aft">[[GA-2/Story#After operation|GA-2 After]]</ref>

Fiametta then arrives at the scene and demands that Ezell hand over the girl. Ezell is forced to comply and follows her to the Basilica with Cecelia. Along the way, they run into a Sarkaz peddler that has somehow infiltrated the Holy City. Fiametta switches priorities and pursues them, instructing Ezell to continue onwards to the Basilica. However, the kind-hearted executor is worried that Cecelia would be condemned to death if she is handed over to the Lateran Curia. He disobeys orders and sneaks her away to the Ecclesia Requietum, a mortuary located at the edge of town where Feoria’s body would have been transported to.<ref name="3Aft">[[GA-3/Story#After operation|GA-3 After]]</ref>

===Revelation===
At the Mortuary, Ezell meets [[Andoain]], a seemingly innocuous friar who is actually the leader of the "[[Pathfinders]]," the same group that tried to kidnap Cecelia at the hospital. He says that their goal is to open the doors of Laterano’s paradise to those who need it, which includes Feoria, who had requested they take care of Cecelia after her death. Even though Ezell is suspicious of him, they come to a mutual agreement to help Cecelia bury her mother.<ref name="4Bef">[[GA-4/Story#Before operation|GA-4 Before]]</ref><ref name="4Aft">[[GA-4/Story#After operation|GA-4 After]]</ref> The funeral, however, is rudely interrupted by Fiammetta and Mostima, who are tasked to apprehend their old comrade.<ref name="5Bef">[[GA-5/Story#Before operation|GA-5 Before]]</ref> With Andoain's [[Originium Arts]], the Pathfinders are able to escape while bringing Cecelia with them.<ref name="5Aft">[[GA-5/Story#After operation|GA-5 After]]</ref>

The incident causes Laterano to be on high alert, but they cannot announce a state of emergency with the Summit of Nations so close. Fiametta deduces that since the Pathfinders have obtained their target, their next objective would be to exfiltrate the city. Border security is immediately tightened and the Apostolic Knights are dispatched to search for them. The authority of the case is handed over to Fiametta and Ezell is forced to take a “day off.” He tries to brush it off as just another day at work, but the incident has clearly disturbed him.<ref name="5Aft"/>

[[File:Amazing Grace.png|thumb|The innocent half-Sankta rings the bell with her Sarkaz song]]
Inside the Pathfinders’ safehouse, Andoain tells Cecelia about Laterano’s bell tower, the Tower of Revelations, which he says is the closest point to heaven. Cecelia leaves the group so that she may say her goodbyes from atop the tower. Andoain relays this information to Ezell, who hurries to find her. He discovers Legatus Oren trying to kidnap Cecelia, intending to use her unique identity to blackmail Laterano’s government. Fortunately, Federico, another executor of the Notarial Hall, arrives to arrest him.<ref name="6Bef">[[GA-6/Story#Before operation|GA-6 Before]]</ref> 

From atop the Tower of Revelations, Cecelia sings an ancient Sarkaz folk song. Everyone listens in pure awe as the tower’s bell suddenly rings for the first time since Laterano's foundation a thousand years ago. From the Basilica, Pope Yvangelista XI instructs Cardinal [[Velliv]] to inform the foreign diplomats that this momentous occasion marks a turning point in Laterano’s history, signalling an era of change. Under the order of His Holiness, an Apostolic Knight is immediately dispatched to the tower to protect Cecelia and escort her to the Basilica.<ref name="6Aft">[[GA-6/Story#After operation|GA-6 After]]</ref> 

===Providence===
[[File:Actus Fidei.png|thumb|left|"Only one of us will survive the ordeal; may the Law bless us."]]
However, at the same time, the Pathfinders launch an assault on the foreign envoys’ village. Amidst the chaos, Andoain finds an opportunity to enter the Basilica and meet the Pope personally. After being plagued by his misery for years, Andoain could finally ask the Pope his question: Why does Laterano refuse to open its doors to outsiders when it is clearly capable of helping them?

To Andoain, this neglect was more than plain ignorance—it was a betrayal that condemned his hometown in [[Iberia]] to destruction and abandoned all who still clawed for life in the brutal wastes. That was why Andoain formed the Pathfinders, to provide salvation for non-Sankta. On the other hand, the Pope criticizes his beliefs as unrealistic, coldly stating that Laterano only exists because of the Sankta, and will exist only <i>for</i> the Sankta. The two then raise their guns and shoot each other to decide whose beliefs are correct. Surprisingly, both Andoain and the Pope remain "unfallen."<ref name="7Aft">[[GA-7/Story#After operation|GA-7 After]]</ref> 

[[File:The Providence.png|thumb|Andoain is allowed to understand the secret of "The Law", but what standing in front of him is simply beyond his comprehension]]
To fully answer Andoain's questions, the Pope reveals the hidden truth. The Sankta is indeed a privileged race thanks to the commandments provided by the Law. [[The Law]] is an “absolute being” that determines the Sankta's fate; only by the Law can Laterano enjoy peace. The Pope then brings Andoain to the underground basement of the Basilica. To Andoain's astonishment (or perhaps horror), he witnesses the true form of the Law–something so incomprehensible that it instantly shatters his beliefs in the Lateran faith.<ref name="7Aft"/>

{{Quote|...It is not something man is capable of establishing or interpreting. It is not something that interpretations, analyses, debates, or reforms could influence. It is not a matter of whether or not you believe in It, why you believe in It, or how you believe in It… It exists.|Andoain}}

===Agnus Dei===
[[File:Martyrdom.png|thumb|He may never become a true Lateran, but his gun will forever remain here.]]
Andoain, broken and lost, stares emptily from the rooftop of the Basilica. From behind, Fiammetta and Mostima have confront him. Rather than arresting him, Fiametta aims to take revenge on the man who put Lemuen in hospital and caused Mostima to become a fallen angel.<ref name="8Bef">[[GA-8/Story#Before operation|GA-8 Before]]</ref> A duel breaks out and Fiammetta's burning anger wakes him up from his misery. From afar, Lemuen uses her sniper to knock Andoain's gun from his hands. Andoain retreats, stating that he will continue walking his path, and that they will meet again one day.<ref name="8Aft">[[GA-8/Story#After operation|GA-8 After]]</ref>

After the chaos is settled, the Pope gathers the foreign envoys in the conference hall. He apologizes for the incident and wishes the Summit can be carried on smoothly. Throughout his speech, he expresses concerns about a potential worldwide conflict, especially since the [[Battle of the Four Emperors]] and the fall of the [[Gaul|Gaulish Empire]]. The purpose of this Summit is to call for every nation to resolve their conflicts via diplomatic means. The Pope's speech is later known as the ''Lateran Declaration'' in Terran history, setting the precedent for future summits.<ref name="8Aft"/>

In the aftermath, everyone goes their own ways. Much to Ezell’s surprise, Cecelia is permitted to leave Laterano, and the Pope even goes as far as to secretly give her citizenship. Together with Ezell, she embarks on a journey to search for her Sarkaz father.<ref name="8Bef">[[GA-8/Story#Before operation|GA-8 Before]]</ref> Fiametta is dispatched to Rhodes Island in order to deepen the Lateran Curia’s relationship with the company. Lemuen officially joins the Lateran Curia’s Seventh Tribunal, thus becoming Mostima’s superior, much to the latter’s annoyance.<ref name="ST2">[[GA-ST-2]]</ref>

As punishment for collaborating with the Pathfinders, Oren is stripped of his title as a Legatus. As for the Pathfinders themselves, they were forgiven by His Holiness and allowed to leave the country. Still, Andoain, Patia, and his followers depart with newfound resolve, vowing to bring the fire of reform to Laterano some day. They will continue to challenge the old order, even if it means becoming martyrs.<ref name="ST2"/>
