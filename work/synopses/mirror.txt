==Hu Jun==
After her resignation from the Judicial Review Office of Yan, [[Leizi the Thunderbringer|Lin Qingyan]] begins her return trip to the [[Tianshi]] Bureau when she receives a pressing letter from Candleholder [[Zuo Le]]. He informs that his companion, [[Yun Qingping]], had disappeared while they were in [[Baizao]]. The only clue was that he had left the city and wandered north towards Mt. Mang. Lin Qingyan follows his directions and stumbles into [[Mangshan|Mangshan Town]] at the foot of the mountain. The townspeople were eccentric, speaking and acting in unusual ways. While trying to gather information, one of the townsfolk she was talking to accidentally drops a conspicuous letter. She follows the girl's trail to return the letter, but the girl has disappeared, leaving only her umbrella behind. Lin decides to open the letter, revealing the location and time of a secret deal taking place.

At one of Mangshan Town's local teahouses, two burly men impatiently wait at the table. A few minutes later, a female [[Liberi]] with black and white hair enters the teahouse. The trio chat over a pot of tea, with the two men demanding that she hand over her treasure: a secret kung fu tome said to be written by the legendary Grandmaster of [[Yumen]] himself, [[Chongyue]]. The two men had caught wind of the deal and intercepted her at the teahouse in order to take the tome for themselves. Just before things escalate into a fight, the three people suddenly collapse. The tea server, Hu Jun, had poisoned their drinks, seeking to obtain the tome as well. 

After tying up all three, Hu Jun is surprised by the sudden presence of another man in the teahouse, who is none other than Yun Qingping. Yun attempts to persuade Hu Jun to let them go, but the latter refuses, and decides to tie him up as well. Shortly after, Lin Qingyan enters the teahouse to confront Hu Jun about the supposed deal taking place there. Hu Jun flees upstairs with the tome while Lin frees Yun from his binds. As Lin heads outside, she witnesses Hu Jun suddenly crashing out of the upper floor window. When he hits the ground, his body inexplicably shatters... into teapot shards. And the tome is nowhere to be found.<ref>[[Hu Jun]]</ref>

==Mr. Pu==
In order to solve the unexplainable "death" of Hu Jun, Lin Qingyan collects his fragmented remains and questions the locals who witnessed the scene. She learns that Mangshan Town has no local government, so any disputes were solved at the town school, run solely by a man they respectfully call [[Mr. Pu]]. Lin heads to the school and asks Mr. Pu some questions about the town's history. However, it soon becomes clear that Mr. Pu is not being entirely honest. During their conversation, Lin's chair inexplicably flips over, and Mr. Pu uses the opportunity to snatch the pouch containing Hu Jun's remains from her. It was only after the Tianshi left that Mr. Pu realize the pouch contained nothing but pebbles.

Still determined to purloin Hu Jun's remains, Mr. Pu stalks Lin Qingyan, accompanied by "living objects" like brooms, bottles, and even roof tiles. He follows her to the local inn, but is suddenly knocked out by a belligerent swordswoman. Mr. Pu wakes up inside a shoddy building, cornered by the swordswoman, who accuses him of tricking her. The swordswoman reveals that she had forcibly taken the legendary kung fu tome from Hu Jun. She herself is also a geist, and wants Mr. Pu to tell her about the "personification" technique within the tome, but the latter states he knows nothing. Before the swordswoman strikes him down, Lin Qingyan arrives and chases off the assassin with her [[Lei Fa]].

Mr. Pu later brings Lin Qingyan back to his school, where he explains the true history of Mangshan Town. A decent percentage of the town's small population are actually geists, which are possessed beings born out of [[Feranmut]] influence. The town's close proximity to the [[Tomb of Sui]] has resulted in the geists developing human-like intelligence, and can even disguise themselves as humans. Mr. Pu states that rumors have spread about a kung fu tome that can make geists become "true humans", sowing unrest amongst geistkind. Lin promises that she will discover the culprit and returns Hu Jun's remains to the teacher.<ref>[[Mr. Pu (interlude)]]</ref>

==The Story of the Stone==
Lin Qingyan is brought to a manor in Mangshan Town in order to solve an unusual case. Master Wan, a wealthy merchant now bedridden with illness, has reported a missing item. The item in question is a stone he found from the nearby riverbank, which he kept as a good luck charm as he built his entire fortune from the ground up. The box where it was kept has been smashed open, and the stone is nowhere to be found. Lin Qingyan does an investigation of the storeroom where Master Wan's accounts and deeds were also stored. Lin Qingyan also asks about the master's three children, with whom he has a poor relationship with.

[[File:63 mini03.png|thumb|A totally ordinary stone, belonging to a totally ordinary person.]]
Lin Qingyan decides to head to the river where Master Wan once found the stone, which became dry long ago. She discovers two geists in the form of children nearby, one of them being the missing stone. Master Wan's strong willpower had imprinted upon the stone, turning it into a geist, but it does not share his determination. The stone complains to the other geist about her foolhardy owner, who spent years building his own house out of mud and clay. After he became a successful businessman, he was robbed of all his possessions, yet insisted on pushing himself to start from scratch all over again. The stone both resented and pitied him for that. Lin Qingyan interrupts their conversation and takes back the stone to the house.

Lin Qingyan gathers Master Wan's three children in order to explain what had happened to the stone. In truth, the culprit had attempted to break into a cabinet containing Master Wan's deeds, but in doing so accidentally caused the box with the stone to fall from its place. Lin quietly whispers to the second child that she knows what he did and reprimands his audacious behaviour. Afterwards, Lin returns the stone to the dying old man. 

In his final moments, Master Wan is visited by a familiar acquaintance who has attempted to purchase the stone from him for decades. Master Wan finally relinquishes his obsession and dies peacefully on his bed, letting the stone clatter onto the floor.<ref>[[The Story of the Stone]]</ref>

==Bai Jin==
At the behest of a mysterious benefactor, Yun Qingping follows an old man to the base of Mt. Mang, the tallest point in Mangshan Town. Despite entering through the main road, he somehow winds up at the back of the mountain, where he meets an elegantly dressed lady named [[Bai Jin]]. The lady decides to join Yun on his trek up the mountain in hopes to find her missing husband, Lan Ke. He had disappeared one night and she had not seen him since.

As the pair make their way up the mountain, they come across an ancient pavilion. An elderly man named Old Luo greets Yun, and the latter notices that Bai Jin had suddenly disappeared. In the pavilion, a young [[Vulpo]] named [[Lan Ke]] is staring at a weiqi board, unsure how to make his next move. Yun observes the board and notices that the game state is a three ''jie'' cycle; a doomed stalemate that can only end in a draw. Lan Ke insists that he must win or else his wish would not be granted. Yun contemplates carefully, focusing on the overall board instead of the ''jie'' cycle, and moves the piece for Lan Ke. In a single move, he is able to break the deadlock and win the game for him.

Lan Ke rejoices and his wife, Bai Jin, appears again before him. She then reveals her true form as a geist. Lan Ke discovers that over a century had passed since the game began, and that he was an apparition born of Lan Ke's obsession, continuing the game for him. Regardless, he has fulfilled his lifelong dream, and he dissipates into thin air. Bai Jin too, has found solace in meeting her husband one last time, and requests Old Luo to burn her at Lan Ke's cenotaph before reverting into her original form as joss paper.

Yun is then approached by [[Liang]], a sentient mysterious construct created by one of the [[Sui]] proxies. Liang congratulates Yun for winning the weiqi game that had been set up by [[Wang]] long ago and takes him further up the mountain. At the top of the mountain, Yun Qingping meets with the one who called him here: the woman who came to the teahouse with the legendary kung fu tome.<ref>[[Bai Jin (interlude)]]</ref>

==Errant Swordsman==
A man by the name of Zheng Chou is waiting for someone at a quiet teahouse in Mangshan Town. Once, he was an unstoppable warrior who freely walked the path of jianghu. However, seven years ago, at this very teahouse, he had lost his signature sword, the Delivering Blade. He interpreted it as a sign from the heavens and decised to retire from the jianghu life. The next day though, a cold woman approaches him, demanding that he teach her how to wield a sword. When he refuses, the woman makes a bet with him. They will meet again next year at the tea house, and if he can live happily without his sword, then she won't never bother him again.

The next year, the two meet again at the teahouse, where the nameless swordswoman shows off her moves. During their conversation, Zheng Chou inadvertently reveals that he had been injured by some enemies who wanted revenge on him. He had been chased by countless foes, but he chose to flee rather than kill them. Year after year passes, the swordswoman gets better and better, yet Zheng Chou's inner turmoil only grows. At the sixth year, he had even heard that someone had taken up his old sword's name and was using it to commit evil deeds. He once believed that he could flee the jianghu, but now realizes that it is an inescapable vortex. When he tells the swordswoman she should leave Mangshan Town and even offers the title of the Delivering Blade, she replies that it is not time yet.

[[File:63 mini01.png|thumb|"Enough talk. This thunder must strike!"]]
In the present, Zheng Chou continues to wait, but hours pass and the swordswoman has not appeared. The teahouse keeper states that she has gotten involved in several murders across town. Upon hearing a commotion outside, Zheng Chou rushes into the streets to find the swordswoman dueling against Lin Qingyan, who recognizes him from the files. Zheng Chou finally realizes that the swordswoman is actually a geist created by his sword, imbued with his obsession with the jianghu. He helplessly pleads with Lin to spare her, but the Tianshi states that the Delivering Blade has gone out of control. In order to stop her rampage, Lin uses her Lei Fa to smite the swordswoman, reducing her to dust. Just as Lin is about to take the book of kung fu she dropped, [[Huai Tianpei]] suddenly appears and snatches it.

Having lost his sword for the second time, Zheng Chou decides that there is nothing left in town for him. As he prepares to leave, he is suddenly approached by the same swordswoman, who seems to have lost her memories and no longer a geist. Together, they set out on a new journey.<ref>[[Errant Swordsman]]</ref>

==Reaching the Top==
Ten days before Huai Tianpei arrived in Mangshan, the burly man suddenly barged into Yumen's barracks bawling his eyes out and knocking out every soldier on sight. As General [[Zuo Xuanliao]] arrives on site to assess the situation, he receives a report that someone took advantage of the chaos to break into the armory. The Book of Kung Fu gifted by Grandmaster [[Chongyue]] has been stolen, and in its place is a letter left by [[Mo Yi]], a member of the notorious [[Shanhaizhong]], the group that terrorized Yumen just six months ago. Mo Yi promises to give the desperate Huai a rematch with Chongyue at the peak of Mt. Mang. Now, she stands before Yun Qingping with the so-called legendary tome that everyone has been searching for: the Book of Kung Fu that he penned himself.

Around the same time, [[Lee]] and [[Waai Fu]] arrive in Mangshan in their search for Huai Tianpei. They discover that the local clinic is packed with dozens of injured people. The staff says that rumors have been circulating about a secret kung fu tome that can make any fighter into "First Under Heaven", drawing in jianghu adventurers from all over Yan to the area. Lee and Waai Fu run into Lin Qingyan at the clinic, who is also investigating the origins of these rumors. While discussing the situation, they hear a commotion outside, and witness dozens of geists start going up the mountain.

[[File:63 mini02 2.png|thumb|Their strikes shatter heaven and earth, their fists move the winds and clouds.]]
At the peak of Mt. Mang, Mo Yi shows the Book of King Fu to the jianghu adventurers and geists. She plans to spur them into a massive deathmatch and proclaims that the last person standing will be the one to obtain the legendary book. Yun Qingping interrupts the crowd before it becomes a bloodbath, stating that while the book is authentic, it was not imbued with any kind of magical power. He then reveals that the book has since been mass-produced and holds up a printed copy for all to see. Mo Yi soon finds herself surrounded from all sides and is confronted by Lin Qingyan. Cornered, she uses an arcane incantation to summon an illusion of Chongyue's [[Feranmut]] aspect &mdash; Shuo &mdash; from the Book of Kung Fu.

The massive shadow is the manifestation of kung fu itself, easily sweeping away everyone in its path. It is then that Huai Tianpei arrives, ready for his destined rematch. The man duels with the "God", throwing one punch after the other and blocking every blow, standing his ground for thirty rounds, an otherwise impossible feat for a mere mortal. Lee and Waai Fu then arrive at the mountaintop, and the latter's yelling awakens Huai from his stupor. With a final blow, he destroys the shadow of Shuo and falls off the cliff. Zuo Le soon arrives with a brigade from Yumen to contain the situation, permitting the jianghu adventurers to leave without arresting them. Mr. Pu also arrives and scolds the geists for their impertinence before guiding them back to his school. With that, the jianghu spectacle in Mangshan comes to a close.<ref>[[Reaching the Top]]</ref>

==Town Chronicle==
Lin Qingyan, Yun Qingping, and Zuo Le give chase after Mo Yi, the perpetrator behind the chaos in Mangshan Town. After a grueling battle, they were about to capture her when the [[Old Tianshi]] from [[Dahuang]] suddenly intervenes. Mo Yi takes the chance to explain her motives: her real goal for stealing the Book of Kung Fu was so she could meet the Sui proxy who presided over the area: [[Yi]], master of the [[Garden of Grotesqueries]].

A long time ago, Mangshan was completely destroyed by the awakening of the Sui. Yi had tasked Liang to clean up the area, destroy the rogue geists, and move all other geists to the Garden of Grotesqueries. However, Liang discovered that the contaminated geists in this area were developing human-like thought and emotional capabilities. Liang then instructed them to restore the town to its original state and learn to live together with humans. Some of the town's original residents began returning one after the other, transforming Mangshan into what it is today.

Mo Yi seeks a certain item within the garden: a remnant of [[Jie]], the Sui proxy who disappeared over a century ago. She claims that with it, they will be able to prevent war with the bestial Sui. Liang then suggests that Zuo Le and the others assist with the annual ceremony in the garden. Every year, the Sui Regulator sends its best Candleholders to conduct the Placation, a rite to reinforce the seal on the Tomb of Sui. At the same time, they can also look for whatever object that Mo Yi is searching for. The group agrees, and Liang opens up a hidden path into the forbidden grounds of the garden.<ref>[[Town Chronicle]]</ref>
