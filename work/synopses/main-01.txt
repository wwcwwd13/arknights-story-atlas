==Wrathful Calamity==
[[File:Devastation of Chernobog.png|thumb|The Catastrophe destroying the city of Chernobog]]
As [[Rhodes Island]] passes through the [[Reunion]]-occupied [[Chernobog]], they begin noticing signs of a worst-case scenario. They pick up a metallic smell in the air, and the sky gets abnormally dark—all signs of an imminent [[Catastrophe]]. The rescue team is forced to take cover as the storm strikes Chernobog, destroying much of the city and leaving behind massive chunks of Originium. Catastrophe events are the main source of [[Oripathy]], an incurable disease caused by the [[Originium]] that these disasters leave behind. Chernobog has now become a living hell.

For the R.I. rescue team, however, there is something worse than the Catastrophe. Intercepting them are numerous skilled fighters and their leader: [[Talulah]] of Reunion. As a fierce [[Draco]], her merciless Flame [[Originium Arts|Arts]] can easily melt the surrounding buildings. As she tries to incinerate the team, she haughtily proclaims that their goal was fulfilled the moment the Catastrophe struck the city. All she wants to see is the common folk suffer the disease and persecution that the Infected have been bearing for ages.

==Ends Justify the Means==
[[File:Reunion Leaders.png|thumb|The wrath of Reunion burns all]]
Even though [[Nearl]] charges forward to deal with the Draco, it's futile. Her Arts easily overwhelm Nearl's shield. If [[Amiya]] had not unleashed her black Arts to defend her in turn, Talulah would have killed the [[Kuranta]]. When everything seems hopeless, [[Ace]] steps out to block Talulah's flames with his squad, drawing her ire and buying time for the rescue squad to retreat. Amiya watches the [[Phidia]] man stand tall with his shield as they run, and in the end only Ace and [[Guard (NPC)|Guard]] survive the onslaught. After dragging Guard to safety and binding his wounds, Ace carries on to face Reunion's leadership alone despite his missing arm—until his death. After the Elite Operator falls, Reunion's assembled leadership proclaim him a brave and true warrior.

When the rescue team arrives at the outskirts of Chernobog to wait for their extraction, they encounter a band of [[Sarkaz]] mercenaries led by the enigmatic [[W]]. She recognizes both the [[Doctor]] and Amiya, but refuses to elaborate, instead letting them go. While the rescue team is puzzled by her actions, they immediately leave Chernobog and finally return to base via VTOL. Though their sacrifices were many, the rescue operation was a success.
