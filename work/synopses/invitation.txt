==City of the Three Mountains==
[[File:Arrival at Shangshu.png|thumb|Kroos and Mr. Nothing enjoying the scenery of Shangshu]]
It is said that "the road to Shu is harder than scaling the heavens." Indeed, [[Shangshu]], the City of Three Mountains and Seventeen Peaks, is known for its precipitous location. Yet, mankind has managed to conquer the mountains by literally building [[Nomadic city|nomadic cites]] along their valleys; some mountains are even said to be moved onto mobile platforms. Under the administration of the current magistrate [[Liang Xun]], the city has been prosperous and peaceful for years. 

However, such peace will be broken soon. 

Days after the [[Who is Real|Hui-ch'i incident]], the [[Lava]] gang split themselves into two groups. [[Mr. Nothing]] and [[Kroos]] head to Shangshu through [[Nian]]'s account to search for her eldest sister, [[Ling]] the alcoholic wandering poet. Upon their arrival, they coincidentally meet [[Lee|Mr. Lee]], the founder of the [[Lee's Detective Agency|eponymous detective agency]] in [[Lungmen]], in an inn. According to himself, he is here to bring a smuggled antique goblet to Magistrate Liang with the guidance of his bodyguard [[Shen Lou]]. But the gang has to flee immediately as they become targets of a rich girl named [[Du Yaoye]] who brings some hired gangsters to snatch the parcel.  

After running up and down, they finally arrived at Liang's mansion. Kroos and Mr. Nothing bid farewell while Mr. Lee stays back to have a short reunion with his old friend Liang. With the parcel being brought to the addressee, everything seems to have gone quite smooth enough. 

==The Sacred Goblet==
Little do they know that the goblet will soon become the center of an unusual conflict.

[[File:Confrontation in Shangshu.png|thumb|"You're not going anywhere, Rhodes!"|alt=]]
Firstly, [[Taihe]] the Vice Minister of the Anti-Corruption Bureau and [[Zuo Le]] the young Candleholder of the [[Sui Regulator]], an agency dealing with fragments of the [[Feranmut]] Sui, are investigating the missing [[Dusk]] and the Hui-ch'i incident. At first, they bump into Kroos and Mr. Nothing, who are believed to be accountable for it. However, not only are Kroos and Mr. Nothing let go, but are also warned not to meddle in their "business." This "business" turns out to be their second mission: to have Liang hand over the goblet. They had originally intended to stage a show in which the goblet will be snatched by a thief and handed to Zuo. But with the presence of Mr. Lee and the [[Rhodes Island]] duo, the plan has changed to avoid the outsiders' interference.

Back in Liang's mansion, Liang, Shen, and Mr. Lee conduct a search for the goblet's origin and its owner. They also guard the goblet from falling into others' hands, and Liang even negotiates with Zuo to delay the handling. However, things do turn out well on Liang's side. A [[Rim Billiton]]ian [[Blacknight|bounty huntress]] manages to steal it through her [[slumberfoot]]s that hypnotize everyone in the mansion. To Liang's surprise, the huntress belongs to the side of the Ministry of Rite, the ministry that has odds against the Sui Regulator. He even finds out that [[Ning Ciqiu]], the Rites' Fourth Vice Minister who has been disguising herself as his close colleague, is responsible for the theft.

Lastly, the goblet ignites another conflict within the escort bureau. [[Zheng Qingyue]], the director of the Xingyu Escort Bureau, is the one whom Zuo requested to retrieve the goblet during the staged show. On the other hand, Du Yaoye, Zheng's adopted daughter, wishes to use the goblet as a bargain chip. She wants to prove herself to be the genuine successor of the bureau so that Xingyu will not turn into an ordinary inn. Meanwhile, up in the mountains, a mountain heaver named [[Shang Zhong]] hears news about the goblet. Furiously, Shang plots revenge on Zheng due to an event in his tragic past.

As it turns out, the goblet does have a shady background. It was previously a part of a "sibling" of Sui, the evil [[Wang|"Second Brother"]] who fragmented himself into 181 pieces of black ''go'' stone that were merged with other relics. The goblet was once retrieved by the Ministry of Rites, and the Xingyu Escort Bureau was entrusted with protecting it. However, ten years ago, Xingyu's escort team was met with a bloody ambush by bandits. This tragedy cost the lives of Du's biological father and Shang's only son. Since then, the goblet went missing for a long time until it was discovered by some treasure hunters who smuggled it to Lungmen.

[[File:Tailing Beast.png|thumb|"What...creature is it?"|alt=]]
The goblet itself also has a peculiar power. It could turn anything into "[[Apparition#Waregeists|Waregeists]]." To prevent it from creating chaos, Zuo and Taihe reaffirm they should retrieve the goblet back as soon as possible. In the meantime, Kroos and Mr. Nothing help deal with some of the raging spirits roaming around the mountains and the streets.

==The Eighteenth Peak==
Long ago, the mountains of Shangshu had eighteen peaks. The eighteenth one, the Xunri Peak, was said to have been destroyed by a [[Catastrophe]] thirty years ago. Legends say that the peak suddenly disappeared, as if someone had uprooted it. Many still believe that the peak is hidden somewhere despite scientific refutation.

[[File:How About a Game of Chess?.png|thumb|"To the Eighteenth Peak," that's what she says.]]
This missing eighteenth peak has become the spotlight of the goblet conflict. Through a mysterious whisper in a dream, Lee and Shen follow their way to the location where the peak once stood. They find nothing but its neighboring Qujiang Peak and the nearby Field of Forgotten Water. Mr. Nothing and Kroos climb up the mountain as well, since according to Dusk and Nian, the place should be Ling's hideout. Zuo Le and Taihe follow suit to stop Nian's plan from uniting the siblings of Sui. Meanwhile, Shang challenges Zheng to a duel at the Field.

When everyone arrives at their destination, there is an instant, chaotic clash between various factions. The duel between Shang and Zheng is immediately interrupted by Du, Zuo, and Mr. Lee. Mr. Nothing and Kroos hold Zuo and Taihe back while having Lee going up the mountain. Liang and Shen too come for support to stop the Rites' interference.

==Wrath of the Feranmut==
[[File:Wrath of Sui.png|thumb|Sui, the haughty Feranmut of Yan, descending into the mortal world]]
Amidst the fray, Nian and Dusk suddenly appear in response to Ling's arrival. Everyone realize that they are already up on the eighteenth peak, which has been hidden for years by Ling's space-manipulating power. In the blink of an eye, Dusk trap everyone around the mountain inside her painted world so that the Sui siblings could have a private talk with Zuo. Zuo insists the sisters surrender themselves to the bureau under order of the [[Grand Tutor]]. But with Ling's keen eyes, they discover that the Grand Tutor's order is actually a forgery.  

It turns out that everything they have gone through is a insidious trap set by the "Second Brother." A humongous dragon representing [[Sui-Xiang|Sui's avatar]] has descended from the sky to the eighteenth peak, and the Sui sisters are ready to defy their nailed destiny. In the painted world, Mr. Lee, Kroos, Liang, and the rest meet a dark shadow representing the brother, and only by defeating him through the ''go'' chess match could they leave. Meanwhile, Shen helps others get out of the painted world by literally tearing it apart. 

[[File:The Grand Tutor Enters.png|thumb|The Grand Tutor finally showing up himself for the mess.]]
After the sisters' victory over their avatar, the conflict comes to a grand conclusion. Under Ling's persuasion, the brother admits his defeat, but he vows to return with a smarter strategy someday. Ling retrieves the goblet and destroys it by throwing it towards the very Catastrophe that wiped out the eighteenth peak thirty years ago. To Shang and Zheng, they still could not forgive each other, but they decide to stop the fight for Du's sake. So, Shang decides to leave Shangshu while Zheng prepares his retirement from the bureau. [[Leizi]] and the Grand Tutor arrive to clean up the mess, and Liang is promoted due to his contribution throughout the incident. Under the Grand Tutor's permission, the Sui sisters are finally allowed to stay at R.I. 

Still, the saga of the Sui siblings continues, and fate will have everyone [[Where Vernal Winds Will Never Blow|meet again]] at the garrison city of [[Yumen]].
