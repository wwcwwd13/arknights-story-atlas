==Crushed Earth==
After the [[Chernobog]]-[[Lungmen]] crisis, Gareth and Illa escape into the barrens. Under Gareth's suggestion, the two decide to join the "[[Rusthammer]]s," a violent anarcho-primitivist movement that disavows all forms of civilizations. Illa fears that what they face will be a bunch of lawless bandits, and Gareth's previous encounter with them reinforces her doubts.

Upon reaching the meeting spot, all they see is a rusty hammer with a black banner stuck on a boulder. Suddenly, a band of Rusthammer members emerge. The leader recognizes them as Reunion, yet he mocks them for being weaklings who fear the barrens. To test their will, they are given a shovel, a pick, and a beast trap, and are told to survive in the gorge for a month. Gareth curses them as people who enjoy persecuting the [[Infected]], but the leader only laughs boastfully and even brings a witness of their promise to the couple, who is none other than a former Reunion member called Boone. To the Rusthammers, everyone is equal regardless of Oripathy, social status, or race. Illa convinces Gareth to take the trial, as they have no other choice.

It is only after fifteen days that Gareth and Illa manage to hunt a [[Burdenbeast]]. The two even daydream of holding important positions if more people were to settle in the area. They start out as hunter-gatherers, then manage to cultivate mossoats at the end of the month, learn to find water sources from rivers and oases, and make their own dried meat. On the twenty-ninth day, Gareth and Illa notice an urgent signal in the wilderness, a trail of shoe marks of [[Ursus|Ursine]] soldiers. Gareth prepares to retreat to avoid the soldiers, but Illa wishes to fight. Gareth agrees and follows suit. Just then, the Rusthammer leader shows up and praises their spirit and congratulates them, declaring that they are now members of the group. The Ursine soldiers arrive then, with one of them mocking Gareth for stealing supplies. The Rusthammers appear, the entire ravine echoing with their pounding and roaring as they bring terror upon the Ursine soldiers. Together, Gareth and Illa join the Rusthammers as they cast away the fetters of civilization.

==Flickering Sparks==
Near the [[Minos|Minoan]] village of Akroti, which borders [[Sargon]], [[Pallas]] and her [[Sarkaz]] bodyguard discover a wounded [[Heavyrain]]. Wary that she is a spy, they watch her struggle for two hours before Pallas considers taking her in, despite the mercenary's warning. She laments the callous demeanor the Sarkaz adopt for their survival and notes that while the villagers are sincere enough to accept the Sarkaz mercenaries, a Sargonian soldier is a different matter. Pallas treats Heavyrain's wounds inside a tent. When Heavyrain wakes up, she is startled, but the tent's canvas prevents her from attacking the priestess. Although wary, Heavyrain eventually relents after hearing she is in Minoan lands. She lowers her weapon as she is in no condition to fight. They then leave the tent and find only the Sarkaz bodyguard waiting outside, as the Minoan guards have left for lunch.

Heavyrain expresses her desire to leave and avoid becoming entangled in the area. Pallas tries to persuade her to stay and visit some places in the village, but Heavyrain refuses and decides to leave. Pallas gives her a compass and prays for Heavyrain to have a safe journey. That night, after Heavyrain's departure, Pallas wonders how Heavyrain pursues her lost freedom despite being just a normal person. Suddenly, a Sarkaz assassin appears, and Pallas's bodyguard steps forward to protect her from his kinsman. The assassin reveals he is there to hunt down Heavyrain but also sees value in killing a Minoan priestess. He belittles the bodyguard, claiming there is no salvation for the Sarkaz. Both Pallas and her bodyguard then proceed to fight him.

==Urban Legend==
[[Bena]] and Annie find the playroom while exploring [[Rhodes Island]], and they become intrigued by the game [[Kirara]] is playing. Bena has Annie hide somewhere and asks to play with Kirara, who still has yet to beat the forced-loss fight. She agrees, then panics because the game is an action-horror suspense with non-PG ratings, so she tones down the disturbing elements before letting Bena play. Looking at Bena's politeness, Kirara muses that she might have grown up to be like [[Utage]] if her friends had been the same, but then berates herself for her thoughts. Kirara is amazed by Bena's gaming skill and helps out with some tips, becoming excited when she is able to clear the forced-loss fight and get the true end. As Bena is unable to read the words, Kirara introduces the story behind ''Nukenin'', where the protagonist is a runaway assassin who defeats the ghosts of Shin-Aki, a nomadic city known for its abundance of ghost stories. The protagonist defeats Sawara the Silentious, the oldest ghost story of Shin-Aki City, and thwarts the ghost's plan of turning all of [[Higashi]] into a ghostly paradise.

Bena, helped by Annie, shares a ghost story too. In a place called Lyndon in [[Victoria]], there was a [[Caprinae]] girl who wanted to find shelter from the rain, but the slum dwellers shooed her away for being [[Leithanien|Leithanian]]. She then helped an old woman who had fallen down. After learning that the girl was abandoned by her parents, the old woman decided to bring her back to her home, and the girl happily carried the woman's fruit basket. She asked why the old lady plucked apples even during a rainy day, and the woman responded that only those plucked in the rain were the sweetest of all. At the old granny's home, the girl was amazed by the warm and beautiful house, and her wound was healed by the granny. She then met the woman's granddaughter, a detailed Caprinae doll about as tall as the girl, named Annie. The old woman asked the girl to close her eyes... and asked her to hand over her delectable, kindly soul. Bena's story spooks Kirara, and Bena adds that there were orphan abduction cases like that all over Victoria, and only a doll named Annie would be found in the abductors' homes.

Bena then introduces Annie to Kirara, who immediately runs away in fear of the doll. Pleased with herself, Bena tells the unamused Annie that she will apologize to Kirara later. The story itself is about Bena's past. Annie tells Bena that Granny would be upset, as the old lady in the story was not evil enough, and Bena recalls that she would have been gone had she run off with the fruit or taken a bite of dinner. Bena concludes that Annie had played the game earlier, which made the controls familiar to Bena, and Annie intentionally led her to the playroom. They then decide to play the game together.

==Forgotten Lighthouse==
[[Glaucus]] and [[Blue Poison]] are tasked by Rhodes Island to investigate a tower along the coast. Glaucus compares the Inquisition's treatment of it to how she never wished to look at her legs when she was a child, prompting Blue Poison to jokingly claim she is starting to feel bad due to Glaucus's constant use of herself for allegories. They make their way down the ruins using Originium lamps to light the path. Blue Poison teases Glaucus about ghosts in the basement, then notes the latter's interest in the technology of the building, although the wirings are so damaged they decide to leave it for [[Closure]] and Engineering to fix. Blue Poison muses about the radiant technology and buried bones from the Iberian Golden Age, prompting Glaucus to apologize to her. Blue Poison reassures her that she no longer despises the [[Aegir (race)|Aegirs]] who took her ancestors away to [[Iberia]], and no longer despises herself, thanking the Doctor and Glaucus for it. Glaucus asks her if the technology in the lighthouse gives her bad memories, and Blue Poison states she only cares if it is useful for Rhodes Island or not as detailed in their mission.

Glaucus agrees but suddenly pulls out and fires her Raythean Franker, having mistakenly thought she saw a ghost. The two discover the corpse of a [[Kuranta]] bounty hunter. Smelling seawater, Blue Poison warns Glaucus to stay back and approaches the corpse to examine it, finding seaweed and deducing that he must have died after getting tangled and drowned, with the rust indicating that the building had been flooded twice. They discuss the disaster that befell Iberia years ago, though Glaucus is at first hesitant, as even fellow Iberian Aegirs like [[Weedy]] see the disaster differently. Blue Poison reveals that it was the Aegirs who came ashore who decided not to speak of it for self-protection, which surprises Glaucus. The [[Anura]] continues, saying that her ancestors were taken by the Aegirs, who wanted to use the Anura's toxin to fight some sort of terrifying entity. She had once wanted to ask the [[Doctor]], [[Kal'tsit]], or even [[Skadi]] about it, but never did. Glaucus then proposes they return, but Blue Poison mistakenly continues straight ahead, musing that her mistake might be a sign that the toxin she never gave up could one day fell the monster the Aegirs feared.

As they continue downward, Glaucus and Blue Poison spot a white light, prompting them to turn off their lamp and stay alert. They make their move in the darkness, but the stranger attempts to negotiate and eventually stops casting the light with her staff, fearful of being attacked again. The [[Phidia]] introduces herself as [[Indigo|Aria]] and explains that she came to the lighthouse after someone told her she could find riches there, having given her last coins to him. Glaucus concludes that she was conned, while Blue Poison continues the interrogation. They learn that the tower was once a lighthouse and that Aria herself grew up in a coastal town with one, having been an apprentice to a lighthouse keeper. Although still an amateur, she says that she and her teacher continued tending to their lighthouse even though ships have long since stopped sailing. The group moves to the top of the lighthouse, where they discover a damaged energy core that does not primarily rely on Originium. Glaucus suggests reporting the finding to Rhodes Island, which excites Aria. The Phidia asks to accompany them, but suddenly grows dizzy and collapses. It is then revealed that she had been struck earlier by one of Blue Poison's bolts during the encounter.

Aria soon regains consciousness after being brought outside, prompting Blue Poison to remark on her quick recovery. They contact [[Elysium]], who welcomes Aria after Blue Poison mentions that she will be joining them, and report that the building is a lighthouse with its inner structure largely intact, though its related technologies have sustained heavy damage. Elysium mentions that some refugees have fled from the destroyed towns to the south, expressing mild exasperation at the thought of having to work back and forth if the lighthouse becomes a new office. Blue Poison muses that the lighthouse could serve as a place for Rhodes Island to provide medical aid and assistance to those in need, watching over the coastline in a new way.

==Different==
[[Carnelian]] has a business meeting in the name of Count Hohenlohe with the attendant of Sir Feuerbach. Carnelian's attention is drawn to the lavish decorations adorning the room. She correctly identifies them as ornaments carved from solidified Origincrystals, an extraordinarily rare and difficult material to procure, typically found only in the deepest, most dangerous Originium veins. Carnelian subtly confronts the attendant regarding the recent deadly accidents in Feuerbach's territory. The attendant, however, dismisses her concerns, stating that for a man of Sir Feuerbach's stature, such considerations simply do not matter. He tells her that their guards and casters are dealing with the accidents, claiming it is as simple as ridding a blanket of dust. Carnelian accepts that she has overstayed her welcome and prepares to bid farewell, reminding him that Sir Feuerbach ought not to look down on the [[Twin Empresses|Empresses]], to which the attendant replies that the Empresses' rule hardly compares to the unreasonable reign of their [[Witch King|predecessor]]. Even though the Twin Empresses rule with authority, he reasons that at the very least they know what they are dealing with, in comparison to the time when a red glow could signify the massacre of an entire town.

After leaving the meeting, Carnelian finds the city's air thick with the smell of progress, a scent she has grown grudgingly accustomed to. Disenchanted by the opulence of the city center, she wanders to the suburbs, where she encounters a frightened girl being pursued by guards. She contacts a specialist beforehand. She helps the girl hide from the guards and points out the crystals under her collar, telling her to drop the formalities while Carnelian introduces herself as a [[Sargon|Sargonian]]. She tells her about Sargon, and the image of the girl not knowing the country reminds Carnelian of herself a few years back, when she first arrived in [[Leithanien]]. Just then, Carnelian notices the approaching guards, and they move to another place while talking about Sargon. The girl reveals that her entire community lived in a segregated district near the mines. After a catastrophic mine collapse killed her parents, Sir Feuerbach's guards sealed off the district and began rounding up all the survivors. She and a few other children managed to escape but have since been separated while on the run.

Their presence almost discovered, Carnelian orders the Infected girl to hide immediately while she approaches a Caster who, as she expected, is part of the [[Echoes of Herkunftshorn]]. She concludes that they have been enslaving the local Infected and using them as test subjects, with Sir Feuerbach covering up their actions. Fortunately, they do not come to blows. The Caster, while not denying the accusations, warns Carnelian that Leithanien has its own unbending rules. Unwilling to escalate the situation into a direct conflict with an envoy of Count Hohenlohe, the Caster chooses to retreat, allowing Carnelian to leave with the girl. Carnelian comforts the sobbing girl, who is now burdened with the terrible truth of her community's fate. Inspired by Carnelian's strength and resolve, the girl declares her intention to find her friends, leave Leithanien, and one day avenge her people.

Nevertheless, Carnelian proposes that she seek aid from outside forces. She calls out to her specialist, revealing a Rhodes Island Operator who had been observing from nearby. Carnelian, confirmed to be an Operator herself, entrusts the girl's safety to her colleague. As they part ways, the girl asks if she and Carnelian will meet again. Carnelian replies that as long as the girl holds onto her beliefs, their paths are sure to cross once more.

==Letter==
Ensia, also known as Operator [[Cliffheart]], barges into [[Courier]] and [[Matterhorn]]'s room early one morning but finds no one there. She then runs into a surprised Courier in the hallway and nearly falls, though he manages to catch her in time. Matterhorn soon joins them and asks if Cliffheart woke up early to prepare for a mission, while Courier muses about her not being used to the beds on Rhodes Island, their recollection of her childhood mortifying her. Upon hearing that they are preparing to return to [[Kjerag]], Cliffheart drags Courier away from Matterhorn, promising to meet up later in the cafeteria. She asks Courier to deliver a letter to her sister [[Pramanix|Enya]] on Mount Karlan. However, Courier declines, explaining that the situation in the Vine-Bead Court has grown tense and that Enya likely would not be able to receive her letter. The news surprises and frustrates Cliffheart, as even though [[SilverAsh|her brother]] withheld information from his sisters to protect them, Enya still left for Mount Karlan. Courier laments that she had no choice, prompting Cliffheart to change the subject and make him promise to keep their conversation a secret before wishing them a safe trip back.

Despite Courier's bad news, Cliffheart decides to gather cold-weather gear and climb the snowy mountains on her own. After completing a field operation, she requests a temporary leave for personal reasons. Meanwhile, Courier returns from Kjerag and learns that Cliffheart was seen packing mountaineering equipment. Alarmed, he rushes to stop her. Cliffheart has already reached the borders of Kjerag, but Courier catches up to her thanks to his familiarity with the terrain. He praises her decisiveness but insists he cannot allow her to do such a dangerous thing again. Cliffheart, however, refuses to stand by and watch the relationship between Enciodes and Enya deteriorate, knowing that Enya is not living the life she truly desires as the Saintess. Eventually, Courier relents in the argument, especially after Cliffheart claims she would commit crimes to climb Mount Karlan if necessary, and instead tells her to hand him the letter, though he cannot guarantee it will reach Enya. Cliffheart thanks him and says she will find another way if it fails, prompting Courier to think that SilverAsh will have a massive headache when that day comes.

Around half a year later, Cliffheart loses an eating contest to [[Closure]], who had been working in her room for three days repairing broken equipment. Closure asks about Cliffheart's cold-weather gear, to which Cliffheart replies that she has does not need it for the time being. Courier then arrives and reports to Cliffheart that he has successfully delivered the letter to Enya. Although initially excited, Cliffheart grows anxious upon seeing Courier's grim expression, but he tells her not to panic. He then informs her that SilverAsh has asked her to deliver an invitation to the [[Doctor]], which will soon become [[Snowcap Incident|the catalyst for changes in the Snow Realm]].
