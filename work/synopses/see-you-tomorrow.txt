==Adhaint an Ghrian==
[[File:58 mini01.png|thumb|"Even living legends have their endings. But surely becoming the kindling of a new legend is better than wasting away in obscurity?"]]
The war in [[Londinium]] had reached a stalemate; after their defeat at Silverock Bluffs, the [[Military Commission of Kazdel]] and the [[Nachzehrer]] Withered Court, led by [[Nezzsalem]], have retreated behind Londinium's walls while the Grand Dukes convene away from the city. Amidst artillery fire, Originium activity, and [[Sarkaz]] witchcraft abound, [[Reed]] and her group of Taran refugees arrive at the outskirts of Londinium. Concerned for the safety of her fellows, Reed decides to continue alone, much to the concern of [[Moran]] and the others. However, Reed tells them that crossing no man's land is a challenge that she alone must undertake, and they agree to wait for her. Reed's goal is to meet with her sister [[Eblana]] aboard the formidable ''Gastrell'', the [[Duke of Wellington]]'s personal flagship. As she crosses the battleground, she aids wounded [[Victoria]]n and Sarkaz soldiers without discrimination, drawing attentions from both sides of the war. Some mistake her for a [[Vouivre]] and are unable to identify her allegiance, while others, particularly the Sarkaz, view her as a threat. She also encounters a dying Taran soldier who [[What the Firelight Casts/Synopsis|recognizes her from County Oak Grove]], and helps him come to terms with his fate, allowing him to rest in peace.

Aboard the ''Gastrell'', [[The Brigadier]] is tracking Reed's movements across the war-torn land, and orders his men to eliminate any threat to her life while also lowering the gangway to allow her to board. After she arrives, she is led to the bridge where Wellington awaits, instead of Eblana. At Wellington's inquiry, Reed confirms that she learned of the ''Gastrell's'' coordinates from the [[Doctor]] and [[Rhodes Island]] as a favour, while reassuring him that they have no intention of meddling in Tara's affairs. When asked the reason for her arrival, Reed simply answers "Peace". She explains the status quo: a pointless siege to starve out Londinium, a strategy that will ultimately harm everyone within the city, including the lives of innocent civilians. Wellington agrees, but also points out that cowardice is the unwillingness to take responsibility, regardless of morality. Undeterred, Reed asks the Duke to mobilize his fleet and break through the Sarkaz line to end the siege, but he sees no reason for him to follow her plea. 

Reed is aware of what the Iron Duke and Eblana seek from her, and she is now ready to oblige. Reed acts in her official capacity as a leader of Tara and directly commands Wellington.

{{Quote|"I order your fleet to smash the weak Sarkaz defense line, to bring Londinium the relief she should have had long ago. I order the Taran army to demonstrate noblesse oblige, not for material benefit, but for all to see our glory."}}

Wellington accepts the order immediately as a subordinate of Tara's leadership and begins preparations. After he leaves the bridge, Reed remains to think over their conversation. An officer offers to show her to a cabin, but she declines. Promising to bring her people to the capital of Tara, [[Na Saoirsí]], Reed disembarks from the battleship as Eblana forbids anyone from interfering with her sister's movements.

Eblana is informed of the meeting's outcome by Wellington, refusing to meet with Reed at this point in time—deeming it unnecessary with no beneficial outcome. Nevertheless, the ''Gastrell'' begins to advance, as Eblana aligns herself with Reed's wishes, moving to the bow in order to demonstrate the true power and glory of Tara.

Atop the walls of Londinium, the Sarkaz spot the ''Gastrell'' and the rest Wellington's fleet break the stalemate and begin their approach, as the Duke of Caster's forces scramble to fall in line. When the fighting erupts once more, Eblana and Nezzsalem engage in combat as death meets death. Reed returns to her people, and begins the journey home to Na Saoirsí.

==Отправляйтесь к Урсусу==
[[File:58 mini02 3.png|thumb|Ideals clash and tensions rise...]]
[[File:58 mini02 1.png|thumb|...But they must unite on a common goal.]]
In a Londinium factory protected by the [[Exemplars]], many [[Infected]] and outcasts arrive to join Reunion's ranks, but are discouraged as [[Talulah]] informs them that they'll be departing for the [[Duke of Gododdin|Gododdin]] duchy in a month's time. Knowing that they have nowhere to go, [[Nine]] insists on taking them in regardless. [[Ch'en]], who is temporarily staying with Reunion to keep an eye on them, refrains from giving her thoughts despite encouragement from both Nine and Talulah. She casts doubt, however, on Nine's decision to allow any outcast into their group, even uninfected.

A Reunion fighter informs Nine that [[Red]] had spotted ducal forces approaching their hideout, and she leaves to investigate. As Nine leaves, Ch'en asks Talulah her opinion on Nine's leadership. While the [[Draco]] doesn't agree with all of Nine's methods, the new incarnation of Reunion is far different from the Reunion Talulah lead across the tundra. Outside the factory, Nine confronts the passing ducal soldiers who are transporting a group of Sarkaz civilians. Despite Nine's efforts, she lacks the authority to take the civilians from the Victorian soldiers, and as the atmosphere grows tense Talulah subtly raises the temperature with her [[Arts]] to hurry the soldiers along before they come to blows.

Nine's encounter with the ducal soldiers reinforces that the Victorian Army has grown increasingly hostile towards all Sarkaz, harassing and arresting innocent Sarkaz civilians, including those born in Londinium. Talulah remains calm in light of their situation, believing that striking back at the Victorians soldiers carrying out the orders will only do more harm than good, as the average soldier is not the root of the issue. Nine argues that the fear and hatred towards the Sarkaz will continue to grow, and that the [[13-16/Story#Before operation|Sarkaz merchant who previously found supplies for them]] was killed by Infected. Nine decides to help the innocent Sarkaz. She asks Talulah to protect their camp, orders Red to inform the group of their next step, and to allow any aspirants to stay and bring those who do not wish to leave Londinium to the Exemplars. She dismisses Ch'en's concerns over provoking the Victorians, coldly telling her that her prolonged stay at Rhodes Island made her forget the suffering of the Infected.

Nine stops a Victorian soldier from executing a group of Sarkaz civilians, and is stopped by Ch'en before she can kill the executioner. They continue to clash over their opposing ideals, and Talulah steps in after Ch'en draws her sword on Nine. The three women are of different minds, but Talulah refrains from judging right and wrong, instead laying out the common goal: Bring those who wish to leave away from Londinium. Talulah suggests that they return to [[Ursus]], having received news that an Infected uprising is in full swing. Because of the Empire's success in covering up the Chernobog Crisis, many Infected on the Tundra only know that Reunion vanished into Chernobog to fight for the Infected. Nine and Ch'en eventually agree, with Ch'en staying behind to catch up later, ensuring that the soldier Nine attacked lives.

==Passing==
Chaos reigns in Londinium as the Sarkaz frontline at the city walls is besieged by the joint forces of the Exemplars and the Grand Dukes' armies. A panicked couple and their son, Ollie, come across Nowell, who came to Londinium after his departure from Reunion. Upon being asked if he was an Exemplar, his response is negative. Rather, he came to the ruined city looking for the source of his immortality, the "Hands of Fate." One of the citizens in distress points out how Nowell seemingly entered the blockade without an injury despite his ragged appearance and offers him the few breadcrumbs he was carrying in his pockets. Nowell knows that the survival of the three people in front of him is at stake, so he refuses to accept the breadcrumbs. Instead, Nowell offers to guide them to the Exemplars' base since he knows its location. 

Along the way, Nowell and the survivors meet [[Clovisia]], the [[Eartha|Self-Salvation Corps]]' young commander, who was also evacuating people. Clovisia thanks Nowell for bringing the survivors to the safe zone, and he volunteers himself to help. Clovisia warns him that it's too dangerous to continue, but Nowell insists in any case. After evacuating the last batch of refugees, Nowell finds an injured Victorian worker in the streets. He asks him if he had seen Clovisia, who previously agreed to meet him right where he was standing, but is met with a negative response. Amidst the evacuation, Ollie's mother desperately comes in, claiming that her son has gone missing. She asks Nowell if he could help look for Ollie, who was last seen in the blockade. Suddenly, Clovisia shows up carrying Ollie with her at the distance, but they are seemingly buried by a collapsing building. The mother's pleading reminds Nowell of a memory with his family, and how he chose to leave them in his search for death. After regaining his composure, Nowell decides to step in and rescue Ollie.

Nowell ventures underground through the debris and finds Ollie safe and sound. Unfortunately, Clovisia was unconscious, with half of her body buried by rocks. Upon looking at her face, Nowell asks himself whether he will make the same expression as Clovisia if he dies one day. The gravel that fell due to the aftershocks prevented Nowell from checking Clovisia's vital signs. Nowell promises to retrieve Clovisia's body later, and prioritizes on evacuating Ollie. While on their way out, a mist suddenly envelopes Nowell and Ollie, while an echoing voice calls upon Nowell. He immediately recognizes the voice despite not knowing what the owner looks like. The voice introduces itself as a "[[Unicorn]]," and claims that it's exactly what Nowell was looking for, the one who bestowed him a "blessing" of immortality. Nowell insists that his "blessing" was just a painful curse, and remarks that he had enough of its tricks. Ollie is aware of the Unicorn's voice, but Nowell tells him to ignore it. The Unicorn pities him for his suffering and intends to make reparations, so it tempts him to grant a wish which he rejects. 

Later, Nowell emerges from the ruins and returns Ollie to his mother. However, just when the locals were expressing their deep gratitude towards him, a pain begins to spread from Nowell's back until he collapses. The people around him starts to panic as his wounds continue to bleed &mdash; something that even he finds strange, as the wound should've healed due to his immortality. When asked about what happened, Nowell comes to a sudden realization; Clovisia was the "Unicorn" he was trying to track down his whole life, yet he missed the chance to directly confront her. He feels like it doesn't matter anymore, and instead focuses on the present. Though exhausted and injured, he decides to move on from his grudge and save more people.

==Canoes off the Riverside==
After Theresa's sudden death and [[Babel]]'s collapse in 1094, the [[Vampire]] spy organization known as "Rose Riverside" scattered into pieces. Their elusive leader—known only as the "Princess"—suddenly disappeared without a trace, and several members later defected to the Military Commission of Kazdel. Meanwhile, many mercenaries have been hunting the "Canoes" of Rose Riverside like [[Entelechia]] for the valuable intelligence they possess. Entelechia manages to overpower a group of Sarkaz mercs trying to kill her, capturing one of them and interrogating him. Just then, she is approached by Milton, a young mixed-blood Sarkaz, who taunts her to slay the mercenary. Instead, Entelechia decides to let the mercenary go so she can confront Milton alone.

Milton says he is not interested in following Regent [[Theresis]] to Victoria, but wants to save his sister, who works as an agent for the Rose Riverside. He is aware that despite the Military Commission's claims about not hunting anyone related to Babel, they will not allow the Riverside's Canoes to walk freely. He believes that if he manages to bring in Entelechia's head, he might have a chance to save his sister. However, Entelechia easily evades his attacks and reveals that she has already taken care of his friends waiting in ambush. Entelechia confesses that she had been too busy with clean-up to save Theresa or the other Canoes, but she can still help Milton and her sister. She gives him her earring—a special blood red jewel—which he can claim as proof of her demise, and leaves shortly afterwards.

Years later, Entelechia meets with Kal'tsit at Rhodes Island's safe house amidst the ongoing war in Londinium. Kal'tsit is surprised to see a canoe of the Rose Riverside, especially since the organization's disbandment. Entelechia states that she has come to Londinium looking for clues about their leader's whereabouts, with the aid of another canoe, a Rose Riverside technician codenamed "Trickster". She also wishes to help [[Ascalon]] travel deeper into the city, though that requires dealing with [[Duq'arael]]'s remaining blood altars. Kal'tsit agrees to help Entelechia, promising to provide her information about the Princess; she advises Entelechia to cooperate with [[Millscar]] for handling the altars. Before leaving, Entelechia hesitantly asks Kal'tsit if Her Highness, [[Theresa]], has really passed away again.

[[File:58 mini03 1.png|thumb|Entelechia's mission has led her to tragedy...]]
[[File:58 mini03 2.png|thumb|But a sister's departure will never be forgotten.]]
After receiving her answer, Entelechia infiltrates the Military Commission's command center after extracting useful memories from the blood of corpses lying near Duq'arael's altars. At Kal'tsit's request, she has destroyed their command structure and lifted the communications lockdown. Entelechia asks Trickster over the transceiver if her actions were a violation of her own rule of gold: ''Never interfere in Babel's affairs''. Trickster soothes her concerns and tells her it depends on how she sees it. Trickster then notices that Victorian forces are clashing with a group of [[Wiš'adel|unknown]] [[Hoederer|Sarkaz]] [[Ines|mercenaries]] who are approaching the command center as well, so Entelechia decides to clear the path for them. Afterwards, she rendezvouses with Kal'tsit, who thanks Entelechia for dismantling the Sanquinarch's altars. She mentions that [[Amiya]] and the Doctor are trying to arrange terms with the Victorian dignitaries to end the war peacefully and have the Military Commission withdraw from Londinium.

As promised, Kal'tsit has sent the information she has on Rose Riverside's Princess to the special frequency Entelechia provided, but she mentions that they received no response from the other party. Entelechia navigates through the war-torn ruins towards the signal's last known location and discovers Trickster's corpse clutching a smashed receiver. Entelechia notices that her blood has minute traces of Arts on it, as it hasn't dried yet. She uses Trickster's blood to communicate with her fading consciousness and asks about her murderer, but Trickster's response is ambiguous. Whether the perpetrator was a Victorian or a Military Commission soldier doesn't matter; Trickster says she's just proud for having been part of Rose Riverside for so many years and bids farewell to her friend.

Entelechia no longer hears anything from the pool of blood, but her fingers touch upon something solid. It is a red crystal, made with a drop of her own blood, representing the oath she made when she first became a Rose Riverside sister. She realizes that the Princess must have been here, as she was the only one who could leave this behind. Entelechia later reconvenes with Kal'tsit. Neither Rhodes Island nor the Military Commission have any clues about the Princess' whereabouts, but Entelechia deduces that she might've come to Londinium in order to bid farewell to Her Highness, or just to stop her and Trickster from tracking her down.

In any case, Entelechia is motivated to continue her search for the Princess and obtain closure for all the Canoes. Before leaving Londinium, Entelechia entrusts Trickster's crystal to Kal'tsit and asks her to return it to Kazdel.

==Another Breath of Death==
[[Paprika]] mourns the fallen [[Steam Knights]] in the Mausoleum of Kings and wonders if the Sarkaz will finally return to Kazdel, their home, once [[Hoederer]] succeeds on guiding them with the [[Lifebone]]. She asks Ines if they can also repatriate the bodies of the mercenaries that fought in the mausoleum back to Kazdel, which she replies that only if she wanted to. An injured Hoederer rendezvous with the group and argues that neither going to [[Columbia]], nor staying in Victoria are the safest options right now. Luckily, the Lifebone is stable enough to stay in one piece by the time they return to Kazdel. Wiš'adel informs that the Victorian forces have advanced as quickly as how the Nachzehrers' defenses fell. Nezzsalem on the other hand is still alive but has retreated to Londinium. Upon being asked by Paprika, she contends that Manfred's whereabouts are unknown, but she doesn't think he would die that easily, moreover with the Military Commission's dire situation. Based on the intelligence Wiš'adel's men have gathered, the Nachzehrer King is far from declaring total defeat. 

Outside, the group comes across a [[Trilby Asher]], who mocks Manfred for supposedly abandoning his men. The Trilby Asher is aware of Hoederer's past as Manfred's deputy and dismisses him despite trying to clear up things. Wiš'adel taunts the Trilby Asher and the soldiers who were accompanying him, but before the situation escalates, Ines senses another shadow approaching &mdash; [[Delphine]]. Although Delphine still holds a hatred towards Sarkaz, she dismisses the soldiers and tells the Trilby Asher that they're an exception since they share the same strategic goals as Rhodes Island and the Exemplars, and he agrees to put his weapons down. Hoederer states that he can't guarantee protecting the loose Sarkaz mercenaries from the unavoidable chaos and from themselves but puts his trust on Wiš'adel and Babel. Delphine orders [[Shearer]] to escort Wiš'adel's group to the Military Commission's command center and tell the ducal forces that this is an order from Delphine Windermere herself, and no one else. The Trilby Asher, however, still holds suspicion over them for being Sarkaz, and remarks Delphine about the responsibility of her actions for when the Grand Dukes arrive. In turn, Delphine asks him if he's going to call the [[Duke of Caster]], to which he replies that he will only report Delphine's actions to her. Delphine also tells him to share the Exemplars' promise to the Duke.

Wiš'adel's group arrive at the Military Commission's command center, surprised that there's only a few people guarding it thanks to Entelechia's work. Paprika assumes that if Manfred is still alive, then he should be alongside Theresis, while Hoederer believes that the Doctor and company should handle the Regent. As for the command center, Ines states that it should be useful in case the Exemplars and Rhodes Island withdraw from the battlefield, but Wiš'adel, in her typical fashion, wants to blow it up in the sky just to prove Babel's actions. Wiš'adel starts to broadcast her message to the Military Commission; Babel has taken over their command center. True to her word, she destroys the command center, drawing the Nachzehrers' attention. But Nezzsalem remains unperturbed as he watches the command center collapse. He knows that the war has once again rekindled the Sarkaz's hatred, and new leaders like Babel will arise to command them.

Meanwhile, [[Siege|Vina]], the Exemplars, and Rhodes Island are heading to the Palace of Westhaleg, but their advance is engulfed by a thick fog. [[Dagda]], [[Indra]], and [[Morgan]] notice a wind-like noise coming from the fog as countless Nachzehrer banners begin to hover in the sky, carrying death's omen. The fog paralyzes the soldiers and corrode their weapons, forcing everyone to retreat. [[Horn]] orders the Exemplars' 1st and 3rd Caster divisions to cover their withdrawal. However, Amiya realizes that she can't dispel the Nachzehrers' witchcraft with her Arts. Logos remarks that the banners are stained with the Nachzeherers' corruption, but neither can he analyze the fog itself, thus he concludes that the Sarkaz are changing their tactics. Kal'tsit argues that the banners are halting the other teams' advance whilst jamming their communications. She orders [[Closure]] and Logos to check their communications equipment and decipher the banners' witchcraft respectively. Elsewhere, [[Bagpipe]] and a Victorian worker named "Big Wrench" are fighting against the Nachzehrers. Bagpipe warns him against staying close to the bodies for too long. Meanwhile, Logos continues trying to analyze the banners and realizes that the Nachzehrers are letting the witchcraft spread arbitrarily, like overflowing water. The Doctor suggests if there's a way to dilute their corruption. 

[[Nearl|Margaret]], [[Nightingale]], and [[Shining]] return to Londinium to evacuate the injured soldiers from a logistics camp. The Doctor asks Shining about Nightingale's status, who's treating the wounded and Infected soldiers despite her weak physical condition. Margaret reassures the Doctor that she's willing to help in the frontline, but they warn her about the spreading fog. The fog continues to cause interference on Closure's equipment. [[Fang]] informs her that they can't directly deploy relays amidst the fog without being corroded, and using the protective shield will only block their signal. Closure is affected by the Nachzehrer witchcraft, but she manages to decipher the interference, which are the howling voices of deceased Nachzehrer warriors. Closure then receives incoming messages from the restored communication channels; [[Misery]], Horn, Vina, Delphine, and the [[Earl of March]], all have managed to reestablish contact. Not only that, [[Charles Lynch|Sir Charles Lynch]] &mdash; The Last Steam Knight &mdash; alongside [[Stainless]] and [[Catherine]]'s Crawler arrived just in time to join the fray, while countless [[Sudaram|Sudarim]] fly around ready to attack.

The fog weakens, but it's still way too early for the Exemplars to celebrate victory. Kal'tsit states that according to Shining's report, the fog is slowly coming back. Nezzsalem emerges from the fog, but he has no intention to fight Kal'tsit or Rhodes Island because they don't belong to the battlefield, Londinium. Nezzsalem argues that thanks to this war, the Sarkaz are now able to control their own destiny as they harness the ''Amnannam'''s powers together, not only Theresis. As a result, he simply fights in the war for the sake of it. Amiya voices her doubt about his conclusion since a war cannot be reduced to a mere conflict without a purpose, but Nezzsalem mocks her attitude whilst recognizing her as the new [[King of Sarkaz]]. Nevertheless, Nezzsalem remains indifferent to the future that will affect all Sarkaz alike, and he simply wants to continue and repeat his cycle of corruption and nourishment. Nezzsalem has no desire to judge Amiya, so he parts the way for Rhodes Island to continue towards Theresis and the ''Amnnanam''. However, Logos decides to stay behind and dissipate the fog. Despite Nezzsalem insisting him to leave, the young [[Banshee]] doesn't respond and swings his bone pen. The two prepare to fight once again.

Big Wrench fights his way to the Palace of Westhaleg, but his once-trusty wrench breaks off. In that moment he spots Nezzsalem, but an Exemplar soldier comes in and hands him the ''[[Sighs of Kings]]'', courtesy of Vina. As he clutches the Realmblade, memories about his daughter began to pass through his mind. Rushing full speed ahead, Sir Lynch knocks the Nachzehrer King down to the ground. Feeling the fire inside Lynch's eyes, Big Wrench courageously rushes against Nezzsalem, but he's easily thrown aside by him. Vina leads more allied forces to the battlefield, marching forward the final battle. As dawn breaks, the Nachzehrer King is finally defeated after an arduous battle, but the sword is nowhere to be seen. The victors wonder if the war will truly be over, and Big Wrench is presumed to have succumbed to his injuries. 

In a flashback, Nezzsalem talks with [[Theresa]] shortly before she leaves Kazdel. He asks her if she could see his demise in the [[Civilight Eterna]], but her response is negative, and remarks that destruction waits for every Sarkaz. In any case, Nezzsalem isn't afraid of death or destruction and understands that this is the sealed fate of the Nachzehrer clan. He argues about the promise Theresa had made during the first Military Commission meeting in 1031 &mdash; to break the cycle of chaos and uncertainty. Theresa can feel Nezzsalem's excitement about this new path for the Teekaz and reassures that Babel will finally come back when the time is ready. A promise that is about to be fulfilled. Back at the present, the blood-red morning glow overlaps the spire of the Palace of Westhaleg, and the battlefield is left in the uttermost ruin as the fog finally dissipates. Nezzsalem has fallen, but the Exemplars question if he is truly dead. Delphine on the other hand still feels an uncanny sense of oppression in the battlefield &mdash; this was no ordinary morning, nor an ordinary victory. Shining questions if war could be killed. And if it's possible, then by whom?

Meanwhile, the Duke of Wellington and Eblana argue about Nezzsalem's death. Eblana agrees that war can ''never'' end, but Nezzsalem simply left because the war is over. At the Command Center, Wiš'adel, Ines, and Hoederer discuss the possibility of a new war to break out, one that could rewrite Terra's history. In the death of silence, a lone bone whistle &mdash; implied to be Logos &mdash; plays to mourn the deceased. At the Palace of Westhaleg's underground throne, Manfred hears the bone whistle's tune and asks if [[Theresis|The Regent]] is dead, but receives no answer from the absent Theresis. The general stands alone and doubts the Nachzehrer, who was once a hero in many Kazdelian legends, is gone. But in the middle of the dark, Manfred envisions a picture of the future; a time when war destroys Kazdel again, and death rises from the mountains at the horizon.

==All the Unrevealed==
[[File:58 mini04.png|thumb|The "Outblood King" steps up, for the fate of the Sarkaz lies in her hands.]]
The Sarkaz retreat from Londinium en masse, guided by the Lifebone. Ines has allowed the elderly, the children, and the wounded to ride on the Lifebone, while Wiš'adel has assigned her remaining subordinates to maintain order and keep an eye on the imprisoned [[Ulšulah]]. Hoederer and Ines are aware that there is not much they can do if they are attacked on the way back, but they must remain strong. For that reason, they are intentionally using the Amnnannam to create a Catastrophe cloud above their heads to defend themselves. Down below, Logos and Misery hold a short funeral to bid farwell to the Sarkaz who died, unable to return home. Misery states he is not fond of using the ''Amnannam's'' Catastrophe clouds to avoid conflict, but agrees that it is still an effective, necessary method.

A lieutenant of the [[Columbia]]n Union Army observes the Sarkaz's retreat during a secret reconnaissance mission in collaboration with [[Maylander]]. Nothing seems amiss at first, but his communications equipment suddenly jams as the Catastrophe Early Warning System from his vehicle starts blaring. Without any warning, a massive Catastrophe cloud suddenly descends upon the Columbian capital of Max D.C.. The Catastrophe cloud then disappears just as quickly as it appeared, leaving only traces of Originium particles high in the sky. Similar Catastrophe clouds are also sighted above the capital city of every nation: [[Kawalerielki]] in [[Kazimierz]], [[Zwillingstürme]] in [[Leithanien]], [[Deity Grypherburg]] in [[Ursus]], Montelupe in [[Siracusa]], and even the Holy City of [[Laterano]]. The governments know full well what this means: it is a deliberate show of power, a warning against those who would try to harm the Sarkaz again.

Wiš'adel is impressed by the Amnnannam's ability to cast Catastrophes anywhere, and wonders if they could destroy every capital city on Terra with a single thought. The captive Manfred tells her that while it is theoretically possible, it will not completely obliterate nations and will only bring stronger retaliation. Annoyed, Wiš'adel reminds him that he's just a prisoner who's being kept alive because Kazdel needs the Military Commission around to serve as a guiding light for the Sarkaz. Manfred states that this was only possible because of Theresis's plans; the Sarkaz have been reunited by hatred and are now working together again for a common goal. Wiš'adel orders him to stop playing the good guy and recognizes that the burden of Kazdel's mistakes fall upon the Sarkaz, and only the Sarkaz.

Not far away, a [[Bolívar]]ian [[Liberi]] man named Paryaqaqa is dropped in the Victorian wilderness by the [[Siracusa]]n mafia, who previously kidnapped him after a certain misunderstanding. He plans to return to him home in the [[Black Flow]], but wants to investigate the strange Catastrophe clouds in the distance first. Unfortunately, his actions draw the attention of Sarkaz mercenaries, who are suspiscious about his apparent ability to repel lightning strikes around him. Wiš'adel arrives to handle the situation as the [[Revenant]] in her weapon senses something amiss with him. Paryaqaqa warns Wiš'adel about a warship belonging to an unknown faction coming towards Londinium. 

The mysterious warship fleet appears at the horizon and blocks the Sarkaz. As the Sarkaz prepare to battle for their lives once more, Amiya steps in and summons a huge black wall from the ground, separating the Sarkaz from the warships. Amiya warns them that the Sarkaz will go home, and violence against them won't be tolerated. Amiya could hear the Sarkaz and feel their mixed emotions flooding into her mind. After a long stalemate, the first warship from the unknown fleet begins to change direction.

[[File:58 mini05.png|thumb|His goals were achieved, and his kin was united once more. Now Theresis embarks on a journey seeking for the forgotten truth.]]
In Laterano, the [[Apostolic Knights]] and the Pontifica Cohors silently observe the Sarkaz in the distance. Suddenly, [[Yvangelista XI]] detects a sudden disturbance from [[The Law]], which established contact with an unknown entity similar to it. Yvangelista decides to ask "Who are you?" and hears Amiya on the other end, who is able to respond thanks to the [[Civilight Eterna]]. The two then engage in a brief dialogue. Amiya argues that even without the myriad souls' influence, it will still take a long time for Terra and the Sarkaz to let go of their hatred. However, one thing worries her above all else: what if they don't have any more time?

Somewhere in the still void of the [[Assimilated Universe]], Theresis stands before the collapsing tower that Theresa previously erected. All he can see is the golden ocean and the lonely diamond in the sky. Having fulfilled his objective of reuniting the Sarkaz, [[Episode 15|will he confront the]] [[Priestess|truth behind Originium]]?
