==Out of the Frying Pan...==
After returning to [[Rhodes Island]]'s temporary headquarters in [[Lungmen]], [[Kal'tsit]] scolds the [[Doctor]] for not taking care of [[Amiya]], who is visibly shaken and forlorn after [[Misha]]'s tragic death. Amiya goes for a walk in the slums to clear her head, where she sees [[Ch'en]] receiving a small teddy bear from a young girl. Amiya asks Ch'en what she is doing in the slums, but the other woman only states that she is "taking care of Lungmen's business." Amiya later tells the Doctor that this experience made her believe that despite their differences, Ch'en is not so different from Rhodes Island after all. Amiya regains some confidence in Rhodes Island's mission. 

Rhodes Island's scout team has found evidence of several Reunion hideouts in Lungmen's residential district. However, when Amiya suggests another joint operation between R.I. and the L.G.D., Ch'en refuses, stating that an Infected faction carrying out more missions in Lungmen will sow disorder.  

Meanwhile, two days after the failed rescue operation leading to [[Misha]]'s death, Rhodes Island dispatches multiple reconnaissance teams to an abandoned city plate not far from Lungmen, which appeared in the aftermath of the [[Chernobog]] incident. Upon their arrival, they realize that the city has turned into a makeshift base for [[Reunion]] after its destruction by the [[Catastrophe]]. Nevertheless, they carry out their mission to search for survivors, explore the city, and gather intel for Rhodes Island.  

As the reconnaissance teams explore the abandoned plate, a team consisting of [[Meteorite]], [[Jessica]], and [[Frostleaf]] record a sudden and drastic drop in temperature. Frostleaf discovers thick frost covering the surrounding buildings and recognizes these as signs of a dangerous faction—the infamous [[Yeti Squadron]] that once wandered the northern Ursus tundra, rumored to have defeated [[Ursus]]' Infected Patrol forces many times in the past. Not much later, the three discover [[Mephisto]] and [[W]] within the ruins. The two leaders exchange pointed barbs before launching a surprise attack on the team, forcing them to retreat deeper into the ruins of the city to shake their pursuers. Jessica calls Amiya during the attack to call for backup, but Frostleaf insists that Amiya ''not'' come, lest she become trapped with them. Amiya convinces Ch'en to agree to another joint operation between R.I. and the L.G.D. in order to rescue the recon team. 

==...and Into the Fire==
Knowing that the recon team is in danger, R.I. and the [[Lungmen Guard Department|L.G.D.]], including Ch'en and [[Hoshiguma]], arrive at the abandoned city plate. Ch'en happens to see [[Talulah]] shortly after landing and breaks off to chase her, leaving the rest of the team behind to be ambushed by Reunion. Amiya decides to goes deeper into the Originium dense zone of the city to find Ch'en and the recon team while the L.G.D. stays behind to guard the entrance to the zone. Amiya eventually finds Ch'en, who denies going after Talulah and turns back to find her squad. R.I. then discovers Frostleaf hiding from Mephisto and his forces in a ruined building. Frostleaf shows Amiya that the cold spread by the Yetis has not only caused frostbite all over her body, but has also frozen over their communication devices, cutting off contact with Meteorite and Jessica. After clearing out Reunion forces, the team find Meteorite and Jessica, but the latter is completely despondent. Amiya manages to shake Jessica from her shell shock by seemingly directly manipulating Jessica's emotions. Frostleaf questions her after witnessing this, but Amiya asks her to keep it a secret from Dr Kal'tsit. Meanwhile, Mephisto continues taunting Rhodes Island loudly, setting buildings filled with corpses ablaze. Rhodes Island emerges and attacks Mephisto directly, when a sudden chill fills the air...

[[File:FrostNova Arrives.png|thumb|The Yeti Princess, arriving alongside merciless blizzard]]
The Yeti Squadron arrives with their leader, the dreaded FrostNova. Upon her arrival, the temperature drops sharply and ice begins to form, extinguishing the blazes that Mephisto has ignited. Surmising that FrostNova is draining all the heat from her surroundings and that they presently have no chance against her, Amiya immediately orders Rhodes island to retreat. Frostleaf puts her life on the line to buy time for Rhodes Island to evacuate, but Meteorite comes to her rescue and bombards the area with her explosives. The team takes the opportunity to escape successfully. Speaking to a mysterious, towering figure, FrostNova vows to go after Rhodes Island.
