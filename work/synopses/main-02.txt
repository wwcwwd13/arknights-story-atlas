==Into Lungmen==
December 27, 1096: four days after their escape from [[Chernobog]], [[Rhodes Island]] arrives at the [[Yan|Yanese]] [[nomadic city]] of [[Lungmen]] to establish a contract to protect the city from the looming [[Reunion]] threat alongside the [[Lungmen Guard Department]]. Upon reaching the Inspection and Quarantine zone at the entrance to the city, the [[Doctor]] and [[Amiya]] meet a stern [[Lung]] woman named [[Ch'en]], the Superintendent of the Lungmen Guard Department. Though she seems a harsh woman who despises the [[Infected]], characterized by her decisive suppression of an Infected riot in the Quarantine zone, she lets the guests into Lungmen as part of the negotiations regardless. As Ch'en leads them to their destination, she speaks down to Amiya, treating her with little respect.

When they reach the office, the Doctor and Amiya find [[Kal'tsit]], the unofficial leader of R.I., debating with [[Wei Yenwu]], the governor of Lungmen. Kal'tsit attempts to convince Wei to consider her plan, as Lungmen will become Reunion's next target, but he doubts Rhodes Island's abilities to handle such an attack. Amiya steps in and promises Wei that R.I. can handle the issues surrounding the Infected. To prove their capabilities, she reveals that R.I. not only survived the catastrophe in Chernobog, but has also fought [[Talulah]], the leader of Reunion. After deep consideration, Wei finally agrees to Kal'tsit's terms and allows R.I. to cooperate with the L.G.D. to eliminate the Reunion threat.

==The Girl and the Key==
[[File:Skullshatterer Meets Misha.png|thumb|Skullshatterer encounters Misha during his search in Lungmen]]
The first mission during their contract is to search for [[Misha]], a Chernobog refugee hiding in the slums who has sensitive intel pertaining to Chernobog. With aid from [[Penguin Logistics]] and [[Blacksteel]], R.I. fends off a force of Reunion infiltrators. When they track Misha to an abandoned building, the Ursus girl doesn't trust them, thinking they are there to arrest and kill her. Amiya persuades Misha of their good intentions by showing her that they are Infected too, and that only by staying with them and the L.G.D. will Misha be safe from harassment. Convinced, Misha decides to follow them and willingly hands herself to the L.G.D..

However, the situation grows complicated as the Reunion Assault Squad, led by [[Skullshatterer]], arrive in Lungmen to search for Misha as well. Their appearance causes skirmishes between Rhodes Island and Reunion fighters. Seeing that the joint alliance has taken Misha into L.G.D. custody, Skullshatterer angrily vows to crush them all. He splits the team by having his men ambush R.I. in the alleyways while [[W]] attacks the L.G.D. convoy and captures Misha. When the battle is over, R.I. immediately regroups with the L.G.D. to prepare for a rescue mission.
