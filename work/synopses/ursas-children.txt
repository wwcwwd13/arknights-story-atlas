==Habits==
Gummy has several habits, some of which are worth worrying about.

She shares a handshake with [[Istina]], who is usually the first to wake up, and then goes to wake [[Zima]], who may or may not return the gesture depending on her mood. 

On one particular day, Gummy waits for Istina and forces herself to wake up, panicking when she realizes her night light is not on. She finds a note from Istina and Zima, learning that they will be back in the evening. Gummy proceeds with her usual routine and helps out in the cafeteria kitchen, passing food to [[Courier]] to serve after getting help from [[Gitano]]. She runs into [[Ceobe]] and bribes her with a cookie from Courier to keep her out of the kitchen.

Eventually, Gummy succumbs to her hunger, to the point of accidentally chewing on [[Dur-nar]]'s hip. Dur-nar treats her to food and watches in astonishment as Gummy scarfs everything down. When Dur-nar points out Gummy's habit of holding back the third strike in combat, or even in the kitchen, Gummy reluctantly says she will try to fix it.

After her training routine, Dur-nar orders Gummy to strike her shield as Gummy continues to stop herself after the second strike. However, on the third consecutive strike, she delivers a full blow and knocks Dur-nar down. Dur-nar immediately stops the training and sends Gummy to rest, apologizing for misjudging her habit. Gummy returns to her room and finds herself disappointed by her bad habits, such as carrying a safe door and a frying pan wherever she goes, sucking the last few drops from a tube of toothpaste, keeping her distance from edges whenever she is somewhere high, fighting the urge to devour the nearest object when famished, and being unable to stay in the dark for too long. Fortunately, Zima and Istina turn the light back on and tease the sniveling Gummy. Zima tells her to clean the room since Leto is coming, and the two hug her. It turns out they went out shopping, as it has been one year since they boarded [[Rhodes Island]]. Zima prepares to sleep, and Gummy wakes her back up while Istina prepares the refreshments.

As they celebrate, Gummy reflects on a habit she has. She laughs whenever she is happy, and she hopes [[Rosa]] will be able to come next time. Ceobe then appears with a cookie for Gummy, who questions its large size but is thankful nonetheless. That night, Gummy finds that her night light is broken, but fortunately Istina and Zima offer to sleep together in her bed.

==The Chosen==
Inside her room, Istina records herself as a form of therapy recommended by [[Closure]]. She recounts the events of Chernobog, lamenting that Gummy and [[Rosa|Natalya]] can never forget, that Zima seems to have been having nightmares lately, and that [[Leto]] appears to be the only one able to move on, but questions if it is possible to do so. She introduces herself and her teddy bear, Vika, and speaks about their group, which was formed while they were locked up for around two weeks because of a white-haired boy from Reunion. Istina recalls Vika's death and how they eventually managed to escape. She is interrupted by [[May]] and Natalya. Over tea, they chat after May returns Istina's book, with Istina rejecting May's insistence on calling her a junior detective and asking Rosa not to tease her. May pushes some biscuits on Istina, prompting Natalya to laugh as the two bicker over whether they are actually close or not. Natalya and Istina reminisce about the house parties they used to attend, though Istina insists those banquets were very different from normal parties. 

Vika catches May's interest, but Istina startles her by sharply stopping her from touching the bear. Natalya realizes that the bear once belonged to the deceased Vika, and Istina asks her to stop talking. To Istina's inner anguish, Natalya reassures her that she had only been a second too late. Istina thanks her and insists that she is fine. May finds the final volume of a detective series and mentions that she has an autographed first edition back home, much to Istina's envy. Nonetheless, Istina allows May to borrow the book for a reread, although May only asked to subdue Istina's envy. She then learns that May and Natalya became acquainted when May was investigating [[Penguin Logistics]].

Istina stops May from spoiling the novel's story for Natalya, and is surprised when Natalya expresses interest in reading more detective novels so she can join their discussions. When Istina asks about a test Natalya mentioned earlier, Natalya replies that Istina will find out soon. Their conversation shifts to the book's themes, and Istina wonders what a detective should do after uncovering the truth. May answers that a detective does not pass judgment or place themselves above the law, but simply brings the truth of the crime to light. Istina falls into contemplation, prompting Natalya to remark that they should choose a lighter topic next time.

May notices a drone caught in the rigging outside and moves to retrieve it, triggering a sudden flashback for Istina of watching Vika fall. When she snaps back to reality, Istina sharply scolds May for jumping out of the window and tends to the scratch on her hand. From Natalya, Istina learns that the note she received was addressed to Natalya and had been sent by [[Silence]]. Istina then sends May and Natalya on their way, though Natalya hesitates, holding back words she ultimately does not say. Left alone, Istina speaks to Vika, wondering what good news Natalya might be bringing. She admits that she has never forgotten what happened to Vika, and that although one day they must all come to terms with what happened in Chernobog, she is still not strong enough to do so yet.

==Meeting in a Dream==
Zima dreams of a past memory. She wakes to have breakfast with her parents. Her father advises her to behave properly and attend a trade school, while her mother scolds him for projecting his own hang-ups and hopes that Zima can complete her studies. Her mother, formerly a maid to the nobility, has managed to secure stable employment, whereas her father is currently unemployed after losing his previous job. Zima is put off by the breakfast before her, sees a doppelgänger of herself mocking her, and realizes that she is trapped in a nightmare. She attempts to strike the other version of herself, but is instead dragged into another scene, where she intervenes to help a bullied student. The scene shifts once more to a bloodstained classroom, where the student she helped has died during the school's lockdown. Zima reunites with Istina, an old neighbor and a student from another school. She accepts Istina's group, which includes [[Gummy|Lada]] and Vika, but sees floating school uniforms representing those she killed in defense.

She finds herself in a classroom. Istina apologizes to Zima for the conflict, and Zima attempts to comfort her by asking how she became the leader of her group in the first place. Istina answers that Lada pushed her into the role, and that she felt she had no choice but to accept it. Zima attributes the school's situation to the fire in one of the food pantries, but Istina disagrees, arguing that something else would have occurred regardless and lamenting that cruelty is inherent to human nature. Zima attempts to divert Istina from her sorrow by asking whether she believes everyone commits wrongdoing. She then promises to become their leader herself, an act that leads to the formation of the U.S.S.G. At this moment, Zima's doppelgänger mocks her, and the scene shifts into an argument between Istina and Vika over whether to align with the aristocrats or remain with Zima.

Meanwhile, Zima sneaks past fighting students, reflecting that Istina is the one who openly despises the nobles for hoarding the remaining food. Her doppelgänger narrates the sequence of events. Zima fails to go straight to Natalya and instead becomes embroiled in a confrontation with the noble students, during which she accidentally knocks over a candelabra and ignites the second fire. The school descends into deeper chaos, and several members of the U.S.S.G., including Vika, go missing.

One day, [[Reunion]] withdraws, but by then the students no longer care. The [[Catastrophe]] strikes the following day, forcing the U.S.S.G. to take refuge in a section of the city not yet overgrown with Originium. They are eventually rescued by Rhodes Island, though Zima nearly attacks the Operator who discovers them. They survive, yet Zima reflects that she has failed to resolve Istina's worries. Visions of the U.S.S.G. members manifest, voicing their doubt, resentment, and condemnation of her leadership. The nightmare culminates in Zima awakening violently. She staggers to the washstand and vomits, lamenting her failure toward Istina as she stares out the window.

==Hypocrisy==
Natalya overhears two Operators praising her for her contribution to the rescue operations, and humbly insists she was simply doing the bare minimum, and that earning the trust of the scared refugees happened to be something she is good at. She is grateful that everyone was willing to put up with her because of her young age. The topic shifts to the U.S.S.G., and Natalya hesitates before answering that they are all close, although she is in Logistics. They notice Zima staring in their direction, specifically at Natalya, who excuses herself. Natalya then approaches Zima, noting her pale face and jumpy attitude, and asks her to come to her room.

Natalya offers Zima coffee or tea, amused at Zima's rudeness, and makes her coffee without sugar at her request. She adds a spoonful of sugar after Zima finds it too bitter, and Zima claims they are not friends, a notion that Natalya agrees with. Natalya reasons that some things are harder to say to those one is close to. Thus, she brought Zima for a conversation precisely because they are not friends, although she muses that Anna would be the best person for her. Meanwhile, Zima only has her as an option, owing to Leto and Gummy's natures. Natalya asks Zima about the last two months fighting for Rhodes Island, teasing her for talking back to the [[Doctor]] and admitting she envies Zima. Zima reveals she has been having nightmares, to which Natalya inquires about her intention to stay in Rhodes Island.

The further Natalya talks, the deeper she falls into her self-guilt, playing with the idea of Zima, the protector of the weak, killing her, much to Zima's disgust. She reveals that some of the nobles escaped Chernobog before the incident, but her family was slow to act. She reasons that the nobility would have fought among themselves anyway, even without the fire, and reveals that during the Peterheim lockdown, she used her position as president of the student council at #4 Secondary School and as the eldest daughter of Count Rostov to unite the noble students, preventing further infighting. However, just as [[Ursus]] gained its strength from waging wars, Natalya allowed the noble students to prey on the commoners. This infuriates Zima, who punches Natalya several times. Natalya simply smiles and admits that it would have been better if Zima had killed her back in Chernobog, as she would have died believing she was a good person.

Natalya shows a letter opener and confesses to her suicidal ideations, even remembering the number of her attempts and considering more elegant methods. However, she admits that she does not want to make trouble, as she stayed for atonement, despite how her past cowardice and hypocrisy disgust her. Despite not killing a single person, Natalya feels she carries the most guilt, and can only wear a façade while not knowing what to tell Zima about what she should do. After a moment of silence, she apologizes for losing her composure and answers black tea when Zima offers to make her a drink. They banter for some more, with Natalya intending to be honest to avoid any misunderstandings.

Zima answers that she wants to stay at Rhodes Island, as her parents both died in Chernobog, and tells Natalya that she hates her because they were previously enemies, and that she has nightmares that make her puke. Natalya bursts into laughter and says Zima completely failed to address her problem. She states that she has already chosen her codename, ''Rosa'', meaning dew.

==Talking in Riddles==
Leto and [[Beehunter]] spar in the training room, with Beehunter promising to give Leto a prize if she can land a good hit. Beehunter remarks on Leto's power and steady breathing, then knocks her out with her special move. Leto then begs her to teach her the move. They spar again afterward, this time with Leto using her polearm while Beehunter uses knucklers. They chat after the session, with Leto mentioning that she once stood up to Reunion. Piqued by the topic, Beehunter sends Leto to get honey drinks from Logistics and snacks from Dur-nar's secret network while she showers. Leto decides to have Gummy do it for her.

In their room, they play a round of cards, and Leto wins, having surprisingly gotten drunk from honey to Beehunter's amusement. While Beehunter goes to get a drink for Leto, Zima knocks on their door, demanding her cut after hearing that Leto sent Gummy to get snacks. Zima asks Beehunter about Leto's drunkenness and decides to leave rather than listen to Leto's grand stories. While outside, Mousse also asks them to continue their conversation inside the room. Despite her drunkenness, Leto decides to narrate her experience during the Peterheim lockdown to Beehunter.

It is revealed that Leto spent her days rejecting gangs trying to poach her. Remarking on the nuts Beehunter brought her, Leto explains that Reunion shoved students from nearby schools into Peterheim. At one point, as food supplies dwindled, Leto decides to join Zima for some peace, but stumbles upon Zima dealing with students to protect her group. Leto helps out but notes that Zima has changed. She is the one who defends the members while Zima is not in great shape, thanking Gummy for treating her injury. Pondering over their dwindling rations, Leto decides to approach the Reunion members, asking to speak to their leader. The combatants respond by raising their weapons, but they are ordered by [[Faust]] to let her through. She returns safe and sound, but only receives words like "your suffering has only just begun" and "enjoy life" from [[Mephisto]]. Reunion leaves the next day, but the U.S.S.G. discovers that things do not get better. Leto finds that Beehunter has fallen asleep.

The next morning, Leto sobers up and finds that she has drooled all over Beehunter's doll. Leto tries to recall what she told Beehunter the previous day, but nonetheless thinks it must have been something fun and intends to proceed with her daily life. She is confused about why her guitar is out, but decides to strum a few chords for a moment.

==Business as Usual==
The story turns the focus on [[Amiya]] and the [[Doctor]] having a discussion. Amiya brings up the topic of Natalya's transfer application to the Doctor, and they discuss the U.S.S.G. girls. Amiya admits she wanted to have Kal'tsit veto their applications out of worry for their mental states. At Amiya's request, the Doctor decides to check on Natalya. They encounter Zima in the hallways, who asks what they are doing. [[Nearl]] then scolds Zima for wandering before deployment, and Zima leaves, not before claiming she will get to boss them around one day, amusing Nearl with her ambition. Nearl explains that Zima does not take well to discipline and suffers from confusion, but says she likes her for her integrity. Later, the Doctor is greeted by Gummy and watches as the girl asks [[Perfumer]] to teach her how to cook with spices before helping [[Matterhorn]] out in the kitchen. Perfumer muses that she once got worried from hearing Gummy's sleep-talking when she fell asleep in her workshop, but says they will be okay in time.

After Perfumer leaves, Istina appears and remarks she has never seen the Doctor in the cafeteria before. She says she will be going to attend Hypatia's history class, as it could provide her with some inspiration. The Doctor asks about Natalya's whereabouts, and Istina says she is likely preparing for transfer, as Gummy told Istina that Natalya applied to be a frontline operator. Meanwhile, Leto is helping Natalya practice with her modified siege weapon. [[Dobermann]] tells Natalya that she lacks stamina and that she is still far from combat-ready, although her being Ursus gives her a nice base, and commends her willpower. She and Grace instructs them for the training session. Dobermann greets the Doctor, but tells them they should be resting during lunchtime. She surmises that Amiya must have sent them, because back when the four girls applied, Amiya was against the idea but changed her mind at some point. She understands Amiya's plight, but says they can only teach them how to fight and protect themselves.

Returning to see Amiya, the Doctor asks if she saw something in them. Amiya answers that occasionally she senses immense emotions from them, naming the main one as "helplessness," as it blocks them from moving forward. The Doctor asks if they can help them, to which Amiya replies they cannot force them to open up or help unprompted. She laments that all she can do is respect their choice and protect them in her own way, somewhat feeling full of herself, and the Doctor reassures her. Amiya decides to approve Natalya's application, hoping that Rhodes Island can be a home for them.

==Before Spring's Coming==
The story follows the perspective of [[Absinthe|Zoya]], the daughter of a policeman in Chernobog during the Reunion uprising. Zoya overhears a discussion between her parents about the conflict between the police and the mob. Zoya bids goodbye to her father before he goes for his night shift.

During the Chernobog crisis, Zoya is held captive at her school along with other students by Reunion. She eavesdropped on the sentries and learned of their reason for gathering the students in various schools. It was to prevent them from getting harmed by the riots under [[Patriot]]'s orders. Zoya's escape is noticed by Reunion combatants, but the Reunion caster intentionally lets Zoya go so that the girl will walk into her own grave.

Upon leaving the school, she encounters Uncle Valery. Valery persuades her to seek shelter until the Army arrives, but she insists on finding her parents. Valery informs her that her mother had likely died, while her father volunteered for a dangerous mission to Peterheim Middle School to rescue the trapped students. Zoya sets out to find him and Valery reluctantly gives her a terminal to contact the military communications network.

Zoya reaches Peterheim Middle School but finds the remnants of students, scorch marks, and school uniforms littered around. She notices that the bodies were not burnt, nor does it seem like the work of Reunion, but she comes across something that does not match the bodies of the students. Her father had been killed by the rioting students. Zoya recalls a conversation she once had with her father.

{{Quote|"Even if the ground's still frozen, there'll be some little flowers blooming. In this city, spring will come as usual."}}

Some time later, Zoya joins Rhodes Island as Operator Absinthe and receives her registration card. Upon encountering the U.S.S.G. girls, she recognizes their uniforms and realizes they are from Peterheim.

==Trivia==
*The story is highly reminiscent to the novel ''[[wikipedia:Lord of the Flies|Lord of the Flies]]'', wherein a group of teenage boys of various social classes were stranded on a deserted island, and, despite, or rather because of attempting to maintain an air of civilization and order, quickly turned on one another. 
**While it was mainly the social classes that was the catalyst for trouble on the island in ''Lord of the Flies'', it is highly implied in Children of Ursus that [[Mephisto]] actively and deliberately created an environment that would cause infighting.
