==Synopsis==

===Chaos in Baizao===
The year has stretched to October 1102. With no news since Wang entered the Tomb of Sui, the [[True Lung]] makes an agreement with the [[Grand Commandant]] and sets the day of the full moon as the deadline before which [[Yan]] will send troops to quell the Sui. [[Zuo Le]], [[Leizi the Thunderbringer|Lin Qingyan]] and [[Yun Qingping]] exit the [[Garden of Grotesqueries]], having finally completed the Placation once and for all after completing it an unknown number of times. This time, however, the proof of their completion is not a jade rule, but a small "doll", a geist of seemingly great importance. Upon seeing it, the [[Old Tianshi]] orders Zuo Le to return it to the [[Sui Regulator]]'s Supervisor, while Lin Qingyan and Yun Qingping stay behind in [[Mangshan|Mangshan Town]].<ref>[[TA-ST-1]]</ref>

[[File:70 i05.png|thumb|"I'll find the answers myself—and I'm not leaving until I do."]]
At a posthouse on the outskirts of the Yanese capital [[Baizao]], [[Ch'en the Dawnstreak|Ch'en Hui-chieh]] has an argument with her uncle and Chief of [[Lungmen]], [[Wei Yenwu]]. Wei has revealed everything to Ch'en; about the [[Feranmut]] [[Sui]] slumbering beneath Baizao, the inevitable war against it, and how everything is in the hands of [[Yan Li|his estranged brother]], the current [[True Lung]]. He advises her to go home before it's too late, but faced with such an unprecedented crisis, she refuses to back down. Shortly afterwards, the [[Grand Tutor]] comes to visit and briefly explains the situation in the capital before leaving. Wei attempts to inquire about [[Talulah]] but Ch'en refuses to answer. Ch'en steps outside to think for a while, when she gets a vision of two strange men and a large building. After she snaps out of it, a sudden commotion brings her back to the posthouse, where she discovers that Wei has disappeared.<ref name="TA1Bef">[[TA-1/Story#Before operation|TA-1 Before]]</ref>

Meanwhile, Zuo Le arrives in Baizao only to discover that the entire city is undergoing evacuation and that martial law is in full effect. He is eventually stopped by a masked candleholder, who claims he has instructions from the supervisor to complete the handover of the token. When Zuo Le insists on delivering the token personally, Patrol Battalion soldiers begin surrounding him, putting him on guard since the Sui Regulator has never cooperated with the army. Before they can seize him, a smoke grenade explodes at their feet, throwing them into disarray and allowing Zuo Le to escape.<ref name="TA1Aft">[[TA-1/Story#After operation|TA-1 After]]</ref>

Zuo Le makes his way to a secluded location, where he confronts the one who threw a smoke bomb: none other than [[Mo Yi]], who had been following him at the behest of the Old Tianshi. As she is a [[Shanhaizhong]], Zuo Le does not trust her but Mo Yi insists that now is the time to work together. After some reluctance he finally hands over the token, which turns out to be the doll, "[[Little Moji]]".  Like how she did in Mangshan Town when she summoned a spectral Shuo, she attempts to do the same to manifest the fifth Proxy, [[Jie]], but nothing happens. Mo Yi then tells Zuo Le she will "borrow" the doll until she is done studying it, before running off. Zuo Le chases after her.<ref name="TA1Aft"/>

Ch'en arrives at the city to search for Wei Yenwu and goes through the checkpoint without issue, only for scriptgeists to begin appearing non-stop. A dignified woman, seemingly oblivious to the dangers they pose, muses about their appearance not having changed in centuries. Ch'en "saves" the woman from the scriptgeists, who recognises her as a descendant of the House of Yan. Still, she thanks Ch'en before melting back into the crowd.<ref name="TA1Aft"/>

===Five Hundred Years of Pondering===
Inside the Tomb, Wang begins his confrontation against bestial Sui, who begins to taunt him and send him back into his memories of the past. In the year 593, [[Chongyue|Shuo]] and Wang play a game of weiqi. After they finish, Wang tells him that a high-ranking official from the Ministry of War had accused Shuo of commanding troops to act without proper authorization, even though their route was affected by a Catastrophe. Fortunately, that same official was caught having accepted bribes. Shuo understands his arrest was Wang's doing, and becomes unhappy as Wang had promised not to meddle in court affairs. Wang justifies his actions by stating that strange things have been happening ever since the "birth" of their youngest brother, Yu. One of [[Fāng]]'s patient committed suicide, fake satirical works have been made under [[Dusk]]'s name, and thieves have been stealing from [[Yi]]'s Garden of Grotesqueries... Wang knows the true culprit behind these events is their Whole self still slumbering in Its' Tomb.<ref name="TA2Bef">[[TA-2/Story#Before operation|TA-2 Before]]</ref>

[[File:70 i01 1.png|thumb|left|"Let's call it 'scholar's barbecue'!"]]
Meanwhile, in Baizao, [[Yu]] is being taught word games by his sister Jie. Jie copies the contents of bamboo slips onto paper (a new invention at the time), while Yu uses the leftover bamboo to make meat skewers. Wang arrives shortly after, commenting that it is "wasteful and sacrilegious behaviour" to use the bamboo slips in such a way, scaring Yu. Jie scolds Wang, saying that their youngest sibling is still afraid of him and his stern look. After Yu cooks them dinner, he informs her of everything that has happened to their siblings recently and Jie is surprised that he is showing so much concern for them as well. Wang tells her they aren't true siblings but Jie dismisses him, wondering if he would regret it if something ever happened to one of them.<ref name="TA2Bef"/>

However, of bigger importance was what happened recently. A book she had been transcribing had been vandalized and only one word was written on every page: Sui. Wang does not know how to deal with It yet but Jie offers a solution: her writing knife. Her powers have been steadily growing alongside Yan's history, and she guesses that it will only be a matter of time before she can excise Sui from existence with her own power. Only then will they truly be free. However, Wang refuses to share his thoughts and plans with her, leading her to become exasperated and asking him if he could bring some of her books with him to be reviewed by the Sui Regulator before they are archived.<ref name="TA2Bef"/>

As Wang does as he was asked, he encounters a strange man on the side of a street. The man claims that a game of weiqi took everything from him and that he cannot save his stones. When Wang hears that the stones are named after his siblings, he realises that it is Sui's trap and stabs the strange man, who then disappears altogether. A Candleholder soon comes to see him to investigate and Wang shares what happens. Wang then hands over the books Jie had finished, and the Candleholder praises her beautiful calligraphy and dedication. But when the Candleholder admires the copy of the Jijiuzhang, the paper is completely blank. A blatant taunt by Sui, reminding Wang that she is gone from the physical realm.<ref name="TA2Aft">[[TA-2/Story#After operation|TA-2 After]]</ref>

Wang refuses to allow Sui to trap him in a cage of memories, so he returns to grab the vandalised Jijiuzhang and begins to remove each "Sui" character one by one with Jie's writing knife. With every word he scrapes away, his surroundings become more and more hellish. At the end of the book is a message left by Jie in a unique style of calligraphy that she uses only when writing to her siblings, but the entire page is covered by the "Sui" character. For each character he erases, a wound opens up on his body. As he removes the last of the vile character, he dies before he could read the message. However, another Wang stands in his place, unharmed, as the body on the ground dissipates into three weiqi stones. Three stones was the price for killing Sui once. Removing Sui from the memories causes Sui Itself to forget those memories, "killing" a small piece of it. However, Sui has many more memories to draw from.<ref name="TA2Aft"/>

===The Pavilion of History===
[[Jiang]] the Candleholder paces around Savors at Yu's, fuming that they have not evacuated with the other residents. Yu insists it's because soldiers and travelers might come by to eat, and one of his siblings is also here for a meal. Just then, another "customer" walks in, who is none other than [[Ya]] the Feranmut. Ya looks down at Yu, in disbelief that a Feranmut would stoop to running a kitchen for "mere ants". When Ya sees Jiang return from the kitchen, Ya immediately raises a sword against him, only for Yu to put himself in front of Jiang. The standoff is interrupted by [[Jun]], the Fourth Proxy, who slams her hand on her table like the fall of a gavel, demanding that Ya either eats or leave. Ya is once more incredulous that the proxies would protect Sui Regulators but Jun simply tells It that It cannot tell them how to live when It itself hasn't. Ya only uncaringly looks at the food in front of Them before leaving, but not without leaving a foreboding warning.<ref name="TA3Bef">[[TA-3/Story#Before operation|TA-3 Before]]</ref>

[[File:70 i21.png|thumb|"Still playing dumb? Don't you even know who your parents are?"]]
Meanwhile, Ch'en heads towards the Sky Mirror Pavilion, the building she saw in her visions. She is confronted by an aloof woman who tells her to leave as she has no business here with the entire city being evacuated. Ch'en offers to help with the investigation and shows her ID. This surprises the woman and she reveals her name: [[Ch'en Chao-ch'ien]], a relative who up until now believed she was the last of the Ch'ens. Ch'en herself had always thought she was the only one. Suddenly, more scriptgeists attack, and Ch'en fends them off as she escorts Chao-ch'ien to the pavilion for safety, only to realise the pavilion is the origin point of the geists.<ref name="TA3Bef"/>

Unbeknownst to them, Mo Yi and Zuo Le are already within the Sky Mirror Pavilion. Zuo Le's attempts to take back the doll from her causes a huge ruckus, before Chao-ch'ien yells at them to stop. However, Mo Yi escalates the situation by holding Chao-ch'ien hostage. The noise attracts the attention of [[Liang Xun]], who is now a transcriber at the pavilion. He convinces everyone that they are all on the same side and Mo Yi releases Chao-ch'ien. As the group begins assessing their current situation, Zuo Le notes that the geists have the appearances of soldiers of the armies of Yan during the Great Hunt, as depicted in the Sky Mirror Pavilion's archives. Mo Yi confirms that they are caused by the breathing of the Feranmut Sui, a clear sign that Sui is near awakening.<ref name="TA3Aft">[[TA-3/Story#After operation|TA-3 After]]</ref>

As to why they originate specifically from the Sky Mirror Pavilion, they speculate that it is because of a special relic that belonged to Jie which remained even after she disappeared: a writing knife, held within her sealed-off office at the top floor of Sky Mirror Pavilion. Two keys are needed to open it: one belonging to the Fourth Proxy, and the other belonging to the Supervisor of the Sui Regulator. Liang Xun confirms that sometime ago, the Ministry of War had people come searching for it. They deduce that the Grand Commandant is acting against the wishes of the rest of the Imperial Court for his own ends, so the group head to Jie's office to find and protect the writing knife.<ref name="TA3Aft"/>

===Three Hundred Years of Planning===
[[File:70 i02 1.png|thumb|left|"Do you really know what you're doing? Or has your obsession with victory blinded you to risks and consequences?"]]
In the Tomb, Wang continues his confrontation against Sui, who again throws him into the past to mock him. The year is 759, and the Yanese army celebrate their final victory against the remnants of the Great Hunt after the defeat of [[Ju]] and the Shanhaizhong. The Imperial Commissioner had nothing but praise for Shuo and Wang, and even promises to be more lenient on them. Once they are away though, Shuo privately scolds Wang for secretly letting Ju go. Wang defends his actions by saying he is simply preparing his cards, so Shuo implores him to share whatever plans he has with him, vowing to help him. When Wang keeps silent, Shuo becomes so angry that he accidentally breaks Wang's weiqi board. Wang leaves shortly after, dismissing his brother's concern.<ref name="TA4Bef">[[TA-4/Story#Before operation|TA-4 Before]]</ref>

As Wang walks through town, he eventually walks into [[Ling]], who immediately deduces that he was arguing with Shuo about Sui again. Ling drags him back to Shuo, and the two make amends. Wang admits that his lack of urgency irritates him but Ling simply tells him that each of them are preparing to deal with the Sui problem. Ling even proposes preparing a dream eternal for them all where they would all be safe even if Sui fully awakened.<ref name="TA4Aft">[[TA-4/Story#After operation|TA-4 After]]</ref>

Wang asks Ling to help him peer into Sui's dreams as it has been unusually active lately, indicating that something might be causing it to wake faster. With her help, Wang enters Sui's dream. Inside he witnesses a battlefield and sees Sui, in pain, afraid and angry, but unable to fight back or even whimper. The reason Sui is suddenly waking fast is because It is experiencing a nightmare, and anyone trapped in one would naturally seek to wake quickly. But before Wang can meddle any further, the Feranmut [[Hou]] suddenly appears and warns him that his very presence here has filled in the void left behind when his siblings separated from Sui, creating a reflection of Sui in Wang's form. However, Wang sees this as a chance to be rid of Sui once and for all. Seeing the decision Wang is about to make, Hou fetches Ling, who in turn reminds Wang that he made her a promise to not do anything foolish. Wang reluctantly agrees to be pulled out of the dream.<ref name="TA4Aft"/>

As the memory ends, Sui reminds Wang that this was the day he made his greatest and most fatal error: Sui had seen him, mirrored him and begun plotting against Its wayward fragments in order to trick them. It then shows another supposed "memory" of a fully awakened Sui rampaging about as Shuo and the armies of Yan attempts to fight It. Wang does not passively watch this and finds the Sui within this "memory", and kills it along with himself. Despite sacrificing more stones to kill Sui again, he is unconcerned with the loss of these stones against the cost of so much of Sui's memories. However, Sui is far from finished.<ref name="TA4Aft"/>

[[File:70 i06.png|thumb|The True Lung's authority over all under heaven, tied to a tiny crystal.]]
During the year 962, 140 years ago, a new True Lung takes the throne. The True Lung learns of [[Bu Fan]], a special system tied to the [[First Originium]] known as the [[Wa Stone]], designed to suppress and even kill Sui should the need arise albeit at the cost of the current True Lung's life. The True Lung however sees the limitless potential behind Bu Fan, if only it was not limited to merely containing the Sui. Seeking to realise his ambition, he summons Wang for a private audience and asks him how he intends to deal with Sui. Wang explains that if he is given time he could eventually replace Sui, but the True Lung only gives him twenty years.<ref name="TA5Bef">[[TA-5/Story#Before operation|TA-5 Before]]</ref>

At the Sky Mirror Pavilion, Jie receives her new Candleholder after the retirement of the previous one. Her new Candleholder is named [[Chun]], the last [[Elf]] descendant of the Feranmut Yaan. As Jie explains to Chun what her role will be like, Chun admits that she heard that Jie has a writing knife imbued with her power. She eventually admits that her interest in it was in the hopes that she could clear the name of the Elves as during the Great Hunt, Elves were seen as "fickle and disloyal" causing Yan to reject them. While Jie is unwilling to use the writing knife for such a task, she nevertheless offers to help find proof of the Elves and how they truly were to clear their names, which Chun happily agrees to.<ref name="TA5Bef"/>

Three years later, during the year 965, work was well underway to turn Baizao into a nomadic city. Jie had just finished arguing about historical records with someone from the Ministry of Works when Chun suddenly barges into Jie's office, exclaiming that Jie's writing knife has been stolen. Later, while she and Jun review the evidence, they deduce that it is more than likely someone acting under orders from the True Lung had stolen it, as only a handful of people know of its existence outside of their family. Jie asks Jun to keep this from Wang because she fears that he would do something irrational if he found out. While Jun agrees, she also asks to know if her and Wang are truly planning anything concerning Sui, which Jie neither confirms nor denies, admitting that she still dreamt of a future where they are all able to truly live as themselves.<ref name="TA5Bef"/><ref name="TA5Aft">[[TA-5/Story#After operation|TA-5 After]]</ref>

Elsewhere, Chun is summoned by the Sui Regulator's Supervisor, who has noticed her close relationship with Jie. She warns that Jie is the most dangerous proxy, which she demonstrates by letting Chun attempt to modify some documents, which immediately revert to their original state when tampered with. The Supervisor uses this as an example as to why Jie should be feared, with her control over Yan's history and her ever growing power tied to that very history, she could grow even more powerful and terrible than Sui itself. Afterwards, Chun returned Jie's writing knife—which had actually been taken by the True Lung—and she apologizes for her negligence.<ref name="TA5Aft"/>

As the memory ends, Sui once again mocks Wang for digging his own grave and for the events that ultimately lead to Jie's demise.

===Ascending the Pavilion===
Two days before the full moon, in the middle of the night, Liang Xun attempts to return the various books that were scattered about during the previous fights and is eventually joined by [[Ning Ciqiu]]. The two talk about personal matters, including what happened to [[Ning Shu]] following [[Such is the Joy of Our Reunion/Synopsis|the chaos at the Feast of a Hundred Delicacies]]. With everything happening in Baizao and around them, the two are able to reconcile their feelings towards one another and enjoy a private night together.<ref name="TA6Bef">[[TA-6/Story#Before operation|TA-6 Before]]</ref>

The next day, Zuo Le continues to deal with more scriptgeists as they head towards Jie's office at the top of the Pavilion. Mo Yi tells him the history of the Sky Mirror Pavilion. Zuo Le begrudgingly admits that his studies have been inadequate but complains that she still should not speak of these secrets, which only further annoys Mo Yi. This results in the two talking about each other's lives, why Mo Yi joined the Shanhaizhong and why Zuo Le joined the Sui Regulator.<ref name="TA6Bef"/>

Meanwhile, Ch'en and Chao-ch'ien work together in another section. Chao-ch'ien is somewhat belligerent towards her due to their family history but she eventually realizes that she should not take out her anger on Ch'en. Chao-ch'ien explains that the Ch'ens were a prestigious family of historians who mostly stayed out of politics until a certain cousin was married into the royal line of the True Lung through Princess Yanjing, Ch'en's mother. While her eventual death in Lungmen did not bring the ire of the True Lung against the Ch'ens, the rumors surrounding her death were more than enough to tarnish the Ch'en name from then on. This resulted in the Ch'ens being ostracised and seen as villains in history.<ref name="TA6Bef"/>

In [[Yumen]], [[Zuo Xuanliao]] stands quietly in his command center as the city steadily approaches Baizao and he reminisces about his past with Shuo. Officials from the Ministry of War suddenly enter, demanding to know why he has delayed the city's approach to Baizao. Zuo Xuanliao and his soldiers confirm that there has been no delay and that they have been on full alert, despite the delays caused by the [[Catastrophe]] that struck [[Where Vernal Winds Will Never Blow/Synopsis|earlier in the year]]. However, the official accuses him of being distrustful of the Grand Commandant and of disobeying his orders, to which Zuo Xuanliao asks if this the will of the True Lung, but the officials only dodge the question, agitating the Yumen sentries. The official further berates Zuo Xianliao by saying that his indecision will cause the deaths of millions, including his son and wife, which only serves to enrage the general, but the official remains undeterred, telling him that they will remain until he comes to the right decision.<ref name="TA6Aft">[[TA-6/Story#After operation|TA-6 After]]</ref>

===One Thousand Years of Regret===
Sui tortures Wang with his most painful memory. The year is 982. Baizao has finished moving onto its nomadic platforms and other such cities are now becoming mobile across the whole of Yan. The Sky Mirror Pavilion has been expanded in size multiple times in order to accommodate the near constant influx of books and other written materials coming in from other cities and even abroad. While many of the siblings have gone to work in other cities or focus on their specialities, Wang has shuttered himself inside a temple for twenty years. Jie cannot help but wonder what exactly he is planning.<ref name="TA7Bef">[[TA-7/Story#Before operation|TA-7 Before]]</ref>

After a long and exhausting day of working in the Sky Mirror Pavilion, Jie asks Chun to bring back some good food or anything else that would interest her. After Chun leaves the building, a Mandarin delivers an urgent letter to her, containing a list of crimes done by Wang across the centuries since his "birth". Chun tries to confirm the truth but the Mandarin's figure suddenly becomes indistinct, only telling her to "eliminate the great evil." As she returns to the pavilion, she hears more mandarins talking about Jie, their faces and forms indistinct, speaking as if Jie was an authoritarian leader. Chun, unsettled, then picks up a seemingly random scroll from one of the shelves only to read predictions of how Jie will eventually gain control of the entirety of Yan, depicting a dark regime where thousands will die by having their existence erased.<ref name="TA7Bef"/>

Chun denies this vision vehemently, knowing that Jie is not like this. A shadow of herself then whispers into her ear, seeding doubt in her, stating that Jie's power will continue to grow until she becomes an even more terrible version of Sui. The shadow tells her that it knows she'll make the right choice before vanishing, but not before revealing its true nature for a split second, that every shadow and every letter was the work of Sui to manipulate Chun.<ref name="TA7Bef"/>

Now that twenty years has passed and the deadline has come, the True Lung prepares to open the Tomb of Sui and readies the armies of Yan. Wang himself however is intending to do the same and finally heads towards the Tomb. Yet much to his shock and dread, upon reaching the tomb, he finds a mirror of himself already awakened. Sui challenges Wang to a game of Weiqi, which the latter barely wins. Sui then reveals their little game had only been a small part of a larger game, hundreds of lines across, with Sui's pieces dominating the board. For all his strengths as a strategist, Wang had failed to grasp the true extent at which Sui had prepared for this day, and now he will suffer the consequences.<ref name="TA7Bef"/><ref name="TA7Aft">[[TA-7/Story#After operation|TA-7 After]]</ref>

[[File:70 i18.png|thumb|She looks at the innumerable lives and deaths recorded upon the shelves... Would she become one of them?]]
With Sui beginning to awaken, every sibling feels Its influence, including Jie, who can feel its heartbeat reverberating. Jie immediately deduces that Wang is responsible. Faced with the thought of losing her brother, Jie asks Chun what it felt like to lose a loved one. Despite her very real and still raw pain of having lost her own mother, the manipulation she received at the hands of both the Imperial Court and Sui causes Chun to lie. Having been tricked into believing Jie is the biggest threat, she tells her that the loss of her mother only hurt for a little while, leading Jie to believe that Wang will be able to move on if he lost Jie. That said, Jie also says that she would have made the same choice regardless of Chun's answer.<ref name="TA7Aft"/>

Jun enters soon after and Jie tells her everything. Jie entrusts her writing knife to Jun, telling her to hand it only to someone who is truly impartial and absolutely just. Jun begins crying but a promise is made that they will one day be together again. Jie thus descends into the Tomb of Sui. The pain of this memory is just as vivid as Wang remembered it. Jie disappeared, whilst he, the failure, remained.<ref name="TA7Aft"/>

The last thing written on the page is always the word "regret."

===Defying the Will of Heaven===
In the Sky Mirror Pavilion, Zuo Le and the others have made it to the top floor, only to see someone guarding the office door. It is the Supervisor of the Sui Regulator, who is none other than Chun herself. She immediately asks that Zuo Le to hand over the token they obtained from the Garden of Grotesqueries, yet instincts tell him that something is amiss. After questioning her, the group realise Chun's goals are aligned with that of the Grand Commandant. Mo Yi deduces that the Grand Commandant plans to use the writing knife to sacrifice Wei Yenwu to eliminate the bestial Sui. The price of using Bu Fan would be the True Lung's life, but in order to spare the current True Lung, they plan to use the writing knife to alter history—if only for a short while—so that Wei Yenwu will be recognised as the True Lung rather than his brother.<ref name="TA8Bef">[[TA-8/Story#Before operation|TA-8 Before]]</ref>

Chao-ch'ien becomes very disturbed that she would be willing to tamper with history now after Chun had helped Jie so much in compiling history. Ch'en sees past Chun's lies, noting that Chun no longer believes in history or in lasting justice because the one who had been at her side on the long road of time died because of her own actions. Chun justifies her actions by saying it was the best decision at the time, believing that if left unchecked, Jie would have become even more terrifying than bestial Sui Itself. Ch'en, however, is unimpressed; she has seen countless people like Chun, lost souls who abandon their loved ones for their duties or their own goals. Even when Mo Yi plainly states that there is a chance that they could bring Jie back, Chun desperately denies the possibility of this idea.<ref name="TA8Bef"/>

Seeing as Chun won't be convinced, the trio fight against her, who proves to be a formidable foe. After a while of fighting, Chun manages to graze Mo Yi, causing the doll to fall out of her pocket. The doll suddenly starts moving towards Jie's office and the shadow of light around the doll is none other than Jie herself, instantly stunning Chun. "Jie" walks towards the door of her office and despite being locked through intricate rituals, opens it instantly. Eventually, her consciousness slips back into the dream and the geist doll once more slumbers into the century long dream. Chun desperately denies what she saw, but Jun arrives and confirms that the doll contains a sliver of Jie's life force, retrieved from Sui's Remnant Consciousness. Chun can no longer deny that everything the siblings have done since that day has not been in vain and leaves in despair.<ref name="TA8Aft">[[TA-8/Story#After operation|TA-8 After]]</ref>

[[File:70 i04 1.png|thumb|She clutches the writing knife. In her eyes, something sinks, and something else burns bright.]]
Jun thanks the group from the bottom of her heart for bringing back her sister. Jun then asks if one of them could bring the doll to Wang in the Tomb, which Mo Yi immediately volunteers out of great respect and reverence, with Zuo Le accompanying her. Ch'en recognises Jun as the woman she had saved at Baizao's gates. Jun judges Ch'en closely, and after deeming her worthy, Jun hands over Jie's writing knife to her, trusting that Ch'en will be absolutely just and impartial with it. The knife, although physically dulled from the loss of the power it once held, still holds more than enough that should it fall into the wrong hands, the damage done would be catastrophic. Ch'en then proceeds to the Imperial Palace, only to be stopped by a very familiar figure, Wei Yenwu.<ref name="TA8Aft"/>

As the situation in Baizao deteriorates, Imperial Guards are deployed to the frontline along with the Patrol Battalion to fend off the waves of geists. In the Imperial Palace, a weak and feeble Grand Commandant meets with the True Lung. The True Lung does not mask his intention to use Bu Fan to kill Sui himself, and questions why the Grand Commandant is so restless when Wei Yenwu will replace him after he dies, seemingly unaware of the Grand Commandant's plot. The Grand Commandant begs His Majesty to consider all the unprecedented turmoil that has happened across the world in the last year, believing that now is their moment to subjugate the weakened nations and become an invincible superpower that can stand up to anything. In response, the True Lung only states that his outlook for Yan is far brighter than his, and that if there is blood to be spilled to end that Great Hunt from a thousand years ago, then it is the True Lung's blood that should be spilled first. The emperor heads out to do just that, leaving the defeated Grand Commandant alone in the throne room.<ref name="TA9Bef">[[TA-9/Story#Before operation|TA-9 Before]]</ref>

Meanwhile, in front of the Palace, Ch'en finally confronts Wei Yenwu, who reveals that he intended to follow the Grand Commandant's plan to sacrifice himself. Ch'en argues he's only doing this for his own ego and glorifying his own death. Wei attempts to ignore her jabs and demands the writing knife but naturally Ch'en denies him, forcing his hand to fight for it.<ref name="TA9Bef"/> As the two battle, Wei asks if she intends to use Chi Xiao's ultimate move, Heaven's Eye, but Ch'en says that she'll never be able to use it because it demands a clear heart that never looks back. No matter what or how impossible the path seems, Ch'en will always hold onto her ideals. Then, like a sigh, she thrusts the writing knife out towards him in a single motion, unleashing her own technique, Heaven's Plaint. With the swordplay she demonstrates, Wei finally concedes. Ch'en promises to see her family matters resolved, and Wei himself plans to do something that he won't regret for once.<ref name="TA9Aft"/>

Mo Yi and Zuo Le move quickly across Baizao's streets to reach the Tomb, only to be intercepted by Ya, who demands that Mo Yi hold her end of the bargain and reveals Sui's location so It can exact revenge. When Mo Yi tries to negotiate, Ya grabs Zuo Le and holds him hostage, leaving Mo Yi with little choice than to hand over a map. Ya takes the map but also decides to take Mo Yi along as It does not trust her. Zuo Le attempts to stop Ya when suddenly two people interrupt them, the Feranmuts Hou and Ju. They attempt to reason with their vengeance-consumed kin but to no avail; Ya despises how human They have become and that They would side with mere "ants", and leaves them in her realm, forcing them to remain on the sideline for the remainder of this crisis, although They atleast managed to rescue Mo Yi in the process. Zuo Le decides to entrust Mo Yi with the doll while he goes and deals with Yumen advancing on Baizao before they attack and ruin the siblings' chance at surviving this crisis.<ref name="TA9Aft">[[TA-9/Story#After operation|TA-9 After]]</ref>

===To See the Old Year Out===
The day of the full moons has arrived.

Zuo Le waits patiently, crouched under Yumen's shadow as the city slowly moves towards the Tomb of Sui. In order to reach his father in time and prevent Yumen from attacking the Tomb, he scales the almost vertical walls before finally reaching the battlements. He barges into Zuo Xuanliao's room and immediately informs him of the Grand Commandant's nefarious schemes. Before long, the Minister of War officials arrive, and when the general refuses to heed their commands, they intend to have him arrested. Monitor Censor Taihe then appears, telling the corrupt official that he intends to report today's events to the government. The corrupt official and his men back down and are taken into custody. However, the Yumen sentries discover that the corrupt soldiers had sabotaged communications beforehand to prevent them from issuing a cease fire command. Zuo Le volunteers to be a runner to spread the word while soldiers begin repairs on the equipment.<ref name="TA10Bef">[[TA-10/Story#Before operation|TA-10 Before]]</ref>

Within the Tomb of Sui, Wang finally reads the message Jie left him: in truth while she agreed the two of them could not be more different, she knew his heart better than anyone, knowing that the two of them simply sought to understand the human heart despite their differing methods at achieving said goal. Sui admits it had feared Jie's power, but her death ensured Its victory. However, Wang declares that Jie had always believed that they would be able to surpass It, and that Sui is doomed to pay for Its folly. Wang has had enough of Its rambling and finally bare steel against It.<ref name="TA10Bef"/>

[[File:70 i16 1 a.png|thumb|left|"To die is your end, not mine."]]
After an intense fight, Wang falls to his knees, wounded. Sui mocks him for one last time, believing that by consuming Wang's strength, It will fully awaken and return to become the true god. However, despite attempting to awaken, It is unable to. Outside the Tomb, Ling and Jun work together to keep it slumbering, combining music and wine to weave a dream entrapping Sui. Wang rises again, saying that it will never have a chance to get its vengeance. Enraged and terrified, but fully intent on crushing Wang right here and there, It manages to shift Its massive body an inch even in slumber, causing a portion of the Tomb to collapse.<ref name="TA10Aft">[[TA-10/Story#After operation|TA-10 After]]</ref>

In Mangshan town, Sui's impending awakening results in rampaging geists to appear in droves. Lin Qingyan, Yun Qingping and the Old Tianshi defend the town, but their numbers prove to be overwhelming even for them. Lin Qingyan prepares to stay behind and hold back the geists so everyone else can escape, but fortunately the proxies Yi and Ji arrive to help. Meanwhile, outside the Tomb, Yu protects the Grand Tutor from encroaching geists. Nian, Shu, and Dusk then arrive on a giant mech that Nian conjured called the Dark Devastator, firing all sorts of weapons and smashing down its fists at geists. Someone flying over just happens to catch the explosive show and lands next to them. He introduces himself as [[Taraxacum]], a medic from [[Sargon]] and apprentice of Fāng. Fāng himself is at the border of Yan, persuading a massive Feranmut to turn back from Baizao in exchange for healing Its wounds.<ref name="TA10Aft"/>

[[File:70 i19 2 a.png|thumb|"As sword of heaven's plaint... Bid dawn as cast."]]
A war horn suddenly blows as Yumen continues to approach the tomb, but Ch'en arrives just in time. Nian recognizes her as her supporting actress for that one movie she shot a while back, but quickly notices Jie's writing knife. Stepping forward, Ch'en holds the knife straight upwards and before long, the knife becomes a longsword and a sandstorm engulfs Yumen. It is not coarse sand but more akin to gentle golden snowflakes and where they land, memories long buried resurface in the hearts of those touched. As the soldiers are overwhelmed by memory, the entire city slows to a crawl, then stops.<ref name="TA10Aft"/>

Meanwhile, Mo Yi finally arrives at the Tomb of Sui. As she tries to figure out how to open the doors, Chongyue suddenly appears and offers to bring the doll inside for her, assuring her that although it will not be used in the fight, its presence will bring Wang some peace of mind.<ref name="TA10Aft"/>

===New Year's Eve===
[[File:70 i14.png|thumb|left|"Live. Live, and come back to us."]]
Inside the Tomb, much of it has crumbled. Sui is enraged at Its proxies, accusing them of betraying It for the sake of remaining as themselves and dressing up as feeble humans. Wang simply tells Sui that It does not understand, It never could understand for It has never lived, It simply declared Itself a god to rule over nothing. Wang has already lost everything; he is not afraid to lose again, but he senses that Sui very much is afraid. In anger, Sui attempts to rouse Its body awake, when Chongyue appears and slams a thousand years worth of martial might into Its body. The punch is so strong that Sui loses connection with Its enormous body. As space warps, Chongyue presents himself, telling Wang that he would be with him to the end, just like what he promised 500 years ago. He shows the doll in his hand, proof that all his years of planning and of hoping to bring Jie back were not for naught. The warped space soon closes again.<ref name="TA11Bef">[[TA-11/Story#Before operation|TA-11 Before]]</ref>

Sui, enraged at Chongyue's interference, claims that his actions do not matter, but Wang ignores Its tantrums, intending to bring Its end now, in every possibility, chains and dreams.<ref name="TA11Bef"/> He infuses his blade with the power of the items given by all his siblings and strikes at Sui. Weakened as It is, Sui ultimately loses against Wang. Wang looks on as the original body and consciousness is defeated, intent on haunting this place of all eternity. Wang approaches the great body as the consciousness within clings to Its last grasps of existence, questioning what It even is and why It will die, never having truly lived, only ever having dreamed of total, egotistical domination and evolution over all.<ref name="TA11Aft">[[TA-11/Story#After operation|TA-11 After]]</ref>

The bestial Sui closes Its eyes for the last time. The bestial Sui will dream eternally of a future that exists only in dreams.

And thus a new “Sui” is born.

[[File:70 i07.png|thumb|The shadow utters what seems to be a threatening, low roar. Within that roar, the True Lung hears a haggard voice, one he heard decades ago.]]
Across Baizao, all the scriptgeists suddenly disappear without a trace. But just as suddenly, the ground begins to shake, signalling that the "Sui" has awakened. The True Lung knows that the time has come to do his duty and enters the tomb to confront the beast, but finds It is no longer Itself, but Wang in Its place. Wang admits that Its memories, thoughts and feelings roil around his consciousness, but though he inhabits Its body, Sui is truly dead. As promised, the True Lung will spare Wang's life and by extension the entire family, but burdened by the years and the weight of his station, he still wishes to surrender his life to Wang.<ref name="TA11Aft"/>

Before that can happen, Ya finally reaches the Tomb for Its vengeance on Sui, only to quickly realize that Sui is dead and that Wang has taken Its place. Ya is actually stupefied that his plan worked and begins to reflect on Itself. Wang says there is still one more thing It could do if Ya wishes for revenge. Ya understands and fulfils Wang's desires; it cuts away the First Originium from the room and plunges it straight into the body that was Sui. The act takes a significant toll on Ya's body, resulting in Her body weathering away.<ref name="TA11Aft"/>

As the space begins to collapse from the removal of the First Originium, the True Lung heads towards the exit, only to finds it jammed shut by rubble. The True Lung thinks that dying wouldn't be so bad, when Wei Yenwu suddenly uses Chi Xiao's technique to slice through the rubble and save him, much to the True Lung's surprise. At the Sui Regulator headquarters, Chun is in disbelief that Wang managed to both eliminate Sui and bring Jie back. She commands everyone to remain at their post, declaring that the menace has been resolved. In the Imperial Palace, the Grand Commandant sees all his plans crumble to ruin. His cough grows worse and before long he can no longer see, but he can still hear [[Chen Che]] from the Ministry of Justice judging him. He hopes that the True Lung would return so he could prove all he had done was not out of selfish desire, but his body finally gives up, and he crumples onto the floor, dead.<ref name="TA11Aft"/>

Meanwhile, after hundreds of years away from the Tomb, the Sui siblings gather once more, waiting for Wang's return. Regrettably, a somber Shuo emerges from the Tomb, without Wang by his side.

===The Ink Settles===
As the crisis in Baizao passes and normalcy returns, Ch'en helps Chao-ch'ien in the Sky Mirror Pavilion, using the writing knife to help correct mistakes in the Ch'en family history records. Neither are certain if Jie will truly come back, although the doll has settled comfortably in the office upstairs. Ch'en suggests to invite Jie back to do history work when she is fully returned, but Chao-ch'ien doesn't think it's a good idea; it would be better to have people be able to think for themselves rather than be reliant on an all knowing "goddess." [[Xingzhu|Ning Yin]] soon enters to discuss recent events with Chao-ch'ien before she is transferred to a research post at the Imperial Historical Cabinet.<ref name="ST2">[[TA-ST-2]]</ref>

At Savors at Yu's, the Sui family is trying to have their long-awaited reunion but Taraxacum informs them that Fāng will not be joining as he is too busy helping those suffering from disease across the land, much to Nian's annoyance. Before he leaves, she and Shu give him gifts for him and Fāng, to which he wishes them well. Yu is a little sad that their family is never complete but as if having heard his plight, Yi drags Ji along, despite the latter saying he wouldn't be coming. Shortly after, Jun comes in with a sleeping Dusk, who complains about not wanting to be disturbed. Yu is exasperated by his rowdy siblings but still happy that so many of them were able to gather in one place today.<ref name="ST2"/>

[[File:70 i09.png|thumb|"Once you grow up, maybe you can make a change. So... Grow up quick."]]
At the Imperial Palace, the two estranged brothers finally reconcile after forty years. The True Lung asks about their deceased sister's daughters, Talulah and Ch'en, to which Wei shamefully confesses that he could not even provide them a safe upbringing. Yan Wu gladly admits that out of the two of them, Yan Li is the one who never brought shame to Yan and that with the end of Sui, he can finally cast aside his burdens and regrets for the moment. Back at the library, Ch'en is approached by a young Lung girl with bright red hair. Ch'en recognises her as the True Lung's daughter and offers to accompany the girl home. The girl tells her that she is studying to be the current True Lung and has to be mindful of everything she says, so she doesn't have any friends. Ch'en tells her that regardless of whether she is the True Lung or not, she should have the right to love everyone no matter who they are and make friends with people she likes. No one should have to shoulder such authority on their own. The girl doesn't understand her words but Ch'en simply tells her that she will understand once she's grown up.<ref name="ST2"/>

At the Tianshi Bureau, the Old Tianshi loudly complains to the Grand Tutor about the bureau's lackluster progress in Originium research in the past four centuries. The Grand Tutor tells her they have invited a guest who is an expert in the field to assist them, a representative of Rhodes Island. Chun resigns from the Sui Regulator after admitting responsibility for her actions. The Feranmutologists are scrambling over each other to study the "Wang-Sui" in the tomb, which has mysteriously fallen into slumber after It merged with the First Originium. On the outskirts of Yan, Zuo Le bids farewell to Mo Yi, who says she'll give the so-called "modern Shanhaizhong" rabble a good talking-to.<ref name="ST2"/>

Ju and Hou plan to take what remains of Ya's vitality far beyond Yan for healing. They bid farewell to Chongyue, saying that while freeing Wang from the tomb may be beyond his reach, there is still the possibility of finding a piece of him elsewhere. Chongyue cannot help but think Wang is avoiding them, wishing to keep them away from his next game, whatever it may be. Far away in an ordinary village, a large group of spectators have gathered to watch a weiqi game. A young child is playing black against a manipulative rich man who offered to pay his family debts, but he is on the verge of defeat. Just as all hope seems lost, a disembodied voice suddenly reaches him and tells him exactly where to put his stone, leading him to discover the weak point in the rich man's strategy. The boy never finds out who helped him though, unaware that the charm around his neck had secretly been infused with a stone belonging to a certain Proxy.<ref name="ST2"/>
