==Plot==

===Ancient Forge I===
[[File:Ancient Forge.png|thumb|left|"Once upon a time, in Lungmen..."]]
[[Yan]]ese folklore speaks of "Nian," a ferocious monster covered in flames that brings havoc on the eve of Lunar New Year. Humans either humble themselves before its wrath or bravely expel the monster with fireworks. In the modern age, most people have forgotten much of the story and dismiss "Nian" as a mythological construct.

On the eve of Lunar New Year, the [[Lungmen Guard Department]] receives an expert from [[Rhodes Island]] who is familiar with "Nian." Reports have been made throughout the city regarding incidents involving huge craters, melted buildings, and massive scorch marks, leading to greater preparations by the L.G.D. Meanwhile, an unknown figure prepares to wreak chaos. It is nighttime, but the city's residents slowly notice the rising temperature, and unidentified personnel bearing the [[Reunion]] emblem appear in the city center.

===Ancient Forge II===
The temperature continues to rise while terracotta figures resembling Reunion fighters and even L.G.D. officers attack approaching officers, causing chaos in the city. Under [[Snowsant]]'s suggestion, the L.G.D. manages to detect the heat source by looking for anomalous areas without any [[Originium]] reactions. Lungmenites such as [[Hoshiguma]] and [[Lee's Detective Agency]] help repel the terracotta soldiers, while Lava confronts the Nian threat, who is revealed to be a woman with peculiar powers.

Lava points out that Nian used data from Lungmen's records to create the illusions and presumes that the power she wields is unlike [[Originium Arts]]. A fight breaks out between the two, and Nian melts down a building to reforge it, prompting Lava to use her reserved trick &mdash; fireworks. Nian cowers from the fireworks and curses [[Dusk|her sister]] for telling the humans. Lava replies that old folk sayings unrelated to "Nian" were what clued her in. Amused, Nian crafts a sword and decides to summon her heavenly forge.

===Ancient Forge III===
[[File:Nian's Kaiju.png|thumb|''Zhendang Kongling, Bayin Siqi—'']]
The battle ends with a devastating result as Lungmen loses control of District 21. Nian gives them a three-day break, which Lava uses to formulate a plan. While [[Ch'en]] and [[Amiya]] lead their combined forces to stall Nian and her ever-adapting terracotta soldiers, Lava studies the infrastructure blueprint provided by Snowsant. She then lures Nian to the core of the city's defensive cannons. The entire building is blown up, yet the explosion fails to fully kill Nian, who accidentally saves Lava. Nian vanishes from the L.G.D.'s attack, but it turns out to be an illusion. The real Nian returns and causes the city to tremble as a gigantic, bellowing monster rises from the ground.

[[File:Ancient Forge Dusk Cameo.png|thumb|left|While the "Nian" collapses, her sister seems to enjoy her demise, mocking her for wasting effort on a meaningless mission.]]
Except all of this is merely part of the script Nian wrote for a New Year's movie. Lava interrupts, frustrated by the terrible writing and nonsensical plot. After some persuasion, Nian revises the movie's ending. Nian is defeated, but a mysterious figure hinted to be one of Nian's sisters laughs at Nian's defeat, declares her interest in Lungmen, and states that she will quench the fires of the world. The movie wraps up, and Lava is baffled by the sequel-bait as well as Nian's decision to vilify her sister over their squabbles. Lava goes to revise her lines. [[Closure]] shows up and calls the plot B-movie garbage, but comes up with the title ''Ancient Forge''.

[[File:My Time Will Come.png|thumb|"Heh, what a lousy villain."]]
Although the lousy movie seems to be a meaningless story, it is merely the prologue [[Through the Ages|to the stories regarding twelve siblings in the land of Yan]] — and [[Great Hunt|an upcoming "war" against]] [[Sui|the awakening, arrogant "god"]].
