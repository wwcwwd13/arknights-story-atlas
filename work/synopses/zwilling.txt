==Die Gnade ist Ewig==

===Zu den Zwillingstürme===
Twenty-three years ago in September 1077, the [[Twin Empresses]] initiated the ''[[Septemberrevolution]]'' to overthrow the tyrannical rule of Kaiser [[Witch King|Herkunftshorn the Witch King]], ending an era of darkness in [[Leithanien]]. To commemorate this victory, ''Das Kaiserinnensfest'' ("The Empresses' Festival") is held every year in the capital of [[Zwillingstürme]] under its Twin Spires. Through a majestic royal orchestra, Leithanian musicians will perform the ''[[Güldenesgesatz]]'', the magical constitution of Leithanien in the form of musical scores, to bless the country with peace and prosperity. And now, in autumn 1100, upon a new century of the [[Terra]]n calendar, the 23rd festival is about to begin.

Yet the brightest lights also cast the darkest shadows. The Witch King's Remnants, also known as the [[Echoes of Herkunftshorn]], plot to create havoc during ''Kaiserinnensfest'', intending to revive the Witch King and return Leithanien to its "former glory." They stole a piece of the Güldenesgesatz, and in the process, murdered Kurfürst [[Werner von Hochberg]], the father of [[Viviana Droste]] the Candle Knight. Viviana was already on her way from Kazimierz to meet him when she heard the news from Werner’s old friend, conductor [[Cora Löwenstein]].<ref name=":ST1">[[ZT-ST-1]]</ref> With permission given by [[Twin Empresses|Lieselotte Ewigegnade]] the White Empress, Viviana is allowed to investigate Werner’s death as a temporary Empresses' Voice along with [[Gesatzswächter]] [[Brandt Reiner]].

Meanwhile, [[Twin Empresses|Hildegard Grimmmacht]], the ruthless black Empress, seeks to retrieve the dark legacies of the Witch King and seal them for good. Her plan includes summoning [[Ebenholz]], the young Graf Urtica, back to Leithanien and retrieve his Voice of Mundane. She requests the aid of an old [[Sarkaz]] named [[Fremont]], the [[Lich]] King, and he dispatches [[Lessing|Lessing Maeyer]] to escort Ebenholz. However, Ebenholz mistakes Lessing for a Remnant, attacking him and fleeing into the underground sewage tunnels.

[[Executor the Ex Foedere|Federico Giallo]], an Executor and Saint of [[Laterano]], silently enters Zwillingstürme. Since [[Hortus de Escapismo/Synopsis|the tragedy in]] ''[[Sanctilaminium Ambrosii]]'', Federico has been busy tracking down the fugitive [[Virtuosa|Arturia]] for over a year. After learning that she has been collaborating with the Echoes of Herkunftshorn, he seeks assistance from [[Loris Bordin]], a former Lateran who is now the captain of Zwillingstürme’s Gendarmerie. Loris claims that he’s too busy dealing with the nobles' complaints, yet Federico urges him to prioritize finding and arresting Arturia. Since Federico's attitude proves to be a hassle, Loris wants Federico to stay under his surveillance at all times.<ref name=":1Aft">[[ZT-1/Story#After operation|ZT-1 After]]</ref>

===Erinnerungen an Ringelblume===
[[File:ZT-Der Tod des Hexenkönig.png|thumb|''Der Tod des Hexenkönig''... An eerie name which evokes the haunting past of the Witch King. Yet Arturia feels such painting fits its name well.]]

At Karl-Schmidt Street, also known as "Marigold Avenue," the Gendarmeries are investigating a mysterious death that might have connection with the Remnants. The deceased, renowned painter Frieda Seemann, suddenly dies of exhaustion inside the art gallery after presenting her final work. A canvas smudged by black [[Originium]] paint mixed with blood, triggering fear in all who see it—an artwork titled ''Der Tod des Hexenkönig'', "The Death of the Witch King." Loris believes that Seemann's last work is some kind of declaration from the Witch King’s followers.<ref name=":1Bef">[[ZT-1/Story#Before operation|ZT-1 Before]]</ref>

During Loris’ investigation, Federico momentarily leaves his side to investigate some strange music, and Loris gets exposed to Arturia’s ego manipulation [[Arts]]. Her Arts frees him from his deepest regrets and reignites his heroic bravado. Hearing the sounds of a fight underground, the Liberi officer charges into the sewers where he discovers Ebenholz being attacked by the Echoes of Herkunftshorn. Loris manages to buy Ebenholz some time to escape, but as a result the Remnants petrify him into a horrible statue. Federico arrives too late and is brought away from the scene by [[Bassline|Michael Lupakarn]], a servant of Empress Hildegard.

[[File:ZT-Erinnerungen an Mutter 1.png|thumb|left|As Viviana dives into the avenue of marigold, she seems to have seen the romantic past of her dearest parents]]

Loris’ sudden demise shocks Viviana, prompting her to investigate the case harder. Believing that Seemann’s last work must have a deeper meaning, she heads towards the painter's residence in Marigold Alley with Cora and Brandt. While investigating, Viviana finds herself being drawn in by strange musical Arts and begins seeing hallucinations of her past, including her dear mother. Cora and Brandt immediately come to her rescue and find themselves surrounded by Remnants who claim to be under the order of the "Concertmaster." Brandt takes the charge and fights the Remnants while Cora brings Viviana to safety. 

After retreating to a secure location, Viviana suggests that they examine the first draft of ''Der Tod des Hexenkönig''. By casting his Arts, Brandt is able to peel back the layers of black paint to reveal a scene of when the rebels broke into the Witch King's Spire twenty three years ago. In it, they pinpoint a man named [[Gerhard Hoffmann]], a former attendant of the Witch King who now works as a university professor, and believe he might become the Remnants' next target. All signs point towards [[Ludwigs-Universität]]—where the Urtica family, the house of the Witch King, has studied for generations.<ref name=":3b">[[ZT-3/Story#After operation|ZT-3 After]]</ref>

===Der Herkunfsthorn Erwacht===
[[File:ZT-Drohender Schatten.png|thumb|left|"It's time to wake up, my lord. You will never escape the shadow of His Majesty."]]
After surviving the attack in the sewers, Ebenholz is dragged away to a spire in Ludwigs-Universität. He sees Lessing, Gerhard, and Professor Fremont attempting to extract the Voice of Mundane from his head. But it seems the three are not in agreement, as Gerhard seems to oppose keeping Ebenholz locked up like this. Ebenholz blindly believes Gerhard's sympathy and escapes with him, only to find himself locked in an underground study. From the very beginning, Gerhard has been working with the Echoes of Herkunftshorn, seeking to sacrifice Ebenholz and revive the Witch King using the Voice of Mundane.

Viviana and Brandt arrive at the university to question Fremont about Gerhard’s whereabouts. Just then, strange music begins to play, affecting the students' minds and causing them to fight each other randomly. Even Brandt is not immune to its effects, until Viviana snaps him back to reality. Together with the local Gendarmerie, they quickly try their best to get the students to safety. Meanwhile, Arturia watches the chaos unfold from atop a spire while communicating with the leader of the Remnants, the “Concertmaster.”


[[File:ZT-Der falsche Herkunftshorn.png|thumb|''Der Herkunftshorn'', The Horn of Genesis, once again appears on Leithanian soil.]]
Fremont and Lessing charge into the underground study in an attempt to stop Gerhard, but the latter reveals the ''Güldenesgesatz'' piece stolen from Werner’s residence. He unleashes the Witch King’s Arts inside it, causing the ground to split open, and a dark spire to penetrate the sky. Everyone in the university becomes horrified upon witnessing the return of the Witch King’s Spire, the Genesis Horn, a weapon that could control even [[Catastrophe]]s. Black flames spread across the entire campus, killing several students and Gendarmerie. Brandt uses a special Gesatzswächter art to summon a magical barrier that will shield the rest of Zwillingstürme from the chaos.

Fortunately, Empress Hildegard soon arrives at the scene and literally strikes down the Spire with her sword. The Gendarmerie are now able to both arrest all the Remnants and restore order to the university. Gerhard, unable to sustain Herkunftshorn’s Arts, decides to commit suicide in an act of defiance against fate after listening to Arturia’s music. Federico and Michael, who have been closely following Arturia’s tracks, arrive on the scene to apprehend her when the Empress Hildegard's casters arrive to take her under custody for her crimes.

And so, twilight has arrived at Zwillingstürme, but the long night has only just begun.

==Die Macht ist Grimm==

===Das Güldenesgesatz erklingen===
After the Ludwigs-Universität incident is resolved, Fremont removes the Voice of Mundane from Ebenholz with ease. Nothing even happens to him. With that, the Empresses now shift their focus towards preparing for tomorrow’s Kaiserinnensfest. While not originally his goal, Federico wants to help fulfill Loris' last wish with Michael, to investigate the unsolved case of his fiancée [[Yulia Schüler]]’s disappearance. While investigating Loris’ residence, Federico sees a piano and is reminded about Arturia. Believing that she may have bigger plans, Federico listens to Michael’s advice to meet Viviana and exchange information.

[[File:ZT-Kerzenhalter und Befreier.png|thumb|left|The Candle Knight seeks her life question from Arturia the fugitive, and what she gets is a rather warm welcome.]]
Now under custody, Arturia is forbidden from playing any form of musical instrument and the casters disable her cello with Arts. Viviana takes this chance to secretly visit Arturia and ask about her involvement with her father Werner. Arturia responds that Werner's death is the result of his courage to protect the ''Güldenesgesatz'', and being a natural-born counsellor, she encourages Viviana to search for the answer of her life by herself.

After the meeting, Viviana regroups with Federico. Through Arturia's hint, she suspects that the Witch King's Remnants might actually be planning to use his legacy to do harm to the ''Güldenesgesatz''. She goes to Cora, and tries persuading her to stop the performance of the ''Güldenesgesatz'', but her response only puzzles Cora. Nevertheless, Cora accepts Viviana's requests and guides her to the hall where the Royal Orchestra performs the score for the festival. 

[[File:ZT-Der Güldenesgesatz 2.png|thumb|''Güldenesgesatz'', the Golden Movement, has blessed Leithanien for generations. Now, it is the time for the Leithanians to exhault its name.]]
As the sun starts to set, the long-awaited performance of the ''Güldenesgesatz'' begins. The Empresses arrive at the bridge of the Twin Spires and give the signal to the orchestra. The audience follows suit by performing their own instrument. Little by little, the musical Arts played by the orchestra and the audience paints the sky with beautiful gold patterns. Just as everyone is struck by the magical performance, the harmony is suddenly shattered when the ''Güldenesgesatz'' becomes corrupted by the Echoes of Herkunftshorn. The audience begins to suffer severe headaches, and the once beautiful twilight is suddenly covered by dark clouds.

Amidst the chaos, Viviana confronts the leader of the Remnants: Cora Löwenstein. Cora confesses that she had initially been tasked by the Twin Empresses to act as the Remnants’ “Concertmaster” and lead them to their self-annihilation. However, Werner’s death made her finally admit that the real problem was with the ''Güldenesgesatz'', the eternal law which dictates the fate of every Leithanian. She aims to use Herkunftshorn’s Arts to completely eradicate the Güldenesgesatz and free the Leithanian people from its binds. While Viviana understands her reasons, she cannot allow the situation to continue, and so stabs Cora in the gut, letting the woman die in her arms.

[[File:ZT-Der wahre Herkunftshorn.png|thumb|Far above the Twin Spires, the true ''Herkunftshorn'' appears among the void, seemingly calling out his loyalists to come to his side.]]

Ironically, Cora’s betrayal was exactly what the Twin Empresses had been hoping for. They too hope for a Leithanien no longer chained by the Güldenesgesatz and so have prepared for this very moment. Hanging above the Twin Spires is the shadow of the Genesis Horn—the genuine one hidden within the dimensional crack. Excited by the Witch King's return, the Remnants commit mass suicide, their bodies vanishing into dust, leaving behind ghostly echoes.

Arturia manages to escape her cell and climbs up the Twin Spires to approach the Genesis Horn when she is confronted by an angry Fremont. He unleashes a special Lich witchcraft called "Exile" to banish her out of existence. The moment he does so, everyone who is not affected by the rampaging ''Güldenesgesatz'' are suddenly teleported to another dimension, including Fremont himself. Shortly after, [[Ermengarde]] and the other Liches arrive at the scene and assist the Gesatzswächters as they summon a protective barrier to stop the corrupted melody from spreading throughout the entire country.

===Kargereich===
The gang wakes up—albeit seperated from each other—and they find themselves inside a strange extraterrestrial dimension. The Liches call it "the beginning and end of all threads", otherwise known in Leithanian as ''[[Kargereich]]''—The Desolate Domain—a place where life and death never exist. Some find themselves standing beneath the shadows of historical Leithanian buildings, as if this place was an archive to commemorate their existence for eternity. Others find themselves trapped inside a never-ending maze, forced to confront their inner demons.

[[File:ZT-Schöpfung und Zerstörung 1.png|thumb|left|The Witch King may be a ruthless tyrant, but he too was once a man of wisdom and a visionary sovereign.]]

On Ebenholz's side, he finds himself face to face with his nemesis, the Witch King. However, this Witch King is not that of the infamous tyrant, but from a time when he was a wise and courageous leader. This version of Herkunftshorn slowly guides the young Graf in realizing this fact: his nightmare has never been "the Witch King" himself, but the fear imposed by others, a spire that he has to climb out by his own feet. Ebenholz finally realises that he has been a fool to be so afraid of a man who has long passed away. He laughs maniacally and proclaims to forever sever his ties with the Witch King, thus escaping the illusory maze.

Viviana is trapped inside a maze as well, coming across countless past versions of herself. Each of them represents a possible future that Viviana could have had, but every single one leaves her only with disappointment. She then recalls the story of "[[The Last Knight]]" she once admired, and remembers her identity, her determination to become an unwavering light that can conquer any adversity. Just then, she sees a ray of light, and standing before her is a blazing spear with a surprising mark—the emblem of [[House Nearl]]. She firmly grabs the spear and cleaves the darkness, allowing her to escape the illusion.

===Der Hexenkönigspavillion===
[[File:ZT-In Anwesenheit der Hexenkönig.png|thumb|Arturia humbly bows before the King of Casters in his Pavilion, and she yearns to gain his wisdom for her long quest.]]
Federico and Arturia simultaneously meet Yulia Schüler, the missing girl whom Loris has been searching for. From Yulia's words, Arturia realizes that within ''Kargereich'' is the Witch King's Spire, and the Spire is in fact a void bubble created by his Arts. She makes her way towards the Witch King’s pavilion and bows before His Majesty. There, Arturia wants the Witch King to fulfill a future she has envisioned—a future where people possess flawless ego. She envisions that this kind of ego will not just be like the [[Sankta]]’s empathy, but a shared like-minded heart regardless of race, wealth, or social status that could overcome even the most horrible circumstance. The Witch King is intrigued by this vision, yet he comments that Arturia herself is the one who is the furthest from that future—a mirror that only reflects others and lacks the understanding needed to materialize that future.

[[File:ZT-Die Zwillingsprätendenten.png|thumb|left|The Twin Empresses were born from the Witch King's Arts. And now, they are fulfilling their purpose as tough rivals of his Arts.]]

Federico learns from Yulia that she simply vanished many years ago after being attacked by the Remnants. Technically, she is physically dead, but her soul has somehow transcended into ''Kargereich'', where time stands still. Federico deems the case as resolved and informs Yulia about Loris’ death and relays his final wish. She thanks Federico for helping Loris when a sudden shockwave shatters the remaining pieces of her soul. In turn, Federico remains silent at her departure, having learnt the meaning of emotion for the first time in his life.

The shockwave turns out to be the Twin Empresses forcefully entering ''Kargereich'' through the Liches' aid. Prepared to embrace his end, the Witch King rises for a rematch against the twins to test their might. A furious clash of Arts follows between the casters. The Witch King's mastery of Arts is unchallengeable, but he himself has been gradually exhausted over a century, allowing the Empresses to overpower him. As the Witch King's soul slowly diminishes, Fremont approaches him for the last time and uses "Exile" on him, hence erasing his haunting shadow permanently from this world.

===Flamme im Chaos===
Fremont makes a worrying observation regarding ''Kargereich''; now that its creator has vanished, it is collapsing at accelerating speed. Cracks appear within the dimension, and intangible demonic beings slowly creep out from the crevices. All these signifies the encroachment of the Collapsals penetrating the reality, and if they are freed, the entire capital will be immediately devoured by the dimensional crack and spread demonic pollution across Leithanien. So, Fremont sends everyone back to reality while staying behind in ''Kargereich'' with the Empresses to keep reality stable.

[[File:ZT-Symphonie der Freiheit 2.png|thumb|left|"I shall perform a song for the desperate crowd, and the subject of the ego shall be I."]]
Federico and Arturia are among the first who return to reality, and all they see is the agitated crowd has fallen into delirium. The Collapsals’ psychic power is slowly corroding the people's hearts, turning them into mindless zombies, repeating the same phrases over and over again. Federico tells Arturia that her ego manipulation Arts is exactly what is needed to solve this problem. With that said, Arturia gathers her resolve and makes another performance on her cello, this time targeting her own emotions, and the Arts that overflows from her cello slowly returns the people’s emotions and cleanses their demonic pollution.

[[File:ZT-Die Macht ist grimm.png|thumb|''Die Macht ist grimm'', the Ruthless Sovereign, it is she whose menacing wrath halts otherworldly demons in the chaotic void.]]
[[File:ZT-Die Gnade ist ewig.png|thumb|''Die Gnade ist ewig'', the Eternal Grace, it is she whose motherly grace pacifies poor souls beneath the heavens.]]
Meanwhile, inside ''Kargereich'', the Empresses fight their way back to reality, but someone needs to stay behind to prevent the space from completely collapsing. Without hesitation, Empress Hildegard decides to remain in ''Kargereich'', using her Arts to reconstruct the Witch King's Pavilion and defend against the Collapsals. While the crack slowly seals up, Empress Lieselotte cleanses off the remaining demons lurking in reality. She descends from above, the sky turns clear again, and the shadow of the Witch King's Spire finally vanishes. The Leithanians greatly praise the gentle White Empress for her heroism, yet it was the ruthless Black Empress whose sacrifice truly saved them.

==Goldene Zwielicht==
A week has passed since ''Das Kaiserinnensfest'' ended. In response to the disappearance of Empress Hildegard, the imperial court declared her ''"Stimmverlust"'' to the public. Ebenholz plans to return to his former territory in Urtica with Lessing in order to improve the region’s prosperity. Viviana reconciles with Brandt and informs her that she will not be accepting Lieselotte’s offer to become an official Empresses’ Voice. She also contacts House Nearl regarding the spear she saw in Kargereich, attracting the attention of [[Młynar]]. Having taken care of their affairs, the Liches finally bid farewell and aim to return to their homeland—Kazdel. Michael continues his service as an Empress' Voice as Hildegard’s representative and has since been acting as an intermediary between Rhodes Island and the Leithanian imperial court.<ref name=":ST3">[[ZT-ST-3]]</ref>

[[File:ZT-Die Suche geht weiter.png|thumb|left|Arturia continues her quest, but she will never forget that golden twilight of Zwillingstürme where she witnessed countless brave souls.]]
The only question remaining is what to do with Arturia. Federico urges Empress Lieselotte to extradite Arturia back to Laterano, but Arturia still remains a honored guest within the country’s borders. Fortunately, Pope [[Yvangelista XI]] contacts the Empress and the two heads of states quietly exchange information regarding ''Kargereich'' and the upcoming "Disaster." As a result, Arturia is temporarily pardoned by the Lateran Curia. For the time being, she can enjoy her limited freedom.<ref name=":ST3"/> The Pope even discreetly visits her later, offering a chance for her to become a new Saint of Laterano under the order of the Law, and he believes that her Arts could be crucial for the Sankta's survival in the "Disaster".<ref>[[Virtuosa/Operator Record|Virtuosa's Operator Record]]</ref>

Still, the threat is far from over. The Law's "vision" predicts that the [[The Masses' Travels|"Disaster"]] is coming sooner than expected...<ref name=":ST3"/>
