==Awake and Alive==
Someone is calling you from the darkness, and you try to respond. Even though you have forgotten everything, she still needs you. The day is December 23, 1096, and it will change your future forever.

[[File:Amiya Awakening.png|thumb|left|Amiya trying to get the Doctor out of the Sarcophagus]]
Within a harshly lit room, a girl with a pair of bunny ears is holding an individual's hand, trying to wake them up. Introducing herself as [[Amiya]], she has arrived with her teammates from a company named [[Rhodes Island]] to rescue the individual from an ancient device. She mentions that the person is formerly a [[Doctor]], but they are suffering from amnesia. Nevertheless, Amiya and her team fight their way out of the strange and mostly empty structure in order to regroup with another team led by a woman codenamed [[Dobermann]].<ref>[[0-0/Story]]</ref>

==Escape from Chaos==
[[File:Lost Girl.png|thumb|Reunion wreaks havoc across Chernobog]]
The [[Ursus|Ursine]] [[nomadic city]] of [[Chernobog]] is currently suffering from bloody riots led by [[Reunion]], a violent pro-[[Infected]] non-state organization. Chaos has spread across the city as order breaks down in the face of infected rioters. Even though Rhodes Island—an Infected organization meant to resolve issues bloodlessly—wished to stop the riot, they are met with firm resistance and scorn from both the Reunion fighters and the anti-Infected local authorities. Despite the dilemma, their first and foremost goal is to evacuate the confused Doctor from the city.<ref>[[0-1/Story]]</ref>

Their escape, however, does not go as planned. The rescue team encounters two of Reunion's commanders: [[Crownslayer]] and [[Mephisto]]. They look down on Rhodes Island, claiming them cowards who refuse to fight for the Infected, and initiate battle with the team. Fortunately, [[Nearl]] and [[Ace]] arrive in time to turn the tides, buying Amiya's squad time to retreat with them. Mephisto decides to inform their [[Talulah|leader]] about Rhodes Island, and implores her to bring terror to the traitors of the Infected.<ref>[[0-4/Story]]</ref><ref>[[0-6/Story]]</ref><ref>[[0-7/Story]]</ref><ref>[[0-8/Story]]</ref><ref>[[0-9/Story]]</ref>

Despite escaping from the Reunion forces, the situation has only worsened as a [[Catastrophe]] is forming over the paralyzed city of Chernobog. Disaster is unavoidable.

==Wrathful Calamity==
[[File:Devastation of Chernobog.png|thumb|The Catastrophe destroying the city of Chernobog]]
As [[Rhodes Island]] passes through the [[Reunion]]-occupied [[Chernobog]], they begin noticing signs of a worst-case scenario. They pick up a metallic smell in the air, and the sky gets abnormally dark—all signs of an imminent [[Catastrophe]]. The rescue team is forced to take cover as the storm strikes Chernobog, destroying much of the city and leaving behind massive chunks of Originium. Catastrophe events are the main source of [[Oripathy]], an incurable disease caused by the [[Originium]] that these disasters leave behind. Chernobog has now become a living hell.

For the R.I. rescue team, however, there is something worse than the Catastrophe. Intercepting them are numerous skilled fighters and their leader: [[Talulah]] of Reunion. As a fierce [[Draco]], her merciless Flame [[Originium Arts|Arts]] can easily melt the surrounding buildings. As she tries to incinerate the team, she haughtily proclaims that their goal was fulfilled the moment the Catastrophe struck the city. All she wants to see is the common folk suffer the disease and persecution that the Infected have been bearing for ages.

==Ends Justify the Means==
[[File:Reunion Leaders.png|thumb|The wrath of Reunion burns all]]
Even though [[Nearl]] charges forward to deal with the Draco, it's futile. Her Arts easily overwhelm Nearl's shield. If [[Amiya]] had not unleashed her black Arts to defend her in turn, Talulah would have killed the [[Kuranta]]. When everything seems hopeless, [[Ace]] steps out to block Talulah's flames with his squad, drawing her ire and buying time for the rescue squad to retreat. Amiya watches the [[Phidia]] man stand tall with his shield as they run, and in the end only Ace and [[Guard (NPC)|Guard]] survive the onslaught. After dragging Guard to safety and binding his wounds, Ace carries on to face Reunion's leadership alone despite his missing arm—until his death. After the Elite Operator falls, Reunion's assembled leadership proclaim him a brave and true warrior.

When the rescue team arrives at the outskirts of Chernobog to wait for their extraction, they encounter a band of [[Sarkaz]] mercenaries led by the enigmatic [[W]]. She recognizes both the [[Doctor]] and Amiya, but refuses to elaborate, instead letting them go. While the rescue team is puzzled by her actions, they immediately leave Chernobog and finally return to base via VTOL. Though their sacrifices were many, the rescue operation was a success.

==Into Lungmen==
December 27, 1096: four days after their escape from [[Chernobog]], [[Rhodes Island]] arrives at the [[Yan|Yanese]] [[nomadic city]] of [[Lungmen]] to establish a contract to protect the city from the looming [[Reunion]] threat alongside the [[Lungmen Guard Department]]. Upon reaching the Inspection and Quarantine zone at the entrance to the city, the [[Doctor]] and [[Amiya]] meet a stern [[Lung]] woman named [[Ch'en]], the Superintendent of the Lungmen Guard Department. Though she seems a harsh woman who despises the [[Infected]], characterized by her decisive suppression of an Infected riot in the Quarantine zone, she lets the guests into Lungmen as part of the negotiations regardless. As Ch'en leads them to their destination, she speaks down to Amiya, treating her with little respect.

When they reach the office, the Doctor and Amiya find [[Kal'tsit]], the unofficial leader of R.I., debating with [[Wei Yenwu]], the governor of Lungmen. Kal'tsit attempts to convince Wei to consider her plan, as Lungmen will become Reunion's next target, but he doubts Rhodes Island's abilities to handle such an attack. Amiya steps in and promises Wei that R.I. can handle the issues surrounding the Infected. To prove their capabilities, she reveals that R.I. not only survived the catastrophe in Chernobog, but has also fought [[Talulah]], the leader of Reunion. After deep consideration, Wei finally agrees to Kal'tsit's terms and allows R.I. to cooperate with the L.G.D. to eliminate the Reunion threat.

==The Girl and the Key==
[[File:Skullshatterer Meets Misha.png|thumb|Skullshatterer encounters Misha during his search in Lungmen]]
The first mission during their contract is to search for [[Misha]], a Chernobog refugee hiding in the slums who has sensitive intel pertaining to Chernobog. With aid from [[Penguin Logistics]] and [[Blacksteel]], R.I. fends off a force of Reunion infiltrators. When they track Misha to an abandoned building, the Ursus girl doesn't trust them, thinking they are there to arrest and kill her. Amiya persuades Misha of their good intentions by showing her that they are Infected too, and that only by staying with them and the L.G.D. will Misha be safe from harassment. Convinced, Misha decides to follow them and willingly hands herself to the L.G.D..

However, the situation grows complicated as the Reunion Assault Squad, led by [[Skullshatterer]], arrive in Lungmen to search for Misha as well. Their appearance causes skirmishes between Rhodes Island and Reunion fighters. Seeing that the joint alliance has taken Misha into L.G.D. custody, Skullshatterer angrily vows to crush them all. He splits the team by having his men ambush R.I. in the alleyways while [[W]] attacks the L.G.D. convoy and captures Misha. When the battle is over, R.I. immediately regroups with the L.G.D. to prepare for a rescue mission.

==Wild Goose Chase==
After [[Reunion]]'s capture of [[Misha]], the joint forces of [[Rhodes Island]], [[Penguin Logistics]], [[Blacksteel]], and the [[Lungmen Guard Department]] pursue the retreating fighters through the wasteland bordering [[Ursus]] and [[Yan]]. Their only goal is to slow down the escaping combatants and arrest them in a single calculated operation. On the way, they receive aid from another team led by [[Hoshiguma]] and easily take down the fighters. 

Meanwhile, somewhere in the wilderness, [[Skullshatterer]] has a short reunion with Misha, who turns out to be his long lost sister. They were separated years ago when he became infected, dragged away by the Infected Patrol of Ursus. Revealing himself as Alex, he wishes for Misha to fight alongside him for the betterment of the Infected. When his Assault Squad informs him of the arrival of the joint forces, he leaves Misha in [[W]]'s care. Misha tried and failed to persuade him not to fight Rhodes Island, so she obeys his instructions and wishes for him to stay safe. W tells Skullshatterer that the easiest way to take down Rhodes Island is to assassinate the [[Doctor]], their best tactical strategist.

[[File:Amiya Strikes Skullshatterer.png|thumb|A tragic death that could have been avoided]]
The joint forces eventually catch up to Skullshatterer's men, and a battle ensues. Alex breaks through the backline and tries to kill the Doctor. In a panic, [[Amiya]] unleashes her [[Originium Arts|Arts]] to protect the Doctor in a surge of frantic emotions, piercing Skullshatterer's heart with a beam of black energy. Skullshatterer bleeds out, cursing Rhodes Island with his last breath. The Assault Squad laments his death and rally once more, fighting even harder to retrieve Skullshatterer's body. Rhodes Island temporarily retreats, and W promises another Reunion member that she will keep Misha safe in Alex's stead.

==Death and "Rebirth"==
Misha mourns for her brother when news of Alex's death reaches her. Shortly afterward, she makes a call to Amiya via W's terminal to inform her that she has decided to side with Reunion. Misha expresses her guilt for not doing anything to support the Infected back when she lived in Chernobog—now, after she has become an Infected herself, she understands Reunion's anger, and wishes to "atone for her sins" by seeking retribution against the oppressors of the Infected. Amiya tells Misha to wait for her, that it's not too late to turn back, but Misha rebukes her and reiterates her allegiance to Reunion. Afterwards, Misha sits with Alex's corpse, lamenting her fate and vowing to bring ''Skullshatterer'' back to life.

[[File:Miracle.png|thumb|"Skullshatterer" returns from the grave]]
On the other side of the battlefield, the joint forces resume the battle and have almost claimed victory over the Assault Squad. Just then, a familiar figure appears once more. Now carrying her brother's weapons and mantle, Misha has become the second "Skullshatterer." Seeing their leader's "resurrection," the Assault Squad's morale soars, and they bravely fight against the joint forces to the last man. However, to Amiya, this was the worst possible outcome, as she never wanted to fight Misha or her fellow infected. Amiya hesitates during the battle, so [[Ch'en]] orders her to remember that from now on, she must treat Reunion as her enemy, Infected or not. She then orders the L.G.D. to put an end to the Reunion forces.

==A Hollow Victory==
[[File:A Tragic Aftermath.png|thumb|Yet another preventable death in their struggle]]
The battle concludes with Misha's death and the annihilation of the Assault Squad. To Amiya, the events that transpired were an unbearable tragedy that never should have happened in the first place. Amiya begins to question if Rhodes Island is even in the right; despite Rhodes Island's mission to help people, they still ended up killing Misha and the Infected of Reunion. Trying to comfort her, Ch'en tells her that if Reunion was really willing to cooperate with her, there wouldn't be any bloodshed. Every decision made will eventually lead to its consequence—that is the lesson she learned. Ch'en also reminds Amiya that she must grow to accept the tragic reality of the fight against Reunion if she wants to fulfill her ambitions.

While the joint forces are conducting a cleanup of the scene, W stands from afar and observes the mess. She mockingly comments that fate always intertwines once again, and that she looks forward to what the future holds in store for Rhodes Island and Reunion.

==Out of the Frying Pan...==
After returning to [[Rhodes Island]]'s temporary headquarters in [[Lungmen]], [[Kal'tsit]] scolds the [[Doctor]] for not taking care of [[Amiya]], who is visibly shaken and forlorn after [[Misha]]'s tragic death. Amiya goes for a walk in the slums to clear her head, where she sees [[Ch'en]] receiving a small teddy bear from a young girl. Amiya asks Ch'en what she is doing in the slums, but the other woman only states that she is "taking care of Lungmen's business." Amiya later tells the Doctor that this experience made her believe that despite their differences, Ch'en is not so different from Rhodes Island after all. Amiya regains some confidence in Rhodes Island's mission. 

Rhodes Island's scout team has found evidence of several Reunion hideouts in Lungmen's residential district. However, when Amiya suggests another joint operation between R.I. and the L.G.D., Ch'en refuses, stating that an Infected faction carrying out more missions in Lungmen will sow disorder.  

Meanwhile, two days after the failed rescue operation leading to [[Misha]]'s death, Rhodes Island dispatches multiple reconnaissance teams to an abandoned city plate not far from Lungmen, which appeared in the aftermath of the [[Chernobog]] incident. Upon their arrival, they realize that the city has turned into a makeshift base for [[Reunion]] after its destruction by the [[Catastrophe]]. Nevertheless, they carry out their mission to search for survivors, explore the city, and gather intel for Rhodes Island.  

As the reconnaissance teams explore the abandoned plate, a team consisting of [[Meteorite]], [[Jessica]], and [[Frostleaf]] record a sudden and drastic drop in temperature. Frostleaf discovers thick frost covering the surrounding buildings and recognizes these as signs of a dangerous faction—the infamous [[Yeti Squadron]] that once wandered the northern Ursus tundra, rumored to have defeated [[Ursus]]' Infected Patrol forces many times in the past. Not much later, the three discover [[Mephisto]] and [[W]] within the ruins. The two leaders exchange pointed barbs before launching a surprise attack on the team, forcing them to retreat deeper into the ruins of the city to shake their pursuers. Jessica calls Amiya during the attack to call for backup, but Frostleaf insists that Amiya ''not'' come, lest she become trapped with them. Amiya convinces Ch'en to agree to another joint operation between R.I. and the L.G.D. in order to rescue the recon team. 

==...and Into the Fire==
Knowing that the recon team is in danger, R.I. and the [[Lungmen Guard Department|L.G.D.]], including Ch'en and [[Hoshiguma]], arrive at the abandoned city plate. Ch'en happens to see [[Talulah]] shortly after landing and breaks off to chase her, leaving the rest of the team behind to be ambushed by Reunion. Amiya decides to goes deeper into the Originium dense zone of the city to find Ch'en and the recon team while the L.G.D. stays behind to guard the entrance to the zone. Amiya eventually finds Ch'en, who denies going after Talulah and turns back to find her squad. R.I. then discovers Frostleaf hiding from Mephisto and his forces in a ruined building. Frostleaf shows Amiya that the cold spread by the Yetis has not only caused frostbite all over her body, but has also frozen over their communication devices, cutting off contact with Meteorite and Jessica. After clearing out Reunion forces, the team find Meteorite and Jessica, but the latter is completely despondent. Amiya manages to shake Jessica from her shell shock by seemingly directly manipulating Jessica's emotions. Frostleaf questions her after witnessing this, but Amiya asks her to keep it a secret from Dr Kal'tsit. Meanwhile, Mephisto continues taunting Rhodes Island loudly, setting buildings filled with corpses ablaze. Rhodes Island emerges and attacks Mephisto directly, when a sudden chill fills the air...

[[File:FrostNova Arrives.png|thumb|The Yeti Princess, arriving alongside merciless blizzard]]
The Yeti Squadron arrives with their leader, the dreaded FrostNova. Upon her arrival, the temperature drops sharply and ice begins to form, extinguishing the blazes that Mephisto has ignited. Surmising that FrostNova is draining all the heat from her surroundings and that they presently have no chance against her, Amiya immediately orders Rhodes island to retreat. Frostleaf puts her life on the line to buy time for Rhodes Island to evacuate, but Meteorite comes to her rescue and bombards the area with her explosives. The team takes the opportunity to escape successfully. Speaking to a mysterious, towering figure, FrostNova vows to go after Rhodes Island.
