==Attack on Lungmen==
[[File:We Are L.G.D..png|thumb|The L.G.D. must restore peace to the streets of Lungmen. Failure is not an option.]]
Aboard the [[Rhodes Island]] landship, [[Swire]] wakes [[Ch'en]] up from a nightmare by pouring a cup of water on her, and informs the [[Lung]] that [[Reunion]] has occupied the headquarters of the [[Lungmen Guard Department]]. Swire blames Ch'en for the embarrassment of losing their headquarters, since she had been knocked out in an ambush while they were returning to Lungmen and was taken to the Rhodes Island landship to recover. Ch'en and Swire bicker over the right course of action, as well as Ch'en's culpability in the loss of their headquarters. Before storming off in anger, Swire tells Ch'en that she ought to resign and pass her position as head of the Special Ops division to someone else. Ch'en gets a call from her informant, codenamed XR-02, telling her that Reunion lured him into a trap at Muk Wo Warehouse and that he is holding necessary time-sensitive intel.

Ch'en speaks to a Rhodes Island officer in order to coordinate a joint attack on Reunion's movements in Lungmen. During the conversation, she reveals that she made the decision to prioritize the defense of [[Lungmen]], and thus chose to leave Amiya and her forces behind in the abandoned Chernobog city plate earlier—[[Amiya]], [[Frostleaf]], [[Meteorite]], [[Jessica]], and the allied forces are now considered missing in action. The Rhodes Island officer leaves in disgust over her actions, but Ch'en is not offended; In light of this, she eventually decides not to employ R.I.'s help in the upcoming operation to retake L.G.D. headquarters. Hoshiguma wonders why Ch'en decided to abandon Amiya's squad so decisively, and speculates that the orders were from [[Wei Yenwu]]. Ch'en, in response, says that while she appreciates all he has done, she will never respect him.

Ch'en and Hoshiguma launch a raid on the Muk Wo Warehouse, the abandoned warehouse in Central Lungmen where XR-02 is being held hostage by Reunion. Ch'en confirms Hoshiguma's suspicions that the informant is actually Phal, someone Hoshiguma has known for ten years, who infiltrated Reunion after recently becoming Infected. However, when they break through the Reunion forces and breach the warehouse, they find Phal mortally wounded. Although it was too late to save his life, the L.G.D. successfully retrieved the information he had on his body, which pointed them towards the location of Reunion's bases.

Phal's intel also indicates that Reunion's objective is not just the construction of a forward base in Lungmen, but also to search for an extremely high value individual. After fighting through more Reunion forces, Ch'en finds herself at the Ch'en Estate, a mansion that belonged to her biological father's clan. Since Reunion had already torn through the house and caused structural damage, Ch'en breaks down the door to let herself inside. Although she tells Hoshiguma that she has no particular attachment to the estate, being inside its walls leaves her reminiscing about her past. Ch'en comes across a cracked picture frame holding a photograph of herself and [[Talulah]] as children, hugging; knowing that Reunion had also broken into the mansion before, she briefly wonders if the photo could have been what they were looking for.

After the brief detour, the L.G.D. forces continue their advance by capturing Paci Plaza, a nearby shopping mall that is owned by Swire's family and currently occupied by Reunion. During the battle, an explosion causes the floor beneath Ch'en to collapse, but she is saved by Hoshiguma, who holds the debris back with her shield so it doesn't crush them. Hoshiguma is badly injured but insists she can still fight; she urges Ch'en to leave her behind to fend off Reunion and focus on recapturing L.G.D. Headquarters, but Ch'en refuses to abandon her. Ch'en admits that she has changed — after abandoning Amiya's squad, she reassessed herself, and has wants to put more trust in the people by her side. For the first time, she calls Hoshiguma her friend, and vows to be her shield. Ch'en and Hoshiguma then fight off Reunion together; after the battle, Hoshiguma is tended to by a medic, and Ch'en heads off to recapture L.G.D. headquarters without her. 

Swire calls Ch'en, irate at all the damage that Paci Plaza had sustained during the battle and blaming her for it. Ch'en promises to treat her to a meal in return for the damages. Their conversation switches tone as Swire solemnly advises her not to throw her life away for the sake of the mission, revealing a more friendly side to their relationship than previously seen, and wishes her good luck.

==Chi Xiao Unleashed==
After numerous skirmishes in the streets of Lungmen, Ch'en and her squad finally enter L.G.D. headquarters. However, the officers immediately feel something amiss about the ease in which they breached the building; the Reunion forces posted seem barely prepared for a fight, and many floors are completely empty. They also receive intel that Reunion's temporary command center is on the rooftop. Ch'en senses the obvious trap, but takes the bait anyways. When she arrives at the rooftop, it turns out to be a trap set by [[Mephisto]]. The L.G.D. is able to defeat almost all of Reunion's forces while weathering sniper attacks from [[Faust]] and his crossbowmen, but Mephisto resurrects the dead Reunion soldiers using his [[Originium Arts|Arts]], which takes the form of a strange white powder. The resurrected soldiers start attacking again, now transformed from corpses into hulking monstrosities highly resistant to physical damage. Mephisto is confused when his arts are attracted to Ch'en, as they should only take affect on Infected individuals. 

[[File:Ch'en Unleashing Chi Xiao 2.png|thumb|Ch'en unleashing her Chi Xiao]]
Knowing that the L.G.D. officers on the roof and below are about to be overwhelmed by the strange new Infected, Ch'en attempts to unsheathe Chi Xiao, the "dragon-slaying sword" imbued with Originium Arts. Wei Yenwu had given the sword to Ch'en in the past, and told her it should only be used against "worthy opponents." However, she is interrupted mid-draw and loses control over Chi Xiao; the blast injures her and kills multiple of Mephisto's puppets. He taunts Ch'en, reminding her that forcefully activating the Originium Arts inside the blade will only injure her further. He tries to convince her to join Reunion, but Ch'en refuses to abandon her duties to Lungmen.

==Boiling Blood, Looming Threat==
Just then, reinforcements from [[Rhodes Island]] arrive on the battlefield via the [[Rhodes Island#Overview|Bad Guy]]. Swire calls Ch'en, revealing that she had been coordinating with Rhodes Island behind her back and had rescued [[Amiya]]'s squad from the abandoned Chernobog city plate. Now joined by Amiya, the [[Doctor]], and [[Elite Op|Elite Operator]] [[Blaze]], the tides of battle finally turn in the L.G.D.'s favour. Mephisto is outraged to see that Amiya is still alive, and demands to know what FrostNova had told them. Meanwhile, Ch'en finally apologizes to Amiya for abandoning her in Chernobog. Mephisto presses Reunion to continue attacking, but knowing that the trap has failed, Faust convinces him to retreat. They purposefully destroy the damaged rooftop and make their escape under the cover of planted explosives. Nevertheless, the recapture of L.G.D. Headquarters is a pivotal moment in the liberation of Lungmen. Swire and Ch'en report their success to Wei Yenwu; during her report, Ch'en credits the joint efforts of Rhodes Island in taking back the headquarters. 

After the battle, Blaze confronts Wei Yenwu for using both Rhodes Island and the L.G.D. as bait in Chernobog to lure Reunion out — Wei had led Reunion to believe that the L.G.D. had been separated and that Lungmen was undefended, drawing Reunion to openly attack Lungmen. Blaze also brings up how Wei ordered the L.G.D. to abandon Amiya and her team, but Amiya talks her down because they have no proof. Wei ends the conversation by reiterating that the relationship between Lungmen and Rhodes Island is still a political partnership, not a statement on the status between illegal Infected and the local authorities.

After returning to his office, Wei orders Swire and her squad to purge all the remaining Reunion forces in a single day, without Rhodes Island's help. Despite Swire's protests, Wei insists it is essential as a show of Lungmen's strength.

Meanwhile, Wei anxiously awaits a message from [[Ursus]]. He tells [[Fumizuki]] of his premonition that the attacks on Lungmen and Chernobog are the start of something bigger—a deeper conspiracy, potentially conducted by the Ursus Empire in order to expand its hegemony.
