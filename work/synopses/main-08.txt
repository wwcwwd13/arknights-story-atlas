==Reunion to Ruin==
During the year of 1089, [[Talulah]] lived with an elderly couple in a small village deep in the northwestern tundra of [[Ursus]]. After arriving two years prior with nothing but fancy clothes and a sword, Talulah was taken in by the couple and treated like their own daughter. She also became close friends with [[Alina]]—an [[Elafia]]n girl—who likewise treated the elderly couple as family. Together, the four of them lived a simple yet good life. One day, the [[Infected]] Patrol Unit arrived for a routine inspection. During the visit, an irritated patrolman punched the old man who had taken Talulah in. Enraged, she attacked the man, her pupils alight with her [[Arts]], and drove off the patrolmen without killing them. But instead of thanking her, the elder responded with panic. Talulah insisted everything would be alright, and helped him walk back home.<ref>[[R8-1/Story]]</ref>

The Patrol Unit soon returns in greater numbers, searching the village for the attacker and threatening severe punishment for anyone harboring them. Talulah gives the old woman the last of her valuables and prepares to draw the patrol away before striking. Before she can leave, Alina arrives with urgent news: the Patrol has learned there is a hidden Infected in the village. Talulah quickly realizes the old man is missing. The couple had known of Talulah’s [[Oripathy]] since the beginning, and the old man has now turned himself in to the Patrol to protect her, as he is Infected himself. Talulah rushes out to save him, but she is too late. Overcome with rage, she unleashes her Arts, killing the patrolmen with fire and sword. Alina and Talulah flee together. Along the way, Alina reveals that she, too, is Infected. She also tells Talulah that the old man knowingly gave himself up to protect them both, and Talulah’s interference disrupted his plan. Vowing to return someday, Talulah and Alina part ways.<ref>[[R8-3/Story]]</ref>

A month later, Talulah arrives at an unregistered Infected village in the tundra and intervenes to stop the Patrol Unit from slaughtering its inhabitants. Moved by her bravery, the Infected decide to follow her.<ref>[[R8-4/Story]]</ref>

Two years later, with Alina once again by her side, Talulah opens up about her past under [[Kashchey]], and how she ultimately killed him. Together, they reflect on how she first encountered [[FrostNova]] and [[Patriot]]—when she led a squad to seek out the Aegis, a band of guerrillas notorious for resisting Ursus authority. Despite her efforts, Talulah only found FrostNova and her Yeti Squadron. The two exchanged words before dueling, with Talulah emerging victorious. She is left speechless by Patriot’s overwhelming presence. FrostNova and Patriot soon agree to join her cause.<ref>[[R8-5/Story]]</ref>

[[File:Good Old Reunion.png|thumb|right|Reunion, when they only sought to survive the harsh winter of Ursus.]]
Talulah shares her vision of creating a lasting legacy for the Infected with FrostNova, though she remains anxious about Patriot's disapproval and quietly seeks his acceptance. During these early conversations, the foundations of the Reunion Movement are laid, inspired in part by an unnamed friend of Talulah's from within the cities. Later, following a mission to liberate an Infected mine, Patriot finally voices his judgment. He calls Talulah an empty idealist, one whose dreams will only lead the Infected to death at the hands of the Ursus Army. Shaken, Talulah turns once more to Alina. After a heartfelt conversation, Alina sees her off with a smile. Each goes their separate way to fight for the Infected—Talulah as a revolutionary, and Alina as a teacher.<ref>[[R8-6/Story]]</ref>

A group of Infected breaks away from Reunion with Talulah’s blessing, taking control of a dilapidated city plate in the tundra as Reunion moves south. Patriot insists this faction has betrayed Reunion’s ideals, while Talulah advocates for their freedom to leave. Eventually, Patriot relents; once Talulah departs, he tells a soldier nearby that Talulah made the right decision, even though the departing Infected have tipped off the Ursus army, using Reunion as bait.<ref>[[R8-8/Story]]</ref>

As Reunion advances into a warmer climate, disaster strikes. The Patrol Unit retaliates against villages that had traded with them, and Alina is grievously injured in the aftermath. Talulah rushes to her side in despair, carrying her through the forest as her Arts surge uncontrollably, scorching the land beneath her feet. Alina refuses to name those responsible, instead urging Talulah never to give in to hatred, warning that hatred itself is the very fate Kashchey believed to be inevitable. Despite Talulah’s desperate efforts, Alina succumbs to her injuries just moments before they reach Reunion’s camp.<ref>[[R8-9/Story]]</ref>

In the months that follow, Talulah grows less energetic, less talkative, and becomes laser-focused on the future of Reunion. The situation escalates when the Royal Guard of Ursus—the [[Emperor's Blade]]s—attack, seeking Talulah. After a tense conversation during which the Blades reveal Talulah’s noble status to the Infected she fights alongside, Patriot arrives, easily driving off the Royal Guards and placing his faith in her.<ref>[[R8-11/Story]]</ref>

Rumors instigated by the Emperor’s Blades cause divisions within Reunion. Many Infected leave, unable to trust anyone of noble birth. These departures wound Talulah deeply, and her ideology suffers as she grows more withdrawn after every defeat. While searching for a missing tractor and group of Reunion Infected, Talulah sneaks into a village under cover of night and discovers that the inhabitants had locked the hungry Infected in their empty granary, leaving them to starve. Enraged beyond measure by this cruel act, the Draco declares her hatred for those who would commit such merciless deeds. In her fury, she burns down the village while recalling Kashchey's words from years ago, marking the moment she fully succumbs to the worldview he instilled in her.<ref>[[JT8-1/Story]]</ref>

==The Shieldguards Advance==
In the present, the core city of [[Chernobog]] has devolved into a chaotic war as the Guerillas and those who were loyal to Patriot fight back against Talulah's Sarkaz mercenaries and those who follow her. Amidst the chaos, [[Rosmontis]] and [[Amiya]] advance towards the control tower, and Rosmontis is blockaded by a group of fanatical Reunion loyalists who refuse to back down. Not wishing to detour and against the wishes of her squad, she collapses the entire building the enemy has occupied with her Arts. It is also revealed that Amiya is prepared to release one of her ten rings that dampen her powers, given to her by [[Theresa]]. She also finds more evidence of [[Ch'en]]'s presence in Chernobog, and witnesses a vision of Ch'en from her childhood after Talulah was kidnapped. Meanwhile, The [[Doctor]] and [[Kal'tsit]] encounter Herdified [[Sarkaz]] warriors as they approach the Sarcophagus, and fall back to find another entrance while the Herd attacks other Reunion forces. They discuss the situation in the city and the appearance of the special Infected while preparing for the operation, donning specialized protective equipment similar in design to the Patrol Unit, and move to break through the lines of the converted Sarkaz.<ref>[[M8-1]]</ref>

[[File:"They".png|thumb|Rosmontis and the Shieldguards united, the Aegis of the Infected.]]
Amiya and Rosmontis' squads dismantle a group of Sarkaz mercenaries blocking their way, and [[Guard (NPC)|Guard]] proposes a formal alliance between Rhodes Island and the Shieldguards.<ref>[[M8-2]]</ref> Amiya meets with the Shieldguard's leader, and they reach a formal agreement. Members of Rosmontis' squad divert to stop Talulah's forces from raising the sealing layer, a wall that would indefinitely cut off access to the control tower, and ruin any chance of defeating Talulah.<ref>[[M8-3]]</ref> However, when they reach the controls for the sealing layer, they find themselves ambushed by mysterious foes disguised as Reunion fighters. Rosmontis hears and feels Bluishsilver's death, and angrily rips open the still-rising wall with her Arts, pushing herself too far but opening a passage for the allied forces before collapsing from exhaustion. Amiya sees another vision of Ch'en's past.<ref>[[M8-4]]</ref>

Rosmontis' squad re-establishes contact, confirming Bluishsilver's death and telling Rosmontis her final words. The Doctor and Kal'tsit continue advancing towards the Sarcophagus, as recon squad members [[Elysium]] and Hawk discover that the Ursus Third Army has infiltrated the city, and were the ones responsible for the attack on Rosmontis' squad. They immediately relay this information to [[Mantra]], who informs the squad leaders.<ref>[[M8-5]]</ref> However, Guard, Rosmontis, Amiya, and the Shieldguards have already encountered the disguised soldiers, and are thrown off by their sudden attack. They fall back to form a defensive line, and Amiya decides to go after Talulah alone while the rest hold the line.<ref>[[M8-6/Story]]</ref> The remaining squads encounter [[W]] shortly after, and the situation grows tense as Rosmontis recognizes her as one of Scout's killers. After a heated argument, W is allowed to walk free at Guard's insistence.<ref>[[M8-7]]</ref> They advance onwards, and [[Raidian]] uses her arts to cause a temporary feedback loop in the headsets of all Third Army infiltrators, allowing the Shieldguards and Rosmontis to take them down without causing more chaos.<ref>[[M8-8]]</ref>

==A Pitiful Song==
[[File:Mephisto, The Poor Songster.png|thumb|This Infected creature only wishes to sing. It only wishes to sing.]]
Kal'tsit and the Doctor's squads finally reach the Sarcophagus proper, and discover the source of the Herd's resurgence.<ref>[[M8-7]]</ref> Following the sound of singing, they find a peculiar crystalline beast, breathing out concentrated [[Originium]] particles at a dangerous rate with its presence alone. The creature isn't hostile, sleeping despite the pain it is in. Kal'tsit comments on the tragic nature of this outcome—she had seen a similar aberration before, long ago. Kal'tsit is troubled by the warped Mephisto, but resolves to kill it for the greater good in her capacity as an Infected doctor. After the assembled R.I. squads felled the beast and powered down the Sarcophagus, the Doctor is struck with a strange feeling of familiarity. The Doctor remembers a name that leaves Kal'tsit in stunned silence—[[Priestess]].<ref>[[M8-8/Story]]</ref>

Amiya's squad stays behind at her insistence as she goes to confront Talulah, and she discovers the sheath of Ch'en's sword, Chi Xiao. Through Ch'en's lingering presence on the sheath, Amiya sees the dirty Originium bomb that caused Ch'en and [[Nine]]'s infection and their differing views of Lungmen. Now, Ch'en faces down Talulah as the young leader of Rhodes Island removes a ring to see the truth behind Reunion's now-tyrannical leader.<ref>[[M8-8/Story]]</ref>

==Raging Flames==
Amiya witnesses Talulah confronting the "Deathless Black Snake"—a deeply internalized ideological framework shaped by Kashchey's influence, Ursus' history, and Talulah's own accumulated trauma and despair—and returns to the waking world just in time to assist Ch'en.<ref>[[JT8-1/Story]]</ref> As they engage in combat, Amiya denounces the fatalistic worldview embodied by the Black Snake, while Talulah recognizes Amiya as the [[King of Sarkaz]] and the inheritor of a similarly immense historical burden. Talulah proves a powerful opponent, and Amiya gives in to the rage she had been burying within herself for a long time. Witnessing a vision of a great [[King of Sarkaz|Sarkaz King]]'s wrath and eventual suicide, Amiya materializes a sword based on that mighty [[Qui'lon|King]]'s blade, the ''Wrathful Cerulean Flame''. With her new sword and the knowledge of Ch'en's techniques obtained through the memories she has seen, Amiya cuts through Talulah's Arts and fights alongside Ch'en.

Throughout the conflict, the "Black Snake" persona invokes memories of Ursus' past grandeur, war, and suffering, presenting itself as the continuation of a historical logic that views violence, sacrifice, and conflict as necessary for the empire's survival. Through Talulah's fractured state and inherited memories associated with Kashchey's era, it mocks Amiya's perceived idealism while recalling armies and sights long lost to history, insisting that Ursus will continue to endure through struggle and contradiction.<ref>[[JT8-3/Story|JT8-3 Before]]</ref>

[[File:Ch'en and Amiya, Crimson and Shadow.png|right|thumb|Amiya and Ch'en, united against the "Deathless Black Snake"]]
After an arduous battle, Ch'en and Amiya finally break through Talulah's defenses. As Talulah's certainty begins to collapse, the ideological framework sustaining the "Black Snake" persona starts to unravel alongside her faltering Arts. Overwhelmed by guilt and despair, Talulah briefly contemplates taking her own life. In response, the persona attempts one final provocation, framing suffering and destruction as inevitable conclusions of history and human nature. However, through the intervention of Ch'en and Amiya, Talulah ultimately rejects this fatalistic conclusion and chooses to continue living.

W arrives and attempts to halt Chernobog's advance, but the key Amiya gave her lacks sufficient clearance. When all seems lost, Talulah reawakens and reveals the true master key she had secretly kept on her person, refusing to allow the city's destruction in a quiet act of defiance against the path she had nearly surrendered herself to. The core city slows to a stop, and the Ursus Third Army is routed as Rosmontis and the Shieldguards celebrate. Talulah and Ch'en speak briefly before discarding their swords and engaging in a fistfight, while W momentarily compares Amiya to [[Theresa]]. After W departs, the Doctor and Kal'tsit arrive just as Amiya loses consciousness.<ref>[[JT8-3/Story|JT8-3 After]]</ref>

Kal'tsit extracts a small amount of the Doctor's blood, and uses it to stabilize Amiya's condition. As the ''Bad Guy'' nears the Control Tower to take Talulah into custody, Kal'tsit explains the perilous political situation Rhodes Island has found itself in. Without taking Talulah prisoner as a deterrent, Ursus and Yan could easily wipe Rhodes Island off the face of [[Terra]]. W reunites with her mercs, and leads them away from Chernobog while Rosmontis bids farewell to the Shieldguards. On the outskirts of Chernobog, an [[Emperor's Blade]] converses with a member of [[Wei Yenwu]]'s [[Shadow Guard]] on the future of Ursus. [[Faust]]'s Phantom Crossbowmen rescue Reunion soldiers from the now-independent and rampaging Shieldguards, while Nine rallies aimless members to carry on Reunion's original goal: To help the Infected.<ref>[[END8-1]]</ref>
