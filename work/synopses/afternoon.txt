==Let's Throw a Party!==
The [[Doctor]] finds [[Vanilla]] in the training grounds, having just finished a session. Just as the Doctor expected, she asks them to help take care of her pet Originium slugs and a desert metal crab. The Doctor jokes about eating them, much to Vanilla's horror, before suggesting she simply ask anyone she comes across for help. Vanilla finds [[Melantha]] and [[Steward]], and Steward agrees to help in Melantha's stead. Their conversation then shifts to [[Franka]] teaching swordsmanship to Melantha, with Vanilla expressing slight worry that her senior might corrupt the timid girl. Shortly after, Melantha excuses herself for a checkup with [[Ansel]]. 

Steward and Vanilla talk about how Melantha's mood has improved recently, and then about the cooperation between [[Blacksteel]] and Rhodes Island, with Vanilla expressing her gratitude for being able to live a fulfilling life. [[Blaze]] joins the conversation, appearing with Dora, a lively little girl who was recently admitted to the ship for treatment. Looking at the uncommon pair, Blaze jokes about holding a mixer at Rhodes Island. Their conversation reveals that HR classifies Rhodes Islanders in many ways, with combatants making up the smallest portion. Even Dora, whose family Vanilla recalls did not look wealthy, can help by doing simple tasks like making paper flowers for the mixer Blaze announces will be held that day. She then mentions that some people call Rhodes Island a sweatshop, to which Vanilla quickly insists it is only a joke. Later, however, Blaze muses that Rhodes Island's education for children might be considered an early investment. A call from [[Amiya]] soon pulls Blaze away.

Steward accompanies Vanilla to her room and is stunned by her pets and their unique personalities. She explains that they are well-behaved but will explode if their spikes are pressed, and she shows him the automatic feeder. She then reminisces about the harsh life in her hometown, which drove her to become a mercenary for money. In turn, Steward tells her about his hometown in [[Kjerag]], and how her request had made Melantha happy. Before leaving for her mission, Vanilla asks him to bring her along the next time Reserve Op Team A4 visits the children, so they can learn how to make paper flowers for the mixer together.

==Diary==
After completing a site inspection, [[Ptilopsis]] receives thanks from [[Silence]]. The Liberi then excuses herself to her room, where she searches for a diary she kept from around the time she arrived at [[Rhodes Island]]. As she reads through the entries, she reminisces about various past events.

A few entries mention the day [[Aak]] and [[Hung]] first arrived at Rhodes Island. Despite his background as a back-alley doctor in [[Lungmen]], Ptilopsis notes that Aak refuses to join the medical department, as his disrespect for the staff is obvious. He often pesters [[Warfarin]], however, after discovering that she is Mr. Blood. She recalls one particular entry was written around the time of their second duel. Ptilopsis writes that only Ansel and Warfarin tolerated his behavior, though she adds that she somehow does not dislike his unpleasant manner.

Ptilopsis then finds an entry about a talk she had with Ansel, where she reveals details such as her status as both a patient and a researcher at Rhine Lab. She also notes that she had completely forgotten her family until Ansel's talk of his own prompted her to search her internal database. To Ansel's surprise, she claims that not seeing her family is not hard and then urges him to continue sharing stories about his hometown. In the present, she remarks that her own past feels distant and resolves to write the diary entry for today.

During a mission, on one of the rare occasions she goes outside, an operator lent Ptilopsis some [[Kazimierz]] coinage so she can relax while they perform a manual recalibration of their tools. While writing in her diary, she remarks that she did not notice the operator feeling guilty for bringing up her condition, as such social cues go unused in her database. The second problem she encountered was not knowing what to buy for the medical department, so the guard operator accompanying her bought local goods in her stead. Her recollection is interrupted by a knock on the door. It is [[Ifrit]] who quickly busies herself with the goods Ptilopsis brought back. The Liberi ponders that her life now rotates between sleep and research after Silence worked out a treatment plan for her, and that she compartmentalizes herself when others discuss their pasts.

A flashback to a time she was surprised by how Silence fretted over Ifrit's birthday present makes Ptilopsis realize something important. More than just remembering facts, she should try to truly understand the people she cares about. At that moment, Silence enters the room, and Ptilopsis reassures her that Ifrit isn't causing any trouble. When asked about her condition, Ptilopsis says she is fine but mentions that she was just about to find Silence to discuss things she has never considered before.

==Today's Kitchen==
[[Matterhorn]] is cooking a traditional Kjerag dish when [[Gummy]] compliments his cooking. He remarks on the wide variety of food supplies Rhodes Island has access to, thanks to its extensive network of branches across the world. Gummy adds that ingredients can even be specially requested by writing a note on the bulletin. While she is trying out his dish, some of the food mysteriously disappears. The two promise to exchange recipes, after which Gummy leaves to bring a stew to the Doctor.

Matterhorn then warns the culprit, who turns out to be [[Ethan]], once again stealing food from the kitchen supposedly because he finds Matterhorn's cooking to be irresistible. Matterhorn lectures him sternly for stealing food as well as for his unsanitary habits. Ethan promises to wash his hands properly next time, though he continues to make glib remarks when asked about the dish. He admits that it has a confusing note to its flavor, observing that Matterhorn had been spacing out and sighing while cooking. Ethan then asks about the braised beastmeat, which Matterhorn explains he is preparing for [[Cliffheart|Ensia]], as he had just met [[SilverAsh]] with [[Courier]] earlier that day.

While Matterhorn prepares fried beastmeat for Ethan, the [[Savra]] asks him questions about SilverAsh and his sister, inadvertently irritating him. After serving the meal to Ethan, Matterhorn leaves to deliver tea to Ensia. Ethan washes his hands with soap and is reminded by [[Vulcan]] to dry them first before eating, as she asks about where Matterhorn is. On his way back, Matterhorn thinks about the Silverash siblings before running into Ethan, who hands him a clumsily made sandwich and then disappears. He then encounters Vulcan, whom he assumes has come to pick up his shield. Vulcan mentions that Ethan also walked off with two plates of food from the kitchen and muses that it almost seems as though Matterhorn enjoys his hooliganism, pressing him to look her in the eye and repeat his denial. Changing the subject, Matterhorn inquires about Ethan's age, still annoyed at being called "Uncle".

== The Wanderer's Home ==
[[Hibiscus]] and [[Lava]] are giving a recently rescued [[Perro]] girl a checkup, bribing her with honey biscuits for an oral exam. It is revealed that the two brought her back to Rhodes Island from the wilderness of [[Leithanien]] after subduing her, as she had been attacking passersby while searching for food, including their squad. According to the laws of Leithanien, a rogue [[Infected]] like her who had somehow learned Arts was bound to be euthanized. Hibiscus praises Lava for communicating with the Perro and bringing honey biscuits for her, much to her sister's embarrassment.

[[Beagle]] barges into the room and reminds Hibiscus and Lava to attend [[Dobermann]]'s class, as she had forgotten to get them out of class. Panicked, they entrust the girl to the first person they see, who happens to be the blacksmith, Vulcan. With the few tips she has, Vulcan takes the girl to her workshop and inspects her weapon at the Perro's request, finding [[Minos|Minoan]] craftsmanship on the axe. The girl says her name is inscribed on the handle in Minoan, and Vulcan pronounces it as Ceobe. Ceobe quickly adopts the name and even insists that Vulcan come up with a nickname for her. After much thought, Vulcan settles on "Kay."

A logistics operator enters the workshop to handle Ceobe's legal matters, although Vulcan ends up receiving the documents. He reveals that Ceobe has been wandering across Terra, from [[Bolívar]] to the east before turning back, searching for Minos. Ceobe adds that she wanted to go to Minos to find someone who could read her name. Through simple suggestions, Vulcan draws out Ceobe's wishes and convinces her to become a Rhodes Island Operator, even agreeing to be her sponsor. Vulcan also promises to have Matterhorn bake honey biscuits for her and accepts her request to make her weapons the same color as her axe. Lava and Hibiscus return from their class and are shocked by Ceobe's development.

Two weeks later, the logistics operator informs Vulcan about Ceobe's results. Although Ceobe made a mess of her tests, she passes her assessment exam and officially becomes an Operator. When asked why she vouched for her, Vulcan replies that Ceobe's well-kept weapons are enough for her to recognize the girl's potential. Although Vulcan cannot forge such an axe herself, the technique feels familiar, and she wants to solve the mystery of the weapon, as Minoans rarely inscribe names on their arms.

==Asking for Trouble==
During a conversation with a logistics operator, [[Orchid]] recalls when she first arrived at Rhodes Island and chose a combat role just to avoid sitting in an office. She tells him that she simply fell into the position, and he responds by inviting her to join the logistics department, noting that combat operators with a civilian background and a year of experience are eligible to apply. As [[Popukar]] appears and they leave, the logistics operator reminds Orchid to think it over. Distressed by the thought of returning to an office job, Orchid asks Popukar how she joined Rhodes Island. When Popukar reveals she worked at a lumberyard before being found by Ansel and [[Kal'tsit]], a horrified Orchid hugs the innocent girl.

[[Catapult]] appears and asks why the two are hugging, mentioning she just finished working out with [[Midnight]], who is currently training with [[Spot]]. The news surprises Orchid. Popukar then recalls that Midnight never asked her to work out with him again after she hit him once, which makes Catapult laugh. Nevertheless, Orchid reminds Catapult not to cause trouble, and Popukar agrees to come to Catapult's room later that night for story time. Afterward, Orchid and Popukar find Spot and Midnight sparring in the training room. Orchid is indignant that Midnight has a session outside of his daily routine without telling her, but he smoothly talks his way out of it. He explains his philosophy of treating all his clients equally and tells Orchid that she shouldn't let her civilian background shackle her.

They then move to the cafeteria, where Midnight treats them to a meal while answering Spot's earlier question about how he became an operator. He was at the top of his industry as an escort, until one day he saw a bright newcomer whom he thought would surpass him. Driven by a hidden envy, the newcomer attempted to kill him once, then managed to have an agent infect him on the second try. He then asks Orchid what kind of life she wants to lead at Rhodes Island, and she thanks him for his advice. After Orchid and Popukar leave, Spot and Midnight continue their conversation, in which Midnight reveals that his most important trick is to always tell the truth. Meanwhile, Orchid tells Popukar not to go to Catapult's room as it is already late, and the Cautus girl expresses relief that Orchid seems happier than before. The two then come across Sora, who introduces herself and asks about Orchid's expertise in fashion design.

Later, Orchid decides to reject the logistics department's offer and signs up for fitness training. She is interrupted by Catapult, who exclaims that Midnight is currently sparring with Popukar. When Catapult insists that she hasn't caused any trouble lately and Orchid says she believes her, Catapult remarks that Orchid seems different. As she watches Catapult rush off, eager to see Midnight lose, Orchid muses about the troubles she might find after choosing to look for them.

==Moving Forward==
[[Reserve Op Team A1]] has just finished their tests, with only Lava and [[Fang]] passing theirs. The Sarkaz twins have already gone back, while Beagle and Kroos gather to see Fang. Instructor Grace assures them that the tests were not designed to be easy, as there is a large skill gap between trainees and instructors. He congratulates them for their efforts against the other instructors, who are experts in their own right, having been trained by a much stricter Dobermann. He further adds that although Dobermann might not be the strongest fighter in Rhodes Island, her tactics and strategies from her time in the Bolivarian army are overpowering. The three Operators listen as Grace reminisces that Dobermann was still like a real soldier during the early days of Rhodes Island and that she had always cared about people. Fang offers to take the test results to Dobermann for Grace, and Beagle comes along with her.

Dobermann receives the results and, despite her reassuring words, she tells Beagle to inform Kroos about a session that afternoon. Curious about Dobermann's past, Fang offers to help her clean her room. The offer surprises Dobermann, who had always thought of them as fearful of her. As they help her sort through the multitude of items in her room, the two ask questions about her past. Dobermann then explains to them the three factions in Bolívar: the puppet Singas dynasty of Leithanien, the Columbian-sponsored Coalition Government, and the grassroots resistance force, the True Bolívarians. She explains that she was once a colonel serving the Coalition government. Beagle sneezes while wiping dust and comes across metal tags, which Dobermann explains belonged to soldiers in the army. A flashback reveals that they were comrades she wasn't close with, but whose deaths caused her to become disillusioned.

She then asks the two trainees what they think is the defining characteristic of a soldier. She dismisses their initial answer and explains that numbers are only one part of it. Beagle answers correctly, and Dobermann points out that it is obedience to the chain of command, which is why the correct orders must be issued. She reveals that she defected to the resistance but eventually left, and was pleasantly surprised when she moved to Rhodes Island. Although the two trainees fail to understand her philosophies, Dobermann tells them to appreciate Rhodes Island. The two then hurriedly leave when they realize it is already lunchtime. Left alone, Dobermann reflects on her teaching at Rhodes Island, willing to believe in the future Amiya and Kal'tsit envisioned, but resolves to one day return to Bolívar.
