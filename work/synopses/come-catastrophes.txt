==Cold Dead Hands==
[[File:A Home in the Winter.png|thumb|left|Davistown, a town doomed to failure as winter sets in]]
A team of [[Blacksteel Worldwide]] Operators is dispatched to [[Davistown]], a once-bustling mining town built atop a nomadic plate, now on the verge of being decommissioned by order of the [[Columbia]]n state government. A squad consisting of [[Liskarm]], [[Franka]], and engineering specialist [[Almond|Laura Arias]] arrives to maintain the town's security before its eventual contact with ''Fort Barron'', Blacksteel Worldwide's nomadic base. [[Jessica]] is also part of this squad, but before her departure, the {{Tip|CEO|chief executive officer}} of BSW, [[Cliff|"Clip" Cliff]], summons her to his office. They discuss several incidents of insubordination she has committed, and he gives her an etched [[wikipedia:.44 Magnum|.44]] bullet, which he instructs her to deliver to a man named [[Woodrow Bianchi]] during the mission. Cliff reminds Jessica that they are mercenaries: their work is business, not duty.

[[File:Old Hunter and Young Lass.png|thumb|]]
The squad first reaches the town by foot, but they meet an ambush by thugs-turned-bandits. Jessica went missing during the engagement and was ambushed by a wild beast, but an old Sankta rescues her and brings her back to Davistown to wait for the rest of the squad. At an old diner run by [[Coldshot|Helena]], she also met [[Leone Theremin]], the city's demolitions expert, both of whom revealed that the old Sankta is none other than Woodrow himself.

Meanwhile, Liskarm and the others meet up with the manager of Davistown's bank and her secretary [[Sylvia]] before regrouping with Franka. The manager explains that the town's financial crisis is so severe that Davistown's mayor has long abandoned the town, leaving the bank as the ''de facto'' administrator of the town. Liskarm and Franka also noticed something odd with the citizens, where some desperately tries to get loans from the bank while others flee into the wilderness without taking anything with them.

Liskarm and the squad reached the diner where they also reunited with Jessica. Leone requested the squad to help him conducting a maintenance for Davistown's aging [[Originium]] power furnace with aid from [[Miles]] the boiler. During the maintenance work, the furnace almost went into an overload but Laura managed to prevent a catastrophic outcome through her engineering skills, while Jessica and Franka keeps the furnace safe from local gangsters. Jessica also thanked Woodrow for his help but missed the chance to give him the bullet Cliff entrusted to her.

==Ill-Gotten Gains==
It did not take long before the truth behind Davistown's situation surfaces before the BSW squad.

Jessica witnessed Leone's adopted son [[Benny]] getting beaten up by local gangsters and managed to save him. Benny reveals that, to Jessica's horror, the supposedly gangsters are actually thugs hired by Davistown's bank to harass their debtors; Leone, in particular, has a tremendously high debt which he had to take to keep the city's mine up and running. Jessica then asks Woodrow to persuade Leone into selling off his share of the mine to the bank to pay off his debt while keeping his house. Initially Leone angrily refuses because the mine is the only thing he had, but relents after learning of what happened to Benny earlier.

Meanwhile, Liskarm and the rest of the squad continues providing security assistance for the bank, during which they noticed that the citizens continues to ask loans from the bank to keep them out of poverty while the bank's administrators lived in luxury as seen during a welcoming party held by the bank. To make matters worse, the squad finds out that the bank had a rather unorthodox but horrible way to deal with debtors who are unable to pay off their debt: forcing them into Columbia's frontiers as Pioneers.

[[File:Cat's in the Cradle.png|thumb|Why does a surprise announcement like this always changes the mood from happy to depressing?]]
Later that night, a party is held in Helena's diner to celebrate Leone being free from his debt. Initially things look happy as everyone drinks crude wine and ate delicious meal cooked by Helena while Leone and Miles sing an old folk song and recounting Davistown's better days in the past. However, Benny interrupts by informing everyone that he will leave Davistown with an upcoming trade caravan alongside his homeschool teacher Selena to pursue better opportunities elsewhere. Helena and Miles can only give their approval with a bitter smile, while Leone was devastated for losing another children; he has another adopted son, Carl, a BSW mercenary Jessica knows under the callsign "Blackplate" who was {{Tip|K.I.A.|killed in action}} during the [[Chernobog Crisis]]. The party ends with a sad note and Jessica tries to persuade Benny to stay with Leone, but he points out to her that the situation is unsalvageable &mdash; just as how she couldn't save Carl.

[[File:The Black Landship.png|thumb|left|Blacksteel's landship arrives in Davistown, for better or worse?]]
The story skips to two weeks later, close to New Year's Eve, as the BSW squad prepares for the arrival of ''Fort Barron''. All of a sudden, a town-wide riot occurs in Davistown, incited by people who had become desperate with the situation and turned into looting, and the bank's hired gangsters agitates the rioters for their own ends. Although ''Fort Barron''&apos;s arrival managed to stabilize the situation, the damage is done: the riots leave many citizens injured and Leone's house was burnt down amidst the chaos.

==Broke, Blind, and Bedlam==
At this point, Jessica realizes that the citizens of Davistown had fallen into one giant trap: The bank has been providing low-interest loans to entice the townsmen along with several bet-on agreements, but the bank secretly increases the interest rate at their leisure, leaving the surprised debtors unable to pay it back in due time. Furthermore, this was a scheme for the bank to illegally seize the debtors' property once they were sent off Davistown as explained before &mdash; a convenient excuse for the bank to evict the debtors &mdash; and then clean them up before Davistown is handed over to the state government so that the refurbished property attracts investors. BSW's security partnership with the bank also gives them the opportunity to use excessive force to deal with those who still stayed in the town.

Having to follow orders as part of their job, Liskarm's squad can only show their conscience to the citizens amidst this darkest hour. While Jessica managed to give Woodrow the bullet entrusted by Cliff to him and persuades him to speak up with Cliff, she later learned from Helena that Woodrow and Cliff are actually on bad terms following the latter's harsh decision during the Columbian Revolutionary War to save a larger force of the revolutionaries over rescuing Woodrow and his comrades who are being held captive in Camp Collus, the notorious [[Victoria]]n {{Tip|POW|prisoner of war}} camp where they are subjected to torture and only Woodrow who managed to survive the ordeal. Still, Woodrow was moved by Jessica to meet with Cliff in ''Fort Barron'' anyway and tries to persuade him into not forcing the citizens of Davistown out, but he refuses as this contract was given by the state government. Jessica then tries to use her status as the daughter of Bernie Brynley, one of [[Raythean]]'s major shareholders, to paid off the citizens' debts, but was met with a shocking revelation that the debts amounted to a grand total of C$27,600,000 &mdash; too much for even someone like Jessica to cover even with her family's support.

[[File:To Hell With Authorization.png|thumb|left|"What are you doing?! Silence this rabble at once!"<br/>"Madam, those are just civilians! We can't hurt them!"]]
The citizens of Davistown later march in a peaceful protest to express their anger over the bank's manipulative practices. As the protesters arrive in front of the bank, the manager orders Liskarm's squad who are standing by in the bank as security detail to ''actually'' use lethal force against the protesters &mdash; even going as far as to show a letter of authorization from the state government to have them comply, but they vehemently refuse, claiming that she is overstepping her bounds on the contract between BSW and Davistown, and remains on standby until the protesters disperse with no shots fired. Even Cliff himself, who was meeting with the bank's director in ''Fort Barron'' at the time and learned the situation while Jessica personally tries to persuade him without success, makes it clear to the director that he will not allow anyone to order BSW around as they please &mdash; they were his, ''and only his alone.''

The protest was ultimately futile as many of the citizens, including Miles, accepts their fate to be sent into the frontiers. After sending Miles off, Helena and Woodrow also reveals that they are going to follow suit &mdash; the former's diner which she inherits from her late lover was going to be dismantled anyway. Only Leone who wishes to remain as Davistown is the only thing he had left. As the BSW squad is about to leave for ''Fort Barron'', Jessica decides to leave the squad out of her sympathy towards the citizens and anger towards BSW's indifference, which Liskarm approves while giving a timid look and Franka and Laura sending Jessica off with a hug. However, the squad secretly aids Jessica by providing her with the equipment she needs.

[[File:The Almighty Dollar.png|thumb|"Dear God, look at all these..."<br/>"More than what any of us could make in a year, no less."]]
Having had enough with the situation, Sylvia decides to stand up against the bank and proposes a heist to "reclaim" the money taken from the citizens to Helena and the others, which they &mdash; including Jessica &mdash; accepts. Helena, Woodrow, Leone, and Jessica break into the bank and aided by Sylvia and her photographic memory, they managed to decode the first password to authorize the vault's lock release, with the second one obtained after the gang "persuaded" the bank's director and manager to cough it up. Upon accessing the vault, what awaits them is the sight of Columbian dollars, gold certificates, and pure gold bars with Jessica being in awe for seeing such valuables displayed vulgarly for the first time.

==High Noon==
[[File:I Have Nothing to Prove.png|thumb|left|Jessica sheds her tears for Leone's sacrifice, having nothing left to prove himself]]
The heist places BSW on full alert as Cliff gave his order to deal with the perpetrators except Jessica and Woodrow, whom he specifically orders to be captured. Upon receiving the order, Liskarm's squad decides to find Jessica before the other BSW units do. On Jessica's side, Helena, Jessica, and Woodrow escapes with the stolen money to the wilderness through underground passages linked with the bank, avoiding BSW forces who are on the hunt for them, while Leone heads to the furnace to set up an explosion to cover their escape. As he finishes his part however, Leone informs Helena and Jessica that he won't be joining them; rather than leaving Davistown without anything in hand, he'd rather meet his end to be together with the town he loves. After saying his goodbyes, Leone detonates the explosives, causing a spectacular explosion that destroys much of the furnace and takes him along with it while Jessica watches in sorrow.

[[File:High Moon.png|thumb|"You know what time it is...?"<br/>"It's high <s>noon</s> moon..."]]
After making his way into the wilderness, Woodrow heads to ''Fort Barron'' to settle the score with Cliff, who was expecting him. Woodrow reveals that he wants to kill Cliff for being a ruthless warmonger, yet thanks to his Sankta empathy, he realizes that what Cliff had been through eclipses the torture he received in Camp Collus. Cliff reveals that having experienced the horrors of warfare yet knowing that conflict is necessary at times as attested by the saying ''Si vis picem, para bellum'' ("If you wish for peace, prepare for war"), the best he can do to realize his dream to build a haven outside of [[Laterano]] is by controlling the flow of conflict so that wars should be &mdash; and have to be &mdash; the last resort, and people shouldn't be forced to fight against their will. Nevertheless, the two Sankta war veterans load their guns and fires at each other, but luckily, they only aimed at their hats, leaving the two unharmed. Although Cliff wants Woodrow to actually kill him, he refuses and spares his life, knowing that only Cliff alone can bear the burden of starting wars to end all wars. With that said, Woodrow surrendered himself to the BSW operators who arrived after hearing the gunshots.

[[File:Enough is Enough.png|thumb|left|Eight shots. More than enough to express Jessica's anger over what the Davistowners face.]]
Meanwhile, Jessica meets up with Liskarm and Franka after eluding the BSW operators pursuing her, although she kept her distance from them. Just then, the rest of the BSW catches up with Jessica and had her surrounded, demanding her to surrender. She complies, but not before firing eight shots into the air to express her anger for her own weakness and the situation that unfolds in Davistown. Nonetheless, Liskarm admires Jessica for her resolve.

As the dust settles, the bank could not recover the stolen money as they are laundered by Helena (with help from BSW's intelligence agent [[Surfer|Tila]] who had been observing the gang from the heist under Cliff's orders) before being handed over to Sylvia to be "returned" to the rightful owners. To add insult to the bank's injury, Cliff himself stated to the bank's director that the heist is outside what covered by the contract and revealing his intent to purchase Davistown rather than handing it over to the state government, which would be converted into a mobile training facility for the BSW in preparation for the Bolívarian Civil War that is heating up. Cliff greets Woodrow as he recovers and the two Sankta went on their separate ways in relatively good terms, having reached an understanding towards each other. As for Jessica, she turned down legal assistance from the lawyer sent by the Brynleys and rather takes the option to serve her sentence in the frontiers as a Pioneer. Furthermore, as pointed out by Liskarm, Jessica technically remains part of BSW as Cliff did not approve her resignation.

[[File:Don't Cry, Baby.png|thumb|Jessica sobs, lamenting that she could not do more for the people of Davistown.]]
As she prepares to head into the frontiers with Woodrow, Jessica remembers her times with her late grandfather, who encourages her to never be afraid to start over; to look towards the future and believe in it. Jessica wholeheartedly believes in this, firm in her decision to go the frontier with Woodrow. A wide-brimmed hat is placed upon Jessica's head by Woodrow, assuring her that if she wishes to cry, then she should, as she's endured a lot of hardship. After a long silence, Jessica begins to sob under the hat, while Woodrow looks to the horizon, tears welling up in his eyes.
