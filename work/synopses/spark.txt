==Maybe==
Every evening, the people of [[Caladon]], [[Victoria]], calls off their day by visiting pubs that scattered across the city, one of which is the "Green Spark." This pub is famous for its meager offerings and nice service thanks to its only bartender, [[Goldenglow|Susie Glitter]], who not only warms their heart, but also she sometimes offers them her extra service with her talented hairdressing skill. Infected workers from nearby factories as well as [[Rhodes Island]] staff like Bitter Root will come here to have their dinner and spend their night drinking.

The "Green Spark" is located in an Infected community, and right now, the locals are witnessing changes in their lives. Recently, the city council is drafting the "New Caladon Infected Act" that might help provide opportunities to jobless [[Infected]] outside the city as well as enacting new labor laws such as decreasing working hours. On the other hand, the new act is seen as taking the bread out of the mouths of the non-Infected due to a flux of Infected migrants, resulting in their constant protests. Even the Infected themselves see this new law with skepticism because it not only does not improve their livelihood, but also they meet hostility from the angry non-Infected. Without [[Red (NPC)|Red]] maintaining order, the protests will soon go out of order.

Nonetheless, Susie does not care about the protests and the constant agitations by protesters. All she focuses on is to earn her money and fulfill her greatest dream – buying the "Green Spark" shop lot and turning it into her own saloon. She has Red the Infected worker, [[Grani]] the Mounted Policewoman, [[Skyfire]] from the [[King's Wand]], and her boss [[Quercus]] as her close friends who could protect them from hostility. She strives to achieve financial independence so that one day she could bring her mother and her five siblings down to Caladon and settle there.

In the morning of November 23, 1097, the day has finally come when Susie could bring out her savings, £6,500, to purchase the building from the landlord. To her amazement, the landlord is none other than Bitter Root who comes along with her boss Quercus. Quercus elaborates that this shop lot has long become an R.I. property, yet Susie could purchase this building by simply signing their contract. Most importantly, Susie is amazed that her friends are congratulating her becoming the legal owner of "Green Spark." It feels surreal when an Infected could have a happy life simply through her own diligence.

But as the saying goes, "Fate plays cruel tricks on us." The next morning after her purchase, the "Green Spark" is engulfed by a great fire. Susie stands by watching her precious dream come into ruin mercilessly.

==Anywhere I Wander==
Since November 19, 1097, the city guards have been chasing after a runaway prisoner hiding in the city. It is [[Haze]], a [[Feline]] fugitive who is the sole surviving witch of the occultic "[[Witch Forest]]" that was forcefully disbanded by Victorian soldiers in the past. After her escape, she has been hiding together with an orphan Infected girl named Minnie inside an abandoned house, and the girl often takes care of her worsening [[Oripathy]] symptoms. Haze thanks Minnie's generosity, yet she deems that she is reaching her limit.

Around 10:14&nbsp;p.m., November 23, 1097, Haze tries to scavenge for food in an abandoned district, but she accidentally eavesdrops on a strange conservation. There are two men in black shirts plotting to kidnap several Infected and an old councilor. Haze tries to escape by casting her smoke [[Arts]], and she stumbles into a small manor for shelter, only to find an old [[Savra]]n man watching her in shock and suddenly collapses from heart unrest. Seeing that things are getting complicated, Haze quickly escapes from the manor, and by the time the black-suited men arrive, they find that she has been hiding inside the manor of Councilor [[Angst]]. After running around the streets, the men deem that Haze is hiding inside a certain pub, so they set it on fire to prevent her escape. And the pub engulfed by fire is none other than the "Green Spark."

Haze finally escapes from the black-suited men's pursuit, but her body is getting weaker and filled with heavy pain due to her worsening symptoms. Two days later on the 26th, Haze realizes that her time to die has finally arrived. Before her departure, Haze conducts a simple farewell ritual to Minnie, gives her a big black hat and her Arts-casting ring, and formally proclaims her as the 114th Witch of the Witch Forest.

After bidding farewell to the girl, Haze carries her pain while hiding inside an abandoned tower; she has prepared herself to quietly wait for her final moment. Just then, she hears loud cry from the top of the tower, and in front of her is Susie yearning to commit suicide by jumping off from the building. Susie laments that her precious dream is laid in waste, and Haze talks her out of her suicide intention by stating that she still has the chance to fulfil her dream even though it is luxurious for an Infected. She even compares herself with her when revealing her worsening symptoms.  Haze immediately collapses, and out of worry, Susie quickly carries her back to the city along with Red's help.

==We will all Go Together==
Around 9:45&nbsp;p.m., November 23, 1097, Skyfire is attending a banquet held by the city council attended by many Members of Parliements. As a representative of Councilor Angst, she is there to persuade others in accepting the "New Caladon Infected Act" drafted by him. On the other hand, she ruffles that those MPs never feel considerate to the Infected; rather, they seek to exploit the labor laws by turning every Infected community into a factory. She even declares that their neglection will one day bring havoc onto Caladon. Standing beside her, [[Kazemaru|Kite the Recorder, a.k.a. Operator Kazemaru,]] patiently listens to her bitter complains while watching her friend getting drunk after drinking countless cups of wine. 

The next morning after getting sober, Skyfire is shocked of the latest news around her. Angst suddenly fell into a heart attack after someone broke into his house, and he is currently in the hospital for treatment. She even could not imagine that an arsonist burnt down the "Green Spark" into ashes. Just then, Skyfire and the Infected workers hears a huge explosion nearby, and following it is a band of Infected mobs kidnapping them to an abandoned factory. 

At the emptied building, Skyfire tries to find an escape route for everyone. She then hears that those mobs are trying to implant bombs around the building to kill all the hostages inside. The mobs also target Councilor Angst, but his disappearance prompts them to immediately carry out their plan. Nevertheless, the mobs' tricks are nothing in front of Skyfire's fire Arts, and she quickly eradicates the explosives and apprehends all the mob, only to realize that these "Infected" mobs are actually hired actors planning to put the blame onto the Infected community; in fact, the "lesion" on their skin are actually fake. Out of rage, Skyfire casts her Arts onto the escaping kidnapers as her punishment, burning down the wicked preparators in the process.

After the kidnap incident, Skyfire elaborated the incident to Bitter Root at the R.I. office and reminds him to take high precautions. Upon finishing her account, the two immediately notices Susie and Red carrying Haze suffering from her Oripathy into the room.

==Lone Ranger==
Back on November 19, 1097, Grani was responsible for a burglary case at Count Bishmer's manor. The case seems to be a strange one as the thief escaped without a trace, and the only clue left is an abandoned tunnel around the Infected community. There have been rumors that people could hear somebody mumbling down beneath the city's underground structure, and the community once experienced a total blackout after their generator was intentionally destroyed. Grani firmly believes that these two incidents could be related to the burglar case, but she finds no progression since then, much to the guanrd's captain's disappointment.

Fast-forward after the arson at the "Green Spark," Grani stands in front of the ruin in disbelief. The firefighters officially conclude that the fire was caused by accident under the police's pressure, but nonetheless, they uncover some clues regarding the fire, a scrap of explosive, and they wish to investigate the arsonist. To Grani's horror, these shards are actually from a military-grade incendiary, and her suspicion on the local police only grows further.

Grani manages to trace the explosive back to a black market trader named Maund, but Maud proclaims that he is just a handyman selling the explosive in cartons to a [[Siracusa]]n brothers, the Dantons. They were produced from Count Bishmer's arsenal factory before shutting down, and they were later circulated in the black market of the Boils, a dirty district at the city's north being infamous for its heinous crimes. All of a sudden, Grani realizes that the Caladon police are actually covering up the matter. The guard's captain is a relative of Count Bishmer, and he keeps Grani from investigating the factory in the name of protecting his estate. Angrily, Grani denounces the captain by proclaiming that she, as a Mounted Police temporarily transferred by Caladon's city council, shares equal power as him, and she personally proceeds in arresting the Danton brothers.

November 28, 1097, Grani finally finds the Dantons at a pub in the Boils. The brothers brag that Grani is no match to the Boils' outlaws, but thanks to her [[Kuranta]] trait, she is able to match with the rascals in the pub, much to the Dantons' horror. As they escape, they seek shelter from an abandoned house, only to find Minnie expelling the intruders with the Arts of the Witch Forest. The brothers eventually hide inside an old house in the abandoned district, and Grani orders them to immediately surrender before she plunges her pike onto them. The brothers plan to retaliate with their only explosive left, but they immediately find out they are actually hiding inside a storehouse filled with boxes of explosives. Worst of all, the brothers accidentally spills a barrel of raw ketone onto the ground. 

Grani throws her pike onto the building, and all of a sudden, the building explodes. The blazing shockwaves  dazzles Caladon's night sky, brings collapse onto the underground plate, costs the lives of the criminal brothers instantly, and finally makes Grani to be utterly dumbfounded by such a "surprised" coincidence.

==Something's Gotta Give==
Between November 26 and 28, 1097, as usual, Quercus is at the city's borderlands, and she is performing her funeral ritual to the deceased Infected as the famous "Druid of Gododdin." She later meets up with Kazemaru to exchange intel regarding [[Londinium]]. The [[Grand Dukes of Victoria|Eight Grand Dukes]] have now mobilized their military forces and are currently laying siege onto the capital against the foreign occupants, the [[Military Commission of Kazdel]]. Both worry that the situation will escalate into a great crisis. proving a great challenge to R.I.'s next mission.

Kazemaru then brings up the news regarding arson on the "Green Spark," prompting Quercus to hurry back. She is glad that everyone is safe. Haze miraculously wakes up from her coma, and after learning the situation, she gives her account of how she was hunted down by two thugs resembling Siracusans and that they were the arsonists behind the tragedy. Upon hearing her account, Susie proceeds to report the case to the Caladon police.

Three hours have passed, yet Susie is still outdoor. Nevertheless, Skyfire returns to the office and angrily grumbles that the police just want to be by-standers of the kidnap incident. Bitter Root links the connection between their accounts. The place Haze encountered the thugs is near the closed arsenal factory of Count Bishmer, Caladon police is led by his nephew, and most importantly, the thugs' original target was actually Councilor Aungst according to Haze's words. He concludes that Count Bishmer and the Caladon guard's captain originally intended to hire the Siracusan thugs to assassinate Aungst due to their hatred against his pro-Infected policies, and after that, they would proceed to eradicate the local Infected community, but Haze's appearance caused the whole plan to be messed up, resulting in the arson at the "Green Spark" and Skyfire's kidnap. All of a sudden, they realize that Susie might have become the new victim, so everyone quickly leaves the room to search for her, and Bitter Root quickly informs Kazemaru to search for intel of possible hideouts, presuming that it might be the city's underground structure.

Susie wakes up, and she realizes that her hands are tied. She recalls that the guard's captain brings her somewhere else, and then he knocks her out. After encountering the guard's captain and Count Bishmer, she confirms to herself that both of them are the true mastermind behind her tragedy. The two of them angrily punches her to vent out their anger and blame her for the mess, and they proclaim that her friends will never reach her because the entrances are heavily guarded by their hired thugs from the Boils. Just then, the two wicked men receive reports of the thugs being defeated one by one. It turns out Quercus has uncovered the entrance, and those rascals are no match to the Arts of the "Druid of Gododdin." Explosions could be heard from above, so the duo run for their own lives. And just before the captain's guard is about to kill Susie before escaping, the ground trembles, and the collapsing rubbles instantly takes away the wicked guard's life. Susie lets herself loose from the ropes, and Red suddenly appears in the midst of the collapse to save her, but another wave of rumbling causes Susie and Count Bishmer to fall into a deep pit. Nonetheless, Red continues his pursuit onto the thugs, and by the time he surrounds them, he calls up his unexpected "friends" to aid him for the cleanup, revealing to be several [[Reunion]] fighters ambushing in the tunnel.

[[File:Out of Darkness to Light.png|thumb|A great loss in her dream and life, yet a lavish reward through tough friendship.]]
Susie and Count Bishmer found themselves trapped inside the pit. Nevertheless, Susie deems that this is the best opportunity to punish the count for his wrong-doings out of her strong vindication. She grabs the wicked count on his necks and releases her electric Arts, planning to end his life by simply electrocuting him. Fortunately, Quercus stops her from her rampage, stating that this man is not worth it for her to take away his dirty life. Susie turns onto her back and wails aloud in sorrow.

Two days later on November 30, 1097, the Caladon riot has been fully settled. The new Infected labor laws are now enacted, yet many anti-Infected noblemen strongly protest it and even write a joint letter to the [[Duke of Gododdin]] to strip off Angst's title. Nevertheless, Angst does not care about the complaint, for he is already on his way for retirement, and he elaborates his reason to enact it is because he takes heed of [[Janet Longfellow]]'s warning concerning increasing Originium industrial pollution. On the other side regarding the "Green Spark" gang, Susie receives a huge honorary certificate by the city council for being Caladon's heroine, but she receives no financial compensation because Count Bishmer's properties are all confiscated. On a positive side, however, Quercus comforts that at least the preparators have received their own judgment. Seeing that Susie has nowhere to go, Quercus then invites her to join R.I. in hopes to bring her see around the world. 

==Ring-a-Ding-Ding!==
December 21, 1097, almost a month after the Caladon riot. Red returns to a Reunion camp in the hostile Bleached Wasteland. As it turns out, Red is actually a Reunion combatant aiding the Caladonian Infected community for a long while. Under reorganization by [[Nine]] and [[Guard (NPC)|Guard]], the newly-reborn, underground Reunion has chosen a much milder route in fight for their rights, hence their support on the Caladonian Infected. Red further reveals that it was Count Bishmer who was responsible for the blackout in the community by bribing the security guard to destroy the generator, and they are lucky enough to have several Reunion technicians installed in the city. The new Reunion has long abandoned its violent past who is willing to seek cooperation with the non-Infected.

Now focusing on their daily lives, The Reunion combatants have recently purchased a VTOL from scrappers, but many of its parts are malfunctional. So, Guard has made a trading deal with the wandering odd merchant, [[Cannot Goodenough]]. Three days later, Guard, Red and some Reunion bodyguards meet him at their destination, but Cannot arrives empty-handed. Nevertheless, Cannot points out that their "goods" is arriving soon—surprisingly, being a convoy of trucks transporting Volvort Kochinski's mechanical parts. Guard protests that they have to raid those heavy-armored vehicles, yet Cannot says that it will be an easy peasy job. As the convoy enters the valley, they meet an ambush attack by a giant [[Metal Crab]] followed by a band of warriors of the [[Rusthammer]]s. The Reunion fighters draw out their weapons to defend themselves against those barbaric scavengers, but all of a sudden, a familiar voice appears on the Rusthammers' side, one who is none other than Gareth. With Gareth mediating both sides, they put down their hostility and bring the trophies back to the camp.

The mechanical parts prove to be effective to the VTOL as Cannot testifies. In the meantime, both members of Reunion and Rusthammers sit down and meet with their former comrades, including Gareth sharing good news to them of his wife Illa being pregnant recently. Nine is puzzled as to why a merchant like him wants to trade with both Reunion and the Rusthammers. Cannot responds that he will cooperate with anyone as long as it ensures the survival of civilization. Furthermore, he elaborates that an upcoming "Apocalypse" is on the horizon through the prophecy of an old [[Sami]] [[Snowpriest]], yet many [[Terra]]n great powers prefer to turn a deaf ear to his warning even though there are already increasing signs of the danger. He is also amazed of [[Near Light/Synopsis|R.I.'s move in]] [[Kazimierz]] after listening to Nine's account, and he comments that R.I. alone will not be able to ensure the survival; it will need aid by others in the future.
