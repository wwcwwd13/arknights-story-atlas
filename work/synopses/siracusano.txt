==Synopsis==
===Patto non Finito===
[[File:L'ombra del Passato.png|thumb|left|"It's time to fulfill the undue oath we made, Celinia Texas."]]
In a lonely night in [[Lungmen]]'s bustling downtown, [[Texas]] wakes up from the nightmare of her family's dreadful purge seven years ago. Beneath her feet, a dark shadow somehow communicates with her. It is [[Zaaro]], a wolf [[Beast Lord]] and the [[Siracusa]]n "Signore dei Lupi," who is here to call Texas back for her oath she made in order to survive the bloody purge onto her family by the Siracusan mafia; now, she has to return to assist his favorite "Fang." Seeing the girl getting trouble, [[Emperor]] approaches Zaaro and mocks him of his meaningless, childish proxy war played among the "Signori" for centuries. Nevertheless, Texas wishes to fulfill such promise after years of peace from the mafias' conflict. After granted a leave of absence by Emperor, she returns to Siracusa with the Wolf through the dark mist.

Meanwhile, far in the Siracusan city of [[Volsinii]], [[Sora]] is having an opera performance for the ''Compagnia dell'alba'' as her company wishes to expand their business there. [[Exusiai]] and [[Croissant]] followed suit by being her bodyguards. Upon their arrival, the girls receive warm welcome by the leader of the troupe, [[Bernardo Bellone]]. The [[Penguin Logistics]] girls also meet [[Penance|Lavinia Falcone]], the local judge of the city, who helps them resolve a small misunderstanding with the local mafiosi, but she gives them a kind warning of not to meddle the famiglie's affairs too much. This is because Lavinia has been under the cover of Bernardo, who is secretly the boss of the powerful Bellone ''famiglia'', and she is tired of the never-ending sabotages among the mafias. Nevertheless, the P.L. members ignore much of those conflicts but hurry themselves for the upcoming play ''La Morte di Texas''. They even befriend [[Giovanna Rossati|Caterina]], the author of the play, who will later assist them in the rehearsal while helping her to complete the story.

[[File:Cellinia, il Omertosa dei Texas.png|thumb|Texas the mafioso or Texas the deliveryman, now it no longer matters.]]
On Texas' side, she follows Zaaro's instruction to travel to Volsinii and meet with [[Vigil|Leontuzzo Bellone]], the chosen heir of the Bellone ''famiglia''. He wishes to use her in gaining political control over the newly-built mobile platform, the Cittanova, that will soon be separated into an administrative new [[nomadic city]]. Following its completion is a deep political struggle between the ''Dodici Famiglie'' behind the scene, especially between the three leading ''famiglie'', the Bellones, the Saluzzoes, and the Rossati. As a government servant of Volsinii, he must not miss this opportunity to solidify the Bellone's leadership in the Grey Hall, but Texas too must be prepared to get herself into the ''famiglie'''s messy problems. Even then, Leontuzzo detest dirty methods but prefers to gain the trust of others through diplomacy. So, he orders Texas to be the bodyguard of Carracci, the current ''{{Tip|Ministero dei Lavori Pubblici|Minister of Public Works}}'' of Volsinni, during a banquet and protect him from any assassination attempt which, in turn, will earn his trust.

Among all the ''famiglie'' involved, the Saluzzoes are the greediest of all. Seeing this ''famiglia'' being overshadowed by the Bellones and the Rossati, the boss [[Alberto Saluzzo|Alberto]] vows to gain control of the Cittanova. However, his plan is messed up by [[Lappland]], the his daughter of the Saluzzoes, along with her newly recruited minions [[Capone]] and [[Gambino]]. She even dares to approach her father and openly declares war onto him as payback for her banishment from the ''famiglia'' seven years ago.

===Una Magnifica Opera===
The banquet is about to start, but following it is an approaching storm that will whirl up all the mafias. Carracci was assassinated by a sudden car bomb before his arrival, and Texas, Leontuzzo, and the others meet a sudden ambush. The assassins also targeted other ''famiglie'' members and politicians such as [[Wallach]] of the Rossati, and [[Rubio]], a government servant who is a neutralist in the mafias' matters. Although the incident is soon suppressed, the mafias are responsible for the investigation. Lavinia even arrests Texas to protect her from being a pawn by others.

Later investigations reveals that the plot is done neither by the Saluzzoes nor the Rossati. Even Lappland admits that she and her fellow minions did not plot Carracci's death, and she coincidentally witnessed the incident while having a brief chat with Texas. All clues links to one fact: there are traitors within the Bellones. Indeed, [[Demetri Certaldo|Demetri]], the Consigliere of the ''famiglia'', even attempts to assassinate Lavinia. The incident causes a division between Leontuzzo and Lavinia; the former wishes to settle it peacefully while the latter vows to arrest any perpetrator, including even the Bellones, to compensate for Carracci's death. On the other hand, Texas comes up with a better idea: she will take the blame for the crime so that the other ''famiglie'' will turn their attention on this last Texas.

[[File:Infrangere il Processo.png|thumb|left|"Surprise, dear judge! Here comes Siracusa's GREAT TRUCK!!"]]
As planned, Texas will attend the court with Lavinia being the judge. Everyone in the jury is shocked by the presence of the last Texas. At the same time, Leontuzzo uses this opportunity to bait out Demetri, the true conspirator. From Demetri's words, his final target is actually [[Signora Sicilia]], the most powerful ''Capo dei Capi'' of Siracusa, whose attention will be seized by igniting conflicts among the mafias. With that said, he orders his newly allied helpers, Lappland, Capone, and Gambino, to create havoc in the court, a total blasphemy to the Signora's order. Capone and Gambino crash into the building with a giant truck, and then Lappland surrenders herself to the judge by claiming herself to be the real murderer behind Carracci's death, creating confusion among in the court.

The conflict has become even more complicated than ever. Leontuzzo learns from his father Bernardo that Demetri is not betraying the ''famiglie'' but actually following his orders. Not only did he reveal much of the family's secret to his son by bringing him to meet Zaaro the legendary "Signore," but he also tells him the family's ultimate goal of overthrowing the Signora and crowning the Wolf to be the "Alpha" of Siracusa. To weaken the Bellones' power, Alberto quietly acknowledges Lappland's actions, but he still despises his maniacal daughter.

On the other hand, Bernardo and Alberto decide to put down their disagreement to take down their third competitor, the Rossati, before they could share their power in the new city. So, Bernardo orders Texas to assassinate the Rossati's head, Giovanna, during an opera in exchange for her total freedom from Zaaro's oath. The mission brings doubt to Texas since she and Giovanna are close childhood friends, but she has to accomplish it nonetheless. On the Saluzzoes' site, Alberto rehires [[Danbrown Leopardi|Danbrown]] the notorious hitman to take part of this mission. At the same time, Giovanna, a.k.a Caterina, bravely accepts this suspicious invitation to counter-attack the other two families and bring them to the Signora's justice. After all, this family of Columbian origin has to humble themselves to the Signora to avoid the Texases' fall.

===L'Ultima Parola===
[[File:Il Palcoscenico e Pronto.png|thumb|Amidst this lavish opera, no one realizes that blood will soon be unveiling its curtains]]
Now, with all factions gathering up in the opera hall and eyeing Giovanna, The P.L. members who attended the show are surprised to see Texas' appearance in secret. With the stage being set, the families are ready to prey on each other starting from a sudden explosion on the stage. Even Lappland takes part in this chaos in the name of assisting both Texas and her father. The Rossati are not simple opponents thanks to Wallach.

[[File:We Are Penguin Logistics.png|thumb|left|"Texas, no matter where you are, Penguin Logistics will always be on your side!"]]
Amidst the chaos, Giovanna and Texas finally meet each other after years of separation in Columbia, but they seem to be unfriendly to each other. Giovanna persuades Texas to join the Rossati and bring back the zenith of the Columbian mafia, but Texas wishes to free herself from the ''famiglie'''s matters after this mission. A fight breaks out between the two eventually and Texas manages to escape from the Rossati's pursuit after reuniting with the P.L. girls with the aid of [[Agenir|a mysterious Sankta priest]].

The conflict eventually ends with solely Bernardo taking the victory. Not only does Rubio breaks his neutrality by volunteering to be the Bellones' puppet in taking Carracci's place, but he also seeks cooperation with the Rossati, trusting that their potential and unique origin could help overthrow the Signora's rule. He also allows both Texas and Sora to be free from the service to the Bellones. Nevertheless, not everyone is happy with the result. Seeing Giovanna being too weak and too conservative to lead the ''famiglia'', Wallach and other Rossati members betray her with a deadly duel between him and her. Giovana almost loses her life had not Sora and Texas encountered the duel.

The next day, it is the inauguration ceremony for Rubio to take the office. His presence becomes a target among the families, especially the Saluzzoes. After having Lavinia to protect her daughter at home, Rubio bravely stays in his office and broadcast his message to the public. In his speech, he speaks of his loathing of the mafia tradition that has been corrupting the nation, while the mafias themselves are getting weaker and more cowardly as time passes. Not only does he admire the political neutrality of his deceased close friend Carracci, but he also dares to accuse the Bellones and Saluzzoes of challenging the Signora's order through the competition for the Cittanova. Hearing Rubio's words threatening his position, Alberto sends Danbrown to kill him. But Rubio has already prepared his death. After asking for an honest answer from the hitman about whether he earnestly wants to serve the ''famiglie'', he commits suicide by firing with a pistol onto his temple while the public broadcast is still on.

===Pistole e Ordine===
Rubio's suicide totally shakes the order of the Volsinii mafia and a deadly fight among the mafias breaks out in Volsinii's streets. With his father suddenly gone missing, Leontuzzo decides to confront Alberto alone to carry on the power struggle. Shaken by Rubio's speech, even Danbrown turns his weapon against his boss as he deems the mafia of this generation to be too weak for him to serve. On the other hand, Alberto is not an easy victim and uses his agility to kill Danbrown. Then he orders Leontuzzo to make a choice within ten seconds: either to be the Saluzzoes' subordinate or to die. 

But before Leontuzzo answers, a short earthquake suddenly occurs in the city. Bernardo hijacks the engines of the Cittanova to force its separation from Volsinii which will further accelerate the rebellion against the Signora. Soon, Leontuzzo meets his father inside a local church. The lad persuades his father to stop such a meaningless fight; not only does it completely stray away from his own goal, but also it will never fulfill what Bernardo wishes to see—a Siracusa without the mafias. Nevertheless, Bernardo is in awe of his son's maturity and he lets his son stop the rampaging city. At the same time, Leontuzzo is not alone either: Lavinia leads her judiciary officers to calm the riot, and even the P.L. girls decide to lend them their help, a sign which Texas shows to the mafiosi that she will never ever let herself escape from her past. 

[[File:Emperor and il Signori dei Lupi.png|thumb|"Shut the f**k up, it's you who have lost the game, Zaaro!"]]
While the fight between the mafiosi is ongoing, the "proxy war" among the Signori's "Fangs" is reaching its climax. Zaaro mocks Bernardo for freeing himself from his role, but is shocked to see the man committing suicide by taking poison. Meanwhile, other "Fangs" such as [[Lunacub]] are gathering up in the city to judge the Wolf for crossing their ultimate red line in the war: never interfere in human affairs. Furiously, the Zaaro casts his wrath onto the gang to prevent them from getting towards the control tower. Just then, Emperor suddenly appears in the street while leading a pack of Wolf Beast Lords, the other "Signori dei Lupi," to take down Zaaro. Seeing himself being completely outmatched, the proud Beast Lord finally bows his head and vanishes into the air.

[[File:La Banda dei Quattro.png|thumb|left|To the Signora, all she sees are four trustworthy forerunners trying to change the very dark system they are living in.]]
Meanwhile, the Signora personally arrives at Volsinii to fix the chaos after listening to the report of Agenir, the [[Sankta]] pastor and her Consigliere. When the dust settles, she summons Texas, Lappland, Leontuzzo, and Lavinia to explain the incident. Even then, the gentle lady is willing to listen to Leontuzzo's mission to reform Siracusa's very political system and the Signora agrees that it is time for the country to move on to a new phase. At the same time, she admires Lappland's stubborn bravery, accepts Texas' wish to spares the Rosatti from her punishment, and finally puts her trust onto Lavinia to change the current judicial system. As the guardian of the old world, the Signora is happy to see the uniqueness of this new generation.

After the Volsinii incident, the Cittanova will be the first Siracusan city to be completely free from the mafias' influence as the Signora promised to Leontuzzo. As the Bellones have stopped existing following Bernardo's death, Demetri and the other mafiosi bid a bitter farewell to Leontuzzo and move on to create their own ''famiglia''. Giovanna recovers from her injuries, and she wishes Texas good luck with her newly written ending for ''La Morte di Texas''. Penguin Logistics finally sets its foot in Siracusa for the first time thanks to Texas and Sora's performance, but their party continues as usual.

[[File:Blood For Blood.png|thumb|The lone wanderer finally returns home, but all she sees is a sacrifice to an unfinished war.]]
Still, the ancient "proxy war" among the "Signori dei Lupi" continues. After [[Reunion]]'s downfall and wandering in the wilderness for three years, the weary [[Crownslayer]] finally reaches her mentor's home, her only safe haven. But she finds her mentor murdered by [[Projekt Red]] the Wolf Hunter. Meanwhile, Lappland encounters Zaaro in the wilderness after leaving Siracusa and the two have a bitter cat-and-mouse game for three months. Zaaro eventually humbles himself to Lappland and seemingly turns her into his new "Fang."
