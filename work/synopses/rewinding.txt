==Homecoming==
[[Surtr]] and [[Meteorite]] arrive at the nomadic village of Bellony, located at the outskirts of [[Kazdel]], to help clear debris from a collapsed cave tunnel blocking the village's travel route. While on duty, Surtr uses the opportunity to find the truth within the memories. Though Meteorite finds it difficult to get along with her, she understands Surtr's actions.

The two girls and the village chief arrive at the tunnel. The cave is too dangerous to be excavated due to the presence of Originium, yet Surtr impatiently enters the caves with her greatsword, the Laevateinn. As she walks inside the tunnel, a flashback plagues her mind: she sees two children holding hands entering the cave, and encounters centuries-old ancient city ruins. Nevertheless, she proceeds to search for the cave-in, only to find several infesting nests of [[Originium Slug]]s. She clears the nests with her greatsword, eradicating the threat to the villagers.

The village chief thank them for their work and asks them to spend a night as guests of honor, but Surtr rejects the offer as they needed to bring their data back to Rhodes Island. She asks for an ice cream.

==Expatriates==
Arene brings his staff to Workshop No.3 to get a weapon reliability certification, but the craftsmen are not present due to an ongoing contest. Arene requests an apprentice with a lopsided halo to do a simple check-up, and the [[Sankta]] apprentice, revealed to be [[Adnachiel]], agrees. As the two carefully process the maintenance, they have a chat about the craftsmen of the workshop, such as Iron Hammer who does maintenance on staves, and Gunject who refuses to repair patrol firearms but only repairs swords. Their discussion shifts to [[Laterano]] as they are both Infected and no longer welcomed by Laterano, despite retaining citizenship. Arene appears to dislike sweets and prefers his staff over guns, while Adnachiel shares the typical Sankta traits despite having a different halo and lacking a gun certification.

The chat is interrupted by [[Ambriel]] who brings her rifle for a simple check-up as well. She complains about [[Executor]] from the Notarial Hall who keeps asking her to fulfill her Lateran obligations. Lunch break is arriving, and the trio disperse after Adnachiel finishes his inspections. Arene returns to the [[Doctor]], who sees through the falsified signatures. Arene promises to get the real signatures as he now thinks spending a little more time is worth it, having met a lot of interesting people.

==Astray==
The Mid-Autumn Festival celebration on the Rhodes Island landship is drawing nigh. [[Purestream]] asks [[Elysium]] to spread the word to operators from [[Yan]] and [[Lungmen]], then for him and [[Thorns]] to come to the festival. [[Absinthe]] joins the conversation, hoping to request Thorns for her staff's maintenance. Seeing the funny reaction between Thorns and Elysium, Purestream informs Absinthe that the two are from Iberia. Elysium describes it as a boring place, while Thorns notes that it is unfriendly to [[Aegir (race)|Aegirs]] because of the Inquisition. The topic shifts to Elysium’s captain, [[Mantra]]. Purestream reminds the other three that they are welcome to join the celebration for the festival, promising they won’t be disappointed. After Purestream and Absinthe leave the room, Thorns asks Elysium whether boring and depressing is all he can say about Iberia. Elysium wonders whether they will ever have a chance to return to Iberia, musing that perhaps he [[Stultifera Navis|will return there someday]].

==Recollection==
Following the aftermath of [[Twilight of Wolumonde/Synopsis|Wolumonde's riot]], [[Mudrock]] leads her squad and the surviving [[Infected]] to find a new settlement. She decides to bring them to [[Kazdel]], where they can find their new home.

However, during the long journey, they are hunted down by a group of heinous casters, forcing them to seek shelter in the foggy woods. These casters can turn the Infected into Arts Units, and countless [[Sarkaz]] warriors and Infected refugees fall to the casters day after day. They deduce that the casters are likely using them as experiments. Mudrock leads them to a nearby village on the outskirts of Kazdel. Fog appears again, signaling the casters’ presence. Mudrock instructs them to go to the village while she resolves to stay and defend the bridge. Most of her loyal men decide to stay with her to fight.

Not far away, [[Logos]] and a band of Rhodes Island operators are carefully observing the situation. Logos laments that the lunatics from the [[Echoes of Herkunftshorn]] are still studying the Arts of [[Witch King|Herkunftshorn the Witch King]] despite the twenty years that have passed since the ascension of the [[Twin Empresses]]. He claims that Rhodes Island does not permit the desecration of Infected lives and finally interferes, destroying one of Mudrock’s colossi and clearing the fog. Mudrock is surprised to see a powerful and young Sarkaz, and Logos introduces himself.

Days have passed since the Mudrock squad encountered Logos, and Mudrock writes a second letter to [[Big Bob]] after boarding the landship and hearing the news about him from [[Grani]]. She wishes for him to live on well, ashamed that she could not find a home for her people like he did, and hopes to meet him in [[Columbia]] one day.

==A Song Called April==
After finishing a mission, April bumps into Ansel while the latter was talking with a messenger. Seeing as they were both [[Rim Billiton]]ian, the two happily introduce themselves and talk more about their hometowns in the cafeteria.

After having their lunch, Ansel continues the topic. He talks about his huge family, a common feature in Rim Billitonian households, and his life in Iron Carrot but Ansel has to take the family's burden after the passing of his parents. He further talks about his experience to become a full-time doctor despite objections from his uncles. April too introduces her background by recalling her past job in a mining corporation and describing the gloomy surrounding of her hometown, the Iron Fist City, but she is optimistic to have a chance to explore the outside world where she learns of pop culture, hence her codename "April" which was inspired by a song. 

After the lunch break, April approaches a Messenger heading for Rim Billiton. She requests him to take a photo from a tower in Rim Billiton so that she can remember her hometown.

==Crisscrossing Shadows==
The story starts from the perspective of Operator [[Phantom]] narrating his past in the form of stage play. While he suffers from memory disorder, he 
has some that he remembers deeply: he started out as a member of the [[Crimson Troupe]] since his childhood and has been trained to be a stage player, but the dark side of the Troupe forced him to conduct a massacre onto the troupe members. Nevertheless, the souls of the troupe members lamented his betrayal and sorrowfully questioned his action that night on the stage. Phantom gives no responds but yearns to find his true self.

Back in the present, the [[Doctor]], Operator [[Mint]], and the staff of the Human Resource Department arrive at Phantom's room for some inquiries. They have been puzzled of Phantom's background; all he gives is the name of a place called "[[Calais-Blason]]" which the staff could not locate. The staff member is angry about him giving false information which is against R.I.'s policies, but Phantom reassures that all the information he shares are genuine. Mint then suggests to have her investigate the place's name since she is familiar with [[Victoria]]'s historical records. Phantom's peculiar actions causes the staff to mumble to the Doctor that whether Phantom actually suffers from mental disorder.

After the inquiry, Mint proceeds to search for the history of Calais-Blason. She accidentally stumbles upon a [[Shalem|Pythian man]] who knows the place's name as well as acquaints with Phantom as an ex-member of the Crimson Troupe. Mint is surprised to know the place being a former [[Gaul]]ish town that has vanished into history, and the Pythian man continues his tales of past. His tale will pave way to [[Phantom & Crimson Solitaire/Synopsis|a rather peculiar misadventure in the future...]]

==Stories from the Sky==
It is the day of the Mid-Autumn Festival. [[Shamare]] has a conversation with Morte about the emotions on Rhodes Island before Morte runs off to get apples. [[Suzuran]] approaches her while she is writing, and they decide to share a table. Suzuran brings a children’s storybook, and she talks about the legend of the Dusk Beauty who flew to the twin moons to find her husband with the help of the gods of Mount T'ien, hence giving rise to a traditional Yanese festival. [[Mousse]] and [[Popukar]] join the table in the middle of the story. Suzuran remembers that today is also the day of a festival in [[Higashi]], where her father will be busy in his shrine. So, she proceeds to narrate the Higashinese legend of Nito-jo, in which three brothers search for an everlasting treasure to court a woman, but it ends in tragedy: two brothers fight each other in a war, while the sky woman disappears, and the third brother becomes a wandering ghost.

Mousse joins the story-telling. She shares a Victorian fairytale about two siblings, a prince and a princess, searching for the "Divine Originium" to save the ill king. They found a nest filled with treasure and fought a monster until the brother passed out. The monster asked them at different times why they entered its nest and they gave the same answer. But the brother changed his mind and stole the treasure, while the "Divine Originium" the princess brought turned the king into a monster. The princess was executed for her mistake, while the greedy brother lived his life in luxury in another country. The unfair ending upsets the listeners.

After a moment of silence, Shamare shares the [[Siracusa]]n tale of the She-wolf. Long ago, the She-wolf gave birth to the Six Tribes dwelling in the Seven Hills, but they fought against each other for food. Not wanting to fight her children, she ascended and became the Moonshadow. The tribes came to their senses and exiled the worst of them, then divided into six families, each taking one hill, with the last dedicated to the She-wolf. This is the origin of the Siracusan ''famiglie'' and the city of Sette Colli.

Next is Popukar, telling a [[Minos|Minoan]] tale she heard from [[Orchid]] about a [[Forte]] inventor who molded a wax figure to be his bride, only to be imprisoned by an envious king. But after the inventor was set free, he learned that the king and his army died from the melted wax of the bride. The inventor was crowned the next king and brought prosperity to the country, yet he shut himself inside a room to recreate his bride to no avail. The king eventually died of old age, and the kind people buried him in the garden with all his wax figures.

Orchid appears, looking for Popukar as it is her bedtime. One by one, the girls bid goodnight and head back to their rooms. Suzuran forgets to bring back her storybook. Morte picks it up and Shamare plans to give the book back after embedding it with her Arts. When asked about the reason behind the sad endings of Terra's fairytales, Shamare tells Morte to think about their world, their food, and where they used to live.
