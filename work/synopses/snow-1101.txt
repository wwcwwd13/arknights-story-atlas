==Synopsis==
===The Catastrophe===
It is December 1101. It had just been an ordinary winter's day in [[Kjerag]]. High atop [[Mount Karlan]], [[Pramanix|Enya]] the Saintess looks after the dying [[Great Elder]]. The doctors have tried for years to purge the poison from his body, but to no avail. In his final moments, the Great Elder feverishly prophesied that calamity would soon befall Kjerag and that [[Kjeragandr]] would abandon her children.<ref name="ST1">[[OS-ST-1]]</ref>

[[File:67 i02.png|thumb|left|Snow poured down the slopes, mixed with rocks and black crystals, and all creation was obscured by gray and white.]]
Tourists come and go around the train station when someone screams about a bomb. As the crowds try to flee, a massive avalanche suddenly plows through the station, obliterating the building and killing dozens of civilians.<ref name="ST1"/> The [[Kjerag/Overview#Tri-Clan_Council|Tri-Clans]] and [[Rhodes Island]]'s local medical team were swift to arrive with emergency aid, clearing up debris and rescuing trapped survivors. However, the locals soon discover shards of black [[Originium]] mixed in with the snow, revealing that it was no ordinary avalanche. Kjerag has been hit by the first [[Catastrophe]] it has ever experienced in a millennium.<ref name="1Bef">[[OS-1/Story#Before operation|OS-1 Before]]</ref>

The [[Vine-Bead Court]] is in an uproar about the incident, as there has never been any such prophecy about Catastrophes in their scriptures. Some of the clerics even believe that the Catastrophe is a sign of Kjeragandr's wrath for allowing [[Rhine Lab]] to "defile" Mount Karlan by building an observation station there. As Rhine Lab is at the forefront of scientific research, [[Gnosis|Gnosis Edelweiss]] and his men quickly head to the station to demand answers about the Catastrophe. The station leader, [[Astgenne the Lightchaser|Elena Urbica]], prepares to fetch a copy of their data when she discovers evidence of tampering just a day before the Catastrophe occurred. The one on observation duty at the time was a researcher named [[Carolin]], who was reportedly sighted at a restricted military zone in Kjerag and has since disappeared.<ref>[[OS-1/Story#After operation|OS-1 After]]</ref>

In wake of the crisis, angry locals protest in front of the observation station and host rallies across the country. Ambassadors from both Columbia and Victoria arrive to consult with [[SilverAsh|Enciodes Silverash]], who is in charge of Kjerag's foreign affairs. The Columbian ambassador expresses deep concerns about the verbal abuse against Rhine's researchers. Meanwhile, the Victorian ambassador offers a generous amount of humanitarian assistance, with [[Harold|Viscount Harold Craigavon]] and his men already working on distributing goods and supplies.<ref>[[OS-2/Story#Before operation|OS-2 Before]]</ref> Neither [[Arctosz Paleroche]] nor [[Ratatos Browntail]] are pleased with Enciodes accepting aid from the Victorians though, as they are aware that Victoria's kindness is never without reason.<ref name="2Aft">[[OS-2/Story#After operation|OS-2 After]]</ref>

[[File:67 i03.png|thumb|Without Kjeragandr, how will they survive their coldest winter?]]
Enya soon discovers that her head maid, [[Kjera|Kjarr]]—whom she knows to be the proxy of Kjeragandr—has suddenly disappeared.<ref name="1Bef"/> As she studies the scriptures for clues, the newly appointed Great Elder, [[Adso Browntail]], offers a possible solution for the turmoil in Kjerag. He advises her to go on a Pilgrimage, a holy ritual where the Saintess walks around Kjerag on foot, praying for Kjeragandr's forgiveness. Although usually held in spring or autumn, carrying out a Pilgrimage would hopefully ease the people's worries and restore their faith. For several days, she travels across the many villages of Kjerag's snowy landscape before making her way towards the mountains. Little did she realise, much more dangerous perils awaited ahead of her...<ref name="2Aft"/>

===Fracture===
One morning, the envoys of the Vine-Bead Court requests Arctosz and Enciodes to come for an urgent meeting. As Enciodes happens to be away at the time, his younger sister [[Cliffheart|Ensia]] takes his place. On the way there, Arctosz's car is nearly destroyed by a rockslide, badly injuring his subordinate [[Gulo]]. Around the same time, a car rams straight into Ensia's vehicle.<ref name="3Bef">[[OS-3/Story#Before operation|OS-3 Before]]</ref> Although both sides managed to survive the ordeal, it was a clear that it was an assassination attempt against the clan heads. Arctosz accuses Ratatos of being behind it, as the Browntails were not attacked, but Enciodes manages to calm him down. Enciodes suspects the Victorians and worries they could also be planning to target Enya, as the Saintess holds religious authority over the country. He instructs [[Degenbrecher]] to track her down and protect her.<ref name="3Aft">[[OS-3/Story#After operation|OS-3 After]]</ref>

[[File:67 i04.png|thumb|left|Under these turbulent times, the two siblings must do what they can to help the people.]]
Enya walks around the forest when an arrow suddenly pierces her leg. Fortunately, she is saved by a local huntress named [[Snow Hunter|Joanna]], who manages to kill the assailant and bring her to safety in her home. Joanna is initially hostile towards Enya, as she holds deep resentment towards Clan Silverash for their brazen redevelopment plans which have damaged the local environment. However, upon discovering that Enya was actually the Saintess, Joanna suddenly gets mixed feelings due to her religious faith. Regardless, she promises to protect the Saintess with her life. Later that night, the two are attacked again by Victorian spies disguised in Paleroche garb, with Degenbrecher intervening just in the nick of time.<ref name="3Bef"/><ref name="3Aft"/><ref name="4Bef">[[OS-4/Story#Before operation|OS-4 Before]]</ref>

Meanwhile, Ratatos decides to meet in secret with the Victorian ambassador and strikes a deal with him. In exchange for the safety and prosperity of Clan Browntail, she will launch a hostile takeover of [[Karlan Trade]], granting Victoria a wider berth in business negotiations. She designates a time and place for a final meeting with Arctosz, and hopes for Victoria to demonstrate their "good faith". Arcotsz is naturally suspicious when he receives the invite, and spares no expense is preparing for the worst. However, it turns out that the assassination plot was a lure for the Victorians, and Ratatos had deployed guards to protect Arctosz in secret. Both the Browntails and Paleroches successfully capture a large number of Victorian spies.<ref name="4Bef"/><ref name="4Aft">[[OS-4/Story#After operation|OS-4 After]]</ref>

Just when Arctosz thinks that the matter is settled, the meeting location suddenly explodes in flames. The only remains of the once indefatigable matriarch of Clan Browntail is a single, charred jacket.<ref name="4Aft"/>

===Science and Politics===
[[File:67 i01.png|thumb|That meeting from a decade ago was the beginning of an opportunity, and a long, silent war.]]
The next morning, dozens of citizens gather at the Vine-Bead Court to hold a funeral for Ratatos. With her gone, the affairs of Clan Browntail fall onto the shoulders of her younger sister, [[Sciurus Browntail]]. The new clan head meets with Arctosz and Matterhorn to discuss how to handle the captured spies. Just then, a court envoy named Father Frey requests that the Tri-Clans surrender custody of the spies to the Vine-Bead Court. Despite objections from the other two, Sciurus assents to the request. Elsewhere, Enciodes has received a letter from the [[Duke of Caster]] herself, who proposes further industrial collaboration in exchange for access to Kjerag's mines. There is no doubt in Enciodes' mind that the duke is using the crisis to put extra pressure on Kjerag's administration.<ref>[[OS-ST-2]]</ref>

At the Kjerag Observation Station, Gnosis works together with [[Silence the Paradigmatic|Olivia Silence]] in thoroughly investigating the missing researcher Carolin's employment history and activities. Meanwhile, Elena desperately tries to recover the missing data but to no avail. After taking a snack break with [[Muelsyse]], she suddenly gets inspiration to look into the surveillance audio recordings. By listening to the sounds made by the machines, Elena is able to decipher what the equipment recorded. The results are astonishing—the data reveals that a day before the Catastrophe, some sort of extraterrestrial object fell through the hole in the Starpod and crashed onto Mount Karlan.<ref name="5Bef">[[OS-5/Story#Before operation|OS-5 Before]]</ref>

Believing that Carolin could have left in search of this foreign object, Elena sets out to prove her theory. She sneaks into the Vine-Bead Court to find a map of Kjerag when she is discovered by Enya and Joanna. When asked about her motives, Elena explains her theory about why Kjerag has never been struck by Catastrophe. She believes that there is a hidden Originium vein surrounding Kjerag, which has created an energy field that prevents Catastrophes from forming. Its energy flow was disrupted when something crashed into it, thus causing the Catastrophe. Although Enya finds it difficult to believe, she nonetheless promises to help Elena in getting to the truth. Elena uses the map to calculate the exact location where the object must have fallen: the Kjerag-Kazimierz mines.<ref name="5Aft">[[OS-5/Story#After operation|OS-5 After]]</ref>

[[File:67 i06.png|thumb|left|All sorts of emotions roil across the unarmed crowd, but they have no effect on the armed soldiers across from them.]]
However, the trio aren't the only ones who have their eyes on the mines. Behind the scenes, the Duke of Caster has quietly purchased the rights to the mine from [[Kazimierz]], as her intelligence believes that it could be the location of Kjerag's hidden military arsenal. The Victorians have also enlisted the help of the Vine-Bead Court to use the mine's railways to smuggle Carolin out of the country, as the duke is incredibly interested in her report about "Kjerag's protection disappearing". Although the handover procedures were still underway, the Victorians insist on conducting an inspection, and the Vine-Bead Court pressures Karlan Trade to comply. They conduct a thorough search of the mines under the supervision of Gnosis and Matterhorn, yet were unable to find sufficient evidence of a proper military arsenal.<ref name="6Bef">[[OS-6/Story#Before operation|OS-6 Before]]</ref><ref name="6Aft">[[OS-6/Story#After operation|OS-6 After]]</ref>

As Enya, Joanna, and Elena make their way towards the mines, Elena's equipment produces an alarming message: a second Catastrophe is brewing.

===Upheaval in Court===
With the mine taken over by Victoria, the Vine-Bead Court accuses Enciodes of illegal collusion with a foreign power and demand that he surrender himself for interrogation. Enciodes is well aware of the Vine-Bead Court's true intentions and arrives together with his elite army of [[Tschäggättä]]. They surround the complex, blocking all exits, ensuring that none of the clerics would be able to escape. Enciodes in turn accuses Great Elder Adso of betraying Kjerag to Victoria as part of his scheme to expel Karlan Trade and the Silverash Clan.<ref name="7Bef">[[OS-7/Story#Before operation|OS-7 Before]]</ref>

[[File:67 i16.png|thumb|left|"Silverash, do you think you've won? I wonder, just how long will your arbitrary reign of terror last?"]]
In truth, Adso is the younger cousin of the previous Clan Browntail patriarch, Luca Browntail. Sharing much of his cousin's conservative beliefs, he proclaims that Enciodes has brought too much radical change to Kjerag, and that he is simply trying to pull back the reins of change. Adso even admits that he was the one who orchestrated the train accident which killed Enciodes' parents, Olafur and Elizabeth Silverash. As Adso tries to goad Enciodes into killing him for revenge, Degenbrecher enters the hall with Father Frey in tow. Gnosis and Matterhorn had found Father Frey at the mine railways, revealing that he was the one who helped the Victorians smuggle Carolin out of the country. With undeniable proof of Adso's guilt, Enciodes orders his Tschäggättä to take him away.<ref name="7Bef"/><ref name="7Aft">[[OS-7/Story#After operation|OS-7 After]]</ref>

Enya, Joanna, and Elena enter the mines via an abandoned mining shaft. However, inside the mines is not Originium, but [[Oriron]]. Although Enya and Elena are disheartened, Joanna is still eager to keep searching, and asks Enya to use her bell to call for Kjeragandr again. Enya rings her bell, and Elena is surprised that the resulting frequency matches the one recorded in her observation station's data. Based on this new information, the group deduce that Mount Karlan must be like a giant reveberating bell, with a massive hollow somewhere in the mountain. Recalling an old rumor about Kjerag's history, Enya leads them towards summit of Mount Karlan.<ref name="7Aft"/>

[[File:67 i05.png|thumb|The fleet that once drove the Sarkaz out of Londinium has now arrived to besiege Kjerag.]]
Faced with extreme amounts of public pressure, Enciodes decides that the best course of action would be to resign from CEO of Karlan Trade. As part of their plan, Gnosis takes over as his position and makes a bold announcement: Karlan Trade will be immediately cease all collaboration with Victoria. The backlash from Victoria is swift and decisive. A public letter slandering Enciodes and Karlan Trade rapidly circulates around Kjerag, deepening public mistrust and sowing further confusion. Not only that, but the Duke of Caster has decided that she will personally come to Kjerag. In the name of military intervention, the duke has brought with her a fleet of steel battleships, ready to fire at a moment's notice.<ref name="8Aft">[[OS-8/Story#After operation|OS-8 After]]</ref><ref name="ST3">[[OS-ST-3]]</ref>

The Duke of Caster gives an ultimatum: if Enciodes does not respond within six hours, or still refuses to cooperate, then things will only end in a rather "regretful" manner.<ref name="9Bef">[[OS-9/Story#Before operation|OS-9 Before]]</ref>

===O Beloved Kjeragandr===
Amidst the unrest in the city, a Victorian reporter named [[Character/NPC-Minor#Alberta|Alberta]] suddenly finds herself kidnapped. When she wakes up, she finds herself facing the supposedly deceased Ratatos Browntail, who had actually faked her death to fool the Victorians. Ratatos accuses Alberta of being another Victorian spy, but contrary to her expectations, Alberta is a genuinely honest reporter who has been trying to protect her materials from being edited by the Duke of Caster's spies. Just then, several [[Trilby Asher]]s try to break into the building, but Degenbrecher manages to stop them. The group retreat to the Vine-Bead Court. The Duke of Caster tries to spin the narrative by saying that Kjerag is keeping Alberta as a hostage, giving them another excuse to invade Kjerag.<ref name="8Bef">[[OS-8/Story#Before operation|OS-8 Before]]</ref><ref name="ST3"/>

[[File:67 i07 1.png|thumb|While her enemies' wily tricks have left wounds all over her body, Degenbrecher has not taken a single step back.]]
A large number of Trilby Ashers swarm the Vine-Bead Court under the duke's orders, believing that Ratatos must be hiding state secrets. Even the fearsome Black Knight struggles to hold her ground against seventeen Trilby Ashers by herself. Fortunately, Rhodes Island [[Elite Op]]erator [[Sharp]] saves Degenbrecher just in the nick of time, with more reinforcements on the way. Down on the ground, Arctosz and the Paleroche Clan construct temporary fortifications to hold off the Victorian fleet. Gnosis meets with Enciodes at their secret military base. The time for Enciodes's final gambit has arrived.<ref name="9Aft">[[OS-9/Story#After operation|OS-9 After]]</ref>

Higher up on Mount Karlan, Enya, Joanna, and Elena continue their search when they discover Great Elder Adso, together with several of his followers. Adso had managed to escape from his cell and is now seeking refuge in the mountains. He admits his attempts at deceiving her, and declares her search for Kjeragandr as utterly meaningless, for he believes that Kjeragandr does not exist. In the face of such blasphemy, even the Saintess Enya struggles to hold on to her faith, but she still her insists on continuing to search for Her. Enya thus makes a bet with him: if they are able to find proof of Kjeragandr, then he must renounce all his schemes.<ref name="ST3"/>

[[File:67 i08.png|thumb|left|For the first time in centuries, the people lay eyes upon a sculpture of Kjeragandr's true form.]]
Adso brings Enya to a cavern located near the peak of Mount Karlan, where the clerics of the Vine-Bead Court once hid during an uprising. Elena discovers a hidden rock door near the back of the cavern and blasts it open.<ref name="9Bef">[[OS-9/Story#Before operation|OS-9 Before]]</ref> When they step inside, Elena's instruments picks up biosignals, as if the rock walls around were beating like a heart. Within is an enormous cavern with dozens of frozen ice statues—the Saintesses of the past who were sent to try awaken Kjeragandr. The frozen maidens are all kneeling towards the ice sculpture of a massive beast, who must be none other than Kjeragandr Herself.<ref name="10Bef">[[OS-10/Story#Before operation|OS-10 Before]]</ref>

The moment Enya touches the statue, her mind is filled with Kjeragandr's memories, and she finds herself face-to-face with her head maid Kjarr. Kjarr finally reveals that She is indeed Kjeragandr, a [[Feranmut]] of a bygone age, and that they are currently within Her body. Kjarr apologises that She is unable to return to Enya's side, but believes that even without Her continued presence, she will be able to free Kjerag from the darkness. Enya then wakes up from her "dream" as Elena warns her that the second Catastrophe will strike in three hours. Joanna and Adso also witnessed Kjeragandr in their dreams, and the latter repents for his faithlessness. Enya forgives the old man for his sins and returns to the Vine-Bead Court to prepare for one final prayer.<ref name="10Aft">[[OS-10/Story#After operation|OS-10 After]]</ref>

===The Eye of the Blizzard===
[[File:67 i17.png|thumb|"If the Duke of Caster and some insignificant nobody died fighting each other in the snow, why, that would certainly bring shame to the Caster name."]]
[[File:67 i13.png|thumb|"But in Kjerag? If I am gone, there are countless others who will do what I would have done."]]
The time limit for the Duke of Caster's ultimatum is almost up. Just as the Duke of Caster is about to order the attack, a single high-speed battleship suddenly appears in the horizon. Enciodes hails the duke on a public frequency, announcing that he is the captain of the ship and would like to hold a pleasant discussion on board the Gloriana. The duke acquiesces to his proposal and welcomes him onto her flagship. Enciodes, [[SilverAsh the Reignfrost|now wearing a glamorous formal attire]], introduces himself as "the sole authority of Kjerag".<ref name="10Bef"/>

Enciodes begins to disseminate the contents of the Duke of Caster's proposal, raising issues with several key clauses in the contract. Enciodes is willing to share a significantly larger portion of Kjerag's minerals with Caster's duchy, given that Victoria is willing to provide mining equipment, specialised consultants, and workers. However, there can be absolutely no military presence from Victoria on Kjerag soil, and Kjerag must maintain absolute sovereignty over all resources within its borders. The duke is naturally inclined to reject his revision when cannonfire suddenly bombards her ship's flank. Enciodes claims he has a large number of artillery cannons hidden in the mountains, with enough firepower to annihilate Caster's entire fleet.<ref name="10Bef"/>

Atop the mountain, Enya changes into [[Pramanix the Prerita|her most solemn of clothes]] and makes a pilgrimage up Mount Karlan. At Karlan's summit, she begins to pray to Kjeragandr, pray that their benevolent goddess will help Her children one last time. Kjeragandr hears her wishes and the thick Catastrophe clouds begin to scatter, showering the land below in radiant sunlight. Even the researchers at Rhine Lab and the duke's soldiers are at a complete loss for words for this unexplainable event.<ref name="10Aft"/>

Having borne witness to a miracle that should no longer be possible and unable to accurately gauge the extent of Kjerag's military capability, the Duke of Caster is forced to concede defeat. She calls for her Victorian fleet to immediately retreat.<ref name="10Aft"/>

===Visitor From Beyond===
[[File:67 i11.png|thumb|left|This is a pure land, one that must not be violated.]]
After returning to her official post in the Vine-Bead Court, the Saintess Enya decrees that Adso's followers must travel across Kjerag and assist the people of the Tri-Clans complete the winter farmwork as atonement. Once spring arrives, they will be allowed to return to the court. Following the crisis, Enciodes attends a meeting with the vice president of Columbia, Conrad Jackson, who is impressed with Kjerag's rapid development and bold actions in the face of crisis. Enciodes has demonstrated that even though they may lack the military prowess of the core nations, Kjerag will not give up its dreams of becoming a developed country capable of standing alongside its peers.<ref name="ST4">[[OS-ST-4]]</ref>

The missing Rhine researcher Carolin was never found in the end, but there was ample evidence proving she was a Maylander spy who had also been an informant for Victoria. She had also been responsible for shouting the bomb warning at the train station in an attempt to evade capture from [[Monch]], before later locking her up for several days until she was rescued by Sciurus.<ref name="ST1"/><ref name="7Aft"/> Elena records her findings of Feranmut biosignatures on Mount Karlan and submits it to Silence. She advocates for Elena to publish it, but Elena says she wants to take things slow and learn more about the subject. More importantly, Elena has finally pinpointed the real location of the object that crashed on Karlan.<ref name="ST4"/>

[[File:Background-RL Observation Point.png|thumb|The mysterious object that fell from the heavens lies in the center of the clearing.]]
Rhine's research teams flock to a remote clearing in the mountains to find the most bewildering sight. They discover a crater holding a gigantic indigo orb, with colourful ribbons of energy swirling in mid-air. A preliminary analysis of the "meteorite" revealed that 70% of its composition is a material completely unknown to man. Its structure has been severely damage, but whether that was from the impact or something else remains unclear. The other 30% is determined to be no different than the somatic cells of Terra's [[Elf]] race, which comes as a great shock to Muelsyse.<ref name="ST4"/>
