==War and Betrayal==
After [[Reunion]] is routed and [[Lungmen]] secured, [[Kal'tsit]] delivers grim news to those gathered in [[Wei Yenwu]]’s office—[[Talulah]] has detached the core city plate of [[Chernobog]] and set it on a direct course toward Lungmen. Making matters worse, Chernobog is still officially an [[Ursus]] city. If the two [[Nomadic City|nomadic cities]] collide or engage in open conflict, it could ignite a catastrophic war between Ursus and [[Yan]]—a war neither nation can afford. In the meantime, the [[Doctor]] brings [[FrostNova]]'s corpse to [[Rhodes Island]]'s bioprocessing unit and meets [[Rosmontis]].<ref>[[7-2/Story]]</ref>

In Chernobog’s control tower, [[W]] questions Talulah about the acceleration of the city. Talulah explains that while Reunion has reactivated the core plate to support their forces in Lungmen, they still require two override keys to gain full control of the emergency brakes. But W’s interrogation is a cover for an assassination attempt—she has rigged explosives in the structure below. However, Talulah sees through the ploy and uses her Arts to melt the bombs before they can detonate. Cornered, W chooses to blow herself up rather than die by Talulah’s hand, her body falling into the city’s depths. Shortly after, Talulah entrusts one of the override keys to [[Patriot]], and promises W’s former Sarkaz mercenaries the one thing they crave most: a war they can call their own.<ref>[[7-1]]</ref>

[[File:Ch'en Threatens Wei.png|thumb|left|"I'll go, even if it means killing my family."]]
Back in Lungmen, [[Wei Yenwu]] receives a communication from his personal Messenger containing information from [[Islam Witte]], Minister of Finance, advisor to the Emperor, and Wei's friend. The message details that Ursus is suffering from internal conflict—Witte was unable to intervene in the Chernobog incident and is unsure who is responsible. Both of their Messengers have been attacked numerous time by foes attempting to silence them for what they know, and Wei's Messenger emphasizes that Lungmen must stop Chernobog. It is unknown if the Messenger survived and safely returned to Lungmen. In light of this info, Wei is shaken, knowing that it is up to Lungmen to solve this incident alone, and a wrong move gives Ursus justification to wage war on Yan. The rotting Ursus Empire will not act, they will only watch.<ref>[[7-2/Story]]</ref>

As those present discuss the next course of action, Ch'en speaks up. She declares that she will head to Chernobog and face Talulah herself. Wei Yenwu vehemently disagrees and they argue until Ch'en draws her sword in a fit of anger. A member of the [[Shadow Guard]] arrives to argue for their own deployment into Chernobog—willing to sacrifice their own lives to destroy Reunion and save Lungmen. The situation escalates, and Ch'en reveals to all present that she is Infected despite Wei Yenwu's attempts to hide the truth for her safety. She unleashes the unstable and partially-sheathed Chi Xiao on the Shadow Guard before jumping out the window, hundreds of meters above the ground. As of that moment, Ch'en renounces her ties to Lungmen, and is acting independently.<ref>[[7-2/Story]]</ref>

During her escape, Wei Yenwu puts out a secret arrest warrant for Ch'en, and the [[Lung]] is intercepted by Hoshiguma, who has recovered from her injuries sustained during Reunion's invasion. Hoshiguma tells Ch'en that Wei Yenwu should be the one to die for Lungmen, not her. Ch'en finally confesses that she is Infected—a fact she had been hiding from her partner for three years—and that Talulah is her sister. Still, Hoshiguma holds her ground, refusing to let Ch'en go to certain death. They fight, and the swordswoman cuts through Hoshiguma's shield, ''Hannya''. Hoshiguma relents, heartbroken, knowing that she cannot stop Ch'en. They part ways with the understanding that they may never meet again.<ref>[[7-3/Story]]</ref>

Back at the office, Wei Yenwu dismisses the Shadow Guard, forbidding them from retaliating or pursuing Ch’en. [[Fumizuki]] moves to bring her back herself, but Wei stops her as well, revealing his own fatal plan—to lead Lungmen’s army into Chernobog and die, believing his death to be [[Kashchey]]’s true goal, carried out through Talulah. [[Kal'tsit]] interjects, proposing that [[Rhodes Island]] confront Reunion instead. As an organization of Infected, they are better suited to fight this battle. Furthermore, they can avoid igniting a war due to their status as an independent company with no affiliation to Lungmen. Wei reluctantly agrees, granting Rhodes Island autonomy during the conflict, though he makes it clear that his distrust of the Infected remains. Before leaving, Kal’tsit requests the remains of [[Edward Artorius]], Talulah’s late father.<ref>[[7-3/Story]]</ref>

==Protector or Traitor?==
The Rhodes Island landship departs from [[Lungmen]] and begins its infiltration of the core city. As part of the operation, [[Dobermann]] joins the pilot of the ''Good Boy''—one of Rhodes Island's two VTOLs—to act as a decoy, drawing the attention of Reunion's casters and crossbowmen within Chernobog. Simultaneously, a second team—led by [[Nearl]], [[Firewatch]], and [[Elysium]]—use specialized equipment to create a sandstorm in the path of Chernobog.<ref>[[7-4/Story]]</ref> Using off-road vehicles, teams led by Amiya, Kal'tsit, Rosmontis, and two [[Elite Op|Elite Ops]] infiltrate the nomadic city via specialized grappling hooks.<ref>[[7-5/Story]]</ref> Once aboard, they eliminate a small Reunion patrol and establish a foothold. However, Rosmontis's sudden display of brutal, overwhelming force unsettles the Doctor, leading to friction between them and Kal'tsit, as the Doctor questions why a child is in possession of such terrifying combat ability.<ref>[[7-6/Story]]</ref> Meanwhile, back in Lungmen, Fumizuki and Wei Yenwu discuss Talulah’s actions and motivations. Both suspect she has been shaped by the influence of Kashchey, who intended her to be his successor. Despite Fumizuki’s objections, Wei orders the Shadow Guard to escort her to [[Sargon]], believing that the conflict must ultimately end with his own death.<ref>[[7-7]]</ref> At the same time, Talulah receives grim news from [[Faust]]'s Phantom Crossbowmen: Faust, the [[Yeti Squadron]], and [[FrostNova]] are dead, [[Mephisto]] is incapacitated, and [[Crownslayer]] has seemingly abandoned Reunion with her squad. Talulah deceives the Phantom Crossbowmen under pretense of grieving, and soon directs the [[Sarkaz]] mercenaries to prevent them from interacting with other Reunion units within the city.<ref>[[7-8/Story]]</ref>

[[File:Return of the Guard.png|thumb|A familiar face with a different uniform.]]
[[Rhodes Island]] uncovers evidence that [[Ch'en]] is alive, but wounded. While searching nearby, they also come across the long-missing [[Guard (NPC)|Guard]]—the sole survivor of [[Ace]] and [[Scout]]’s final stand in Chernobog. To their surprise, he is now aligned with [[Reunion]], serving under [[Patriot]]’s disciplined guerrilla forces.<ref>[[7-9/Story]]</ref> The team finds him in a warehouse comforting an Infected Ursine woman named Tatiana, who accuses Reunion of deliberately spreading [[Oripathy]] and creating new Infected. Guard defends Patriot’s guerrillas, asserting that they are nothing like the rest of Reunion and are committed to protecting Infected civilians rather than exploiting them. He insists he joined Patriot not out of ideology, but to help the innocent survive. Guard explains that it was thanks to Patriot’s efforts—distributing supplies in devastated areas—that so many Ursus citizens were able to escape the Chernobog catastrophe. His respect for Patriot runs deep, and he pleads with Rhodes Island to see the difference between the guerrillas and Reunion’s more radical factions, insisting that conflict between them is not inevitable. However, [[Kal'tsit]] informs him that it was Rhodes Island who confronted and ultimately caused [[FrostNova]]’s death. No matter Guard’s intentions or ideals, they may still find themselves on opposing sides now that he has betrayed Rhodes Island.<ref>[[7-10/Story]]</ref>

Now that they’ve reached the center of the core city, [[Rhodes Island]] pushes through the central district toward the bridge and generator complex that controls Chernobog’s movement. Rosmontis uses her Arts to detect and eliminate the guerrilla scouts, crippling their communications while Amiya supports her by regulating her unstable emotions through her own Arts.<ref>[[7-13/Story]]</ref> After neutralizing several enemy scouts, Amiya, Kal'tsit, and Rosmontis are suddenly confronted by none other than Buldrokkas'tee, the Patriot, Reunion’s backbone and Ursus' incarnation of war. Accompanied by his elite [[Guerrilla Shieldguard]]s—the famed "Aegis of the Infected"—Patriot stands, immovable and indomitable.<ref>[[7-17/Story]]</ref>

==The Last March==
[[File:The Wendigo and the Sarkaz King.png|thumb|"Rhodes Island, I advance!"]]
Amiya, Kal'tsit, Rosmontis, and the Doctor attempt to negotiate with [[Patriot]], hoping to avoid further bloodshed. Though Patriot recognizes Kal'tsit from their past encounters and holds a degree of trust in her, he is aware of the role Amiya and the Doctor played in the death of his daughter, [[FrostNova]]. He is also aware of [[Talulah]]’s plan to crash Chernobog into Lungmen, but remains loyal to her—not out of blind faith, but to preserve the fragile unity within Reunion and prevent infighting. Despite his personal feelings, Patriot bars Rhodes Island’s path, not for revenge, but for Reunion’s ideals. To him, Reunion must not fall until the Infected are liberated. He declares that only those capable of defeating him can be trusted to stop Talulah.<ref>[[7-18/Story|7-18 Before]]</ref>

In the fierce battle that follows, Patriot's immense strength and impenetrable armor make him a nearly unstoppable force. Yet, through the combined effort of Rhodes Island's present Operators and the worsening toll of his [[Oripathy]], he is finally halted. Acknowledging Rhodes Island’s resolve, Patriot entrusts them with the key Talulah gave him to stop Chernobog. As he dies, Amiya conjures a vision of his family to bring him peace. This has the opposite effect, as this reveals that Amiya is the new Lord of Fiends—the successor to [[Theresa]] and the ruler of the [[Sarkaz]]. Patriot is enraged, knowing that his death was long prophesized to come at the hands of the King of Sarkaz—in the end, he failed to defy fate. In that moment, the [[Wendigo]]'s ancient [[Arts]] resonates with his emotions, revealing a new prophecy. The Lord of Fiends will destroy all peoples, and become the most horrific disaster to affect Terra. With a final burst of strength, he moves to crush Amiya's skull—but stops at the final moment as she stands tall, staring into his soul. Patriot speaks to Amiya through her telepathic Arts in his final moments, and entrusts the future to her. With the belief that she might overturn the fate he so despised, Patriot dies standing, arm outstretched, tears and blood dripping from his helmet.<ref>[[7-18/Story|7-18 After]]</ref>

Even after his death, the wrath he held in his heart continued to emanate from his body. Aboard Rhodes Island, FrostNova's corpse began heating up rapidly in an Arts reaction, causing damage to the processing unit she was contained in and nearly ruining the entire cabin. Through means uncertain, their combined deaths resonated, changing the weather and creating a heavy snowfall. The remaining Guerrilla Shieldguards see it as a sign from their fallen leaders, and honor their memory. Although they initially move to kill Rhodes Island in retaliation, they eventually agree to set aside their differences and work together to stop Talulah’s destructive course.<ref>[[7-19]]</ref>

==Fanning the Flames==
In the wake of Patriot's death, civil war erupts within the core city. Reunion fractures, as fighters loyal to the Shieldguards rise against Talulah’s followers, denouncing her as a traitor to Patriot’s ideals and to Reunion itself. Amidst the chaos, the Rhodes Island team splits: The Doctor and Kal'tsit descend toward the Chernobog Sarcophagus, while Amiya, Rosmontis, the Shieldguards, and others head for the control tower. En route, the Doctor’s group encounters a severely wounded but still defiant [[W]], who survived her failed assassination attempt on Talulah. Even at a disadvantage, she hurls venomous accusations—branding the Doctor a murderer and Kal'tsit a lunatic. Kal'tsit responds with chilling calm, commanding [[Mon3tr]] to carry W back towards the conflict, removing her from the conversation. Meanwhile, in the city’s depths, Mephisto crawls toward the Sarcophagus, seeking to erase his memories and escape the weight of his actions. Above, [[Ch'en]] reaches the control tower at last, ready to confront Talulah.<ref>[[7-19]]</ref>

Far away, in the royal palace of [[Londinium]], [[Victoria]], a different conflict is brought to light. [[Theresis]], the Regent of [[Kazdel]], enacts a shadowy plan to resurrect the late [[Theresa]], hoping to reunify the fractured Sarkaz people and bring an end to their ancient strife. A new [[Episode 10|storm]] is brewing.<ref>[[7-20]]</ref>
