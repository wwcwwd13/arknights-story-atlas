==The Sarkaz United==
[[File:27 i12.png|thumb|Long divided, the Sarkaz will soon be whole again. Even if they must wage war on all of Terra to see it done.]]
[[Londinium]], capital of the [[Victoria|Victorian Empire]], has fallen under [[Kazdel]]ian occupation. Within [[The Shard]], [[Theresis]] convenes a meeting with the [[Sarkaz]] Royal Court and the resurrected [[Theresa]], discussing their roles in the upcoming war. Theresis would stay and defend The Shard, while Theresa would board the airship being constructed within, once completed. Both of these superweapons are essential for their goal of uniting the Sarkaz and standing against the nations of Terra. The three Sarkaz lords in attendance depart as well, knowing their role in the twins' plan. Before they part ways, Theresis mentions that [[Rhodes Island]] will be arriving in Londinium soon.<ref name="10-1">[[10-1]]</ref>

True to his word, Rhodes Island is in the process of approaching Londinium. [[Kal'tsit]] takes command of the [[Rhodes Island (landship)|landship]], anchoring outside the vicinity of Londinium while teams approach the city, including the [[Doctor]], [[Amiya]], [[Closure]], [[Misery]], [[Logos]], and members of the [[Glasgow Gang]] led by [[Siege]]—[[Indra]], [[Morgan]], and [[Dagda]]. After engaging in multiple lossless skirmishes with hostile patrols and contacting [[Jessica]], the teams split up to handle separate missions via vehicle. Logos and Misery lead their teams ahead to infiltrate Londinium using their unique [[Arts]], while the remaining teams must assist in the rescue of a downed [[Blacksteel]] pilot and a Londinium citizen who can find them a conventional way into the massive [[nomadic city]].<ref name="10-1">[[10-1]]</ref>

Dagda and Indra, supported by a squad of Rhodes Island operators, locate the wreckage of the Blacksteel aircraft and engage the [[Dublinn]] soldiers holding their targets hostage. After dispatching the arriving reinforcements, they successfully rescue the civilian, Thomas, but find the pilot had already been killed before they arrived. Upon reconvening, Amiya informs Jessica of the pilot's death, and Thomas agrees to help them infiltrate Londinium through Sudean's port. An enraged [[Mandragora]] receives news of the ambush, revealing in her anger that [[Horn]] survived [[County Hillock]] and was taken prisoner, only to escape after being brought to Londinium. Dublinn's alliance with the Sarkaz is tenuous, and Mandragora is running out of options to appease the Sarkaz. She rallies her men after correctly deducing that their ambushers might be looking for a way into Londinium. Within the city, Horn eliminates a group of Dublinn fighters to save captured Victorian soldiers who have been betrayed by their commanding officers.<ref>[[10-2/Story]]</ref>

==Chaos in Londinium==
[[File:Heading for Londinium.png|thumb|Rhodes Island, ready to infiltrate Londinium]]
The Rhodes Islanders arrive at port 309, witnessing the long line of civilians attempting to leave the city. Thomas is about to part ways with the squad when approaching Sarkaz fighters throw the plan into disarray, forcing them to hide. The Sarkaz stop the civilians, searching them, killing some, and taking others prisoner. Horn witnesses the Sarkaz blockade as she attempts to evacuate the rescued but demoralised soldiers from Londinium, and forbids them from charging out and attacking the Sarkaz as they take on civilian disguises and join the line. Thomas panics, stealing an operator's weapon and fleeing, only to run into Mandragora and her soldiers. Dublinn captures Thomas once more, but are interrupted by the Sarkaz fighters, who demand that Thomas be handed over to them instead. The two sides argue, escalating to near-blows. An unknown fifth party coincidentally attacks the arguing soldiers via drone the same time Horn's men fire on the group, inciting conflict as the Sarkaz and Dublinn engage in battle. Seeing the civilians trapped in the crossfire, Amiya's squad breaks concealment to save as many as possible.<ref>[[10-3/Story]]</ref>

Atop Londinium's wall, General [[Manfred]] of the [[Military Commission of Kazdel]] is informed of the chaos unfolding at port 309, and is unsurprised. He doesn't send backup and instead instructs his men to continue preparing the first test-fire of the city's secondary artillery, seized and modified to fire into the city itself. The mercenary [[Hoederer]] arrives, now Manfred's trusted subordinate. The two converse, and Manfred explains that he had intentionally left port 309 unguarded to lure the rebels that had been harassing Sarkaz forces. While he was unable to attack Dublinn directly before, due to the threat the [[Duke of Wellington]] posed, Mandragora has now openly attacked the Sarkaz, giving him the freedom to remove Dublinn from the board. The free-for-all in the port is interrupted as Manfred orders his men to fire the [[Londinium Secondary Defense Artillery]] on the port. The chaotic battle swiftly comes to a halt as the Sarkaz retreat, immediately followed by a barrage of artillery. All sides swiftly retreat, and Thomas is presumed dead, caught in the line of fire. Manfred focuses fire on the fleeing Dublinn forces, and the Doctor is temporarily taken hostage as Rhodes Island is brought away from the port by the rebels Manfred sought—the [[Londinium Citizens' Self-Salvation Corps]].<ref>[[10-4/Story]]</ref>

The Rhodes Islanders are led through the substructure into Londinium proper, where the Doctor removes themselves from the hostage situation, knowing that the two rebels, [[Stainless|Feist]] and [[Rockrock]], were not in a position to hurt them. They pool information, but are interrupted by an emergency meeting called by the leader of the Self-Salvation Corps. Horn leads the Victorian soldiers who decided to fight once more to their safehouse, intentionally set up dangerously close to Dublinn's headquarters to avoid the Sarkaz. The Rhodes Island landship is host to the Lord of the [[Nachzehrer]], Kal'tsit and [[Warfarin]] face him in discussion as the Lord seeks the details of [[Patriot]]'s death. Satisfied by Kal'tsit's answers, he departs without violence. Meanwhile, Ascalon infiltrates The Shard and encounters [[Qui'sartuštaj]], leader of the [[Confessarii]], and withdraws before she can reach Theresis.<ref>[[10-5/Story]]</ref>

Manfred deduces that Rhodes Island has at least one agent within Londinium feeding them information, and instructs Hoederer to investigate the captured rebels. Feist talks down his allies, who are in a panic upon seeing a Sarkaz travelling with them. The group finally meets the leader of the resistance, [[Clovisia]] and establishes an alliance. Manfred suggests active cooperation between his men and Dublinn. While Mandragora is absent, Horn prepares to raid Dublinn's headquarters in order to steal much-needed food and medicine, and Amiya is shaken after hearing Rockrock describe a Sarkaz leader matching Theresa's description. Once Manfred is alone, "Thomas" arrives, having survived the bombing of the port. The general thanks the [[Damazti Cluster]] for their help, and seeks their assistance in his upcoming plan.<ref>[[10-6/Story]]</ref>

Rhodes Island and the Self-Salvation Corps discuss terms and prepare for their first joint operation: To rescue [[Heidi| Heidi Thomson]], Kal'tsit's Messenger within Londinium and an ally to both factions. Closure works with Feist and helps him with the Self-Salvation Corps's gear, while Rockrock leaves with a resistance member named Bill and their squad to meet with a contact sent by Lady [[Golding]], an informant providing crucial information on the location of the temporary prison where Heidi was being held. After retrieving the intel, they are spotted by a Sarkaz patrol, and Bill is captured while Rockrock flees.<ref>[[10-7/Story]]</ref>

Hoederer is informed that [[W]] is making a move by a [[Ines|shadowy figure]]. Mandragora and her men are tasked by Manfred to guard the factory-turned-prison, while Horn returns with the medicine to discover that another one of her men has passed away from his injuries. They decide to raid the prison that night to rescue captive Victorian soldiers, knowing the Sarkaz will transport the prisoners the next morning. Rhodes Island and the Self-Salvation Corps prepare as well. Soon, all five sides would descend on the factory.<ref>[[10-8/Story]]</ref>

==Breakout==
[[File:Liberation Battle of the Factory.png|thumb|left|Who will be the winner of this battle?]]
The R.I.-S.S.C. alliance meticulously prepares to breach the Kazdel-occupied factory. Siege and Indra stay behind to defend the Self-Salvation Corps's underground bunker while the rest of the team heads to the site. There, Dagda and Morgan distract the Sarkaz soldiers guarding the prison while Heidi leads her own uprising on the inside, allowing the prisoners to take over the factory. Heidi meets up with the Doctor, Amiya, Feist, and Rockrock, who have also infiltrated, though Sarkaz soldiers surround them. When all hope seems lost, however, W and her mercenaries, sent by Kal'tsit, appear on the side of R.I., fighting back their fellow Sarkaz. Feist and Rockrock run off and find Bill, who is still grievously injured. They also run into Hoederer during the rescue, who proves to be a formidable foe, but they narrowly manage to escape up a vent to reconvene with Rhodes Island. Hoederer's presence, and the growing number of Sarkaz surrounding the facility, prove to the group that the prison was a Sarkaz trap meant to lure out both the Self-Salvation Corps and R.I.. They decide to all make a run for it in a desperate bid for survival. W's escape is foiled by Hoederer, however, who W confronts for killing Schwab to gain Theresis' trust. The two begin exchanging blows, though Hoederer soon gains the upper hand. Just as he is about to kill her, [[Ascalon]] appears and deflects his blade. It is revealed that Hoederer is actually working with [[Ines]], who was supposedly executed by soldiers, to inform W about the resurrected Theresa and the plot within the Shard.

[[File:The Fall of Mandragora B.png|thumb|Live by the sword, die by the sword]]
Meanwhile, Horn and her squad have also found their way into the factory and encounter Mandagora. Though tensions are still high, with Mandagora having killed some of Horn's teammates and taking her prisoner. Both sides understand, however, that the Sarkaz are presently the greatest threat to both of them. Mandagora orders her men to clear out hordes of Sarkaz soldiers as vengeance for the deaths of her comrades during the infighting; she also intends to rescue "the Spy," a Dublinn informant and her childhood friend. Horn and her group pursue Dublinn, picking off stragglers left behind. She soon comes across a group of Victorian prisoners, who she frees and adds to her squad. She also manages to escape from Manfred's grasp after Misery, a R.I. Elite Operator, appears and uses his hallucination-inducing Arts to allow Horn to make a run for it. Mandagora, meanwhile, successfully rescues Spy, but is ambushed by Manfred and his men, who have caught on to her betrayal. Manfred reveals to Mandagora that the Leader of Reunion is displeased with her unauthorized actions at County Hillock and her violent instability and is unwilling to let her compromise Dublinn's relationships with Theresis and the Victorian nobility; in other words, the Leader has abandoned her and given full authority to Manfred to dispose of Dublinn forces within Londinium. After a fierce battle, Manfred leaves a critically wounded and despondent Mandragora for dead, where she resigns herself to joining her Taran compatriots in death. However, Misery appears before her, offering her a second chance.

Siege and Indra soon grow worried about the status of the operation after not hearing from the rest of the team for a while. They come across the corpses of the Self-Salvation Corps's Team 6 on the surface, who have been torn apart by the Sanguinarch's blood Arts, and hurry to meet up with Amiya and the others, who are still running from the Sanguinarch. Upon finding the rest of the squad, Siege and Indra quickly explain that the Self-Salvation Corps has come under attack; this forces R.I. and the Self-Salvation Corps to flee back to the underground base. Dagda, meanwhile, goes off to seek the help of the Victorian nobles' mercenary forces using her connections.

==Insurgency Above, Threat Below==
Despite their narrow escape from the factory, the threat to the alliance is still far from over. The Kazdelians have somehow gained the intel on the Self-Salvation Corps's underground passages and the location of their bunker. Clovisia also receives the news that all of the Self-Salvation Corps's contact points above ground have been attacked at once. The traitor within the Self-Salvation Corps's ranks turns out to be "Bill," who reveals himself to Amiya to be the [[Damazti Cluster]], a shapeshifter who had transformed into the likeness of the real Bill before R.I. and the Self-Salvation Corps had infiltrated the prison—the man that Feist and Rockrock had rescued and brought along with them was not Bill at all, but the disguised Cluster, while the real Bill had died of his injuries within the factory. They had also been the one to give away R.I.'s coordinates during their escape from the factory, explaining why it was so difficult to get away from the Sarkaz. The Doctor, Clovisia, Rockrock, and Feist find Amiya holding a blade to the imposter's throat, but the latter two believe Amiya when "Bill's" behavior grows more suspicious. The confrontation is interrupted, however, when Closure rushes in to tell everyone that Londinium's cannons are aimed right at their location and that they must evacuate as soon as possible. The group flees to a safe location, but the Cluster escapes in the chaos and makes their way back to Manfred.

[[File:The Insurgency Begins.png|thumb|left|Horn leading the Victorian soldiers on the walls in an uprising against Kazdel]]
Clovisia tells the others that the only way to escape the underground with their lives is to recapture a train station located a few kilometers above them, and to take the train out of Sudean before the entire area is bombed. After they have successfully arrived at the next station, Heidi's contacts will help disguise them until they can reach Central Londinium. The only way to stop the Kazdelians' advance is to recapture Londinium's walls. Rockrock and her men go to get the train started before the Sarkaz can destroy the underground, while Amiya, the Doctor, and Feist head off to the walls to face Manfred, whose Arts are perfectly tailored to resist Amiya's. Meanwhile, Horn's squad has also successfully climbed the walls, rallying the soldiers stationed there who have been forced by the Sarkaz to fire upon their own city; this sparks a full-blown insurgency against the Kazdelians. Horn stays behind to fight Manfred and stall for time while the rest of the group heads to the control room. They are able to set off explosives which destroy the control room, severely weaken the structure of the walls, and shut down Londinium's defense artillery system. As the walls begin to crumble, Manfred orders his men to retreat and leaves Horn barely clinging to life. Thankfully, however, she is saved at the last minute by her squad.

In the meantime, Clovisia, Siege, Indra, and Morgan have stayed underground to fend off the Sanguinarch. However, they are powerless against such a powerful Vampire, and are forced to run for their lives. Siege inadvertently finds a control panel with a button on it at the end of a passageway—the same button she once pressed over twenty years ago, when Gawain had brought her underground following the uprising against the king. Siege presses the button, which moves the tunnel beneath their feet upward and allows the group to escape to the surface.

==Unthinkable==
[[File:The Resurrected Sarkaz King.png‎|thumb|"H-How can this be..."]]
The reunited Glasgow Gang (including Dagda, who has returned with the Victorian mercenaries), the Doctor, Amiya, Clovisia, and Feist all make their way to the train station with the last-minute help of Dagda's guards and board the train leaving for Auchterigg Borough (i.e. Central Londinium). Just then, however, the Kazdelians chasing the train stop. Standing in the middle of the tracks watching the train depart is Theresa, the former Sarkaz King who had "died" long ago. Her presence horrifies the R.I. operators, especially Amiya and the Doctor, who must now accept the fact that Theresa has somehow come back to life.

Upon reaching Auchterigg Borough, [[Allerdale|Allerdale Cumberland]], the chief commander of the Self-Salvation Corps and the late Duke of Cumberland's daughter, welcomes Siege warmly. She then proposes a plan for Siege to lead Victoria to battle against the Sarkaz and take back the throne using the relic of Edward Artorius, as Siege is actually Alexandrina, the last surviving heir to the Aslan throne. W, who had also escaped the city off-screen, confronts Kal'tsit for letting Theresis get hold of Theresa's body, having witnessed her appearance at the train station. Theresis orders [[Qui'sartuštaj]] to continue supervising Theresa and Amiya while Theresis seemingly plots to destroy the power of the Lord of Fiends. Outside of the city, the Leader of Dublinn receives the news of Siege's arrival in Central Londinium and Talulah's escape from R.I. and subsequent return to Reunion. Knowing of the rumors that Talulah may be a Draco, and thus a potential threat, The Leader prepares herself to enter the capital. A bloody battle surrounding the throne seems inevitable.

Meanwhile, in northern Ursus, Talulah tracks down [[Koshelna]], a [[Hippogryph]] living under the guise of a college lecturer. During their confrontation, Koshelna attempts to provoke Talulah into killing her, but Talulah refuses, concluding that the Deathless Black Snake will continue to return so long as [[Ursus]] itself remains unchanged. Afterward, Talulah reunites with [[Nine]] and a group of former Reunion members before accompanying them to Victoria.
