==Sky Fever==
[[File:68_i01_2.png|thumb|"Propeller Paradise proves that every Columbian can be the greatest of Pioneers. There'll always be a place for you somewhere beyond the Starpod."]]
In the two years since [[Columbia]] witnessed the [[Lone Trail/Synopsis|Trimounts Arc]] tear a hole in the skies above, the sky has become a brand new obsession. [[Kristen Wright]] became an idol for everyone, and her legacy has launched aerospace science into the mainstream. Nowhere is this more obvious than in the construction of [[Propeller Paradise]], the first ever experimental levitating plate on [[Terra]], flying high at 600 meters above the ground. Consisting of dozens of floating platforms and pods connected with tube passages and cable cars, Propeller Paradise is like a giant amusement park, representing a brand new age of the Columbian dream.<ref name="ST1">[[UR-ST-1]]</ref>

Compared to Volvort Kochinski's high-precision astronomical telescope and Mama John's's all-weather floating billboards, [[Rhine Lab]]'s presence is much more muted on Propeller Paradise. Much of their work has been focused on providing foundational engineering support for the levitation plate whilst continuing research into improving levitation technology. [[Nasti Lunorey]], Director of Engineering, is currently developing a plant-based energy transmission system based on the data left behind by the [[Galleria Stellaria]]. For this purpose, Rhine has constructed another Vivarium on the park, which they plan to showcase during the upcoming Air Expo Columbia.<ref name="ST1">[[UR-ST-1]]</ref>

In a week's time, Propeller Paradise will dock with the capital city of Max D.C., officially cutting the ribbon on the science exhibition. Thousands of venture capitalists and tech startups are eager to show off all sorts of wild ideas, from flying appliances to floating bars. Yet in this glitzy paradise where even the most absurd dreams appear within reach, things are not what they seem.

==On the Loose==
[[Skybox|Gordon Tannen]], an officer at the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Arts Units and Originium (ATAO) had left his job to do administrative work at Propeller Paradise in hopes of pursuing a life of wealth and luxury. Although appearing as a diligent entrepreneur, Gordon has secretly been smuggling contraband equipment from all across Terra and selling them to desperate startups. Gordon believes that ATAO's rule to only use Columbian tech stifles innovation. Six months later, he was promoted to chief executive of the park. His former superior, [[Gustave]], is the first to congratulate him, though reminds him not to get cocky. Rhine Lab still has a stranglehold over public opinion and their frequent inspections of the exhibits remain a risk that he has to squash.<ref name="ST1">[[UR-ST-1]]</ref><ref name="1Aft">[[UR-1/Story#After operation|UR-1 After]]</ref>

[[File:68_i15.png|thumb|"You're holding my lunch, Mercia, so kindly tell me the truth; what exactly does Mr. Tannen want to 'discuss' with me?"]]
[[Nasti]] is visited by Gordon's secretary, [[Mercia Selene|Mercia]], who tells her to skip the technical inspections before the expo's opening to avoid any delays. Nasti initially rejects the proposal, but compromises to conduct future inspections in the order of the list that Gordon provided.<ref name="1Bef">[[UR-1/Story#Before operation|UR-1 Before]]</ref> On the levitation layer, Nasti's aide, [[Sky Jagger]], is busy conducting inspections. One particular company, Lucky Cystybeast Food and Beverage, failed to meet Propeller Paradise's stringent regulations even after receiving a notice. The owner, [[Aspen]], could only watch as Sky unsympathetically tears his bar's operating permit. Devastated, he goes to the office to handle the cancellation process, when the desk staff informs him of a potential lifeline.<ref name="1Aft"/>

Later in the day, Gordon receives a visit from a man named Oakley, who claims to be their newly hired tour guide. This comes as a shock as Oakley had supposedly been working at Propeller Paradise for a month already. He summons the other Oakley, who confesses that her real name is [[Cairn|Ferne Barn]]. Ferne explains her current client is an old [[Banshee]] named [[Væla]] who got lost in the Bleached Wasteland, and is suffering from partial memory loss. Gordon arranges for Mercia to take Ferne to see Nasti, hoping to use her as a chance to detain Nasti's team.<ref name="2Bef">[[UR-2/Story#Before operation|UR-2 Before]]</ref>

[[File:68_i03.png|thumb|left|Gordon & Ferne: "Oh crap..."]]
Upon meeting Nasti, Ferne asks Nasti all sorts of Banshee-related questions. While nobody was looking, she snatches her bone pen from the vivarium, recognising it as a Banshee artefact. However, she doesn't realise the pen's incantations control the vivarium, and all the plants instantly wilt within five minutes. Ferne rushes to find Væla, but the elderly Banshee has disappeared from her room.<ref name="2Aft">[[UR-2/Story#After operation|UR-2 After]]</ref> At Mercia's advice, she searches the VIP rooms near Gordon's office, but instead finds Oakley, who claims the police locked him up. She gives her tour guide's guarantee that she can get him out safely and the two flee from the police.<ref name="3Bef">[[UR-3/Story#Before operation|UR-3 Before]]</ref> Unfortunately, she accidentally crashes right into Gordon's secret contraband hideout, and the two are swiftly arrested.<ref name="3Aft">[[UR-3/Story#After operation|UR-3 After]]</ref>

The police put the Vivarium on lockdown following the incident. Nasti orders everyone to take some time off while the senior researchers assist in writing up a report. Sky dejectedly wanders around the park when he bumps into Aspen, who should have already cancelled his business registration after failing the re-check. The man proudly explains that Lucky Cystybeast had miraculously solved their engineering problems overnight. Sky conducts a secret inspection and realises Aspen must have used contraband equipment procured from outside Propeller Paradise.<ref name="3Aft"/>

Suspecting that Gordon has been smuggling contraband, he hands an investigation report to Gustave, hoping the former ATAO chief can put Gordon in place. However, Gustave snatches the report and puts Sky in custody. To protect him, Nasti later apologises to Gustave and officially withdraws Rhine Lab from the upcoming expo and cancels all future inspections. Sky confesses he acted brashly and feels ashamed, but Nasti encourages him to keep going.<ref name="4Bef">[[UR-4/Story#Before operation|UR-4 Before]]</ref>

==A Tree in the Clouds==
[[File:68_i04_2.png|thumb|left|"What's in those two big boxes of yours? Don't tell me they're presents for Rhine Lab?"]]
Gordon receives a phone call from Gustave, who questions him about the smuggling operation he heard from Sky. Knowing the government can't press charges without evidence, Gordon pressures Ferne and Oakley into helping him dispose his remaining contraband. He leads them to the underground engineering tunnels beneath the Rhine Vivarium, which are currently abandoned due to the lockdown. However, they inexplicably run into Nasti, who acts unusually cheery. As Gordon tries to reason with her, Nasti's form suddenly melts as water droplets hang in the air, revealing her identity to be a water clone of [[Muelsyse]]. "Nasti" threatens to kill Gordon, causing him and the others to flee in a panic.<ref name="4Bef"/><ref name="4Aft">[[UR-4/Story#After operation|UR-4 After]]</ref>

Meanwhile, Væla has ended up in Mercia's custody, and she has been using her [[Arts]] to help recover her memories. She surmises that Væla is looking for Nasti, and hands her the confiscated bone pen. Væla, who is actually Nasti's adoptive mother, yearns to see Nasti one more time. While in the middle of work, Nasti discovers the tip of her bone pen at the lab door. She leaves the lab to track the other half down, giving Mercia a chance to slip in. In reality, Mercia is actually an agent of the [[Maylander Foundation]] working undercover as Gordon's secretary, assigned to monitor the tech firms on Propeller Paradise.<ref name="4Aft"/>

[[File:68_i13_1.png|thumb|The roots of the tree are not secure, swaying in the breeze. From a distance, they sway like weeds, like fate.]]
Mercia and other Maylander agents follow the trail left by Muelsyse's water clone. She discovers a secret passageway that expands into a vast underground area previously undocumented in Rhine's blueprints. Her intrusion causes the ground to suddenly crack open. A gigantic white glowing structure shaped like a tree emerges floats into the sky, seemingly without any engines. The police point their spotlights at the tree, discovering Gordon, Ferne, and Oakley—together with boxes of contraband. Gordon lowers his head, knowing that the jig is up.<ref name="ST2">[[UR-ST-2]]</ref>

Nasti gets close to the other half of her bone pen when she is drawn away by the tree's emergence. She is unaware that Væla had just been a few steps away in the shadows ahead. The floating tree stirs some memories in Væla, reminding her of the Roosting Tree from the [[Convallis of the Banshees]], where she had raised a young Nasti.<ref name="ST2"/>

==No Cost Too Great==
The chief executive of Propeller Paradise being found with contraband has thrown upper management into disarray. Many are concerned whether Air Expo Columbia will still go on as planned. Maylander's Emergency Management Council intervenes in order to ensure park security. Left with no other choice, Gordon turns to his old boss Gustave, who has brought in an external scientific consultant to handle the matter. From the consultant's observations, the tree requires two Rhine Lab directors to be fully activated, and its true purpose cannot be sufficiently determined. Fortunately, that leaves plenty of room for them to twist the narrative in his testimony.<ref name="5Bef">[[UR-5/Story#Before operation|UR-5 Before]]</ref><ref name="5Aft">[[UR-5/Story#After operation|UR-5 After]]</ref>

[[File:68_i05_2.png|thumb|left|Væla's adopted daughter is about to embark on a journey, yet she has no idea how it will end, or what sort of advice to impart.]]
Nasti visits Ferne in her holding cell and asks about her Banshee client. From Ferne's explanation, Nasti now understands that the elderly Banshee must be her mother, Væla. Her deteriorating memory means that death will soon come for her, and she must have come here in search of Nasti before she passes away.<ref name="5Aft"/> Nasti is then summoned by [[Tin Man]] of the Maylander Foundation, who wishes to discuss the upcoming hearing with her. Despite unambiguous testimony and evidence from multiple parties, Maylander has continuously delayed the hearing, knowing that the expo will be cancelled as a result. To avoid this, Maylander wants to pin the incident on Rhine Lab. However, Nasti volunteers to take sole responsibility for the incident.<ref name="6Bef">[[UR-6/Story#Before operation|UR-6 Before]]</ref>

The park then broadcasts the results of the hearing: Nasti has confessed to her crimes and is now under arrest. Air Expo Columbia will proceed as planned. Gordon feels relieved at the outcome and starts dressing up for the opening speech. He receives a letter from Gustave, but instead of a welcoming speech, it contains an apology script regarding a major accident on Propeller Paradise. His blood runs cold when his robot assistant [[054|Space Fin 054]] informs him that cargo carrying high-powered [[Originium]] products bearing his identification tags are being transported to the levitation layer. Gordon fears that something terrible is about to happen to Propeller Paradise.<ref name="6Aft">[[UR-6/Story#After operation|UR-6 After]]</ref>

==The Price of Dreams==
[[File:UR-Flag_of_Freedom.png|thumb|"We'll overcome everything that tries to block our path and change us, without ever trying to understand us. We're the ones who'll decide Propeller Paradise's path!"]]
Mercia and Væla had also been listening to the hearing from their room. Mercia considers erasing memories about Nasti's hearing to relieve Væla's stress, but decides against taking more of her fading memories. Mercia begins to suspect that Maylander has another reason for being on Propeller Paradise. Her doubts grow when she hears from a fellow agent that the Tin Man already knows about Væla's presence and plans to restrain her. Mercia decides to disobey orders, manipulating the memories of a Maylander squad, giving the opportunity for Væla to go to Nasti's flying tree.<ref name="6Bef"/><ref name="6Aft"/>

Ferne receives a visit from the police, who offer a deal to release her and wipe her criminal records if she presents Propeller Paradise for the expo. Ferne agrees and is brought to the broadcast room to prepare. She is suddenly approached by Gordon, who has changed into a makeshift spacesuit. Gordon wants Ferne to convince as many people as possible to return to the levitation layer, and retrieve all the contraband he sold. Propeller Paradise is running low on fuel, but with the smuggled items, they can make a makeshift propulsion system that could turn away the levitating plate from Max D.C..<ref name="7Bef">[[UR-7/Story#Before operation|UR-7 Before]]</ref><ref name="8Bef">[[UR-8/Story#Before operation|UR-8 Before]]</ref>

High above, Rhine's tree begins to glow and grow unnaturally. Sky is the first to notice the changes and sneaks into the holding cells to meet Nasti, who states that the only way to determine the cause is to check inside the tree. They are interrupted by Gordon, who unlocks the cells and returns Nasti's tools to her. Gordon tells her that Gustave is colluding with Maylander and planning to put the entire park in danger. Just then, explosions detonate across Propeller Paradise, resulting in dozens of pods beginning to fall from the sky. However, some mysterious force is keeping them suspended in mid air, preventing them from crashing onto the lower platforms. Seeing this, Nasti knows she must get to the tree as soon as possible.<ref name="7Bef"/>

[[File:68_i08_3.png|thumb|left|"You decided to pay a price for Columbia's progress. You shouldn't be allowed to forget. You should remember the lives and dreams that were part of the sacrifice."]]
Maylander is bewildered that the bombs have failed, but Tin Man recognises it must be the work of Væla's incantations, which have fundamentally changed how the levitation layer facilities are connected. He summons Mercia to a closed room and addresses her insubordination. He then reveals Maylander's true objective to her: from Maylander's perspective, the sky fever is a massive waste of time and money. As Columbians dream about living in the sky, other nations like Leithanien and Kazimierz have rapidly been making progress. Maylander's goal is to destroy Propeller Paradise and frame it as sabotage by "foreign spies", so that the Columbians' fanatic zeal for the sky will be turned into a burning rage towards their "enemies" on the ground. The remains of Propeller Paradise will become a military base, and the people's hatred will fuel the fire of Columbia's arms race.<ref name="7Bef"/><ref name="7Aft">[[UR-7/Story#After operation|UR-7 After]]</ref>

The Tin Man reminds Mercia that she is [[Maylander Selene]]'s adopted granddaughter, and that she has a sworn duty to her family and country. Mercia disagrees with him, arguing that countless people's dreams and livelihoods needn't be sacrificed for the sake of Columbia's progress. She uses her Arts to bind him and his [[Revenant]] friends in their memories in order to buy time for the others. On the levitation layer, Rhine Lab's scientists and testers scurry around to retrieve the contraband items from the exhibits. Maylander eventually tracks them down and form a blockade, but Ferne rallies them to fight head-on. Everyone battles against Maylander's agents as Ferne hurries to deliver the contraband items to Gordon.<ref name="8Bef"/>

[[File:68_i07.png|thumb|"Did you forget that Gordon Tannen is a bandit who never gives a damn about the rules?"]]
Gordon meets with Gustave at a restaurant located at the pinnacle of Propeller Paradise. Gustave reveals that he knew about Gordon's smuggling from the very beginning, and even Gordon's position as chief executive was a "gift" from him. All of that had been to place culpability onto Gordon. Gordon isn't going down without a fight though; the park broadcast suddenly activates ahead of schedule, and Gordon orders Space Fin 054 to incapacitate Gustave while he is distracted. Gustave's defeat can be seen on every TV screen in the park. Gordon waits for Ferne, but Gustave's scientific consultant appears instead—with the contraband goods she was supposed to bring.<ref name="7Aft"/><ref name="8Bef"/><ref name="8Aft">[[UR-8/Story#After operation|UR-8 After]]</ref>

Just when Gustave thought he had gotten the upper hand, the consultant hands the goods to Gordon. It turns out the consultant was none other than [[Andenate Maryam]], Director of Rhine Lab's Scientific Investigation Section. Maryam had never really intended to collaborate with Gustave. With the parts, Gordon is able to build the propulsion system, and begins to turn Propeller Paradise away from Max D.C..<ref name="8Bef"/>

==A Banshee's Farewell Elegy==
[[File:68_i11_3.png|thumb|Mother and daughter gaze at each other silently, as they hoped it would make this moment an eternity.]]
[[File:68_i11_1.png|thumb|If this tree is the new abode for the souls of the Sarkaz, then Væla will be its first denizen.]]
Nasti successfully reaches the tree platform and enters the inner chamber to check the data for any issues. She discovers Væla has fused with the tree, turning her into an ethereal being covered in white branches and resin. The tree has shared Nasti's memories with Væla, showing the path she once walked. She confesses that her deepest regret was the creation of the Convallis, in the aftermath of the Eastern Crusade against Kazdel. She strived to create a home for the Banshees that was free of death, concealing the tokens that marked the war's casualties for two centuries. Væla had hoped that Nasti would live in the Convallis forever, but even a child like her, who had lived in the wartorn streets of Kazdel, could see how fake life felt there.<ref name="7Aft"/><ref name="8Bef"/>

Væla was relieved when Nasti had left, because Nasti will never expose the truth about how harsh the outside world was like to the other Banshees. On the other hand, her departure proved that the Convallis' purpose had failed, a fragile paradise that could never house a Sarkaz from outside. When Væla began showing signs of forgetfulness, she moved into the Roosting Tree, where all Banshees slowly met their ends. However, she was filled with guilt over her failure, and ran away from her sanctuary. She followed Nasti's path, from Leithanien to Laterano, and eventually into Columbia. When Væla saw that Nasti had planted another Roosting Tree, she was afraid Nasti would repeat her mistakes. To reverse its decay, she fused her body with the tree and rewrote its operating logic.<ref name="8Bef"/><ref name="8Aft"/>

Nasti realises that Væla's consciousness is now part of the tree, and casts an incantation that begins to wipe the preloaded data from the tree. Væla is initially horrified, but Nasti tells her that she had never stopped searching for a way to make Væla happy. That was why she had devoted so much time to researching engineering, for the Sarkaz's promised land could also be in the sky. The shape of Rhine's tree itself was inspired from the Roosting Tree in the Convallis. Unlike the Convallis tree though, it will serve as both a home for the dead and the living, and will contain joy as well. In her last moments, Væla recalls the good memories that once made her smile, before fading away. All that's left behind is a leaf. Nasti vows to remember her mother and blows a gentle tune with the leaf.<ref name="8Aft"/>

==Good Night, Propeller Paradise==
[[File:UR-Paradise_Lost.png|thumb|left|The remains of Propeller Paradise are scattered across the barrenlands, like gifts that have been returned.]]
Maylander agents successfully routed all of Rhine Lab's people, but realise too late that the levitating plate was starting to move away from Max D.C.. They change plans and activate the bombs, knocking out the levitation layer's entire power grid. Scientists and businessmen alike rush to safety as the ground literally starts falling beneath their feet. Millions of people in Max D.C. watched in shock as hundreds of pods become flaming meteors in the sky. Columbian news outlets are quick to accuse foreign nations of this "blatant act of terrorism", and the people wait for Vice President [[Conrad Jackson]] to speak about the disaster.<ref name="8Aft"/>

Inside the State Building, VP Jackson and former Rhine HR director [[Jara Wilson]] watch the fiery wreckage falling apart in the distance. Jackson is surprised to see one structure still flying: Rhine Lab's tree. The Department of Defense argues that they should stick to the established narrative and shoot it down, while Maylander proposes that they can dress it up as a miracle. However, Jara says that Rhine's tree is just what it looks like: a tree where fowlbeasts can roost. Its existence requires no complicated justification whatsoever. Jackson is moved by her words, and exits the building to formally make his public speech.<ref name="8Aft"/>

[[File:68_i14_2.png|thumb|"Rhine Lab has already made preparations, but that alone is not enough. We must respond to the fear of the unknown, and go boldly where no one has gone before..."]]
In the end, the remains of Propeller Paradise crash landed in the barrens before it could reach the border. Gustave was arrested over its destruction instead of Gordon while Sky is placed in charge of Propeller Paradise's project management. Mercia visits Væla's last resting place at the Rhine tree before returning to Maylander. Jackson's tear-jerking speech shot his popularity to new heights, and his words saved Rhine's flying tree from the DOD and Maylander's scheme. [[Saria]] negotiates with the vice president to lift the restrictions on Rhine Lab and allow the levitating plate to return to Max D.C. for repairs.<ref name="ST3">[[UR-ST-3]]</ref>

The leaders of Rhine meet up at their headquarters and discuss their findings, from the [[Retracing Our Steps/Synopsis|recent Catastrophe in Kjerag]] to the [[Stargate|massive superstructure]] in the [[Infy Icefield]]. More recently, science teams have disappeared in the [[Black Flow]] of [[Bolívar]], and [[Magallan]] is already on her way to investigate. No matter what happens, no matter what secrets they will discover, Rhine Lab will continue facing the unknown, until they find the truth behind Terra's most well-hidden secrets.<ref name="ST3"/>
