==Synopsis==
===Silent North===
[[File:66 i03.png|thumb|Rhodes Island returns to Ursus, where things seem far too quiet.]]
Following the destruction of the [[Rhodes Island (landship)|Rhodes Island landship]], the company rebuilds their new headquarters on a city plate in Butchart, [[Victoria]]. The [[Doctor]], [[Amiya]], and [[Mon3tr]] conduct a meeting about their mission to bring Kal'tsit back, which requires a [[Sarcophagus]]. They discover that the Sarcophagus in [[Chernobog]] has since been recovered by the [[Ursus]] government and its present location remains unknown. Their only lead is a woman related to the Sarcophagus's previous research team, a researcher by the name of [[Snegurochka|Ksenia]]. Considering their limited intelligence about Ursus's current state, [[Raidian]] volunteers to go ahead, as her squad specialises in intel gathering.<ref name="16-3">[[16-3/Story|16-3]]</ref>

A couple of months later, Rhodes Island loses contact with Raidian's squad. Their last known coordinates is somewhere within the Central Far Northern Mining District, one of the biggest mining territories in Ursus. In autumn 1101, the RI team head to the Breskenoy Governorate and arrive in its capital city, [[Bresk]]. There, they meet an Imperial Guard Sergeant named [[Eloysius Gorchekova]], who informs them about rumors of "mutant [[Infected]]" in the mining district exhibiting symptoms very similar to the ones described in Rhodes Island's latest [[Oripathy]] research paper. The governor, [[Elisabeth|Grand Duchess Elisabeth]], had recently made a sudden decree that all residents living within thirty klicks of the Central Mining District had to evacuate the area. Since then, the mining district has been under total lockdown, and any inquiries have been met with silence.<ref>[[16-2/Story|16-2]]</ref><ref name="16-4b">[[16-4/Story#Before operation|16-4 Before]]</ref>

[[File:66 i05.png|thumb|left|The Grand Duchess Elisaveta Konstantinovna Lacitsara welcomes Rhodes Island to Bresk.]]
Eloysius uses her status to arrange a meeting with Grand Duchess Elisabeth. The Doctor explains the circumstances of their visit in detail and requests permission to enter the Far North Mining District. Although the Grand Duchess initially appears reluctant, Eloysius and the Doctor are able to persuade her to grant access to Rhodes Island.<ref name="16-4b"/>

Upon arriving in the central mining district, Mantra detects traces of [[Dominion]] around, the trademark signature of an [[Emperor's Blade]].<ref>[[16-1]]</ref> They quickly head to the Central Far North Mining District Laboratory where the abnormal Infected are supposed to be kept, only to find it completely deserted. Amiya and Eloysius search the morgue in the basement where, much to their horror, discover coffins containing non-Infected, each corpse twisted beyond recognition. Meanwhile, Mon3tr and Doctor search the upper floors, and discover the researcher they are looking for, Ksenia, about to burn herself alive. They manage to stop her in time and learn about a mysterious substance known as [[Originium#Oka|Oka]] that was discovered in the mines.<ref name="16-4a">[[16-4/Story#After operation|16-4 After]]</ref>

Ksenia claims that she witnessed the local miners kill the Royal Guard and flee south with Raidian's squad. The real reason for the lockdown is becoming clear: the Grand Duchess is trying to cover up the fact that a Royal Guard died in her domain. Ksenia is alive but still delirious, so the Doctor and Mon3tr decide to stay behind with the researcher while Amiya and the others pursue the escaped miners. Following the clues the miners left behind, the group begin to grasp the full picture...<ref name="16-4a"/><ref name="16-5">[[16-5/Story|16-5]]</ref>

===Red Snow===
Five days before Amiya's group arrived at the mining district, the mine foreman, [[Rankin]], leads a massive group consisting of hundreds of miners on a great campaign towards the Ursus-[[Kazimierz]] border, where he has heard of [[Reunion]] establishing a village for Infected there. Amongst his group is former city boy [[Nikto]], [[Vetochki]] the bell toller, and her grandmother Nadia. However, the journey is not easy, as the miners constantly run into Patrol Units who block their way.<ref name="16-3"/>

Rankin eventually learns from a captive guard that the chips planted in the miners' bodies—initially thought to just be an infection monitor—also transmits geolocation and will explode if it detects them leaving the mining district. Rankin thus requests the help of Raidian and her squad to extract the chips. Raidian creates a decoy signal to prevent the chips from exploding while her squadmates conducts surgeries on the miners.<ref name="16-3"/> Two days later, the miners use the extracted chips to set up an ambush for the Patrol Units, taking their equipment and supplies. The truck driver turns out to be the son of Hawley, one of the miners. Rankin forces Hawley to make a choice between killing or releasing him. Refusing to believe that his son would commit such evil deeds, Hawley brutally beats him to death.<ref name="16-5"/><ref name="16-6">[[16-6/Story|16-6]]</ref>

[[File:66 i08 1.png|thumb|The campfire should have brought warmth, but every part of Vetochki's body feels cold.]]
After wandering for three more days, the miners eventually arrive at a village where they trade parts for food.<ref>[[16-8/Story|16-8]]</ref> They decide to settle down for the night, but Hawley goes insane and sets fire to the houses while everyone is asleep.<ref name="16-9">[[16-9/Story|16-9]]</ref> The villagers, enraged by their kindness being repaid by betrayal, demand the death of Hawley. Rankin blames himself as the source of Hawley's madness and offers his own life instead, but the villagers refuse, so Rankin publicly executes the mad miner. Rankin then realises he has been forcing his ideals onto people and tells his followers to make a choice: stay with him and march a dangerous path towards freedom, or remain in Ursus.<ref name="16-10">[[16-10/Story|16-10]]</ref>

===Black River===
After Ksenia begins to recover, the Doctor and Mon3tr ask more questions about what happened in the lab. Ksenia explains that approximately a month ago, Oka was brought to the Central Far North Mining District Laboratory. Infected miners exposed to Oka displayed abnormally high infection readings yet remained physically healthy. They soon received orders from the Grand Duchess to immediately start human testing. Ksenia attempted to defy orders and was excluded from being involved in the experiments.<ref name="16-7">[[16-7/Story|16-7]]</ref><ref name="16-12">[[16-12/Story|16-12]]</ref>

[[File:66 i01.png|thumb|left|Rankin had slain an Emperor's Blade, a sight that Ksenia will never forget.]]
What she didn't realise was that the miners and their families were forcibly conscripted as test subjects, whether they were Infected or non-Infected. Those who were not Infected suffered rejection and died, becoming horribly twisted abnormalities. It was Vetochki who first discovered that the missing miners never returned and reported it to Rankin. Rankin thus spurred the miners into a rebellion, attacking the labs and chasing off the researchers. Rankin, who had been exposed to Oka, even managed to kill an Emperor's Blade, the most fearsome soldier of the Ursus Royal Guard.<ref name="16-7"/><ref name="16-9"/><ref name="16-12"/>

Doctor and Mon3tr later go down to the mines to investigate. They discover an obsequent river at the deepest section of the mines, flowing with black liquid. Mon3tr deduces the true nature of Oka–it is the liquefied form of [[Originium]]. While it still retains its ability as an information carrier, it can no longer be harvested for energy. This unique attribute means that it can neutralise Originium activity in the Infected, removing the pain that comes with Oripathy while it continues to spread in the body. The presence of Oka is a clear indication that the [[Originium Prime Vein#Kolossal|Kolossal Prime Vein]], the lifeblood of Ursus, was rapidly shrinking. Without the mines, Ursus will inevitably collapse.<ref name="16-12"/>

[[File:66 i06.png|thumb|Within these mines is the dark secret Ursus is so desperately trying to hide.]]
Further back, the group find a waterfall that is vibrating at specific frequencies, saying a single word in multiple languages: Debate. It is an invitation from [[Priestess]], the mastermind behind the Kolossal Prime Vein's decline. As they attempt to leave the mines, they encounter [[Rampaging Abnormal Manglerbeast|a humongous enraged manglerbeast]]. Originium has penetrated its organs and transformed it into an aberration. After managing to stop its rampage, Mon3tr puts it out of its misery.<ref name="16-13">[[16-13/Story|16-13]]</ref>

After exiting the mines, the Doctor comes to the conclusion that the Armies plan to silence everyone in the Far North. Just then, the skies turn red at the Armies's arrival. They rush back into the lab and manage to send an emergency broadcast before an explosion blasts the room into pieces.<ref name="16-15">[[16-15/Story|16-15]]</ref>

===Endure Pain Without End===
Rhodes Island and Eloysius follow a beacon that Raidian left behind and the vehicle tracks in the snow, before eventually reaching the ruins of a recently destroyed village. They are timidly approached by Vetochki, who notices that they wear similar uniforms to Raidian. She leads them to a house where Raidian and some of the miners are currently huddled up. Raidian is relieved to see Mantra and Amiya here and informs them about Rankin's actions for the past week.<ref name="16-11">[[16-11/Story|16-11]]</ref>

[[File:66 i09.png|thumb|left|"Desire not your own life, decide not your own death."]]
[[File:66 i10 2.png|thumb|left|"Love makes us endure, makes us believe, makes us persevere."]]
The miner rebellion has been split into two: Rankin, Nikto, and those brave enough continued further south towards Lake Neva, while Raidian and her squad stayed with Vetochki, Nadia, and the other miners in the village. Eloysius boldly proclaims that they should escort the miners to Deity Grypherburg, where they will be placed under witness protection by the State Council and testify against the Grand Duchess. Raidian is unwilling to trust an Imperial Guardswoman, but since Amiya is vouching for her, she tries to convince the other miners. However, everyone except for Vetochki appears completely unresponsive. A sudden mental attack penetrates her mind, so powerful that even Amiya and Mantra can sense it from outside. Mantra recognises that it can only be one entity: [[Yurodstvo]], a mystifiying holy figure of ancient Ursine folklore.<ref name="16-11"/>

Mantra enters the house and witnesses the miners worshipping the blurry red figure of Nadia in the corner. Mantra is able to confirm her suspicions: the Yurodstvo hid amongst the miners and helped them kill the Royal Guard. Since the Yurodstvo relies on the cognition of others to establish itself, Mantra focuses on the key to its establishment of the 'Nadia' identity: Vetochki.<ref name="16-13"/> Mantra projects her voice into Vetochki's mind and helps her see through the illusion. The Yurodstvo then reveals its true form and engages in a psychic war against Mantra. Within the cognitive realm, Mantra tries to build a barrier to shield the miners from its influence, but a split second of weakness allows the Yurodstvo to break through.<ref name="16-15"/><ref name="16-16">[[16-16/Story|16-16]]</ref>

Raidian suddenly receives the emergency broadcast sent by the Doctor: Ursus's Orginium veins are drying up, and the armies are trying to hide the truth. Eloysius insists that Deity Grypherburg will be safe from the advancing armies, but Rhodes Island is still distrustful of her. Thus, she decides to reveal that she had always intended to approach Rhodes Island in order to meet Kal'tsit, who was involved with the Chernobog Sarcophagus research. Although she has no desire to abandon the miners to their deaths, she can only afford to wait another hour.<ref name="16-16"/>

===A Voice to Cry Suffering===
[[File:66 i21.png|thumb|"Choose to live, when living may be worse than dying."]]
Mantra tries again, this time using her mental Arts to show the miners that the Yurodstvo has been controlling them from behind the scenes. Vetochki helps her by using her bell to awaken the miners from their stupor. Vetochki rejects the Yurodstvo and it seemingly disappears. There is no time to rest though; the sounds of cannonfire reverberate through the taiga. Eloysius states that she has vehicles stationed in Lake Neva, and the group make haste.<ref name="16-17">[[16-17/Story|16-17]]</ref>

Far ahead, Rankin, Nikto, and the rest of the miners arrive at the edge of Lake Neva. Across the lake is Deity Grypherburg, and behind it lies the border. However there is an outpost on the lake with an estimated 100 guards stationed there. Rankin follows a hidden mining tunnel in hopes of circumventing the outpost, but discovers that it has collapsed. On the other hand, he discovers a colony of hundreds of miners living in secret underground. He rallies them together to capture the outpost, so that they may finally have a shot at freedom.<ref>[[16-14/Story|16-14]]</ref>

[[File:66 i14.png|thumb|left|Only now do they understand what they are dealing with: Ursus.]]
Equipped with weapons they raided from the Patrol Units, the miners charge the outpost. Nikto, fearful for his life, flees as the group launches their assault. Rankin is badly injured but survives. Although half of them didn't make it, the miners have successfully captured the outpost. Their dreams of revolution are soon dashed when they spot the shadow of an enormous military vessel approaching them. The sky glows blood red from the warning lights in the distance, a portent of doom. Without a second thought, the army bombards the outpost and everyone inside into smithereens.<ref name="16-16"/>

Raidian intercepts internal communications from the military, and discovers a horrifying truth: the Ursus armies are purging all the mining districts across the Far North. It becomes abhorrently clear that Ursus is willing to do anything to suppress information about the mines' exhaustion, even if it has to slaughter tens of thousands. The Grand Duchess has subtly instigated the Fourth Army–who have long viewed the current Tsar as a weakling–into committing a massacre under the pretense of suppressing an Infected uprising. Rhodes Island knows that they cannot let the truth be buried. Although Mantra is still exhausted after her fight with the Yurodstvo, she uses the fullest extent of her Arts to expand their communication range. With Raidian and Elysium's help, Mantra and Vetochki broadcast their voices to all of Ursus and expose the nefarious atrocities taking place in the Far North.<ref name="16-17"/>

[[File:66 i24.png|thumb|Until the flames cease, until the score is settled—None shall pass.]]
The Fourth Army continues shelling indiscriminately as Rhodes Island tries to evacuate everyone across the ice. Some of the miners decide to stay behind and sacrifice themselves so that others can make it. Even as the ice cracks beneath them, even as they drown in frigid waters, they continue to shout and raise their torches in defiance. Feeling the miners' intense anger, Amiya finally unleashes her repressed emotions and summons a great wall of blue fire with Ying Xiao. Just then, the Doctor, Mon3tr, and Operator [[Crownslayer]] drive a truck onto the lake to rescue the remaining survivors. As the Fourth Army glares at the blaze separating them from their targets, they receive orders from Deity Grypherburg to immediately cease fire. The Fourth Army has no choice but to back off.<ref>[[16-18/Story|16-18]]</ref>

Everyone sighs in relief; they will live to see another day.

===The Embers of Revolution===
[[File:66 i12.png|thumb|left|The armies crack down on innocent protestors as civil unrest continues spreading throughout Ursus.]]
After arriving safely in Deity Grypherburg, Eloysius informs her superior, Chairman [[Islam Witte]] of the Ursus State Council, with details on the Far North Incident. The surviving miners have been placed under witness protection and will testify against Grand Duchess Elisabeth for the damage she has done in Breskenoy. Newspapers and talk shows discuss the ore exhaustion non-stop. Students, workers, and even wealthy citizens begin to question the empire's policies. In exchange for all their help, Eloysius secretly gives away information about the Sarcophagus to Rhodes Island.<ref name="16-19">[[16-19]]</ref>

The Ursus Imperial Army is once again fractured, like it has been during the [[Great Rebellion]]. As the mines begin to reopen gradually in the Northern Tundra, the Fourth Army continues its illegal presence on the border of the Central Governorate, and the Third Army is starting to make its move. The First Army is conscripting dozens of students from universities without authorisation, resulting in countless bloody protests. Under this immense pressure, Deity Grypherburg has mobilized the Second and Seventh Armies to control the situation.<ref name="16-19"/>

Some of the other miners from Rankin's group who survived the massacre at Lake Neva eventually made it to the Ursus-Kazimierz border, and tell Reunion about what happened in the Far North.<ref>[[EG-7]]</ref> Somewhere else in the Northern Tundra, a group of miners were brought to Lake Angara to be executed, when the sunken nomadic city of Simonch suddenly rises through the ice, swallowing everyone.<ref>[[EG-8]]</ref> [[Hierda]] and her congregation wander around Ursus, continuing to bestow "miracles" upon those afflicted by Oripathy.<ref>[[EG-10]]</ref>

Slow, gradual improvement is not enough for the pressing needs of Ursus's development. Things must change, before even more people are fed into the hungry machine of the Empire...
