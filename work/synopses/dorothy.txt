==Red Alert==

===The Silver Trail===
[[File:Oath of the Grass and Mountain.png|thumb|Different paths always lead to the same goal]]
In the city of [[Trimounts]], [[Saria]] has been conducting a private investigation into whatever’s been going on at Rhine Lab. She plans to meet with [[Muelsyse]], Director of Rhine’s Ecological Section, who has been secretly providing inside information to her even after her unofficial resignation. However, after receiving a distress signal from her, Saria urgently contacts Rhodes Island for help.<ref name="ST1">[[DV-ST-1]]</ref> At almost the exact same time, Operator [[Ptilopsis]] and her co-worker [[Astgenne|Elena]] are taken hostage at one of Rhine’s test sites. The timing of events is too close to be called a coincidence, so the Doctor, accompanied by [[Elite Op]]erator [[Mechanist]], decides to personally assist Saria in the investigation.<ref name="1Bef">[[DV-1/Story#Before operation|DV-1 Before]]</ref>

Meanwhile, Doctor [[Silence|Olivia Silence]] and engineer [[Greyy the Lightningbearer|Greyy]], are dispatched to handle the hostage situation at Site 359, a research lab located far in the wilderness of [[Columbia]]. A group of pioneers staying there led by [[Sonny Romano]] have forcefully taken control of the base. The police, led by [[Mary Banner]], as well as Rhine’s Defense forces, have surrounded the perimeter and are preparing to cut out lights and communications to the pioneers’ quarters. However, Ptilopsis gets a sudden [[Oripathy]] attack, causing the pioneers to panic. As Ptilopsis’s physician, Silence negotiates with Sonny over the radio to allow her and Greyy into the base immediately. In exchange, she promises to provide free Oripathy treatment to all Infected pioneers.<ref name="1Aft">[[DV-1/Story#After operation|DV-1 After]]</ref>

Silence works to stabilize Ptilopsis’s condition while Greyy restores power to the pioneers’ quarter. Their work is interrupted when strange, amorphous silver constructs appear and begin attacking the group.<ref name="2Bef">[[DV-2/Story#Before operation|DV-2 Before]]</ref> These "objects" are called Transmitters, a byproduct of Rhine’s experiments designed to receive and encode neural signals, originally designed to replace traditional [[Arts]] units.<ref name="5Aft">[[DV-5/Story#After operation|DV-5 After]]</ref> They appear to be sentient and possess surprising coordination, their corrosive nature allowing them to reconstruct any matter, even an entire building. However, they seem to lack true intelligence, and Greyy is able to provide cover by using his bright Arts to lure them away while the others retreat to the central laboratory.<ref name="2Aft">[[DV-2/Story#After operation|DV-2 After]]</ref>

Sonny, Silence, and the others hear a broadcast from Dorothy, the Director of Originium Arts and overseer of Site 359. She calls them to come to her office, promising that they will be safe inside. Silence is unsure how to proceed, as both Elena and Sonny share different perspectives about what Dorothy has been doing at the research base. Sonny reiterates that he wants to put a stop to the mad experiments Dorothy has been doing, which has caused so many of his friends to disappear. Ultimately, Silence states that to find the truth, they have to meet Dorothy first.<ref name="3Bef">[[DV-3/Story#Before operation|DV-3 Before]]</ref>

===Collective Consciousness===
Obeying Dorothy’s instructions, the group eventually arrive at her office, where she has been awaiting them. Sonny then reveals his true intentions, ordering the pioneers to shoot her and destroy the lab.<ref name="5Bef">[[DV-5/Story#Before operation|DV-5 Before]]</ref> However, Dorothy’s Arts easily repel their attacks, and the Transmitter aberrations soon converge inside the office to protect their creator. Sonny accuses Dorothy of not caring about them, but the latter objects to his accusations, firmly believing that this experiment is for the greater good for civilization. Moreover, she states that if it succeeds, the pioneers will no longer have to roam the wastes and be subjected to harshness of the environment.<ref name="5Aft">[[DV-5/Story#After operation|DV-5 After]]</ref>

[[File:Questionable Ethics.png|thumb|Dorothy may believe that what she does is for the greater good, but others see her as trying to rob their free will]]

Just then, Ptilopsis suddenly wakes up from her coma and points to a section of the room. Following her hint, Elena uses her Arts to break open a secret door. Stepping inside, Elena is horrified to discover both Sonny’s pioneer friends and her lab colleagues, lying on metal beds and connected to a large central device. The true nature of the experiments have been revealed: the Transmitters are actually linked together with the test subjects’ consciousness, combining to create a sort of "collective consciousness." They no longer need biological necessities but will live inside an eternal "Dreamland," safe from harm. On one hand, Dorothy believes that by synchronising everyone’s minds in this Hub, they will be able to create a completely equal and fair world. On the other hand, Silence believes that this technology could be a catastrophic weapon in the wrong hands. Even Sonny agrees, saying that this “utopia” is nothing more than a cold monoculture experiment that will wipe out humanity’s individuality and rob people of their freedom.<ref>[[DV-6/Story#Before operation|DV-6 Before]]</ref>

As Silence deliberates on what they should do, Ptilopsis suddenly speaks out. A special neural chip (Device #9) implanted inside her brain is somehow able to temporarily link with the collective consciousness, allowing Ptilopsis to sense their emotions and thoughts. Ptilopsis says that all the test subjects willingly chose to participate in the experiment despite the risks. Still, Silence is reminded of what happened to [[Ifrit]] in the [[Rhine Lab#Diabolic Crisis|Diabolic Crisis]], and believes that stopping the experiments is the correct course of action. Dorothy finally relents, suspending the project and freeing the test subjects connected to the Transmitters.<ref>[[DV-6/Story#After operation|DV-6 After]]</ref>

==Awakening==
===The Hidden Hand===
[[File:Honey and Poison.png|thumb|left|"Word of caution: Don't dig into this matter deeper, or you'll regret it."]]
Meanwhile, back at Trimounts, the Doctor, Saria, and Mechanist follow the trail of Muelsyse’s disappearance to an incredibly advanced power armour unit controlled by Transmitters.<ref name="2Aft"/> Upon uncovering this, Saria decides to meet an ex-employee of Loken Watertank who may know something about the liquids. Their meeting is interrupted when a strange mercenary known as [[Ho'olheyak]] suddenly appears and discreetly threatens the informant. Believing that she must be hiding key intel, Saria and the others give chase and follow her to a bunch of power armours waiting for them in an alleyway.<ref name="4Bef">[[DV-4/Story#Before operation|DV-4 Before]]</ref>

Unexpectedly, they find the missing Muelsyse trapped inside one of the power armours and manage to save her. Upon regaining consciousness, Muelsyse informs them about the abnormality in Site 359 outside Trimounts. Dorothy isn’t the real mastermind; it is her co-worker, [[Ferdinand Clooney]], the Director of Energy, who has been secretly making deals with a high-ranking [[Blake|”Colonel”]] within the Columbian army. He is most likely offering the Transmitters as a weapon, and the power armors are proof of that. If the experiment there is completed, Ferdinand will gain enough support to seize control of Rhine Lab and solidify his position as the company’s head. In a bid to stop this, the group hurries to Rhine’s headquarters to meet the Control of Rhine Lab–[[Kristen Wright]].<ref name="5Bef"/>

No one at Site 359 is aware of this conspiracy. Elena watches as one by one, the pioneers surrender themselves to Rhine’s Defense forces. As they are escorted off the premises, the pioneers receive special injections coupled with treatment bands as a safety precaution. However, the Rhodes Island’s Operators familiar with Rhine’s protocols realize that this isn’t part of normal procedure.<ref>[[DV-6/Story|DV-6]]</ref> Elena manages to stop Sonny from taking the injection, but it’s too late for everyone else–they suddenly fall into a coma as they are reconnected with the Transmitter’s Hub. Following a sudden earthquake, [[Awaken (enemy)|a giant formless cluster]] made of silver fluid rises into the air, devouring the laboratory in its wake. It completely defies any previously known laws of Originium Arts.<ref name="7Bef">[[DV-7/Story#Before operation|DV-7 Before]]</ref>

===Dreamland===
[[File:The Awakening.png|thumb|Dorothy's Dreamland has awakened... but at what cost?]]

Ferdinand has taken complete control of the project; the director is now going to demonstrate to the military just how powerful this creation is. Rhine Lab’s Defense forces throw bombs to provoke the construct, causing it to unleash its energy beams at Silence and the others, consuming any object nearby. Silence comes up with a plan to reestablish communications with Rhodes Island and inform them of what’s happening here. Even though Defense has sealed off the site, Ptilopsis' Device #9 does not rely on regular data transmission methods.<ref name="7Bef"/> Meanwhile, Elena gets into Mary’s vehicle and drives to the nearby observation station to confront her superior, Ferdinand. She expresses disgust at his manipulation, even punching him in the face. Ferdinand remains undeterred, foreseeing that this is the beginning of a new era where Columbia will be exalted as a superpower.<ref name="7Aft">[[DV-7/Story#After operation|DV-7 After]]</ref><ref name="8Bef">[[DV-8/Story#Before operation|DV-8 Before]]</ref>

[[File:29 i06.png|thumb|left|Everyone is equal and free of discord in the Dreamland. However, even its own creator realizes that's too good to be true…]]

As the cluster mercilessly expands, there is only one option that could stop its rampage. Dorothy willingly connects with the Dreamland by injecting a Transmitter into herself. Inside the Dreamland, Dorothy witnesses the utopia she yearns for with her own two eyes, a beautiful landscape that stretches endlessly into the horizon. However, although her "paradise" at first appears to be idyllic, her beliefs are shaken when the collective consciousness expresses that they would rather face the challenges of [[Terra]] and advance onwards towards their goals, rather than live an eternal life of peace with no change or progress. Dorothy realizes that she shouldn't have the right to command other people's futures and that she should instead give them the chance to witness their future. Her emotions resonate with the collective consciousness, and together they destroy the Hub. The cluster gradually collapses into nothingness, along the vision of her dream.<ref name="8Aft">[[DV-8/Story#After operation|DV-8 After]]</ref>

Ferdinand is shocked, but he doesn’t get the chance to process his defeat when the Columbian military breaks into the observation station to arrest him. He tries contacting the "Colonel,” but realizes that the military has abandoned him the moment the project failed. He escapes using a secret trapdoor and is forced to humble himself by hiding inside a power armor suit and flee into the wastes. Sonny bids his farewell to Mary and leads his pioneers into the frontiers to meet the so-called "[[Dijkstra|Mr. D]]." The clean-up begins under Muelsyse's supervision to avoid further leak of the experiment's information. While the Transmitter remains inside Dorothy's body, she has no regret but sees it as being part of herself, an unfulfilled dream.<ref name="ST2">[[DV-ST-2]]</ref>

==Looking at the Same Sky==
Saria, Doctor, and Muelsyse infiltrate Rhine Lab’s headquarters, which is packed full of armed forces. Muelsyse accidentally triggers the security alarm, leading to an intense brawl with Rhine’s Defense soldiers.<ref name="7Aft"/> Saria manages to break through the blockade and reach Control’s office, meeting her former partner, Kristen Wright. She states that she never authorised the experiments at Site 359, but it is apparent that she doesn’t care anyway. However, Rhodes Island has received a message from Ptilopsis containing sensitive information about the Transmitter project, which they threaten to leak to Rhine’s rivals or even government bureaus. As a result, Kristen is forced to contact Ho’olyehak, who is actually an agent of [[Maylander]], a deep state organization possessing close ties with Columbia’s government.<ref name="8Aft"/>

The situation is quietly resolved behind closed doors as full authorization of Site 359 is handed over to the state. Ho'olheyak personally meets Kristen to discuss their report to the government and extends an offer of cooperation as the president of the Columbian Astrological Research Association. While Kristen herself does not care much, each step further accelerates her ultimate goal &ndash; to pierce the sky and [[Lone Trail|explore the untouched beyond]].<ref name="ST2"/>
