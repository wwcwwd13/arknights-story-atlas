==Synopsis==
===Ad Sanctuarium===
Late September 1099, Pope [[Yvangelista XI]] suddenly announced the anointment of a [[Lateran]] Saint. To further surprise the Lateran citizens, the new Saint is none other than [[Executor|Federico Giallo]] &mdash; the stern Executor of the Notarial Hall. This is the first time in Lateran history for a non-Papal figure to be anointed as a Saint, the most honored title in Laterano. According to the Pope's explanation, he received revelation from "[[The Law]]" that danger is coming soon, and Laterano must act quick for the preservation of the [[Sankta]] race. He wishes for Federico to lead the Laterans through the uncertain future.<ref name=":ST1">[[HE-ST-1]]</ref>

[[File:39 i01.png|thumb|The ''Sanctilamilum Ambrosii'' is once a pride of Laterano's and Iberia's diplomacy, but now it is left stranded to rot in the wilderness]]
The Pope then hands the newly anointed Saint his first mission: retrieving the ''[[Sanctilaminium Ambrosii]]'', a nomadic abbey built by a joint Lateran-[[Iberia]]n project that went missing after the [[Profound Silence]] around six decades ago. Several days ago, Laterano received an emergency signal to Lateran requesting for aid, as the monastery was stranded in the barrenlands an running out of food. However, after [[Lemuen]] and [[Oren Argiolas|Oren]] were dispatched there, they went missing in action. Thus, Federico is tasked to both help the abbey and search for the missing envoys.<ref name=":ST1"/>

A team of trio, Federico, [[Spuria|Serpilia]], and [[Insider|Richele]], drives to the spot where the stranded monastery is located. They first make contact with the abbot [[Stefano Torregrossa]] and [[Clément Dubois]], an [[Elafia]]n gardener. The trio try to calm down the abbey's inhabitants by stating their intention, but the monastery's residents were surprisingly unwelcome. Just then, a group of bandits rush to attack the monastery, so the trio help deal with the troublesome bandits. Abbot Stefano then allows the group into the monastery.<ref>[[HE-1/Story#Before operation|HE-1 Before]]</ref>

===Fragilis Spera===
Federico's investigation reveals the monastery's situation to be more complicated than expected. For starters, the Ambrosii was sheltering many [[Sarkaz]] refugees and even [[Kazdel]]ian ex-mercenaries.<ref>[[HE-1/Story#After operation|HE-1 After]]</ref> Such action is considered a grave violation of the Lateran law, but the local Sankta consider the Sarkaz as friends. Lemuen and Oren had disagreed with this, so the two nuncios were placed under custody, but Oren managed to escape and has disappeared. Stefano pleads with the Federico to allow the Sarkaz into the Holy City, but he bluntly refuses.

In addition, Abbot Stefano has been hiding two mysterious guests from the Lateran envoys. One is [[Virtuosa|Arturia Giallo]], the heinous Lateran criminal and Federico's cousin, who claims that she is just here to "play some music". However, the residents' frequent contact with her begin to stir their long-buried emotions. The other is [[Aulus]], a bishop of the [[Church of the Deep]]. Stefano secretly requests help from Aulus to maintain harmony between the monastery's residents by using the collective consciousness of the [[Seaborn]]. Despite being a senior member of the Church, Aulus warns him of the consequences and gives him a day to think through the decision.<ref name=":4a">[[HE-4/Story#Before operation|HE-4 Before]]</ref> Meanwhile, Aulus also has a personal matter to help "someone" bid farewell.

[[File:39 i02.png|thumb|"Tell me, Grandpa Stefano.... What has happened to me?"<br />"My child, I'm afraid it's a sign of sin."|left]]
That afternoon, an unexpected incident worsens the tense atmosphere. Originally wishing to make peace with the envoys, a Sankta resident named [[Fortuna]] has a heated debate with her friend Delfina over the former's wish to leave the abbey. The argument soon turns into bloodshed as Fortuna unintentionally discharges her old patron firearm onto Delfina. Delfina dies as a result, causing Fortuna to become "fallen".<ref>[[HE-3/Story#Before operation|HE-3 Before]]</ref> Fortuna's fall prompts the Lateran trio to contain the situation and reestablish order despite Stefano's protests with their methods. Serpilia puts Fortuna under her supervision to avoid scaring the residents.<ref>[[HE-3/Story#After operation|HE-3 After]]</ref>

While maintaining order, the trio finally gets in touch with Lemuen and Oren. Thanks to Serpilia's help, Lemuen is able to escape from her custody. Oren is still hiding in the building trying to escape, but he leaves a message to Richele that he is on his way to "fully take control" over Ambrosii. Knowing that the situation is irreversible, [[Gerald]], leader of the Sarkaz refugees, decides to lead his people to quietly depart from Ambrosii after the next and last Communion. He thanks Stefano for sheltering them for a decade as well as the abbey's generosity, but believes it is time to leave before their declining friendship with the Sankta is broken. Gerald's decision angers [[Raimund]] the young mercenary, but he begrudgingly accepts the decision.

Up in the monastery's sacrarium, a despondent Clément conveys his worries and despair to Arturia. In response, she plays him a melody, affecting him with her ego liberation Arts. This brings out Clément's extreme nihilistic emotions and prompts to set a trap that would set his beloved sacrarium ablaze. Hearing the sound of a cello, Federico rushes up towards the sacrarium, where he runs into Lemuen chasing a strange sea monster. Lemuen encourages him to trust his instinct and go after the strange noise while she follows the monster into the Sarkaz's residential area.<ref name=":4a"/>

[[File:Incendium.png|thumb|The fire is the final spark to the ignite the monastery residents' anger.]]
Upon arriving at the hall, Federico only finds a pair of [[Liberi]] twins, who ask him to help find their mother [[Hyman]]. While they were talking, Clément's trap is set off and a fire suddenly burns through the abandoned hall.<ref name=":4a"/> Federico saves the twins from the fire as the residents rush to put out the fire. Regrettably, the fire had destroyed the flower garden inside the sacrarium. After the fire is extinguished, everyone tries to figure out who the culprit is. Though lacking any solid proof, the residents start slinging accusations towards Raimund, but he angrily rebukes them. Federico intervenes by stating there was no evidence to suggest any of the Sarkaz were behind it, and not even Sankta should be exempted from suspicion.<ref name=":5a">[[HE-5/Story#Before operation|HE-5 Before]]</ref>

[[File:39 i04.png|thumb|"It seems I have no choice, huh?"|left]]
Nighttime arrives, and everyone head back to their shelters as usual. The Sarkaz residents grow restless, with more believing that they should leave as soon as possible. Federico brings the tired Liberi twins back to the Sarkaz's settlements, which they say to be where their mother will return. Lemuen too patrols around the settlement in search of the sea monster until she encounters Aulus, who warns her not to interrupt a "reunion." As the master of the Destreza sword style, Lemuen is forced to stand up from her wheelchair to fight him more directly.<ref name=":5a"/>

[[File:39 i05 3.png|thumb|Even a monster knows how to demonstrates her motherly love to her child]]
Federico is the one to discover the Seaborn that Lemuen was looking for. It was standing at the door of Estara and Erendel's house, seemingly non-hostile but nonetheless unpredictable. The executor was prepared to shoot the monster on the spot, but Gerald identifies the creature as Hyman and persuades Federico not to kill her.<ref>[[HE-5/Story#After operation|HE-5 After]]</ref> He decides to follow Gerald's advice to not tell the twins the truth about their mother.

==="Deus est mortuus"===
Oren appears once again in the envoys' intel. But this time, he brings bad news: Reinforcements have finally arrived from the Lateran Curia, and they have captured Gerald's Sarkaz scouts outside the abbey. Once dawn arrives, Oren will take the abbey by force and arrest all the Sarkaz refugees. However, his plan would only bring panic to the monastery's residents and destroy any hope of peaceful reconciliation. Serpilia, Richele and even Lemuen discourage him to stop his maniacal plan, but Oren proclaims that they must quickly stop the Sarkaz from committing anything "evil", believing that letting them go would be "a mistake."<ref name=":6b">[[HE-6/Story#After operation|HE-6 After]]</ref>

[[File:39 i06.png|thumb|left|"Sir, here's Gerald's last words: 'please spare those innocent Sarkaz in exchange of my own life.'"]]
Just then, Clément enters the room and presents a shocking display: Gerald's severed head and dagger on a tray. To protect the Sarkaz settlers, Gerald has decided to trade his own life in exchange for sparing the rest of the Sarkaz. Clément relays Gerald's final wish to the Lateran envoys.<ref name=":6b"/> However, Oren remains unswayed. The next morning, he prepares to rendezvous with his troops when Lemuen confronts him.<ref>[[HE-7/Story#Before operation|HE-7 Before]]</ref> Before the two start fighting with each other, Federico fires a warning shot, ordering them to cease their pointless debate and take control of the situation before things get out of hand.

[[File:39 i09.png|thumb|Stefano bids his farewell to the residents of Ambrosii, and it is great that the Communion proceeds as usual]]
The morning Communion begins, and as usual, Stefano brings the wine and the bread to the brethren. However, Richele has reason to believe that the bread may be contaminated with Seaborn cells. Not wanting to cause further panic, Richele and Oren decide to wait outside the hall until the Communion ends.<ref>[[HE-7/Story#After operation|HE-7 After]]</ref> They anxiously wait for the worst result and mentally prepare to kill anyone who transformed into Seaborn. Surprisingly, nothing happens. Abbot Stefano confesses that while he did prepare the contaminated bread, he faltered at the last moment and decided to lock it up. He also declares that he would officially hand over the Sanctilaminium Ambrosii to the Curia.<ref name=":8a">[[HE-8/Story#Before operation|HE-8 Before]]</ref>

Meanwhile, Federico heads up to the sacrarium, where he finds Clément and Arturia. Federico correctly deduces that Clément is responsible for the fire in Ambrosii's chapel hall, though he struggles to understand his motive. He admonishes the teachings of Lateran religion, having realised that its so-called "paradise" is nothing but a false promise that could not save him nor this monastery. To end their suffering, he plans to ignite explosives hidden beneath the monastery, believing that it would be better for everyone to die now than to live in eternal pain and anguish.<ref name=":8a"/>

[[File:39 i11.png|thumb|left|"So, God is really dead... and paradise never existed after all..."]]
Despite Clément's threat, Federico successfully stops the explosion by shooting the lighter in his hand. Even then, Clément unleashes his second option – consuming the polluted bread he stole to transform himself into a Seaborn. However, Clément's strong desire to end his own life prompts the hivemind to reject him and the bread poisons him instead. Clément curses that all faith is false and the ordinary gardener soon surrenders his last breath.<ref name=":8b">[[HE-8/Story#After operation|HE-8 After]]</ref>

Federico then turns his attention onto Arturia, pointing his gun at her and intending to execute her on spot. In turn, Arturia entices him into doing so. A [[Leithanien|Leithanian]] envoy suddenly approaches Arturia to bring her back, and Oren and Richele attempt to stop Federico, revealing that the envoy belongs to Kurfürst [[Werner von Hochberg]], one of the most powerful Kurfürsten in Leithanien. Harming Arturia while she is under their protection would only bring a diplomatic crisis to Laterano. Nevertheless, Federico fires his gun, knowing full well that Arturia's Arts would block it. The black-haired Sankta formally bids farewell before departing and coyly states that they [[Zwillingstürme im Herbst/Synopsis|will meet again someday]] in the future.<ref name=":8b"/>

[[File:39 i12.png|thumb|The two cousins will continue their cat-and-mouse game, yet Arturia seems to be enjoying it]]
Ambrosii is eventually retrieved by the Lateran Curia. The Notarial Hall settles down the abbey's residents while helping them adjusting to life in Laterano. Fortuna is allowed to stay with Raimund and the Sarkaz, but she must make frequent contact with Serpilia. With that said, the refugees are allowed to leave Ambrosii undisturbed. Aulus and Hyman has finally finished their mission; they quietly sneak away and head back to the sea, as Hyman now views the Seaborn as her kin. Federico meets the Pope once again, and the old man praises him for a good job. Still, he puts high hopes on him in to create a path for the Sankta's survival. Meanwhile, Stefano feels remorse for his wrongdoing and decides to give up his position as abbot. Still disillusioned with Lateran faith, Stefano leaves the abbey alone while embarking on a new journey to search for his true goal &mdash; a real paradise for everyone.<ref>[[HE-ST-3]]</ref>
