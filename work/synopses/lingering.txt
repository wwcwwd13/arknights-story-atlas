==Das Abschiedskonzert==
The [[Leithanian]] city of [[Vyseheim]] is preparing for a musical concert to celebrate the farewell of its beloved musician and pianist, [[Czerny|William Fichte Czerny]]. The concert is sponsored by the city’s ''Lehnsfrau'', [[Gertrude Strollo]], and will take place in the city’s famous Afterglow Hall. Gertrude also invites a young man named [[Ebenholz]] for a chance to be one of its performers. An incredibly pessimistic noble, Ebenholz doesn’t completely believe in Gertrude’s goodwill, but heads to the concert hall for the audition anyway. After arriving, Ebenholz sees a poor [[Caprinae]] boy named [[Kreide]] being distressed by the long queue, and uses his status to allow them both to proceed first. At the audition, Kreide starts by playing the cello, and the young noble soon joins in with his flute. Their duet astonishes those present and the two are accepted for the selections tomorrow. Kreide was disappointed when he learned that the concert offers no monetary reward, but Ebenholz quickly offers to provide money as recompense. With it, Kreide was able to get his grandpa treated at the local [[Rhodes Island]] branch where [[Hibiscus the Purifier|Hibiscus]] was temporarily staying.<ref name="ST1">[[LE-ST-1]]</ref>

[[File:LE-Practice.png|thumb|Ebenholz and Kreide practicing their duet]]
The next day, Ebenholz and Kreide arrive at Afterglow Hall for the selections. They will be performing ''Morgen und Abend'', one of the musician’s signature works. However, Czerny is not so easily pleased. He disapproves of Ebenholz’s performance, remarking that he lacked musical coordination and promptly disqualifies him. The noble is offended, but Kreide insists that they must perform together, leading Czerny to dismiss them both. However, after Ebenholz sulks back to Gertrude, he is informed that Czerny had actually liked their performance enough to designate them as backups.<ref name="1Bef">[[LE-1/Story#Before operation|LE-1 Before]]</ref>

Gertrude then reveals her ulterior motives for inviting Ebenholz. She explains her plan to rid Ebenholz of the Voice of Mundane, a strange affliction that torments him with nightmares and haunting whispers. She reveals that his new acquaintance Kreide also possesses a Voice of Mundane, much to Ebenholz’s surprise. When they perform together at Czerny’s concert, it will trigger a 'Resonanz,' and with the Afterglow Hall’s unique capability as an [[Arts]] conductor, Gertrude will transfer Ebenholz's Voice of Mundane into Kreide. Afterwards, Ebenholz is free to go live life as he wishes until Gertrude requires his assistance once more. Ebenholz agrees to the plan.<ref name="1Bef"/>

[[File:Sympathie für den Teufel.png|thumb|left|Kreide attempts to shield Hibiscus from the angry mob]]
The next day, Czerny informs Ebenholz and Kreide that the main performers got seriously injured in a brawl and that they will have to perform instead. He gives them one day to rehearse before performing for him, providing Kreide with a cello. Ebenholz requests to stay over at Kreide’s home for practice but is appalled by its poor condition. Despite this, the two begin rehearsing and they bond over their shared passion for music.<ref>[[LE-1/Story#After operation|LE-1 After]]</ref> However, on their way back to Czerny’s estate, they find an angry mob harassing Hibiscus, and Kreide rushes to her defense. One of the drunken locals destroy Kreide’s cello in retaliation. Thankfully, Ebenholz manages to scare off the locals with his Arts before things could escalate, unaware that one of Gertrude’s lackeys, [[Lachmann]], was the instigator.<ref>[[LE-2/Story#Before operation|LE-2 Before]]</ref>

Later that evening, the two explain what had happened to Czerny. Kreide is forced to borrow a second cello from Czerny for their test. Despite their melodies still not synchronizing well, Czerny declares that they at least meet his minimum requirements. Since the second cello is a commonplace mass-produced one, Ebenholz takes Kreide to the best music store in Vyseheim at the Cliffy Patio, where all the nobles reside. Although Kreide is initially refused entry due to his inappropriate attire, Ebenholz fiercely argues on his behalf and they manage to purchase a fine cello. He also orders a suit for Kreide, and the latter is overjoyed by Ebenholz’s generosity.<ref>[[LE-3/Story#Before operation|LE-3 Before]]</ref>

Ebenholz continues practicing under Czerny’s guidance. Although he and Czerny clash at times, they develop an understanding. All the while, Ebenholz couldn’t help but think that Kreide felt familiar to him...<ref>[[LE-3/Story#After operation|LE-3 After]]</ref>


==Songs of the Past==

===Prophecies and Curses===
Early in the morning, Hibiscus urgently seeks out Kreide and Ebenholz. She explains that she had arrived in Vyseheim to investigate the 'Serendipitous [[Infected]] Prognosis Phenomenon,' where Infected residents were suddenly reporting better health. She discloses that this anomaly is actually a 'false recovery' and that their Oripathy is rapidly deteriorating. After reviewing the research at Rhodes Island’s branch office, Hibiscus believes that, however outlandish it may seem, that Kreide and Ebenholz are the primary factors behind this phenomenon.<ref name="4Bef">[[LE-4/Story#Before operation|LE-4 Before]]</ref>

Ebenholz realises that the Voice of Mundane must be involved somehow and runs back to confront Gertrude. She reveals that unlike Ebenholz, Kreide cannot completely control his Voice of Mundane, meaning that he is constantly casting Arts subconsciously. This impacts the [[Originium]] inside of the Infected residents, causing their bodies to enhance their compensatory functions, triggering the false recovery. Ebenholz’s presence near him is causing Resonanz, thereby amplifying the effect.<ref name="4Bef"/> Gertrude also reveals an undivulged detail about the plan: after the concert, she would kill Kreide and use his body to fake Ebenholz’s death. She argues that Kreide will soon die anyway due to his strengthened Voice of Mundane, and that the happiest ending Ebenholz could offer him is finishing the concert together before putting him to a peaceful rest.<ref>[[LE-4/Story#After operation|LE-4 After]]</ref>

[[File:Böses Erwachen.png|thumb|Kreide's melody made Ebenholz realized that he is also like him]]
Ebenholz, angry and exhausted, returns to Kreide’s home. He begins suffering from intense headaches caused by the Voice of Mundane and passes out. Ebenholz wakes up upon hearing Kreide playing a strangely familiar melody on the cello, and finally recalls a long-forgotten section of his past. In truth, both him and Kreide were distant descendants of the infamous [[Witch King]], the most powerful ruler in Leithanien. After his death, many adolescent descendants were kidnapped by his devout followers, the [[Echoes of Herkunftshorn]], who sought to revive the Witch King’s powers using his melodies, the accursed Voices of Mundane. Most of them were lost to the torturous experiments, until only Ebenholz and Kreide remained. Although Kreide survived, he was taken away, and Ebenholz never saw him again. Now, the two of them have been reunited.<ref>[[LE-ST-2]]</ref>

===Das Letzte Sinfonie===
[[File:Der Spion und der Graf.png|thumb|Ebenholz and Biegler repelling the swarm of Originium Slugs|left]]
Ebenholz’s recovered memories strengthen his resolve to find a way for both he and Kreide to survive. Overhearing a suspicious conversation, he tails Lachmann into the sewers. A barista named [[Biegler]] suddenly appears, revealing himself to be a spy for the Twin Empresses who has been carefully watching Lachmann’s actions within the city.<ref>[[LE-5/Story#After operation|LE-5 After]]</ref> Despite his distrust of Ebenholz, Biegler agrees to pursue Lachmann with him and they encounter a swarm of [[Blackwater Originium Slug|noxious Originium Slugs]]. The slugs have become irritated by Lachmann’s movements and are gushing up towards the streets, emitting a poison gas that causes many Infected to fall ill. They quickly incapacitate Lachmann and work together to clear out the slugs.<ref>[[LE-6/Story#Before operation|LE-6 Before]]</ref>

Above ground, Kreide lures the Slugs away from the Infected residents with his cello. He had intended to set both the slugs and himself ablaze in order to save everyone when Ebenholz stopped him. Kreide confesses that he knew he was behind the 'false recovery' of the Infected, but didn’t understand why until Ebenholz tells him the truth about the Voice of Mundane. The two then synchronize their respective Voices to repel the swarm of Originium Slugs. They succeed, but Kreide faints and they rush to stabilize him at Rhodes Island’s branch office.<ref>[[LE-6/Story#After operation|LE-6 After]]</ref>

[[File:Kampf der Künste.png|thumb|"I'm not holding this one back, Dame Strollo!"]]
Kreide’s grandpa arrives and cryptically tells them to investigate the Afterglow Hall’s lounge with Czerny, where the concert is supposed to take place. They are horrified to discover that Gertrude has installed dozens of amplifiers that would convert Afterglow Hall into a massive Arts amplification device. The 'Resonanz' caused by the Voices of Mundane will affect all of Vyseheim, which would kill all the Infected and turn the rest into Infected..<ref>[[LE-7/Story#Before operation|LE-7 Before]]</ref> Just then, Gertrude reveals herself and mocks Ebenholz. The two engage in a fierce duel of Arts, with Ebenholz easily defeating her and turning her over to Biegler. Their discovery of her plan is insufficient to convict her, but Gertrude is nonetheless arrested for doing forbidden research on the Voice of Mundane.<ref name="7Aft">[[LE-7/Story#After operation|LE-7 After]]</ref>

However, there is still the problem regarding the Voice inside Kreide, which is slowly killing him. Kreide’s grandpa provides them with documents he stole from Biegler containing details about the Voice of Mundane, revealing that he is also a spy of the Twin Empresses assigned to protect Kreide, determined to save him. Czerny studies the documents carefully. Realising that the Voices are remnants of the Witch King’s melodies, Czerny decides to compose a new piece that could extract their Voices. Challenging himself to balance the two melodies, Czerny reflects on his life and uses all his strength to complete the score. So intense was the process that he collapses the moment he finishes.<ref name="7Aft"/>

===Auf Wiedersehen===
[[File:Licht und Schatten.png|thumb|The concert went smoothly at first...]]
Dawn breaks, and the day of the concert has arrived. Hibiscus helps Czerny to the Afterglow Hall while Ebenholz and Kreide prepare for the performance. There, Czerny reveals the name of the piece, ''Licht und Schatten'', and the trio begins their performance on stage. Amongst the crowd are the Echoes of Herkunftshorn, who are interested in seeing the Voice of Mundane in action. Kreide’s former [[Virtuosa|teacher]] is also in the audience, eager to witness the final note of her student’s magnum opus.<ref name="8Bef">[[LE-8/Story#Before operation|LE-8 Before]]</ref>

Meanwhile, Gertrude is imprisoned in a nearby building, where she hears the performance underway. She reveals to Biegler that the amplifiers were merely a ruse and that she had already made preparations by altering the very construction of the concert hall. Gertrude starts playing on her harp, further tampering the symphony as the Voices of Mundane begin to destabilize. Without a vessel to contain them, the Voices will collapse into an all-consuming void and kill everyone nearby. The three performers know that they must do something, but they are helpless to stop the performance.<ref name="8Bef"/>

[[File:Der Schatten von Seine Majestät.png|thumb|left|...but now Ebenholz has to confront the Witch King's shadow taking over Kreide]]
Hibiscus, realising that something is wrong, tries to evacuate the audience, but is stopped by the Echoes of Herkunftshorn. Seeing no other choice, Kreide decides to become the receptacle for the two Voices. After the performance concludes, his body begins to be rapidly encrusted by Originium. Ebenholz hurries to bring him outside as his mind is gradually corrupted by the Witch King's consciousness. Before succumbing, Kreide declares that despite their suffering, their lives were fortunate, having fought against their fate, and commands Ebenholz to end his life.<ref name="8Bef"/>

Using all his power, Ebenholz manages to defeat the transformed Kreide. The dying Kreide tells Ebenholz to live on, to remember how they fought against their cruel fate, and that he will always be by his side. Kreide’s grandpa could only watch in sadness. Ebenholz bursts into tears as Kreide passes away in his arms. He brings Kreide inside the Afterglow Hall’s lounge, taking his cello as a memento, and leaves his own flute with Kreide’s body.<ref name="8Bef"/>

Gertrude is killed by Biegler for her transgressions. The Afterglow district recovers from the incident, but despite everything they’ve done, the Voice of Mundane remains in Ebenholz’s head. Together with Hibiscus, he and Czerny travel to the Rhodes Island landship. Ebenholz is permitted to stay as a Rhodes Island Operator and soon receives a passport for his new identity along with the death certificates of Kreide and Graf Urtica. He would have rejoiced once, but now he chooses to burn them instead. He plays Kreide’s favourite song with his cello on the landship’s deck… lamenting the death of his dearest friend.<ref>[[LE-ST-3]]</ref>
