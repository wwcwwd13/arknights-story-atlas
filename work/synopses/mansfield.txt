==Step One: Enter the Giant's Lair==
During their visit to [[Columbia]], the ex-[[Rhine Lab]] personnel [[Silence|Dr. Olivia Silence]] and [[Mayer|Mayer Stony]] are summoned by an unknown individual to a bar for "a chat." Upon arrival, that individual is revealed to be [[Muelsyse]], the [[Elf|Elven]] director of R.L.'s Ecological Department where [[Magallan]] belongs to.

[[File:Anthony Simon, The Mountain.png|thumb|That one prisoner, Anthony Simon]]
Muelsyse explains that she wants to learn more of Mayer and Silence's apparent involvement in a recent jailbreak in [[Mansfield State Prison]] where [[Mountain|Anthony Simon]] had escaped from. Silence does not deny Muelsyse's inquiry as she explains that she is not involved with HydeBro, a Columbian construction supply company responsible for the fall of Simon Co. of which Smith Simon, Anthony's father, is the director. However, Muelsyse was curious about what really happened during the jailbreak, and wanted Silence and Mayer to explain it to her.

Silence starts her story with [[Kafka]], her mischievous [[Liberi]] acquaintance who intentionally made herself a prisoner in Mansfield to get in touch with Anthony. The infiltration went smoothly as Kafka blended in with the inmates just fine and encountered Anthony himself, who gained strong influence among both the inmates and wardens thanks to his wits and prowess. However, unbeknownst to Kafka, an [[Anaty]] inmate named [[Robin]] had also entered Mansfield for her own, possibly malicious, motives.

==Step Two: Awaken the Sleeping Beast==
[[File:High Value Target.png|thumb|As the last of the Simons, HydeBro sets their eyes on Anthony]]
Silence paused her story to reveal that HydeBro had made dealings with R.L.'s Energy Dept. and sent several hitmen to Mansfield to eliminate Anthony in order to finish off the Simons (whose other members had met their end in the Bunkerhill State Prison). She explained the darker, "commercialized" side of Mansfield, which was exploited by Smith Simon to ensure Anthony's safety by having him incarcerated in Mansfield instead of Bunkerhill. The R.L. Energy Dept.'s involvement in HydeBro's plot infuriates Silence to the point that she cannot continue telling the story; Mayer steps in.

While checking in on Silence, Mayer listens to her troubles and grows worried after explaining to her what she knows about R.L.'s Energy Dept. and their director [[Ferdinand Clooney]]. Silence doubts whether she is doing the right thing. After comforting the Liberi with a hug and some tea, the two returns to Muelsyse's side and Silence resumed her story after explaining why she left R.L., though she stressed that the [[Rhodes Island's Records of Originium Rhine Lab|"Diαbolic Crisis"]] involving [[Ifrit]] is only one of several factors influencing her decision.

Picking up where the jailbreak story left off, Robin gets wounded during a prison brawl and is taken to Mansfield's morgue and clinic, run by the [[Sarkaz]] [[Domma]], to recover, while Kafka makes contact with her accomplice, a temporary construction worker named [[Pinecone|Mina]]. Robin is revealed to be the daughter of the director of BlackCloud Trade, who fell into depression after their company was overthrown by Simon Co.; she was hired by a mysterious individual known only as "Generous J" to infiltrate Mansfield and assassinate Anthony, using her situation to justify it.

[[File:Anthony Ganged Up On.png|thumb|"You picked on the wrong Feline to mess with, buddy."]]
Robin had an opportunity to kill Anthony when he was attacked by several of HydeBro's hitmen disguised as inmates. Kafka, who befriended him before, also fought alongside the [[Feline]], but was eventually knocked out cold as the hitmen easily took down the jailers sent to quell the chaos. However, Robin too was knocked out before she could even lay a finger on Anthony, who managed to beat down the hitmen single-handedly as she passed out. In the aftermath, Anthony and Kafka meet with Domma, whom he had gotten to know during his stay in Mansfield. After learning from Kafka that she had entered the penitentiary to free him, Anthony admits that while he was used to a life behind bars, he yearned to be a free man.

Anthony approaches Robin, having seen through her cover, and offered her the chance to help him break out of Mansfield instead of continuing her mission to kill him. As she considered the offer, a warden meets with Robin and reveals himself as her employer, [[Jesselton Williams]]; he had also overheard their conversation. Jesselton persuades Robin to continue with her original mission, once again stressing what happened to her father and that Anthony's predicament means that he won't be able to compensate Robin anytime soon. In the end, Robin decides to continue with her mission and accepts Anthony's offer, giving her an opportunity to strike. Domma initially refuses to tag along with Anthony and co., but Robin convinces the Sarkaz that a better life awaits her in the outside world.

==Step Three: Let Hell Break Loose==
Silence explains how the jailbreak was pulled off, elaborating on the weaknesses of Mansfield's aging infrastructure, the C-Zone tower, and a way out underground. To do this, Anthony and co. waited until Mansfield docked at Nulaiteburgh, then acquired the key to Mansfield's control center held by the warden Barton before using it to access the control center and retract the C-Zone tower. Using the distraction to head into Mansfield's morgue, they breach the walls for a way out, where Mina would then take them away from Mansfield and towards their freedom.

[[File:Mansfield Tower Sinks.png|thumb|The C-Zone tower sinking on Anthony and co.'s jailbreak]]
On the day of the jailbreak, everything went as planned until Robin attempts to kill Anthony once more when he and Kafka reached Mansfield's control center. Although she failed, she damaged the control panels, nearly foiling the jailbreak. Kafka's acumen saves the day as she successfully initiates the retraction of the C-Zone tower, cutting off the power of the entire penitentiary. Despite her actions, Anthony forgives Robin after learning of her situation and gives her a pep talk.

==Step Four: Keep Your Friends Close==
[[File:That All You Got.png|thumb|left|"Is this all you can do, Anthony Simon?"]]
Upon reaching Mansfield's morgue, Anthony and co. find Jesselton in their way, taking Domma hostage and threatening to kill her unless Robin kills Anthony. Robin seemingly complies and throws her dagger, only to free Domma instead and giving an opening for Anthony and Kafka to dispatch the rest of the inmates before they confront Jesselton. During the fight, Jesselton activates the imprisonment collars worn by Anthony and Kafka, paralyzing them, but they manage to remove the collars before the fight intensifies. Jesselton unleashes his ferromagnetic [[Arts]] to even the playing field.

[[File:Saria vs Jesselton.png|thumb|Okay. Let's dance!]]
Jesselton's might was too much for Anthony and co. to handle, and as he gloats his apparent victory and awaits his "reapers" out to finish the job, he is confronted by another warden, revealed to be [[Saria]] in disguise. Eager to settle the score with the [[Vouivre]] who previously rejected his job application in R.L. because of his attitude, Jesselton fights Saria in a one-on-one duel where the latter unleashed her calcification Arts. She manages to overpower Jesselton, resulting in his defeat and allowing Anthony and co. to successfully escape from Mansfield.

Back in the present, Muelsyse was surprised to learn of Saria's interference, only to hear from Silence that Saria was actually trying to keep Anthony out of Ferdinand and Muelsyse's hands. Muelsyse then reveals her motivation of summoning Silence and Mayer: by having Anthony, she could keep the Energy Dept. in check and improve her position in R.L., and offers a trade with Silence by handing over the Feline in return for Muelsyse reinstating her and Mayer's positions in R.L.. Silence refuses, and before Muelsyse could use force to convince her otherwise, she summons Anthony. Muelsyse asks Anthony himself whether he would come with her, but he would only accept her offer if she could defeat him in a duel. She concedes and excuses herself, revealing that the Muelsyse Silence and Mayer were speaking with all this time was actually a water clone of herself, but not before telling Silence to know what she's doing before she acts and that she doesn't always have to bear the consequences of her decisions.

With everything settled, Silence, Mayer, and Anthony return to [[Rhodes Island]].
