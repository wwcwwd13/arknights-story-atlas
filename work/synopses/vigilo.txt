==Spes==
Somewhere in [[Tkaronto]], [[Columbia]], the [[Rhodes Island]] operators are hurriedly searching for an escape route from the police. Originally, they arrived at the city normally for a business talk with [[Mama John's]] the mega conglomerate which ended in failure. But the gang were soon ambushed by policemen bribed by Mama John's, and the [[Oripathy]] suppressant sample inside their hotel room was then confiscated. The heist forces the group to split into two: Dylan the aircraft pilot and [[Blaze]] hidden in the street, and [[Amiya]] and the [[Doctor]] seeking refuge in the wilderness of Gaspar Wildland.

The Doctor drives the car into the wilderness until it eventually runs out of fuel. After wandering around the desert under scorching heat, they are finally rescued by [[Cannot Goodenough]] –  a mysterious merchant wearing an iron helmet. Cannot suggests they should reach for the nearest [[Catastrophe Messenger|Messenger]] outpost to return to civilization. Along the journey, they have to be precautious against the anarcho-primitivist [[Rusthammer]]s who often raid traveling caravans. The Doctor slowly remembers their skill in wilderness survival, and Amiya mentions that their survival skill was how both of them managed to return to [[Babel]] safe and sound from the [[Rim Billiton]]ian outback three years ago [[Babel (event)/Synopsis|after they saved young Amiya from a ruin]]. Despite this, the Doctor still struggles to regain their former memory, so Cannot gives him a wise quote that they should put down their past and focus on the pleasant events of the current.

The trio eventually reach the Messenger outpost. There, the Doctor notices that the local clinic is distributing to the frontier pioneers their so-called "Oripathy suppressants" purchased from Mama John's that could miraculously ease their Oripathy symptoms. But the Doctor immediately recognizes that these "suppressants" are actually normal painkillers mixed with nutrient solution. Cannot elaborates that by performing deception onto the pioneers, Mama John's could receive fame and money from them despite their illiteracy even upon their death bed. Furthermore, Mama John's stealing their formula is an act of intellectual property theft. What Mama John's does will only worsen the pioneers' clinical conditions.

So, the Doctor comes up with a rather tricky plan to fool Mama John's, much to Amiya's and Blaze's worry. They first surrender themselves to the Columbian policemen for importing unauthorized suppressants, hence putting them under custody. On the other hand, this allows the Columbian Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Arts Units, and Originium, the A.T.A.U.O., to confiscate Mama John's suppressants. Three days later, R.I. bails the Doctor out, but that means R.I. has become a company with a blackened record. Cannot reassures that the Columbian government would not care much since many of the companies during business there are not clean as well.

After bidding farewell with the Doctor and the R.I. operators, Cannot regroups with his fellowmen, revealing to be the scavengers of the Rusthammers. They argue that why Cannot is willing to spend time with them that might become their enemy in the future, but Cannot responds that only the Doctor is able to aid in his accomplishment of saving lives across the land.

==Memoria==
The Doctor is having a {{Tip|MRI|magnetic resonance imaging}} test on their brain as their usual treatment under [[Kal'tsit]]'s order. Kal'tsit comments that nothing bad happens to their brain, and their memory recovery is in a gradual progress. Nevertheless, she is still puzzled that how come their memory was erased by an unusual method three years ago, something that is not the result of the Sarcophagus' malfunction. She still entrusts the Doctor to have their job well done in their daily office works.

Later in the afternoon, the R.I. Catastrophe observers led by [[Earthspirit]] announce bad news to Amiya and the Doctor: the landship is approaching an upcoming [[Catastrophe]], and there will be a heavy sandstorm followed by strong cyclones which cannot be avoided from their route. The Doctor proposes a daring plan of braving the Catastrophe, but many fear that it is a very risky plan. Even then, The Doctor calculates a formula that could have the landship altering its route at the outer zone of the Catastrophe's core, and they reassures that their calculation will be very precise in the landship's escape route.

The Catastrophe arrives sooner than expected. Amiya orders everyone to return to the basement immediately. As the landship trembles, the Doctor orders the control center to navigate through the Catastrophe's outer zone through their calculation. After several minutes facing the wind, the landship finally leaves the Catastrophe zone, and not only is everyone safe and sound, but also the damage is less than 5%. Everyone cheers for the Doctor's miraculous plan.

After the incident, the Doctor could finally take a rest. Kal'tsit comments that the formula they came out with was in fact used long ago during the Babel era, and that the Doctor still retains basic knowledge regarding Originium's nature. This causes Kal'tsit confirms that the Doctor's amnesia is intentional and was done on purpose by somebody else, and Kal'sit still anticipates the day when they recover their memory to interrogate the tragedy in Babel three years ago. Still, she wants them to enjoy the current moment.

Later in their office, [[Eyjafjalla]] appears to hand in research data to the Doctor. She is amazed that by simply glancing through her report, the Doctor was able to formulate a calculation to flee from the Catastrophe. She is eager to have the Doctor guiding her in her research in the future

==Valorem==
It is an off day for the Doctor, so they take the opportunity to tour around the landship to familiarize themselves in this new environment. Upon arriving at the Integrated Bioprocessing Unit, they could find countless mementos left behind by deceased operators, including a shield belonging to [[Ace]]. There, the Doctor also notices [[Beagle]] routinely arriving here to wipe Ace's shield so that she will never forget her senior. Among the piles of mementos, they uncover those belonging to [[Reunion]] fighters as well, and there is one that catches the Doctor's eye, a strange red crater-like machine covered with dust.

The Doctor continues their tour to the landship's basement where its furnace is located. They greet several Goliath [[Sarkaz]] boiler workers for their hard work even on weekends. They learn from their accounts that [[W]] and [[Ascalon]] are heading towards [[Londinium]], the capital of [[Victoria]], to prepare for [[Episode 10|a greater battle there]]. The Doctor is amazed that W, despite being a former Reunion fighter, is still allowed to be an R.I. member, and the Goliath comments that both W and the Doctor themselves were already its members back in the Babel era.

The Doctor eventually reaches the control center to meet Amiya and [[Closure]] to talk about the red machine in the room. Amiya elaborates that this red machine was a scrap metal collected by the Doctor themselves, Ace, and [[Scout]] in the wilderness three years ago, and they seemed to have made a bet on what is inside this spoiled machine. Out of curiosity, the Doctor, Amiya, and Closure decide to jack out the machine, which Closure presumes to be a safety vault. But it turns out the machine is actually a spoiled vending machine with a can of beer coming out from its pick-up spot. The Doctor chuckles and then places the can of beer beside Ace's shield, declaring that is is Ace who has won the bet.

==Cogitatio==
The story changes its focus onto the past during the Babel era. Since the landship's departure from Kazdel to flee from the civil war, [[Theresa]] the "King of Sarkaz" and her loyalists have been facing numerous obstacles from their enemies supporting her brother Regent [[Theresis]]. They fear that with the strong support from [[Duq'arael|the Sanguinarch]] and the [[Nachzehrer King]] of the Sarkaz Royal Court, Theresis and the [[Military Commission of Kazdel]] will become more militant and violent, which will worsen the contested relationship between the Sarkaz and other [[Terra]]ns. Kal'tsit reminds her that there have been espionages between both Babel and the Military Commission, and traitors are on the rise on Babel's side. Nevertheless, Theresa trusts the Doctor in handling the traitors.

Thirteen days later after their departure from Kazdel, the Doctor encounters in their camp a Sarkaz traitor named Marco trying to kidnap them, but fortunately, Scout comes for the rescue. Upon apprehending the traitor, they bring him in front of Kal'tsit and Theresa for trial and request their final judgment. Marco yells that Theresa is losing support from her loyalists the moment when she decides to incorporate non-Sarkaz into Babel, and what he does is just to eradicate her majesty's obstacles. On the other hand, the Doctor suggests having the traitor kept alive by sending them into exile seeing that he is still supporting her deep in his heart. Nevertheless, many oppose the Doctor's suggestion, fearing that it will breed more traitors, so the Doctor hands their final judgement to Theresa. After deep consideration, Theresa pronounces the death sentence onto Marco, and Marco begs her to reconsider her judgment, stating that he is too sinful when standing in front of the great, merciful King of Sarkaz.

Days later, Marco commits suicide in his cells, and Babel receives reinforcement from mercenaries led by [[Hoederer]] who seeks cooperation from them. Both Kal'tsit and the Doctor remind that Babel is still at a disadvantage, yet she entrusts both of them to accomplish her order.

==Anterior==
[[File:Doctor and Kal'tsit.png|thumb|"Even if no one understands, we must continue to walk this path...."]]
[[File:Kal'tsit Melancholy.png|thumb|"...Doctor, you too will find your path."]]
The story begins with the humane side of the Doctor's personality in the past despite its merciless command as the infamous "Ghost of Babel." At one time, Scout fails in a field mission. Due to his hesitation to defeat an enemy squad, he accidentally falls into their ambush. Scout wanted the Doctor to punish him. However, the Doctor not only admires his calmness during the retreat but also pointed out that his team was still safe. Also, the Doctor sees him struggling with himself and discovers that Scout does not want to have young Reyka punished. Scout then mentions that all that Reyka did is just purely for Theresa's honor despite her bitter childhood. To his surprise, the Doctor does not punish Reyka and him and only gives a simple warning for his future action.

The Doctor also demonstrates their concern on Theresa's activities. As the King of Sarkaz, she is burdened with tons of responsibility, but both the Doctor and Kal'tsit hopes that she can take a break if needed so that she can regain essential energy in her leadership. Just then, a young Amiya appears in front of them, saying that she cannot sleep because of the noises of the soldiers as well as her worry for Ascalon after her departure for a field mission. Both the Doctor and Theresa comfort her and assures that everything will be fine. Being given the courage, young Amiya happily returns to her bedroom and says good night to them.

Fast-forward to the present, the R.I, operators are transferring their patients to County Toron upon reaching the Victorian border. Closures has been busy analyzing the possible route to reach Londinium which is currently under a siege by the [[Grand Dukes of Victoria|Eight Grand Dukes]]. Kal'tsit and the Doctor walk around to supervise the process, and along the way, she reassures her patients that everything will be fine.

Later in the afternoon, the Doctor and Kal'tsit could finally take a break at the landship deck where they watch the sunset. Kal'tsit trusts that the Doctor's leadership will bring success in the upcoming quest in Londinium, yet she reminds them to watch over Amiya as well to ease her burdens. She then elaborates that this quest will overhaul the Doctor's view in regards to Terra which would even bring their memories back, but she questions what will be the Doctor fighting for in the future. Nevertheless, the Doctor confirms that they will always fight for both R.I., the Infected, and Terra as a whole regardless of their circumstances.

==Pignus==
Closure is conducting her usual checkup on [[PRTS]]. As she flips through its records, she uncovers a file regarding the Doctor's [[Prologue|rescue mission back in]] [[Chernobog]], and she is surprised that out of the 12,010,955 processes PRTS analyses, only 167 are successful. PRTS then comments that even with the 167 analyses, most of them would result in severe casualty, and only two results could ensure the Doctor's safety, and the one they executed resulted in three times the possible analysis. 

The two then proceed to have a chat. Closure narrates how she started out as a Vampire who only shuts herself and hides inside her room to conduct hacking to the chief engineer of both Babel and later Babel within only three years thanks to Theresa's leadership. When requests PRTS to help uncover the Doctor's secret, not only PRTS refuses, but also she jokingly threatens to leak her funny "Closure's Midnight Video Market" in the Doctor's office for a month every midnight, prompting Closure to add a restriction onto its program.

[[File:Doctor and Priestess.png|thumb|As old as this photo goes, the Doctor and Priestess had been very close to each other.... until certain tragedies that separated their path.]]
Just then, the Doctor appears in the database room. Both Closure and PRTS are amazed of their presence as they rarely step inside this room. The Doctor commands PRTS to search up any data regarding [[Priestess]] after they are plagued by that flashback memory back in Chernobog and that Kal'tsit refuses to elaborate further. Suddenly, PRTS remains silent, and after a few minutes, it states that the Doctor's permission is insufficient. Being baffled, the Doctor decides to seek for Kal'tsit again, and Closure follows suits upon completing the checkup.

Minutes after their departure, PRTS somehow automatically activates itself. It shows to itself its first record, a file record titled "<Ship Video Record 000000001α>" when the Rhodes Island landship was first built, and it features the Doctor and Priestess wearing an R.I. uniform standing at the landship's deck. A few seconds later, PRTS shuts itself down upon expiry of the lifted restriction.

Perhaps one day, PRTS will reveal the Doctor of their real identity, one [[Predecessor|whose civilization]] is responsible for the birth of the current Terran lives.
