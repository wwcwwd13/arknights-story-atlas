==Wild Goose Chase==
After [[Reunion]]'s capture of [[Misha]], the joint forces of [[Rhodes Island]], [[Penguin Logistics]], [[Blacksteel]], and the [[Lungmen Guard Department]] pursue the retreating fighters through the wasteland bordering [[Ursus]] and [[Yan]]. Their only goal is to slow down the escaping combatants and arrest them in a single calculated operation. On the way, they receive aid from another team led by [[Hoshiguma]] and easily take down the fighters. 

Meanwhile, somewhere in the wilderness, [[Skullshatterer]] has a short reunion with Misha, who turns out to be his long lost sister. They were separated years ago when he became infected, dragged away by the Infected Patrol of Ursus. Revealing himself as Alex, he wishes for Misha to fight alongside him for the betterment of the Infected. When his Assault Squad informs him of the arrival of the joint forces, he leaves Misha in [[W]]'s care. Misha tried and failed to persuade him not to fight Rhodes Island, so she obeys his instructions and wishes for him to stay safe. W tells Skullshatterer that the easiest way to take down Rhodes Island is to assassinate the [[Doctor]], their best tactical strategist.

[[File:Amiya Strikes Skullshatterer.png|thumb|A tragic death that could have been avoided]]
The joint forces eventually catch up to Skullshatterer's men, and a battle ensues. Alex breaks through the backline and tries to kill the Doctor. In a panic, [[Amiya]] unleashes her [[Originium Arts|Arts]] to protect the Doctor in a surge of frantic emotions, piercing Skullshatterer's heart with a beam of black energy. Skullshatterer bleeds out, cursing Rhodes Island with his last breath. The Assault Squad laments his death and rally once more, fighting even harder to retrieve Skullshatterer's body. Rhodes Island temporarily retreats, and W promises another Reunion member that she will keep Misha safe in Alex's stead.

==Death and "Rebirth"==
Misha mourns for her brother when news of Alex's death reaches her. Shortly afterward, she makes a call to Amiya via W's terminal to inform her that she has decided to side with Reunion. Misha expresses her guilt for not doing anything to support the Infected back when she lived in Chernobog—now, after she has become an Infected herself, she understands Reunion's anger, and wishes to "atone for her sins" by seeking retribution against the oppressors of the Infected. Amiya tells Misha to wait for her, that it's not too late to turn back, but Misha rebukes her and reiterates her allegiance to Reunion. Afterwards, Misha sits with Alex's corpse, lamenting her fate and vowing to bring ''Skullshatterer'' back to life.

[[File:Miracle.png|thumb|"Skullshatterer" returns from the grave]]
On the other side of the battlefield, the joint forces resume the battle and have almost claimed victory over the Assault Squad. Just then, a familiar figure appears once more. Now carrying her brother's weapons and mantle, Misha has become the second "Skullshatterer." Seeing their leader's "resurrection," the Assault Squad's morale soars, and they bravely fight against the joint forces to the last man. However, to Amiya, this was the worst possible outcome, as she never wanted to fight Misha or her fellow infected. Amiya hesitates during the battle, so [[Ch'en]] orders her to remember that from now on, she must treat Reunion as her enemy, Infected or not. She then orders the L.G.D. to put an end to the Reunion forces.

==A Hollow Victory==
[[File:A Tragic Aftermath.png|thumb|Yet another preventable death in their struggle]]
The battle concludes with Misha's death and the annihilation of the Assault Squad. To Amiya, the events that transpired were an unbearable tragedy that never should have happened in the first place. Amiya begins to question if Rhodes Island is even in the right; despite Rhodes Island's mission to help people, they still ended up killing Misha and the Infected of Reunion. Trying to comfort her, Ch'en tells her that if Reunion was really willing to cooperate with her, there wouldn't be any bloodshed. Every decision made will eventually lead to its consequence—that is the lesson she learned. Ch'en also reminds Amiya that she must grow to accept the tragic reality of the fight against Reunion if she wants to fulfill her ambitions.

While the joint forces are conducting a cleanup of the scene, W stands from afar and observes the mess. She mockingly comments that fate always intertwines once again, and that she looks forward to what the future holds in store for Rhodes Island and Reunion.
