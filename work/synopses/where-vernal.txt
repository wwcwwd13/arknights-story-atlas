==Synopsis==
===Endless Sands===
[[File:35 i01.png|thumb|left|The day has finally come for Yumen to prepare for the vindicative wrath of the reviving God.]]
Following [[Invitation to Wine/Synopsis|the Shangshu incident]], the northern garrison city of [[Yumen]] prepares itself for the ultimate revival of [[Sui]] under the [[Grand Tutor]]'s order. After refuelling and replenishing supplies at [[Lungmen]], it will head towards the imperial capital where Sui's body is sealed. Yumen's course to the capital also coincides with two important activities. Firstly, it is the week of the Wangfeng Festival, a celebration that venerates the countless soldiers who sacrificed themselves to defend Yumen over the years. Secondly, the city's military grandmaster and the first fragment of Sui, [[Chongyue]], has finally been granted permission to retire from the imperial court after quietly guarding the city for centuries.<ref name=ST1>[[WB-ST-1]]</ref>

Yet an issue remains about what to do with his sword, which conceals his [[Feranmut]] power. They believe that the evil brother, [[Wang]], will try to take the sword in his quest to awaken the bestial Sui. The new advisor of Yumen, [[Liang Xun]], suggests an irrational yet clever move: they will entrust the sword to the strongest contender in the Yumen Arena, who will be able to protect it.<ref name=1BEF>[[WB-1/Story#Before operation|WB-1 Before]]</ref> However, there are rumors that dangerous individuals are hiding within Yumen's high walls. For that reason, the [[Lin]] [[Rat King|family]] and [[Wei Yenwu]] pay a visit to Yumen's general-magistrate, [[Zuo Xuanliao]]. Meanwhile, [[Waai Fu]] and [[Lee]] also arrive in Yumen in search of the former's missing father, [[Huai Tianpei]].<ref name=ST1/>

Little did they realize that someone is carrying out an insidious plot to devastate the city.

===Rising Storm===
[[File:35 i02.png|thumb|No one knows who "She" is and where "She" is from; "She" only comes and goes with the crisscrossing seasons.]]
While the city's [[Tianshi]] and astrologists are impatiently waiting for news from their [[Catastrophe Messenger]]s, Yühsia and her men discover that the Messengers have been murdered by some bandits. Fortunately, they manage to recover the Catastrophe data and hurry to bring it back to the city.<ref name=ST1/> The data shows that a [[Catastrophe]] is currently approaching Yumen. While General Zuo discusses this issue with his delegates, a mysterious woman suddenly appears out of thin air and nearly assassinates Wei Yenwu. Chongyue and her sister [[Ling]] attempt to pursue her, yet she manages to escape. [[Zuo Le]] suddenly barges into the room to report that a girl has stolen Chongyue's sword.<ref name=1BEF/><ref name=1AFT>[[WB-1/Story#After operation|WB-1 After]]</ref>

General Zuo believes that all this is the work of the notorious [[Shanhaizhong]], an extremist [[Feranmut]] worshiper group who once wrecked the city twenty years ago. To avoid repeating the same mistake, he orders the city to be under strict curfew. Spies and soldiers are sent down to Yumen's streets to hunt for the Shanhaizhong.<ref name=1BEF/><ref name=1AFT/> Everyone else also pitches in. Waai Fu offers help to [[Du Yaoye]]—who is now opening her own logistics company in Yumen—and her uncle [[Meng Tieyi]], who owns the famous Sword Forge. Du is investigating the deaths of her employees, who had been escorting the city's Messengers. Lin investigates the local market for smuggling activities that may be linked with the Shanhaizhong, while Zuo Le roams the streets in search of the sword thief.<ref name=2BEF>[[WB-2/Story#Before operation|WB-2 Before]]</ref>

[[File:35 i07.png|thumb|left|What does the lost wanderer see in this ancient weapon?]]
Elsewhere, the [[Anasa]] girl, [[Jieyun]], tries to escape the city with Chongyue's sword. After she passes out from her injuries, she is miraculously rescued by a strange [[Feline]] man who brings her to his clinic for treatment. When Zuo Le arrives to investigate the clinic, Jieyun hurriedly leaves and then heads towards Meng's forge.<ref name=2BEF/> Upon hearing that Jieyun was a student of his old friend, Meng chooses to hide her inside and tells an escape route for her. He says that the only way to get out of the city is to pass through Yumen's sluice gates while it is slowing down during the Catastrophe.<ref name=3BEF>[[WB-3/Story#Before operation|WB-3 Before]]</ref>

While following the Shanhaizhong's trail, Censor [[Taihe]] is mortally wounded by the same mysterious woman who had attempted to assassinate Wei Yenwu. This news greatly aggravates Zuo Le, prompting him to accelerate his investigation. Upon hearing that Taihe had been injured near the Sword Forge, he hastily orders the military to search the area. Around the same time, Lin informs Du Yaoye that she has discovered evidence connecting the Shanhaizhong's activities with Meng, coaxing her to investigate the sword forge. Du and Zuo Le end up running into each other at the forge at the same time, believing that there must be something hiding there.<ref name=3AFT>[[WB-3/Story#After operation|WB-3 After]]</ref>

===Pandemonium===
Zuo Le and Du Yaoye enter the Sword Forge. Upon their arrival, they are suddenly ambushed by Shanhaizhong members, and a fierce battle breaks out. Jieyun, who had been hiding inside, joins the fray in order to protect the forge. They are soon joined by [[Qiubai]], a disciple of Chongyue who had been tasked to help Zuo Le.<ref name=4BEF>[[WB-4/Story#Before operation|WB-4 Before]]</ref> Together they confront the mysterious woman, who is the Shanhaizhong's leader. In spite of the group's skill, the woman mercilessly crushes her opponents through her "[[Arts]]" that can manipulate the seasons. Suddenly, the strange feline man from the clinic—revealed to be Huai Tianpei—arrives and snatches up both Jieyun and the Grandmaster's sword.<ref name=4AFT>[[WB-4/Story#After operation|WB-4 After]]</ref>

The locals arrive at the scene upon hearing the commotion. Thinking that Zuo Le is there to cause trouble, the citizens block his way, demanding answers. Zuo Le orders the military to arrest all civilians while he chases after the sword. Huai tells Zuo Le and Jieyun to duel; whoever wins will get the sword. Whilst Zuo Le is hesitant to fight, Jieyun beats him down without a second thought. Thus, Huai hands the sword back to Jieyun, leaving the young candleholder shaken for the first time in his career.<ref>[[WB-ST-2]]</ref>

[[File:35 i05.png|thumb|left|"It's our showdown to stop the Shanhaizhong's plot!"]]
On the other side of the city, Yühsia meets with Meng alone inside an old warehouse. Meng admits responsibility for the whole incident as he is dissatisfied with the general's ever-increasing harsh policies and wants to ignite an uprising. It turns out that he even swapped the Catastrophe survey data with a fake one, as he believes that leading Yumen into a Catastrophe will only unite its people. Meng then hands Yühsia the data capsule that stores the real Catastrophe's information and frees Du's men, who are actually still alive.<ref name=6AFT>[[WB-6/Story#After operation|WB-6 After]]</ref><ref name=7BEF>[[WB-7/Story#Before operation|WB-7 Before]]</ref>

Just then, the Shanhaizhong arrive together with their leader. Meng had asked them to come here so that he could finish them all off. An intense battle breaks out in the storeroom, but Meng unfortunately dies trying to protect Du from the thugs. Yühsia manages to escape after the arrival of her old friend and enemy, [[Ch'en]]. After handing the capsule to Du, Yühsia teams up with Waai Fu, Ch'en, and Qiubai to stop the leader's advance.<ref name=7BEF/> However, the girls are still overpowered by the woman's bizarre "Arts." Tianpei's surprise arrival turns the tide as he manages to deal several heavy blows to her, thus chasing her off. The fight ends with a cold reunion between Tianpei and Waai Fu, with the latter swearing to surpass him one day.<ref name=7AFT>[[WB-7/Story#After operation|WB-7 After]]</ref>

===Braving the Storm===
Even though the capsule has been safely delivered, it is too late. The Catastrophe's size is three times larger than expected and there is no way for Yumen to evade it. The Imperial Astronomical Bureau advises that they separate Yumen's city plates and scatter them. However, reconnecting after separation would waste too much time, and Yumen needs to arrive at the capital as soon as possible. General Zuo makes a daring decision: Yumen will face the storm head-on. He orders every civilian to shelter in the city's western side where the Catastrophe waves will be lighter whilst stationing casters along the inner city's walls to combat the storm.<ref name=7AFT/>

[[File:35 i10.png|thumb|An immortal grandmaster and a mortal man, standing menacingly amidst the approaching storm.]]
A few hours before the Catastrophe, Tianpei and Chongyue finally meet again for a martial arts duel. For three years, Tianpei has been training himself in Yumen to defeat the immortal grandmaster; he seeks to become the First Under Heaven in kung fu after defeating him. Everyone watches with bated breath as the two masters unleash their full strength. Even though Tianpei eventually loses the challenge, he laughs heartedly at the thrill of their fight. Chongyue is amazed by his skill and admires him as the first one who has ever dealt him a heavy blow.<ref name=8BEF>[[WB-8/Story#Before operation|WB-8 Before]]</ref>

Zuo Le finds Jieyun trying to fend herself against the Shanhaizhong near the edge of the city. This time, he decides to help her, providing the girl an opportunity to escape through the sand sluices. After a brief trek through the desert, Jieyun makes a finally arrives at her master's grave; the real reason she had stolen the sword was so she could pay respects to her deceased master, who was one of the grandmaster's disciples. There, she presents Chonyue's sword and bids her farewell to her master. She is then escorted by Zuo Le back to the city.<ref name=8AFT>[[WB-8/Story#After operation|WB-8 After]]</ref>

===Duel of the Gods===
[[File:35 i11.png|thumb|left|Within the crisscrossing seasons, the Man accepts the challenge of a Vengeful God.]]
As the Catastrophe finally arrives, Yumen's "Wind Aegis", a series of fortified walls, rises into the sky. Countless Tianshi and imperial casters aided by both Ling and the Lin family prepare themselves to block the heavy sandstorm using their Arts. As sand and gravel batter the city, the Shanhaizhong use the opportunity to wreak havoc on Yumen. They pillage the streets and attack critical infrastructure to weaken Yumen's defenses. Fortunately, the local martial artists put down their disagreements with the authorities and aid the garrison soldiers in defending the city. Wei's [[Shadow Guard]]s also secretly take care of any remaining threats.<ref name=8AFT/>

As the Catastrophe starts dying down, Chongyue takes the opportunity to face the Shanhaizhong leader one last time. It turns out their "leader" is [[Ya]], a Feranmut that was banished from [[Yan]] after the [[Yan#The Great Hunt|Great Hunt]] a thousand years ago. They angrily questioned Sui's betrayal of Its kin and seeks revenge against It. Ya sought to discover the location of Sui's tomb by examining the coordinates in Yumen's control room.<ref name=9BEF>[[WB-9/Story#Before operation|WB-9 Before]]</ref> A battle occurs in a magical space created by Ya's power, ending with Chongyue finally crushing Them down with his fiery fists. Chongyue spares Ya, and the latter vows to meet Sui again before departing.<ref name=9AFT>[[WB-9/Story#After operation|WB-9 After]]</ref>

===Names are Everlasting===
Yumen survives the Catastrophe but it damages many parts of the city. Even then, reconstruction has already begun the moment the Catastrophe stopped, and Yumen will return to its course to the capital two months later. The Lungmenite visitors are in awe to see such a marvelous building still standing in after such a heavy sandstorm.<ref name=9AFT/>

Far in the wilderness, the surviving Shanhaizhong have quietly snuck away from the city. They receive a mysterious letter branded with an exquisite seal reserved only for top imperial mandarins, revealing that they have been collaborating with pro-Feranmut factions in the imperial court; the Yumen incident is just a façade to arouse the authorities' attention in handling the Feranmut problems. With that said, the bandits head for their next mission to prepare for the eventual revival of Sui.<ref name=9AFT/>

[[File:35 i14.png|thumb|Yumen will forever remember the name of the Grandmaster, a secret guardian of the city for centuries.]]
Meanwhile, Chongyue finally bids his farewell with the city following the incident. Jieyun returns his sword as promised, and he then hands it to Tianpei as a reward for the duel, telling him to keep it as a keepsake for their next fight.<ref name=9AFT/> Qiubai too says goodbye to her master and heads on [[A Death in Chunfen|her own journey]]. With his affairs settled, Chongyue quietly leaves the city before vanishing into the wilderness. As a final gift to him, General Zuo personally performs the drumming for the last day of the Wangfeng Festival. The drums are beaten seventeen times, each one signifying a disaster that Yumen has survived. A special eighteenth drum reverberates throughout the desert, commemorating Yumen's victory against their most recent trial.<ref>[[WB-ST-3]]</ref>
