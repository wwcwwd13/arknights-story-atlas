==Storm Over the Knolls==
Eleven months and twenty-seven days after the [[Chernobog]]–[[Lungmen]] crisis, [[Horn]], her subordinate [[Bagpipe]], and the 2nd [[Tempest Platoon]] are dispatched to [[County Hillock]], a small county in southern [[Victoria]]. Their mission is to investigate the movement of stolen [[Originium]] weapons from a Victorian military convoy while investigate the spectre force, a mysterious group reportedly attacking other counties in the region. However, their efforts are repeatedly obstructed by Victorian army soldiers from the local barracks, who are officially responsible for the county’s security.<ref>[[9-1]]</ref>

The 2nd Tempest Platoon attempts to cooperate with [[Hamilton|Colonel Hamilton]], the officer in charge, and his second, Adjutant Hill. However, they flatly reject their requests despite their shared loyalty to Victoria. They quickly realize that Hamilton harbors a deep-seated resentment toward the local Taran population. His hostility is so severe that he executes several Taran suspects implicated in the Originium smuggling case without presenting any evidence. It is revealed that Hamilton’s hatred stems from recent acts of Taran retaliation against ongoing mistreatment—retaliation that has escalated into violence. A small insurgent group has been ambushing convoys, attacking soldiers, and planting explosives to sabotage local barracks, resulting in the deaths of many under Hamilton’s command.<ref>[[9-2/Story]]</ref><ref name="9-4/Story">[[9-4/Story]]</ref>

While Horn believes these attacks are the work of the elusive spectre force, Hamilton remains convinced the local Tarans are complicit, using this belief to justify his harsh reprisals. Bagpipe, meanwhile, grows increasingly uneasy after witnessing a confrontation between Victorian soldiers and a group of outraged Tarans, who accuse them of carrying out extrajudicial killings.<ref name="9-4/Story">[[9-4/Story]]</ref>

Meanwhile, [[Outcast]], a [[Rhodes Island]] [[Elite Op]], arrives at the local R.I. office to complete routine paperwork. There, she meets and befriends [[Saileach|Jane Willow]], a ceremonial guard who has volunteered to assist Rhodes Island during Outcast’s stay. Jane is well-known in County Hillock for her compassion toward both the Infected and the Tarans, earning her respect from both communities.<ref>[[9-3/Story]]</ref> Later, Jane reconnects with her friend Saoirse, who is distraught over the recent execution of her cousin by Victorian troops. During their meeting, Saoirse reveals her involvement in the Taran insurgency and secretly provides Jane with the address of an upcoming gathering. Jane discreetly relays this information to Horn and Bagpipe later.<ref>[[9-5/Story]]</ref>

=="Come Out, ye Victorians..."==
[[File:Dublinn Attacks.png|thumb|The ghosts of the past have finally resurfaced.]]
Bagpipe and Horn infiltrate the Tarans' nighttime rally at the address Jane provided, hoping to gather intelligence. To their surprise, the attendees are primarily Taran aristocrats. Their surveillance is cut short when Hamilton and his men—having secretly followed them—storm the meeting and arrest everyone present, despite lacking evidence of any criminal activity. 

Before the arrests can be completed, a bomb is hurled into the building, and the resulting explosion forces Hamilton’s forces to retreat. In the chaos, a group of unidentified soldiers launches an assault on County Hillock, and a [[Reed|cloaked woman]] revealed to be the leader of Dublinn's present forces engulfs an entire street in flame. These attackers are soon revealed to be members of [[Dublinn]], a militant faction representing the Taran nationalist movement. With support from the Taran population, the enemy rapidly infiltrates the city and plunges it into disorder.<ref name="9-7/Story">[[9-7/Story]]</ref>

=="...and Fight us like Men!"==
Meanwhile, when the Leader discovers that Saoirse betrayed the insurgents by tipping off the Victorians, she is pressured by those present to carry out Saoirse’s execution despite her unwillingness.<ref name="9-8/Story">[[9-8/Story]]</ref> Jane is devastated upon hearing the news and blames herself for her friend’s death, going to the site of her execution to try and retrieve her ashes. Outcast finds her, and brings her back to the Rhodes Island office.<ref name="9-9/Story">[[9-9/Story]]</ref>

Horn orders Bagpipe to locate the local Messenger office and escort a Messenger out of the city to bring news to Londinium. They part ways, and Horn is contacted by Triangle, a trusted subordinate leading a group of Tempest Platoon soldiers. They have located the stolen Originium weapons—in the hands of County Hillock's artillery battalion. Triangle's team has discovered the Originium weaponry to be modified, the active Originium removed for an unknown purpose. Horn implores them to fall back as they are attacked by unknown hostiles using strikingly familiar tactics and weaponry. An explosion rings out over the radio, and Horn loses contact with Triangle.<ref name="9-9/Story">[[9-9/Story]]</ref>

Horn confronts Adjutant Hill and is taken prisoner. Bagpipe and a Messenger move to flee the city, but the Messenger is killed when the artillery battalion begins a full-scale bombardment of County Hillock. The shells, constructed with the stolen Originium, are faulty—resulting in incomplete combustion and the proliferation of Originium clusters.<ref name="9-10/Story">[[9-10/Story]]</ref> Bagpipe is able to recover the intel from the Messenger's body, and tries to regroup with Horn.<ref name="9-12/Story">[[9-12/Story]]</ref>

Hamilton’s bombardment of Hillock results in heavy civilian casualties, but Dublinn's forces are still active. The Rhodes Island branch office, joined by Jane, mobilize to save as many lives as possible in the disaster, regardless of affiliation. Outcast discovers the grievously wounded Leader, abandoned in the street and suffering an acute infection, and saves her life. Elsewhere, six elite commanders of Dublinn—the Bandit, the Elocutionist, the Toxicologist, the Immolator, the Accountant, and the Convict—search for the missing [[Draco]] Leader. Their conversation reveals that she is not the true head of Dublinn, but rather an individual chosen to represent the movement by the true Leader.<ref name="9-13/Story">[[9-13/Story]]</ref> Jane stands up to Dublinn soldiers to protect wounded Tarans.<ref name="9-14/Story">[[9-14/Story]]</ref>

[[File:The Ultimate Sacrifice.png|left|thumb|What price must we pay to survive?]]
Outcast escapes Dublinn's search parties, chancing upon Bagpipe and hitting it off. Outcast hands the Draco over to her, and stays behind to face off against Dublinn's elite. Bagpipe catches a brief glimpse of purple flames and a mysterious woman, before rendezvousing with Jane and the R.I. Operators, who are treating the wounded civilians in a safe location. Suddenly, a massive pillar of light rips into the sky, illuminating the entire city.<ref name="9-18/Story">[[9-18/Story]]</ref> Outcast had fired the sixth bullet of her revolver, breaking her covenant with [[Kal'tsit]] and killing herself alongside Dublinn's six commanders in a blaze of light.<ref name="9-19/Story">[[9-19/Story]]</ref>

Horn, Bagpipe, Cello, and Oboe, the surviving members of Tempest Platoon, advance on the communication center to get word out, but are intercepted by Dublinn forces. Cello and Oboe are killed, and [[Mandragora]], one of Dublinn's strongest casters and most fanatical supporters, attacks Horn and Bagpipe. [[Harmonie]] uses the communication center to broadcast a message to all neighboring cities, declaring Dublinn's intent to purify Victoria. Eventually, Horn and Bagpipe lose, and Horn forces her subordinate to flee using her Piledriver spear. Horn faces Mandragora once more, despite being wounded, exhausted, and out of ammo. Jane and the Rhodes Island Operators lead civilians away from County Hillock to safety as the sun rises.<ref name="9-19/Story">[[9-19/Story]]</ref>

[[File:Talulah Carries On.png|thumb|The flickering flames still burn.]]
Months after the atrocity, the ''Rhodes Island'' landship faces its first attack since the days of [[Babel]], launched by the remnants of [[Reunion]] under [[Nine]]’s leadership. This new branch of Reunion seeks to break Talulah out of Rhodes Island and exact punishment for her betrayal and the bloodshed she caused. With most Elite Ops absent, [[Kal'tsit]]’s delayed response, and the betrayal of one of Closure’s allies, Reunion succeeds in escaping with Talulah—who chooses to leave rather than remain imprisoned aboard Rhodes Island. Haunted by the destruction left in Chernobog and seeking answers regarding Kashchey and the shadow of the Deathless Black Snake, she sets out across Ursus.
