==Synopsis==
===Absinthe's Testimony===
The year is now 1102. Faced with an unprecedented [[Originium]] energy crisis caused by the Kolossal Prime Vein's rapid shrinking, the mighty Empire of [[Ursus]] faces yet another challenge to its unquestionable order. On December 24th 1101, hundreds of students from across the capital city of [[Deity Grypherburg]] protested in front of the imperial palace during the annual Winter Ball. This protest eventually led to a series of escalating events, resulting in hundreds being quarantined and dozens killed, a tragedy now referred to as the Katorga Incident. Today, hundreds of nobles and council members have gathered in the State Council's hall for a hearing that will define the truth behind what happened in Katorga Raion, and decide who is to blame for the incident.<ref name="ST1">[[PA-ST-1]]</ref>

[[File:72 i01 1.png|thumb|left|"Let the proceedings commence, in the name of His Imperial Majesty's justice..."]]
The arbiter summons the first witness to the stand: Zoya, also known as Operator [[Absinthe]]. As an eyewitness to the Katorga Incident, she was questioned several days ago, and submitted a report regarding the matter. She was close with the members of the [[Ursus Student Self-Government Group]] (USSG), although she is not an official member herself. She also has numerous contacts within the police, giving her access to a lot of information. The committee begins to read her report and cross-examine her testimony.<ref name="ST1"/>

Everything began when news about the [[Far North Rebellion]] broke out in late 1101, resulting in countless student protests. The tragedy spurred a member of the Student Assembly by the name of Rania to vandalise the State Council's hall. His actions took the news by storm and he was promptly arrested. To discuss how to save him, the Student Assembly—a collective of progressive school organisations—gathers their members for a meeting in their secret theater base. Taking part in this meeting is the Ursus Student Self-Government Group, led by their leader Sonya Mikhailivna, also know as [[Zima]]. However, many of the other students look down on the USSG and often accuse them for repeated contempt against the Assembly.<ref name="ST1"/>

The Symposium, represented by Leonid, proposes printing more newspapers to highlight the injustices Rania and other arrested students have suffered to attract more sympathisers. The Vanguards, represented by Faddey, brand these methods as ineffective and advocate for a more direct approach of raiding the prison while the police are preoccupied with the upcoming Winter Ball. Neither the Symposium nor the Self-Government Group support this decision, which they decry as a suicide mission.<ref name="ST1"/>

With the Student Assembly in a stalemate, their leader, [[Olga Trepleva|Olga Danilovna Trepleva]], steps in with a plan that will satisfy all sides. They will hold a peaceful protest in front of the imperial palace on the night of the Winter Ball. Olga says she has organised similar protests in the past, and claims that as long as they exercise restraint, neither the nobles nor the police will lay a hand on them. Most members of the Assembly agree, save for the Self-Government Group. [[Istina]] calls the proposal ill-advised, saying that they'd be offering themselves like meat to the slaughter. Zima however agrees to the proposal, on the condition that anyone who refuses to participate can pull out at any time without affecting their future standing in the Assembly.<ref name="ST1"/>

[[File:72 i03 2.png|thumb|"Death to the <i>mudak</i> Emperor!"]]
On the night of the winter ball, hundreds of students stood outside imperial palace with torches in their hands, shouting and yelling at the nobles watching through the windows. But the students were not coordinated, some were even drunk. Caught in the heat of the moment, they shouted brash slogans and yelled death threats to the emperor. All of a sudden, tear gas is fired at the students, resulting in mass panic. Police officers charged into the crowd, beating up any who tried to resist and arrested hundreds of protestors. Amidst the commotion, Zima was caught while trying to save [[Gummy]]. Before she was arrested, Zima's last words is a plea to her teammates to help the others get to safety before they are arrested as well.<ref name="2Aft"/>

The local police commissioner found the mess to be a huge headache. Most of those arrested were children of the aristocracy, which made it inconvenient to punish them. Their prisons were also full and lacked the necessary capacity to hold all the students. Wanting an easy way out, the police decide to dump the students in Katorga Raion, a former penal industrial plate, home to criminals and factory workers alike. It is a derelict area that has seen power and heat cut completely upon work stoppage. Katorga is currently administrated by Magistrate [[Pavlovich]], who saw the crisis as an opportunity to extend his influence. Shortly after the students were moved into Katorga, the plate was put under complete lockdown.<ref name="2Aft">[[PA-2/Story#After operation|PA-2 After]]</ref>

===Ukusik's Testimony===
The arbiter proceeds to summon the second witness to the stand: a young girl named [[Ukusik]], an orphaned vagrant who grew up on the streets. A committee member tells the arbiter that Ukusik is not exactly friends with the USSG, believing that they can use it to their advantage, but it turns out Ukusik has no filter when it comes to speaking. After being reprimanded twice for talking about things she shouldn't, Ukusik begins talking about the day she met with [[Antosha]], the leader of a rebellious group of students know as the Unbound. They were originally known as the Petrachevsky Group, a collective of students from taught by the eponymous Professor of Literature at the University of Deity Grypherburg, who was executed for his progressive ideas and acts of criticising the empire.<ref name="2Bef">[[PA-2/Story#Before operation|PA-2 Before]]</ref>

Ukusik had been detained at a prison after she was caught stealing on the streets. Antosha and his group suddenly broke into the prison to rescued some of the Unbound's captured members. After Ukusik begged them to free her, Antosha reluctantly brings her back to their base in Katorga. Although she didn't completely understand their ideals, she boasted that she had many useful skills and joined the Unbound. She lived together with them for over a month, growing close with another Unbound member named [[Matvey]].<ref name="2Bef"/>

The Unbound had been preparing to assassinate Pavlovich at the Winter Ball. However, the protest held by Student Assembly interrupted their plan, and they were not able to proceed. Perhaps owing to her small size, the cops didn't notice Ukusik and she got away unscathed. However, she decides to return to Katorga to find Antosha and the others. She crawls into Katorga via the energy pipes and bumps into [[Leto]], who also came to Katorga in search for Zima. They also encounter two Symposium students named Naum and Pencil Stub. After getting spotted by street thugs, the group flee and come across Antosha and Zima beating up Pavlovich's thugs. Seeing that they are stuck in Katorga together, the Unbound and the Student Assembly decide to work together.<ref name="2Aft"/><ref name="2Bef"/>

The next two witnesses are of the nobility, so while they wait for them to prepare, the arbiter goes over Zima's actions in Katorga Raion.

===Cruel Reality===
Life inside Katorga was cruel and unforgiving. Ruled by a gang of ruthless hired thugs known as the Sixteen Warriors, the students' lives were in constant danger. Despite Pavlovich's strict warnings against hurting any of the noble kids, the thugs still bullied and beat them. The group receives a fake letter from Naum, which insisted that the thugs' leader, [[Gromov]], was a "reasonable man". Zima heads out to look for Leonid and saves two students named Nika and Yeremey who were being threatened by Gromov's thugs. She learns that Gromov even set up an auction where students were graded like meat; a slaughterhouse where the rich were extorted for their money, and the poor were killed for fun.<ref name="3Bef">[[PA-3/Story#Before operation|PA-3 Before]]</ref>

Leonid had been killed while attempting to negotiate with Gromov because he wasn't an actual noble. Nika killed the thug who was looting Leonid's body and buried him. However, A haggard old woman appears and asks where here husband is. Nika and Yeremey dig up the body again, learning they had killed an innocent civilian. They surrender whatever money and supplies they had and apologise to the woman, but she screams at them to leave. Matvey stays behind to comfort the grieving woman, telling her to keep living for her husband.<ref name="3Bef"/>

Zima, Leto and the other students continue going around Katorga fending off the Sixteen Warriors and saving more students, but they soon  run into their leader Gromov. Zima is unfazed by his burly stature, but her confidence disappears when Gromov reveals they are in possession of cannons. As the cannon fires, Faddey saves another student by shoving her away and is killed by the blast. The other student survives but her legs were shattered. Thankfully, the Unbound arrived to give fire support and help the rest evacuate to safety, later providing them with medical attention at their camp. Antosha questions why Zima bothers saving the noble students, who he considers to be spoiled and stuck-up, but Zima argues that regardless of their status, there are always people willing to fight.<ref name="3Aft">[[PA-3/Story#After operation|PA-3 After]]</ref>

===Rosa's Testimony===
The arbiter announces their next witness: Natalya Rostova—also known as [[Rosa]]—a young noblewoman who is a member of the USSG. On the day of the Winter Ball, Rosa had entered the imperial palace as a representative of the House of Rostov in order to mingle and gather intel from the aristocracy. Due to special circumstances, she is unable to personally attend the hearing, but has written a letter that is to be read aloud. Countess Vorontsova, a close friend of her family whom she reacquainted with at the ball, has the letter with her. Before she can speak, Absinthe proclaims that Rosa had given her testimony to her. Although surprised, Countess Vorontsova decides not to object and listens together with other members of the hearing.<ref name="4Bef">[[PA-4/Story#Before operation|PA-4 Before]]</ref><ref name="1Bef">[[PA-1/Story#Before operation|PA-1 Before]]</ref>

Not long after the incident at the ball, Rosa contacted the parents of most of the students who were arrested that day under Istina's instructions.<ref name="5Bef">[[PA-5/Story#Before operation|PA-5 Before]]</ref> She learned that the students had been locked away in Katorga, which was now referred to as a 'quarantine zone.' Several of the noble families entrusted Rosa to help look for information of their children's safety, so she went to visit Magistrate Pavlovich for answers. Pavlovich reassured her that all the children were perfectly safe, including Zima. He allows Rosa to meet with Zima under the condition that she persuades the children's parents—many of whom are influential industrial nobles—to repeal the proposal to cast off Katorga Raion into the wilds.<ref name="4Bef"/>

Rosa then meets with Zima at her camp, who says that the Sixteen Warriors are gradually bringing in more heavy firepower. If it continues, they won't have the strength to go up against Pavlovich and his men. Zima requests that Rosa investigate and see if there is any way she could stop it.<ref name="4Bef"/> After asking around, Rosa met a noble whose only child had been detained, and he eagerly introduced her to his superior, who held a position in the State Council. Although the letter does not disclose the councillor's identity for the sake of anonymity, it is actually none other than the council's Vice Chairman, [[Vasily Gorchikov]]. Rosa tells Vasily about everything she saw in Katorga Raion and hopes that the legislature can publicise Pavlovich's actions and strip his authority, but Vasily explains that Pavlovich has many powerful allies within the state council, both old and new nobles. Not even he or the chairman has the power to overturn this. However, he does reveal something crucial: His Majesty the Tsar has disappeared since the winter ball, and they believe that he may have somehow ended up in Katorga Raion.<ref name="4Aft">[[PA-4/Story#After operation|PA-4 After]]</ref>

[[File:72_i06_1.png|thumb|left|"...Believe me when I say that there will come a day that you, too, will defend that 'mob'."]]
Using this information as a bargaining chip, Rosa then visited many noble mansions as a pretence for aid, but their masters were conspicuously unenthusiastic. These nobles insist the tsar's disappearance is simply a rumour without bothering to check, and some of them even wanted to use Rosa to extort the parents of the captured children. It was clear that certain factions may have preferred if the tsar died in Katorga. Rosa eventually managed to win the support of some nobles, and persuaded a particularly important duke into cutting his support to Pavlovich.<ref name="4Aft"/>

A noble named Duke Treplev suddenly stands up in the council hall and admits that he is the anonymous duke Rosa mentioned in her letter. He confesses that he reduced arms shipments to Pavlovich after Rosa blackmailed him by kidnapping his daughter Olga and threatened to spread information that she had been engaging in student protests. A mob of students even swarmed his estate. Duke Treplev argues that Olga had been coerced by the Student Assembly and was completely innocent. Thus, the arbiter prepares to call Olga Trepleva as the fourth witness.<ref name="4Aft"/>

A noble sitting next to Countess Vorontsova questions why she did not present Rosa's "true testimony" when Absinthe had been speaking. Countess Vorontsova recalled the heartbreaking confession Rosa wrote, which included details of [[Children of Ursus/Synopsis|the tragedy which occurred in Peterhemin Middle School]] in [[Chernobog]] during [[Reunion]]'s invasion. Rosa had told her if she had to choose which right she wished everyone would be born with, it would be the right to choose. The Countess responds to her fellow nobles by saying Rosa has simply made her choice, nothing more.<ref name="4Aft"/>

===Olga's Testimony===
[[File:72_i07.png|thumb|"Tell Ursus that we are fighting for our fellow students, our friends, our families!"]]
Olga comes up to the stand, where she begins the explains the actions of Anna Morozova, also known as Istina. Olga had been badly injured following the protest, and the remaining students who had not been captured were in complete disarray. When the other students turned to her for guidance, all she could do was tell them to go home for now. Istina then stops everyone and critises all of them for being cowards and hypocrites. She steps onto the stage and makes a speech to motivate everyone. Only by fighting can they break their shackles and see their friends again.<ref name="5Bef">[[PA-5/Story#Before operation|PA-5 Before]]</ref>

Istina began assigning missions, but Olga soon realised something was amiss. She would not let her go home and insisted that she stay at the theater to recuperate. She was kept inside a locked room with students constantly guarding her door in shifts. Meanwhile, Istina's efforts finally paid off when an infiltration mission at a garment factory revealed that Olga's father, Duke Treplev, had been supporting Pavlovich behind the scenes and was planning to send reinforcements to Katorga. One of the students accidentally leaked this information to Olga and said there were even rumours they would ambush the duke's estate. Olga grew fearful, so in the middle of the night, she escaped the theater and sought refuge at her school.<ref name="5Aft">[[PA-5/Story#After operation|PA-5 After]]</ref>

Istina managed to track down Olga her all the way to the school and confronted her. Olga accused Istina of deceiving her and in her panic, she fell out the window. Olga claimed that she miraculously landed safely and hid in the city until she was found by her father's men. When the arbiter asks whether she wished to officially accuse Istina as the main culprit behind the Student Assembly's heinous crimes, Olga instead boldly accuses herself as the one responsible for the students' actions in the Katorga Incident. Her confession causes such a commotion in the council hall that the arbiter orders for a recess immediately.<ref name="5Aft"/>

The truth was, Istina and the others had saved Olga from falling out the window. Anna said Olga's problem wasn't that she wasn't ruthless enough, but that she was simply running away. Olga's fearlessness came from the fact that she could always run around and return to her father's protection; she has never seen the true face of Ursus, unaware of how difficult it was to survive. Istina says that if she wants to save the other students, she must be willing to put everything on the table, even her own family. Istina needed Olga to "disappear" for a while as part of their plan. After Istina reassured Olga they were not going to assassinate her father, Olga willingly revealed the location of a secret passage to her manor's wine cellar. She vowed to defend the honour of her house in her own way.<ref name="5Aft"/>

===The Tsar's Whereabouts===
In an office in Tsarevgrad, Chairman [[Islam Witte]] and Vice Chairman Vasily discuss a private matter. There is one part that no one at the hearing is allowed to know about: the truth behind the disappearance of His Imperial Majesty the Tsar during the ball.<ref name="1Aft">[[PA-1/Story#After operation|PA-1 After]]</ref>

On the night of the Winter Ball, Tsar [[Fyodor Vladimirovich]] had been arguing with Witte, saying that he should feed the old nobles to flesh buthidas so they can confiscate their assets to fund the treasury. He was incredibly frustrated at having to solicit with the aristocracy, but Witte insisted it was necessary to win their support if they wanted to deal with the encroaching Fourth Army. After Witte leaves, a blackout occurs, and the Tsar hears the students shouting death threats to him from outside. There were also multiple assassins infiltrating the palace. However, he refused to be escorted by the Royal Guard, and steps outside in a plainclothes disguise to see the protest for himself. He was appalled to see a police officer beating up a student and reprimanded him, only to be deemed a traitor and locked up together with the other students and shipped to Katorga Raion.<ref name="1Aft"/><ref name="6Bef">[[PA-6/Story#Before operation|PA-6 Before]]</ref>

In Katorga, the Tsar witnessed countless atrocities firsthand. He had given a chervontsy to a student named Shorty as thanks for saving him, saying that he can use it to bribe the guards. However, Fyodor could only watch as the guards mercilessly kill Shorty. He spends his days mingling with students under the pseudonym Fedya, constantly listening to his earpiece, which relays news from [[Botani]], the Student Assembly's comms specialist. For days, she has been trying to broadcast to the outside world about the brutal killings in Katorga, but due to the radio blackout in the quarantine zone, her voice falls on deaf ears. After hearing that she is trapped in a wardrobe in the thugs' base, Fyodor begins to speak to Botani directly through the earpiece. He calms her down and advises her on how to escape. Thanks to his help, Botani was able to regain her courage, escaping the enemy base and taking a valuable hostage with her back to the student's camp.<ref name="6Bef"/><ref name="6Aft">[[PA-6/Story#After operation|PA-6 After]]</ref>

[[File:72_i09.png|thumb|left|"This is for all the people you murdered, all the evil you've allowed, the judgment and punishment that you've dodged all this time..."]]
Zima had spent the earlier half of the day training the students about squad formations and combat tactics.<ref name="6Bef"/> After Botani comes to their camp, Zima interrogates Pavalo extensively and manages to extract a map of the Sixteen Warriors' headquarters. They have plenty of supplies in store there, which is fortunate as food supplies had been running low. Due to Duke Treplev cutting his support to Pavlovich, many of the Sixteen Warriors' supplies were cut, and Gromov is especially furious that his cannons were taken away. The next day, while he negotiates with Pavlovich, he receives a report that the students are attacking his headquarters. Gromov and his thugs quickly rush to the scene, ruthlessly killing any students standing in their way. He enters the Honor Room so he can distribute the weapons inside, only to find Zima, Leto, and Antosha already in the room.<ref name="6Aft"/>

Gromov evades their attacks and pushes a secret button on the wall, causing an extremely thick glass wall to drop down, separating him from the rebellious students. As Zima thinks of a way to break though, she notices the hamaxe on display nearby. Out of their deep admiration to the sixteen heroes who fought alongside Igor Velikiy, the thugs recreated the legendary hamaxe they used, only to discover it was too heavy for them to wield. Zima breaks the display case and lifts the hamaxe with all her strength, smashing it through the reinforced glass and right onto Gromov. The leader of the Sixteen Warriors was crushed by Zima, but their victory came at the cost of the lives of many brave students.<ref name="6Aft"/>

[[File:72_i08_3.png|thumb|Fyodor cannot describe his feelings in words. He can feel his excitement, and genuine fear...]]
Back at the camp, Antosha wants to kill Pavalo since he's a potential risk, but Zima says they shouldn't needlessly kill people. Refusing to listen to her, Antosha ends the Unbound's cooperation with the Student Assembly. He goes to find Matvey, who is disappointed in his behaviour. Meanwhile, Fyodor does a headcount of the remaining students. Botani laments on whether her actions had made things worse, but Fyodor reassures her that she had done the right thing and that her efforts were not futile. Afterwards, he sits down alone by the campfire and thinks about Pencil Stub, Nika, and Yeremey, who all tragically perished in the fight against Gromov. He begins to realise just how harsh Ursus really is beyond the palace walls.<ref name="6Aft"/>

===Secret Message===
While the rest of the USSG is busy trying to save the students inside Katorga in their own way, Gummy is left stuck at Rhodes Island's provisional office with [[Dur-nar]]. Gummy laments the fact that Zima got caught because of her when they are visited by [[Crownslayer]]. Ever since Crownslayer arrived in Ursus, she has been helping to build Rhodes Island's intelligence network in the Central Governorate, and has been secretly stealing electricity and supplying it to the locals. However, her communications with her supplier is potentially compromised, so she asks whether they have any advanced communicators that run on an independent network that doesn't use a commercial base station. Gummy says Botani's communicator may work, but only Zima knows where she is, so Crownslayer decides to sneak into Katorga with their help.<ref name="7Bef">[[PA-7/Story#Before operation|PA-7 Before]]</ref>

Crownslayer enters Katorga through the underground mobility layer and obtains communications equipment from Zima. Zima also tells Crownslayer to persuade the underground workers to assist in ther final assault. On the way back, she recognises Tsar Fyodor and has a heated argument with him. The Tsar claims he has tried hard to change things in his country, but all Crownslayer can see is that most of Ursus is still rife with suffering. When the Tsar challenges her to kill him, she refuses, saying that it would accomplish absolutely nothing. After she leaves, an [[Emperor's Blade]] appears from the shadows, revealing that they have been following the Tsar. However, Fyodor steadfastly refuses to leave Katorga.<ref name="7Aft">[[PA-7/Story#After operation|PA-7 After]]</ref>

[[File:72 i10 1.png|thumb|left|The voice seems so strange, yet so familiar. A voice that Lada will never forget.]]
Crownslayer rendezvouses with Dur-nar and Gummy in the mobility layer and tries to connect to her supplier's channel. The connection goes through, but the person on the other end is not her supplier. They simply repeat a string of seemingly incoherent words. Gummy however recognises it as a secret code that her father once taught her when she was little. Believing that her father is on the other end, Gummy tries to make him remember, but the man soon hangs up. She then explains to Crownslayer how the secret message works, to which Dur-nar realises that the deciphered message would correspond to the standard format of Ursine military installation identification codes.<ref name="7Aft"/>

Dur-nar and Gummy head into Katorga while Crownslayer stays behind to talk with the workers. Zima is relieved to see her instructor again. She says that Pavlovich's men had burned down their camp as well as Matvey's house, which had served as the Unbound's headquarters. Zima however believes it is a sign that Pavlovich has lost his heavy weapons and is trying to spread fear. She resolves to launch the final assault with the Unbound tomorrow.<ref name="7Aft"/> On the eve of the battle, Antosha tells Ukusik his backstory and entrusts her with letters dedicated to each member to be sent to their parents if any of them die in battle. After Ukusik falls asleep after stealing and drinking Antosha's beer, Matvey leaves her in Zima's camp, telling her to look after the young Liberi.<ref name="7Aft"/><ref name="8Bef">[[PA-8/Story#Before operation|PA-8 Before]]</ref>

===Escape from Katorga===
[[File:72 i12.png|thumb|"Once more unto the breach, dear friends, once more—For all the children of Ursus who will not kneel!"]]
As the day of the attack arrives, Pavlovich is throwing a fit in his room. Duke Treplev had promised him armies, artillery, and tanks, but not a single one has arrived. Pavlovich is then visited by Captain Chicherin of the Ursus Guards serving under Duke Treplev, who informs him that Duke Treplev is “no longer interested” in Katorga. The captain has only brought thirty elite soliders with him and a limited supply of crossbow and explosives. Pavlovich wants to annihilate the entire district, but the captain says that Katorga still remains valuable. Pavlovich is forced to swallow his pride and requests the captain’s help to deal with the Unbound. He orders the rest of the Sixteen Warriors to prepare for battle.<ref name="8Aft">[[PA-8/Story#After operation|PA-8 After]]</ref>

Despite their numbers, the Sixteen Warriors prove to be no match against the now-trained students. Dur-nar, Gummy, and the workers Crownslayer persuaded all join the fight. Backed into a corner, Pavlovich retreats into his residence at the top of the walls. Sonya and the others march towards the wall, stopping before the statue of Igor Velikiy. She begins repeatedly swinging her hamaxe into the statue’s pedestal, which begins to crack. The other students join in on the action, and even Tsar Fyodor slams his weapon into the statue. The statue topples and falls directly on top of the walls, crushing Pavlovich and finally breaking down the impregnable barrier separating them from the outside world. The students march outside the quarantine zone, waving red flags in their hands, proving that they will not let the nobles of Grypherburg oppress them any longer.<ref name="8Aft"/>

Regrettably, everyone in the Unbound except for Antosha perished during the fight against Captain Chicerin and his corps. Matvey did not survive the battle either. Despite being wounded, Antosha tells Zima that since she still refuses to punish Pavalo and the rest of the nobility, she should kill him and pave her new order with his dead body. Zima outright refuses, asking whether believes that his death would somehow lay the foundation for some kind of future, or if he just wanted to die a poetic death. Leto socks him in the face, telling him that his dreams are the meaning of their sacrifice.<ref name="8Aft"/>

[[File:72 i14.png|thumb|Fyodor has lived in this city for decades. Now, for the first time, he walks its streets on his own two feet.]]
Tsar Fyodor exits Katorga and is welcomed by a group of concerned nobles, along with Chariman Witte. Fyodor declines to be driven to the palace, asking whether the fire in Katorga is the culmination of all they have accomplished in the last twenty years. He decides to walk through the city streets himself, letting the cold snow fall upon him. He has not been the same since.<ref name="8Aft"/>

===The Hearing Ends===
The committee has finished hearing all of the witnesses' statements and will adjourn to deliberate behind closed doors. They conclude the hearing and plan to announce the final verdict on the Katorga Incident in two hours. As Absinthe leaves, she catches a glimpse of the notebook in the parlimentarian's hands. A notebook that, normally is supposed to contain a written statement of all witness accounts, is completely blank. It is clear that even before the hearing, the committee has already arrived at a conclusion. In their eyes, Zima is nothing more than a scapegoat that they need to turn into a cautionary tale for any belligerents who would dare oppose Ursus' ironclad rule.<ref name="ST2">[[PA-ST-2]]</ref>

While they wait for Duke Treplev, a police officer suddenly barges in, shouting that students have broken into Prison No.3 and rescued dozens of captured students, including Rania, the one who defaced the council hall. Most officers in Grypherburg had been called in to maintain order in front of the council hall, resulting in lax security at the prison. After rescuing the captured students, the USSG reconvene to celebrate their victory. Zima thanks Absinthe for stalling for time at the hearing and gives her a badge, officially declaring her as a proper member of the Ursus Student Self-Government Group. Together, the USSG heads to Crownslayer's safehouse, where they will be operating discreetly from now on. They will continue their original goal, which is to establish Rhodes Island's first branch office in Ursus. Following the intel Gummy uncovered, Crownslayer prepares to leave Grypherburg for her next mission. Before she leaves, Gummy makes Crownslayer promise to inform her if she finds any news about her parents.<ref name="ST2"/>

[[File:72_i15_3.png|thumb|left|He still lives, but part of his soul will remain beneath the rubble of Katorga forever.]]
In the aftermath of the crisis, Antosha returns to Deity Grypherburg and leaves a letter for Matvey's mother on her doorstep. As a burdenbeast carrying old furniture passes by, he sees Matvey smiling in the reflection of a mirror. Antosha seems to hear Matvey's voice telling him to let go of his burdens and live the life he wanted. Antosha vows that one day he will carve Matvey and all of his comrades' names on a plaque, and joins the workers' procession. On the other end of the procession is [[Nikto]], who survived the Far North massacre. He gazes upon this now unfamiliar city, saying that it has been a hard journey to get here.<ref name="ST2"/>

Inside a command room, officers from the First Army are summoned by a high-ranking marshal. Even after the Katorga Incident shook the capital, the Fourth Army remains locked in a stalemate with the Second Army, and the First Army may soon be dragged into the quagmire. The marshal says their new weapon has passed theoretical validation. It is now ready for testing...<ref name="ST2"/>
