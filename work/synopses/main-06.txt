==Silent Streets==
After repelling [[Reunion]]'s occupation of the [[Lungmen Guard Department]]'s headquarters, [[Rhodes Island]] and the L.G.D. perform a joint operation to rid the city of Reunion once and for all. Rhodes Island deploys [[Blaze]], [[Amiya]], [[Doctor]] and the un-Infected [[GreyThroat]]. Meanwhile, [[Mephisto]] uses his [[Originium Arts]] to convert more of his men into mindless puppets, turning them into his Herd and attacking anyone in the vicinity, including their fellow Reunion comrades. The Herd rip Originium from their bodies to use as projectiles, aiming to infect as many as possible to add to their ranks. Mephisto plans to use the horde of puppets to decimate both Rhodes Island and the L.G.D., allowing him and [[Faust]] to escape Lungmen in the chaos. Faust, however, is disgusted by his actions. Instead, he rallies his Phantom Crossbowmen and sets out to save as many people as possible from the Herd, abandoning a confused and distraught Mephisto.

While [[Ch'en]] and [[Swire]] lead their squads through the slums to cut off the scattered and retreating Reunion forces, Ch'en encounters a band of mysterious soldiers wearing [[Shadow Guard|black raincoats]] mercilessly cutting down Reunion fighters and thinning out Mephisto's horde. Three [[Yan|Yanese]] Imperial Censors are present as well—[[Leizi]], [[Taihe]], and [[Jie Zhen]], monitoring the situation in Lungmen with a critical eye. To the puzzlement of both the L.G.D. and Reunion, the slums are nearly entirely abandoned and the streets are empty. Ch'en chases one of the Raincoats to a roof only to be surrounded. Instead of attacking her, they tell her to cease her aggression, and to instead focus on the task of destroying the last vestiges of Reunion. Ch'en demands to know where the slum dwellers went, but they warn her not to investigate, implying that they have [[Wei Yenwu]]'s authority backing them. The shadowy figures then disappear, leaving Ch'en with more questions than answers.

Swire immediately contacts [[Lin Yühsia]], the daughter of the [[Rat King]] who rules the slums and Swire's friend. Yühsia does not reveal much, but makes clear that she is in on Wei's plan to remove any chance of Reunion hiding in the slums amidst the Infected and escaping Lungmen. With this, Ch'en and Swire conclude that Wei is planning to destroy the slums and kill the residents using the Black Raincoats, having been ordered by the censors to conduct the purge.

==Broken Bolts==
Faust returns to Mephisto while rallying those he rescued, as the only way they can escape the L.G.D. is if they combine their forces. He tells Mephisto not to use his Arts again, but Mephisto resists, believing the only thing that matters is their survival. It is revealed that Mephisto was once a boy named Eno, whose only friend was a boy named Sasha who lived in the sewers. Eno gave Sasha bread and taught him how to read and write, while Sasha defended Eno from bullies; however, Eno suffered terrible abuse at the hands of his family. One day, Eno became Infected when his stepmother forced him to swallow a piece of Originium. He was able to manifest his arts through his Oripathy, giving him the ability to heal wounds and manipulate other Infected while rapidly progressing their Oripathy, which he used to compel the other neighborhood kids into killing his abusive family. Eno and Sasha vowed to survive together, and the latter also swallowed Originium so they could both be Infected together. The pair later encountered Reunion and [[Talulah]], and were taken into her care where they shed their old names and their past lives — Eno became Mephisto, and Sasha became Faust. 

[[File:Faust's Crossbow.png|thumb|[[Faust]]'s broken crossbow after his death]]
Rhodes Island and the L.G.D. prepare to push further into the slums to engage Reunion. Meanwhile, Faust orders the Reunion survivors to continue retreating through the slums, as they are the only conceivable way out of Lungmen. Faust decides to stay behind to create an opening, and his last wish is for Mephisto to survive on his own. He orders his men to restrain Mephisto and take him with them, then stays behind and uses his Arts to cover their tracks. Not long after, he dies at the hands of the LGD's crossbowmen. In his final moments, he refuses to fire his own crossbow in retaliation. GreyThroat witnesses Faust's death and reports back to Amiya. Amiya and Ch'en then receive intel that FrostNova's Yeti Squad has rendezvoused with Reunion's forces through a passageway in the city substructure, under the slums.

Meanwhile, [[Crownslayer]] and her men are ambushed by [[Kal'tsit]] and [[Projekt Red]]. Crownslayer is enraged to see Kal'tsit, who she believes sold out Ilia, her father, and the other scientists researching the [[Sarcophagus]] to the Ursus government, causing their deaths. Kal'tsit allows Crownslayer to attack her, summoning [[Mon3tr]] as her opponent. Once Crownslayer was overpowered by Mon3tr, Kal'tsit tells her the truth: that Sergei only betrayed her father because the lives of his children, [[Skullshatterer|Alex]] and [[Misha]], were in danger. However, the scientists did not die in vain; because they had sealed the Sarcophagus, they prevented the Ursus Empire from using the nigh-inexhaustible power source inside it to resume their war machine. Kal'tsit instructs Crownslayer to leave Reunion and the path of revenge behind, blaming it for Sergei, Alex, and Misha's deaths. Afterwards, Crownslayer escapes Lungmen with her squad, and they part ways as she leaves Reunion.

[[Nine]], Ch'en's senior in the L.G.D. and an informant within Reunion, decides to finally join Reunion entirely after witnessing the massacre orchestrated by Wei Yenwu. Ch'en organizes a plan to steer the Yeti Squad towards the Black Raincoats, so that the L.G.D. can confront them at the same time. Meanwhile, Mephisto is traumatized by Faust's death. FrostNova and the Yeti Squad take over, holding off the Black Raincoats through FrostNova's worsening condition. She eventually seals off multiple roads with her Arts, to the point that she falls unconscious from overuse. However, FrostNova's sacrifice allows the rest of the surviving Reunion groups to pull ahead of the L.G.D. and evacuate. Yeti Squad instructs the Phantom Crossbowmen to take FrostNova back to Chernobog so she can be with [[Patriot]] when she dies, then move to hold back the L.G.D. and buy them more time to escape.

==Fading Frost==
Rhodes Island and the L.G.D. join forces one last time. Ch'en saves Blaze from an encounter with the three Imperial Censors, who disparage Blaze and Rhodes Island as a whole for being Infected. Meanwhile, FrostNova wakes up and discovers that the Yeti Squad has died without her, killed by the Black Raincoats; she then elects to stay behind, instructing the Phantom Crossbowmen once more to take Mephisto and go to Chernobog. Rhodes Island later finds her in the same spot, Oripathy worsening to the point of repulsive dissociation, an advanced stage where shards of Originium are attracted to the Infected, painfully resonating with internal Originium and floating around the individual. Despite the excruciating pain, FrostNova is powerful enough to single-handedly overpower the combined might of Amiya, Blaze, and Greythroat. However, she betrays her true feelings during the battle, confessing that she believes Reunion has failed in their mission to help the Infected, and that she and her brothers have fought and died for nothing. She finally challenges them directly, asserting that Rhodes Island cannot hope to achieve its goals if they can't defeat her, let alone Talulah.  

[[File:FrostNova's Death 2.png|left|thumb|"I... would like to join Rhodes Island."]]
The chilling cold finally withdraws when Rhodes Island defeats FrostNova, and the Doctor stays behind to be with her in her final moments. FrostNova regrets her choices and the atrocities she has committed in her last moments, but implores the Doctor to save the ''real'' Talulah from this destructive path. She joins Rhodes Island before dying in the Doctor's arms. 

In the aftermath, Reunion is completely expelled from Lungmen. The slums have been dismantled and its people murdered or displaced. Many Reunion fighters fell during the bloodshed, and many Infected of the slums have joined the remnants. Despite Swire's warning, Ch'en continues investigating the matter and discovers a mass grave of Infected within the sewers, confirming that many innocent Infected were intentionally slaughtered and dumped. An enraged Ch'en moves to directly confront Wei Yenwu.   

[[File:Chernobog Inbound.png|thumb|Reunion is not yet finished with Lungmen]]
Amiya meets with Wei Yenwu to cancel the contract between Rhodes Island and Lungmen, making it clear that Rhodes Island cannot work with someone who would so callously manipulate those who trust him. Without warning, a furious Ch'en barges into the room, accusing Wei of not only eradicating the Infected of Lungmen, but of killing her sister Talulah's father. Wei rebuffs the accusations and tells Ch'en to stand down, but the argument is interrupted by Kal'tsit's arrival. She informs the group that Talulah has maneuvered the urban core of Chernobog to collide with Lungmen in thirty-six hours. While this would normally not be an issue as Lungmen's defenses can easily destroy an already damaged rogue city plate, Chernobog's core has already begun spamming urban identification codes to all of Lungmen's sub-cities, all with the same message: {{Quote|This city belongs to Ursus.}}
