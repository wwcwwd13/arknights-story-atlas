==The Anonymous Ones' War==
After the battle in Chernobog, in which [[Rhodes Island]] managed to escape thanks to [[Ace]]'s sacrifice, it is revealed that [[Guard_(NPC)|Guard]] has survived the ordeal and retrieves a note from [[Scout]]. Before he can finish reading it, he notices the presence of two men: [[Hellagur]] of [[Azazel|Azazel Clinic]], and [[Patriot]], a [[Reunion]] commander. The two veterans exchange greetings and recall memories from the past, but note that they are now [[Infected]]. Patriot invites Hellagur to sit with him for a drink, an offer that the Liberi regretfully rejects. Hellagur admits that he intentionally stayed to see how one of the few remaining brothers he had during his military career had weathered through the years. Although those ties are now broken, Patriot offers to punish the perpetrators who destroyed Hellagur's clinic and invites him to join Reunion, but Hellagur declines. He explains that he disagrees with Reunion's methods and is planning to head to Rhodes Island. Although Patriot is disappointed with his decision, he states they share the same sympathy for the Infected and have a common goal, albeit in different ways. They have a conversation about Ursus, the empire's treatment of the Infected, and about Reunion's actions. It is also revealed that Patriot gave certain rules for the Reunion troops under his command, punishing anyone violating them. 

The conversation between the two veterans ends on a friendly note, with both wanting their daughters to meet one another. In the end, Patriot makes a vow that he will never make Hellagur his enemy nor harm him. He then orders his men to escort Hellagur out of the city safely as they part ways, and wishes that Hellagur finds a "true home" for himself. After this, Guard, who watched the entire conversation, reads Scout's letter in his frustration. In it, Scout hopes for the [[Doctor]] to begin anew, abandoning their cruelty in the past and avoiding the fate of a war machine. Scout writes to Guard to treasure his life. Patriot notices Guard's presence and gives him two options; wait until nightfall to escape, or to stay and help those in need. Eventually, Guard takes the second option and stays with Patriot's forces, setting the stage for his reunion with Rhodes Island [[Episode 07|much later]].

==Destroyer==
On the outskirts of Chernobog, [[W]] learns that a group of Sarkaz mercenaries has deserted and orders her men to stall the deserters. When she arrives, it is revealed that the deserters are led by a Sarkaz swordsman known as [[Flamebringer]], a mercenary W considers an old acquaintance. She tells the Reunion troops to retreat unless they wish to die, and then the two Sarkaz have a conversation.

After Flamebringer reveals he killed all of his ambushers during the civil war in [[Kazdel]], W invites him back to Reunion. Flamebringer explains that he began to question Reunion' methods of wanton destruction, describing them as a flickering match rather than a real war, which led to his desertion. His only request is to have a fight and test [[Talulah]]'s swordsmanship. Flamebringer reveals he witnessed the deal she made with Scout, and in response W reveals that the person who commanded his old squad was the same one responsible for their deaths. She allows him to walk away unharmed. Later, Flamebringer approaches Rhodes Island and decides to join them. After being briefed by [[Dobermann]], he meets the [[Doctor]]. Amused that the Doctor doesn't remember him, he remarks that living longer has its perks.

==Sniper Training==
During a training session in the lower deck firing range of Rhodes Island, [[Liskarm]] notices [[Jessica]] slowing down in the fifth round, having missed several shots to the target's vitals. Jessica lists the mistakes she made, mortified as she realizes she has messed up almost everything. Liskarm advises Jessica to maintain stable control of her [[Arts]] and points out that she worries about every little thing while shooting. They continue for another five rounds.

After three hours of training, [[Franka]] chimes in to taunt Liskarm, noting that what Jessica really needs to work on is her confidence. Liskarm argues that preparation will still benefit her, even if she will be accompanied by [[Frostleaf]] and [[Meteorite]] on the [[Episode 04|upcoming mission]]. She confesses that she wants to share as much of her firearms experience with Jessica as possible before they return to [[Blacksteel]] to report on the situation in Lungmen. Franka muses about having a [[Sankta]] teach Jessica, an idea Liskarm quickly rejects due to [[Laterano]]'s complicated practices, so she jokingly tells Liskarm to just teach Jessica to carry a big shield. Much to her surprise, Liskarm insists that Franka's experience and Arts mastery will be more valuable for Jessica. Looking over Jessica's scores, Franka realizes that her numbers are not much worse than theirs, and she tells the stunned Liskarm not to teach Jessica to become a dumbass.

==Survival Notorization==
In the wilderness of [[Siracusa]], a [[Vulpo]] named [[Vermeil]] sings while hiding in a cabin. She encounters [[Executor]], who claims to be representing the [[Laterano]] Notarial Hall and bears no ill will, but her distrust causes her to flee from him. Vermeil uses her hunting traps to threaten him, but the unfazed Executor instead elaborates on his mission. A Sankta named Dürrenmatt had once saved Vermeil, crafted a metal prosthetic for her, and named her his heir. He had hoped for her to live not only for revenge.

Executor gives Vermeil a letter from the deceased, its lingering scent of blood unsettling the Vulpo and forcing Executor to restrain her. He explains that Dürrenmatt died during an operation for his [[Oripathy]] and added a subordinating clause to his will to ensure Vermeil could live, having sold all of his property to cover the costs of her medical treatment. The truth he does not reveal is that Dürrenmatt did not die on the operating table but instead received long-overdue justice from the Notarial Hall. Upon hearing his words, Vermeil reluctantly accepts Executor's request and agrees to follow him to Rhodes Island for her treatment. Before setting off, Executor decides to assist her in dealing with the fugitives who are targeting her.

==After-class Homework==
Much to her annoyance, [[Ifrit]] is given homework by the [[Doctor]], and she angrily complains about not being able to go on a cleanup mission with [[Silence]]. Later, she has another occurrence of a dream influenced by the [[Diablo]] [[Sarkaz]] in her consciousness. Feeling frustrated, she fills in "C" for all the questions in her homework. While undergoing a routine medical checkup, she gets irritated by the medical personnel's behavior and accidentally burns her homework in the process.

Meanwhile, Frostleaf encounters [[Projekt Red]] and tells the Lupo that she will scare other people if she keeps walking on the walls, although Projekt Red does not understand what she means. Frostleaf is explaining to Red about a word in the girl's homework when they are approached by Ifrit. They have a conversation about Red's defensive habits until Red notices that Ifrit has burned her homework. Provoked, Ifrit goes into another rampage and almost loses control. It is then that [[Muelsyse]] intervenes and gathers water molecules to pacify her. Ifrit is surprised by appearance and begs for her help. Muelsyse comforts her, reminding her to keep her presence a secret and to remember their agreement.

The Doctor appears, noticing that Ifrit nearly burned the corridor down, but commends her for suppressing the Diablo and gives her her favorite dried peppers. Ifrit hands over her charred homework to the Doctor and promises to apologize to the people she troubled. Nevertheless, the Doctor gives Ifrit the opportunity to take part in an operation and to come along to the beach after she apologizes. Ifrit then confronts the Diablo consciousness, insulting it and vowing to never burn everything again.

==Polaris==
[[Rhine Lab]] explorer [[Magallan]] is staying at a scientific outpost in the north to survey the region. A heavy blizzard keeps her stuck inside the outpost, so she works through the items on her to-do list until an unexpected guest appears at the door. Although initially surprised, Magallan lets in a [[Sami]] named [[Santalla|Simone]], who explains that she is searching for someone. Magallan allows Simone to use the supplies at the outpost, unbothered by her infection, and the two chat about Magallan's life as an explorer as well as the cooperation between Rhine Lab and Rhodes Island.

Soon, the two notice that the temperature inside the base is dropping because the heating system has malfunctioned. They argue about repairing the heating pipes to ensure the outpost lasts until the next season, with Magallan insisting that other explorers might arrive, no matter how small the chances, since all explorers search with purpose. Simone then introduces herself as Santalla, an Operator of Rhodes Island, and reveals that Magallan is the one she has been searching for. She volunteers to calm the storm with her power. Santalla steps out of the outpost and recalls the time her eye was stabbed by an [[Ursus|Ursine]] soldier. With a song, she calms the blizzard and hopes that Magallan will continue to [[Expeditioner's Joklumarkar/Synopsis|follow her dreams]].

==Among the Light and the Dust==
One morning on Rhodes Island, [[Kal'tsit]] is called to [[Closure]]'s office. Kal'tsit questions the Vampire about her plans, recalling several pranks she has pulled in the past and reminding her to make a better impression on the Doctor than [[Warfarin]] did. By Closure's own admission, she and the Doctor had "never been close" in the earlier days. Kal'tsit then mentions that Lungmen has grown increasingly dangerous due to its proximity to Chernobog. Closure then asks Kal'tsit to accompany her in inspecting some cables, a request Kal'tsit reluctantly accepts.

On their way to the corridor with broken lights, the two encounter [[Heavyrain]], whom Kal'tsit has brought along so she can acclimate herself to the layout of the landship. They exchange a few words while Kal'tsit checks the numbers on her monitor. Although Heavyrain insists on guarding her, Kal'tsit curtly sends her off, pointing out that she does not need protection. Afterward, Closure gushes about Heavyrain's politeness and teases Kal'tsit about taking in another of her strays. She then realizes that Heavyrain is the patient Kal'tsit once operated on during a 72-hour surgical procedure. Noting that the other staff had taken breaks, Closure bluntly asks if Kal'tsit had been drugging herself to keep working, but Kal'tsit refuses to answer.

The corridor turns pitch black as Closure fixes the cables, and they discuss whether the Chernobog rescue mission was worth the trouble. Kal'tsit points out that it was a very risky decision, but [[Amiya]] would not budge on the issue, causing Closure to claim that the operation only happened because Kal'tsit abstained from voting. In the end, Kal'tsit asserts that she can only support Amiya's choice while Closure reminds her that she is the one dealing with the consequences and questions if Kal'tsit is tired. After they finish the repairs, Kal'tsit questions why Closure was grilling her. Closure reveals her true intentions of getting Kal'tsit to open up and stop bottling her feelings. Although she dismisses it as a waste of time, they make a deal of her opening up to Closure in exchange for coming along whenever there are lights that need fixing. Kal'tsit muses about the strange deals she always ends up making with Closure, but is visibly warmed.
