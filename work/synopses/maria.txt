==Synopsis==
===A New Challenger Appears!===
[[File:MN-Memories of Margaret.png|thumb|The Nearls' creed: "Fear neither hardship nor darkness!"]]
It's the 24th season of the trillenial [[Kazimierz Major]], an annual competitive jousting event held in [[Kazimierz]] that could make or break a knight's life. During this time, the famed [[Pegasus|Pegasian Kuranta]] family, [[House Nearl]], are in danger of losing their knighthood due to the absence of a Knight Primus since the exile of [[Nearl|Margaret Nearl]], the Radiant Knight from Kazimierz. To save the Nearls, Margaret's sister [[Blemishine|Maria]] decided to participate in the Major without consent from her uncle [[Młynar]] and with support from her aunt [[Whislash|Zofia the Whislash Knight]] and their accomplices, which include the [[Ursus (race)|Ursus]] ex-craftsman [[Kowal]], the half-[[Nightzmora (race)|Nightzmora]]n ex-campaign knight [[Vogelweide]], and the [[Liberi]] bartender and ex-competitive knight [[Marcin]].

Nonetheless, the news of Maria's participation in the Major draws the public's interest and hopes that the young knight could come close, if not match, the Radiant Knight's legendary performance. Unbeknownst to the public however, an intrigue involving the Kazimierzian corporates sponsoring the Major for their own interests, all of which are part of the [[Kazimierz General Chamber of Commerce|General Chamber of Commerce]], is happening behind the scenes, which could be detrimental to Maria's professional career.

===Maria Nearl vs. the Plastic Knight===
Maria makes her Major debut in the first match of the preliminaries, where she is pitted against [[Szewczyk|Szewczyk, the Plastic Knight]] from the Roar Knightclub. Before the match, Zofia, acting as Maria's second, warns Szewczyk to not underestimate Maria.

During the match itself, Maria was initially overwhelmed by Szewczyk's tactics of withstanding attacks with his nigh-impenetrable armor while barraging her with crossbow shots and attacking her in melee at times until she noticed a flaw within his armor. Maria exploits the flaw by forcing Szewczyk to exert himself so the armor would overheat and leaves him helpless in close combat, allowing her to defeat the Plastic Knight and earning her first victory. Maria's performance also piqued the interests of [[Flametail|Sona, the Flametail Knight]] and [[Ashlock|Greynuty, the Ashlock Knight]], members of the [[Infected]] knightclub [[Pinus Sylvestris]].

While celebrating her first victory alongside their accomplices, a salaryman named [[Malkiewicz]] approaches Maria and offers her a deal to join the Eszeweria K.C. jointly sponsored by Słoma Food Co. and the [[Kazdel]]-based MARTHE. Despite the potential benefits, Maria turns down the offer, not wanting to tarnish her family's honor.

===Maria Nearl vs. the Brassrust Knight===
The second match pits Maria against [[Olmer Ingra|Olmer Ingra, the Brassrust Knight]] from the Bloodboil K.C., infamously known for his brutality. Despite Zofia's concerns and advice to forfeit the match, Maria pushes through with the match. Sure enough, Olmer was able to overwhelm Maria with his relentless attacks, giving her almost no chance to retaliate, but Maria refuses to give up and fights with all her might. In the end, Maria is knocked out, but Olmer also passes out due to fatigue, resulting in a draw.

In order to get a higher score to progress further, Maria participates in the Fireblade Melee battle royale under Zofia's advice. During this time, she comes faces-to-face with Sona as the two fight their way through other knights and each other. Maria's determination throughout the bout impresses Sona, while Maria learns that the [[Zalak]] is an Infected. However, the revelation also caught the attention of the Kazimierz National Council, prompting them to send the [[Armorless Union]] to "clean things up", while Malkiewicz warns Zofia that the Council are also keeping their eye on Maria.

Maria is later confronted by Zofia and Młynar about her participation and actions in the Major. Meanwhile, Szewczyk expresses his disdain towards his removal from the Roar K.C.'s lineup before getting hospitalized after a certain "incident", and the A.U. assassin [[Platinum]] makes her move towards the Ashlock and Flametail Knights.

===Maria Nearl vs. the Left-handed Knight===
[[Tytus Topola|Tytus Topola, the Left-handed Knight]] is chosen as Maria's next opponent. In the meantime, the Council's speaker [[Czarny]] visits Marcin's bar to inform Maria about it and offers her more proposals to join the Kazimierz General Chamber of Commerce-sponsored knightclubs, all of which she turns down. Right after Czarny leaves, Sona and Greynuty seek refuge in Marcin's bar after their encounter with Platinum left them injured.

[[File:MN-Maria Nearl, the Blemishine.png|thumb|left|"I won... and for what...? Fame...? Money...? Honor...?"]]
During Maria's match against Tytus, the Left-handed Knight turns out to be a formidable opponent for her, who struggles to hold him at bay and is eventually on the verge of losing. Only through Zofia's encouragement and her realization that she is a proud Pegasus who must "fear neither hardship nor darkness" is Maria able to renew her resolve and put up a good fight against the Left-handed Knight, eventually defeating him after a dragged-out match. As she questions what she would gain in return for her participation in the Major, Zofia apologizes for being too hard on her.

[[File:MN-Czarny and Platinum at the Champion's Wall.png|thumb|"...Would anyone want to have a meeting in such a boring, historical place?"]]
Later that night, Czarny invites Platinum to the Champion's Wall where they discuss the famous MVPs of the Major, including the three-time MVP [[Degenbrecher|Degenbrecher the Black Knight]] who is now in the service of [[SilverAsh|Enciodes Silverash]] from [[Kjerag]], and the last Major season's MVP [[Dikaiopolis|Dikaiopolis the Blood Knight]], before the Speaker orders Platinum to carry out her mission and keep a close eye on Młynar.

===Maria Nearl vs. the Executioners===
Maria's next match is a team battle against the Snowyheel K.C., and she was to be paired with the rising knightess [[Fartooth|Justyna Valentine, the Fartooth Knight]]. However, on the day of the match, she was forced to fight alone after Justyna reportedly disappeared following a violent brawl. At that point, the National Council viewed Maria as a threat to the ''status quo'' in the Major, and, in an effort to dispose of her, sends the twin [[Sarkaz]] "executioners", the [[Corrupted Knight|Corrupted]] and [[Withered Knight]] as her new opponents. The Nearls' accomplices realize that Maria is in danger after learning what happened to Justyna and attempt to save her, only to be stopped by Platinum.

What's happening in the arena is no longer a match, but rather a one-sided bloodbath as the twin Sarkaz knights relentlessly assault Maria, leaving her severely injured whilst the twins' [[Arts]] prevent her from even fighting back effectively. At this rate, death is all but certain to Maria.

===Return of the Radiant Knight===
[[File:MN-Nearl Sisters Stand Together.png|thumb|The Pegasus sisters, reunited and fighting side-by-side]]
Just before Maria can meet a grim fate, someone suddenly crashes into the arena and overpowers the twin Sarkaz knights. That someone is none other than Margaret Nearl, the Radiant Knight herself, who had returned to Kazimierz not only to save Maria, but to also restore honor to all Kazimierzians. Together, the Nearl sisters defeat the Corrupted and Withered Knights. Margaret's return was celebrated alongside their "victory", while her sisters-in-arms of the [[Followers]], [[Nightingale]] and [[Shining]], save the Nearls' accomplices from Platinum. The Nearls and their accomplices then return to their residence where Margaret settles her "old scores" with Młynar.

The Radiant Knight's return also caused a huge stir in Kazimierz; Czarny was unceremoniously fired and exiled for his failure, allowing Malkiewicz to succeed him as the Speaker of the Kazimierz National Council; Pinus Sylvestris prepare to strike back in the Kazimierzian Infected's name; and [[Rhodes Island]], who had sheltered Margaret, arrives at Kazimierz, setting the stage for [[Near Light]].
