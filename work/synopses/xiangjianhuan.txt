==Synopsis==
===City of Stoves===
It is now Mid-Autumn in [[Baizao]], the capital of [[Yan]], and the city is bustling with activity as it prepares for the Feast of a Hundred Delicacies. Dozens of mandarins will gather at the imperial palace for a grand banquet with their emperor, the [[True Lung]].<ref name=1Bef>[[OR-1/Story#Before operation|OR-1 Before]]</ref> Yet while everyone else is enjoying the festivities, Deputy Minister [[Leizi|Lin Qingyan]] finds herself investigating a bizarre case.

[[File:58_i05.png|thumb|Decades of hard work are burned to ash in a single night, erasing the past with it.]]
On the 15th of August 1102, a massive fire broke out in the scrollery of Yan's Judicial Review Office, destroying over 70,000 precious case files. Lin Qingyan's mentor, former deputy minister [[Yu Cheng]], confesses to doing the deed. There is just one problem; he was incarcerated in prison at the time of the crime. Despite this, he continues to insist on being the culprit. Unable to find a reason for his actions, Lin Qingyan looks into the last case that Yu Cheng was investigating before his retirement: the murder of the Minister of Justice in 1062. The case was hastily closed with many unknowns and an unsatisfying conclusion. She discovers a link connecting the case with the death of a low-ranking ceremonial officer named [[Gu Quan]], which occurred 30 years later in 1092.<ref name=1Aft>[[OR-1/Story#After operation|OR-1 After]]</ref>

Unfortunately, no follow-up investigation was conducted, so any clues Gu Quan may have found were taken to his grave.<ref name=2Bef>[[OR-2/Story#Before operation|OR-2 Before]]</ref> Any records about him were remarkably scarce. However, there was one thing that Lin Qingyan knew that the imperial court didn't; Gu Quan had a daughter whom he raised in [[Victoria]]. This daughter is [[Blaze]], one of [[Rhodes Island]]'s [[Elite Op]]erators. Lin Qingyan found out about this connection while she was working aboard Rhodes Island as [[Operator]] Leizi. She firmly believes that Blaze could be the key to unraveling this decades-old mystery.<ref name=1Aft/>

===Flavours of the Past===
[[File:58 i04 1.png|thumb|left|Baizao has the best food in all of Yan; whether it's sweet or savory, you'll find it.]]
Blaze is informed by Lin Qingyan about the suspicious circumstances behind her father's death and decides to travel to Baizao with her. Qingyan gives a set of rules for Blaze to follow, including not telling anyone about her father. She is instructed to remain inside her room, but Blaze gets impatient and decides to venture out onto the streets on her own. She goes to a restaurant named Savors at Yu's, where she eats a tangerine cake. She is surprised to find that they taste different from what she remembers. Her nonchalant remark invites the wrath of the restaurant's head chef, [[Yu]], and they get into a heated argument.<ref name=1Bef/>

The two end up making a bet: if Blaze can find better tangerine cakes anywhere else, then Yu must let her eat for free, for a month, as well as teach her how to cook Yanese cuisine. Following the dramatic exchange, Blaze is approached by a woman named [[Xingzhu]], a local food critic who has been writing a book about Baizao's culinary scene. Interested in uncovering the mystery behind these tangerine cakes, she agrees to meet Blaze tomorrow to help with her search.<ref name=1Aft/>

Lin Qingyan is displeased that Blaze disobeyed her orders and decides to lock her room from the outside. In the meantime, she decides to pay a visit to [[Ning Shu]], former Minister of Rites. Based on the archives, Gu Quan worked in the Ministry of Rites for approximately ten years before suddenly getting promoted to envoy and later dying en route to Victoria. Ning Shu calmly explains that it was Gu Quan himself who requested the transfer; he was apparently ill and wanted to spend the rest of his days in his hometown in Victoria. Still, Lin Qingyan believes there must be more to the story.<ref name=2Bef/>

===Master Chef===
[[File:58 i14 2.png|thumb|Logic dictates that you can't become a master chef in just a day... unless your teacher is the best in all of Baizao.]]
Blaze manages to escape her room by jumping out the window, conveniently landing right in front of Xingzhu. Xingzhu then brings Blaze to dine at dozens of restaurants across Baizao, yet they are unable to find the tangerine cakes that Blaze is looking for. She comments on how unusual it is for tangerine cakes to taste sweet, as it would ruin the flavor profile. After a day of fruitless searching, they end up meeting back with Yu. He tells them if they are still unable to find the right cake, then there is one person who might know how to make it: the head chef of Ding Feng Lou–one of Baizao's Four Great Restaurants–[[Mo Bufu]].<ref name=2Aft>[[OR-2/Story#After operation|OR-2 After]]</ref>

However, Chef Mo is extremely busy as his restaurant is in charge of the food for the Feast of a Hundred Delicacies. Fortunately, Ding Feng Lou is hosting a competition to recruit extra chefs for the feast, which would provide an opportunity to meet with him. Yu decides to teach Blaze how to cook so she can enter the selection herself. He gives her a simple yet delicious recipe: a bowl of longevity noodles, which are used to celebrate the True Lung's birthday. He thought it would be simple enough, but soon finds it to be an insurmountable task as Blaze barely knows how to cook, and her fire-based Arts don't help with handling the temperature-sensitive dish. It takes a very long time of mastering the techniques and some encouragement from Xingzhu to meet Yu's high standards.<ref name=3Bef>[[OR-3/Story#Before operation|OR-3 Before]]</ref><ref name=3Aft>[[OR-3/Story#After operation|OR-3 After]]</ref>

[[File:58 i01 1.png|thumb|left|A simple dish, but the skill required to craft it should not be underestimated.]]
The day arrives for Ding Feng Lou's Chef Selection. Blaze enters the luxurious restaurant alongside dozens of other aspiring chefs. With the knowledge imparted by Xingzhu during their city tour, her father's story about Yanese cuisine, and some luck, Blaze is able to make it to the final stage of the competition. Her practice pays off and she manages to cook a bowl of longevity noodles for Chef Mo. Upon tasting it, he immediately recognizes the flavor, but is not pleased in the slightest. In fact, he is angered at the fact that Yu would send someone else to represent his restaurant's cooking rather than attend the competition himself. Before he storms off, Blaze quickly asks him about the sweet tangerine cakes. Chef Mo bluntly replies that blindly adding sugar to tangerine cakes is a feeble attempt at masking poor cooking skills.<ref name=4Bef>[[OR-4/Story#Before operation|OR-4 Before]]</ref>

Just then, the kitchen suddenly catches on fire. With her quick reflexes, Blaze manages to save Mo Bufu. Lin Qingyan then arrives to escort the two to a safe location. After investigating the files at Baizao's imperial academy and the Ministry of Justice, she discovered that Gu Quan had been sent to death row in 1092.<ref name=3Bef>[[OR-3/Story#Before operation|OR-3 Before]]</ref><ref name=3Aft>[[OR-3/Story#After operation|OR-3 After]]</ref> Mo Bufu had previously worked at the prison, serving last meals for inmates, leading Lin Qingyan to believe that he may have met Gu Quan. Chef Mo elaborates that Gu Quan was imprisoned for supporting the Grand Preceptor, a prominent educational reform figure who had plotted treason against the previous True Lung. While in prison, Gu Quan had only been in contact with two people: then-Deputy Minister Yu Cheng, and then-Minister Ning Shu, who, shockingly, ordered his release.<ref name=4Aft>[[OR-4/Story#After operation|OR-4 After]]</ref>

===Cold Hearts===
The interrogation is soon interrupted by Lin Qingyan's superior, Minister [[Chen Che]], who drags her back to the Judicial Office building. Minister Chen had previously warned her to stay away from the case due to a conflict of interest. Chen Che now threatens the deputy minister not to pursue the matter further. Should the full truth be revealed, there would be serious consequences, both for her and Blaze. With this warning in mind, Lin Qingyan is forced to conclude her investigation into this unresolved case.<ref>[[OR-ST-2]]</ref>

[[File:58 i13.png|thumb|left|"If it's a lie, we'll smash it to pieces!"]]
Blaze's morale is crushed when she hears the news from Lin Qingyan. As she dejectedly returns to Savors at Yu's, she finds a note left behind by Xingzhu. Blaze is invited to come to her residence: Ning Manor. Upon arriving, Xingzhu reveals that her real name is Ning Yin, the adopted daughter of former minister Ning Shu. Ning Yin says that Gu Quan had actually worked as a transcriber at the Ning Manor since she was fifteen years old. Ning Yin regrets that she was not able to do more for him, especially now that she realized that he was Blaze's father. She encourages Blaze to keep digging for the truth, and the latter leaves with strengthened resolve.<ref name=6Bef>[[OR-6/Story#Before operation|OR-6 Before]]</ref>

Meanwhile, Lin Qingyan is still grappling over how to proceed with the case. Censor [[Jie Zhen]] arrives to question her about what she intends to do next. He is struck by how powerless she appears despite her usually relentless pursuit for justice. He understands that Lin Qingyan is worried that she will end up implicating Blaze in Yan's top secret affairs, but tells her to keep sticking to her principles. Following the discussion, Lin Qingyan decides to quietly resign from her position as Deputy Minister of the Central Judicial Office.<ref name=6Bef/>

Blaze and Lin Qingyan run into each other by chance and regroup. Even with all the additional information, they are still missing crucial context. Blaze suggests that they go ahead and confront the one person who can answer all of their questions: His Imperial Majesty, the True Lung himself.<ref name=6Aft>[[OR-6/Story#After operation|OR-6 After]]</ref>

===A Feast to Remember===
[[File:58 i12.png|thumb|Fire sweeps across the tables, igniting the feast into a cacophony of chaos.]]
Following the Ding Feng Lou selection, Blaze receives a letter of acceptance to become a chef for the Feast of a Hundred Delicacies. They will use this opportunity to ask the True Lung to reopen the Gu Quan case. Using her newfound privilege, Blaze manages to sneak Lin Qingyan into the palace kitchen. Lin Qingyan then heads to the dining area where the True Lung will be present. Just as the banquet is about to begin, a raging fire suddenly spreads through the entire courtyard, consuming the tables with demonic flames. The grand feast descends into chaos as the guests panic and scream.<ref name=7Bef>[[OR-7/Story#Before operation|OR-7 Before]]</ref>

Blaze flees the kitchen to search for Lin Qingyan and runs into Ning Yin, who advises her to flee quickly. Blaze almost manages to reach the exit when she is intercepted by the Junior Drillmaster, captain of the Yulin Guards. She tells Blaze the shocking truth behind her origins: she is in fact, not Gu Quan's biological daughter. Rather, she is the daughter of the traitorous Grand Preceptor. Lin Qingyan then arrives to rescue Blaze and blasts her straight out of the palace walls. Although the deputy minister gets captured, Yu manages to find Blaze and safely brings her back to his restaurant.<ref name=7Aft>[[OR-7/Story#After operation|OR-7 After]]</ref>

It is then that Yu informs her that Gu Quan was actually a regular patron at Savors at Yu's, although he did not remember until recently. The scholar would process the restaurant's tangerine cakes with loads of sugar so that they can be preserved and sent back to Blaze in Victoria. In fact, he came to the restaurant to buy a tangerine cake on the day he died.<ref name=8Aft>[[OR-8/Story#After operation|OR-8 After]]</ref>

[[File:58 i08 1.png|thumb|left|Gu Quan has never forgotten about the daughter he left behind.]]
Inside the palace, Ning Yin is knocked out by an imperial guard and brought back to her estate.<ref name=7Aft/> Her grandfather, aware that his past has now caught up to him, decides to surrender himself to the Judicial Review Office, leaving behind a forbidden history book containing a long-buried secret. During the Baizao coup in 1062, the sitting Minister of Justice had used the Grand Preceptor as a scapegoat, resulting in her suicide and the imprisonment of all her family members. Gu Quan went inside the Grand Preceptor's home and rescued the sole survivor, who was a newborn baby. He attempted to flee the country, but was caught by an imperial guard, and forced to surrender his own newborn child instead.<ref name=ST1>[[OR-ST-1]]</ref><ref name=8Bef>[[OR-8/Story#Before operation|OR-8 Before]]</ref><ref name=8Aft/>

Gu Quan's child fell into the hands of the Minister of Justice. Ning Shu–once a student of the Grand Preceptor–was fed up with the minister's extreme actions, then ordered his assassination. He thereafter adopted the child and raised her like his own.<ref name=ST1/><ref name=8Bef/>As it turns out, Gu Quan later returned to Baizao to find this child. Realizing that she is actually Gu Quan's biological daughter, Ning Yin heads towards the restaurant to meet with Blaze. Ning Yin and Blaze come to terms with their shared past, reuniting like long lost sisters. Blaze promises her that she will demand answers from the True Lung, for everyone's sake.<ref name=8Aft/>

===The Taste of Justice===
[[File:58 i02.png|thumb|Blaze arrives to ask her question: was the sacrifice of the Grand Preceptor truly worth the price?]]
Blaze returns to the imperial palace to confront the True Lung. This time, she is accompanied by Lin Qingyan—who was rescued from prison by Censor Jie Zhen—and Vice Minister [[Taihe]]. As they prepare to fight their way inside, a horn blows loudly, signalling the guards to make way for them. Blaze enters the palace alone and presents the True Lung with a bowl of longevity noodles. She asks the emperor what the Grand Preceptor was like, and why such a kind person was punished so unjustly.<ref name=9Aft>[[OR-9/Story#After operation|OR-9 After]]</ref>

The True Lung stated that it was an act born of necessity. At the time, the old True Lung had suddenly died, and his older brother—crown prince [[Wei Yenwu|Yan Wu]]—quietly fled the capital. To stabilize the situation, he was forced to take the throne and purge countless traitors from the court. The Grand Preceptor, who had always stood by his side, was made to take the fall for plotting treason in order to legitimize his reign. His heart is filled with regret, yet he cannot rescind his words nor admit that he made a mistake. Blaze herself knows this, and firmly tells the True Lung to never forget those who sacrificed themselves for their country.<ref name=9Aft/>

White snow begins to fall from the sky, as if Heaven itself is mourning those who have passed.

===You and I, Us and We===
While Blaze and the others are at the palace, Yu leaves his restaurant to deliver a special dish. He entrusts the ownership of Savors at Yu's to his assistant chef, [[Jiang]], who also happens to be the [[Sui Regulator]] Candleholder in charge of supervising him. In truth, Yu is actually the youngest fragment of the bestial [[Sui]]. He heads towards a decrepit temple and enters, finding his customer already waiting: [[Wang]], his second eldest brother.<ref name=9Bef>[[OR-9/Story#Before operation|OR-9 Before]]</ref> Much to Wang's surprise, Yu lays out an entire feast for him, alongside a special bowl of soup, containing the flavour of "regret". Yu does not try to stop Wang from fighting the bestial Sui, but instead wishes him good luck and prays that he comes back safely so they can have a real family reunion someday.<ref name=9Aft/>

[[File:58 i07.png|thumb|"We have long since ceased to be your vassals. Now we must fight, for victory or death."]]
Accompanied by an army of imperial guards, Wang and [[Ji]] arrive before the Tomb of Sui. Ji hands him the items that they've procured from their other siblings: [[Chongyue]]'s sword, [[Shu]]'s seeds, and finally Yu's soup. Wang tells Ji to leave so he can settle the matter for himself. This time, he will succeed. He will avenge his sister Jie and safeguard the future of all his siblings. At the True Lung's decree, the Tomb of Sui gradually opens. From across the other corners of Yan, [[Nian]], [[Dusk]], and [[Ling]] sense that the time has arrived.<ref name=9Aft/>

The final battle against the bestial Sui is about to commence.
