==An End, and a Beginning==
[[File:Abrupt Salvation.png|thumb|The hand of "God" reaches out to save a forsaken child...]]
The war for [[Londinium]] has reached its final stage in October of 1098. As the Exemplars and the Ducal forces clash with the [[Nachzehrer]] army, a young [[Cautus]] by the name of [[Hierda]] roams the treacherous battlefield, treating wounds and saving lives. She rescues a lost [[Sarkaz]] child and escorts him to [[Reunion]], who promises to keep him safe. The offer is extended to Hierda as well, but she refuses. Instead, despite her rapidly worsening [[Oripathy]], she continues with the act of saving lives in [[Londinium]]. Eventually, her Oripathy and exhaustion win out, and she crystallizes alone on the battlefield. As she thinks of her faith in a ''wonderful future'' during her final moments, she feels a hand reach out and grab her own.<ref name="15-1">[[15-1]]</ref>

Not far away, [[Amiya]] is able to hear Hierda's crystallization, just as she has heard many others pass on during the [[Victorian Crisis]]. Her, [[Kal'tsit]], [[Ascalon]], and the [[Doctor]] carry on to confront [[Theresis]] at the [[Palace of Westhaleg]]. However, unbeknownst to them, the Regent has already entered the [[Assimilated Universe]] to follow in [[Theresa]]'s steps. He climbs the tower Theresa created, seeking to sever the creator's control over Amnannam, with the goal of giving the Sarkaz a future free from the ghosts of the past.<ref name="15-1">[[15-1]]</ref>

[[File:60 i23.png|thumb|left|The Regent confronts the arrogant evil spirit who treats his entire people's history as a mistake.]]
Once the Regent reaches the pinnacle, he is confronted by a manifestation of Ascalon created using his memories. He easily defeats the construct, and plunges into the golden sea—diving into its deepest depths.<ref name="15-2">[[15-2/Story|15-2]]</ref> As he slices through the chaotic information, the creator awakens and finally acknowledges his presence. She introduces herself as [[Priestess]]. She remarks that his and Theresa's crude actions have greatly limited her influence over Amnannam, but overall remains unconcerned. Theresis lambasts the creator's arrogance and severely weakens her before he is forced to return to Terra.<ref name="15-16">[[15-16]]</ref>

In the ancient throne room beneath the Palace of Westhaleg, the Rhodes Island leadership battles [[Manfred]] before they are interrupted by Theresis's return. However, he is now being corroded by the information he absorbed in the Assimilated Universe—and is being consumed by Originium at an astounding rate. He uses the First Originium to shepherd Manfred to safety, and Rhodes Island is able to force him to yield as Ascalon pierces his heart with her blade, only to find that his internal organs have long since been overtaken.<ref name="15-2"/>

Ascalon takes Amnannam, and they decide to bring it to [[Kazdel]] as Theresis wishes. The Doctor is no longer able to alter the First Originium, for it has been successfully modified by Theresis, and lies solely in the control of the Sarkaz. After a brief exchange, the Rhodes Islanders leave with Amnannam, leaving Theresis where he sits on the ancient Victorian throne. [[Civilight Eterna]] appears in order to bring him Theresa's parting words. With a final goodbye to Theresa and a smile on his face, Theresis dies alone beneath the Palace of Westhaleg.<ref name="15-2"/>

Hierda awakens in a strange yet warm place despite having perished from her Oripathy. Believing that she has reached the afterlife, she wanders, guided by shifting patterns on the walls. She eventually witnesses a machine that she sees as 'god' drift by her, and is approached by another mechanical being. It tries to communicate with her, and scans her brain in order to learn the Terran language. Hierda asks the machine if it is her god, but the machine does not understand what she seeks. It tells her that the idea of 'god' is unnecessary, and shows her where they are—aboard a ship orbiting a dead planet, punctuated by a dying sun trapped in an endless cycle of exploding in a supernova.<ref name="15-3">[[15-3/Story|15-3]]</ref>

==Inspecting the Unknown==
===Abyss===
Three years have passed since the horrific Victorian Crisis. Rhodes Island has participated in handling numerous other major incidents since then and has welcomed many new operators onto the landship. The Medical Department has also seen a huge uptick in Oripathy patients as a result of the Victorian Crisis. One of the most notable patients is, ironically, an average Victorian woman by the name of Chloe. Her file indicates that a majority of her internal organs have been assimilated by Originium, yet at the same time, they are also supporting her body's functions. [[Warfarin]] and [[Touch]] suspect that this could be a new mutation that could yield new results for Oripathy research.<ref name="15-3"/>

[[File:"Abyss".png|thumb|Kal'tsit knows many secrets, but not even she is sure of what lies inside the belly of Rhodes Island.]]
In the command center, Kal'tsit discusses their current issue with the Doctor, Amiya, and [[Closure]]. Although everything seems to be business as usual, the landship's terminal system—[[PRTS]]—has recently been exhibiting strange irregularities. Kal'tsit has concluded that PRTS is slowly spiralling out of control and decides that they must prepare to safeguard against possible internal threats. They draw two conclusions. The first is that they need to shift their reliance on PRTS, and the second is to simplify the landship's structure, thereby boosting its defenses and improve response time during emergencies.<ref name="15-4">[[15-4/Story|15-4]]</ref>

As much of the landship's medical and resource allocation calculations depend on PRTS, replacing it proves to be a difficult task. As a precaution, Closure disconnects [[Friston-3]] from PRTS and continues development on an independent terminal system codenamed [[ZOOT]]. Meanwhile, Rhodes Island leverages its connections with its various allies and plans to distribute important personnel and assets to [[Milliarium]], [[Volsinii|Nuova Volsinii]], and Kazdel.<ref name="15-4"/>

Only one problem remains: a mysterious area located deep within the middle area of the ship, known as the [[Rhodes_Island_(landship)#Abyss|Abyss]]. It is where PRTS's core server and power source is stored, and most likely has the answers to its recent anomalies. The operation will be incredibly risky, so Kal'tsit declares that they have fifteen days to migrate everyone out of the landship before they can prepare to enter the Abyss. The Doctor and Kal'tsit later do a preliminary search around the perimeter of the Abyss. The Doctor surmises that the Abyss is related to [[Priestess]], and Kal'tsit grimly states that Amnannam's descent has likely caused her awakening. Theresis's actions have bought them some time, but that time will soon run out.<ref name="15-4"/>

===Deep Dive===
During the personnel and asset transfer process, Rhodes Island's operators are repeatedly ambushed by mercenaries and attackers of untraceable origin. Kal'tsit believes that classified information is being purposefully leaked in order to sabotage their operation. Someone is manipulating different parties behind the scenes, yet no one on Rhodes Island has the means or motive to do so. If they want to prepare themselves against the internal threat, they require more proactive measures.<ref name="15-5">[[15-5/Story|15-5]]</ref><ref name="15-6">[[15-6/Story|15-6]]</ref>

The Doctor comes up with a bold plan: to connect the Civilight Eterna to their thoughts in search of information. Although Kal'tsit rejects the plan, she correctly predicts that the Doctor and Amiya will not listen to her, and has already left behind clues inside Civilight Eterna to facilitate the process.<ref name="15-6"/> Amiya links to the Doctor's mind using the Black Crown and the two find themselves standing on a windowless hallway that forms a complete circle. Floating in a void at the center of the circle is a condensed singularity of information. The Doctor can see long forgotten fragments of the [[Predecessor]]s' past drifting in the empty space. Civilight Eterna explains that this space is a "cognitive barrier" created by the system to prevent people from accessing knowledge that would break the natural learning progress of civilization.<ref name="15-7">[[15-7/Story|15-7]]</ref>

[[File:60 i04.png|thumb|left|The Doctor and Amiya prepare to touch the forbidden knowledge hidden away within the Black Crown.]]
Using the access records left by Kal'tsit and Theresa's research data, Amiya creates a "bridge" using the decipherable information found in the space. She attempts to cross the bridge first, but soon finds herself overwhelmed by the sheer density of information. Fortunately, the Doctor comes to her rescue and helps her make sense of the information around them. As they get closer to the singularity, they witness a memory of the Doctor—then known as Oracle—communicating with Priestess, from a time before Originium existed. Using additional information extrapolated from their conversation to extend the bridge, the Doctor and Amiya finally reach the singularity.<ref name="15-7"/><ref name="15-8">[[15-8/Story|15-8]]</ref>

Inside, they discover a set of formulas developed by Oracle. They only had limited time to transcribe the information before Civilight Eterna ejects them from the space. Upon waking up in reality, Kal'tsit does a quick medical checkup on Amiya and the Doctor. After ascertaining that the Doctor's cognitive functions remain normal, Kal'tsit reviews the formulas they found. She explains that it is a model based on a project called "Slumber Bridge," which aims to reduce the loss of meta-information during transmission through special mediums. Realizing that this model can be applied to Originium research, Kal'tsit and the Doctor develop a new version of the "Originium Arc," an external tool that improves the adaptability and combat ability of elite operators in high-Originium environments.<ref name="15-9">[[15-8/Story|15-9]]</ref>

As the day to enter the Abyss approaches, Rhodes Island has been distributing the Originium Arc to its operators and gradually moving out all personnel to an isolation zone one kilometer from the ship. Little did they realize that the true enemy would be the landship itself.

==The Awakening==
===Don't Leave===
On one seemingly ordinary day, [[Popukar]] wakes up to the sound of clanking machinery. She walks out into the landship's hallways, only to find it completely deserted. Furthermore, she sees an image of Hierda communicating with the Presence in every porthole. She eventually manages to find her way outside, but inside the landship, everything has devolved into total chaos.<ref name=11Aft>[[15-11/Story#After Operation|15-11 After]]</ref>

PRTS has gone completely rogue. Communication channels have been shut down and all sectors of the landship have been sealed off by rapidly growing Originium clusters. The rooms themselves seem to be constantly shifting and changing, as though the ship itself was alive. Maintenance machines and drones have been corrupted by the Originium inside their power systems and begin to attack Operators still on board the ship. Familiar enemies from PRTS's database manifest into reality using Originium, attacking anyone in sight. PRTS has also hijacked the landship's controls, making it accelerate rapidly towards the Tartangils Prime Vein in [[Rim Billiton]].<ref name=11Aft>[[15-11/Story#After Operation|15-11 After]]</ref><ref name="15-13">[[15-13/Story|15-13]]</ref>

[[File:60 i22.png|thumb|If it means safeguarding the future of Terra, Kal'tsit is willing to bear the consequences of her actions.]]
In the lower decks, Kal'tsit and Mon3tr fight through the horde to reach the Sarcophagus that once held the Doctor. Kal'tsit enters the Sarcophagus while Mon3tr stands guard.<ref name=11Aft/> Much to her surprise, Kal'tsit discovers that Oracle has left behind a simulated version of themselves inside. Kal'tsit consults Oracle on how to enter the Abyss, as there are restrictions which prevent her from approaching. Oracle replies that she must sever the gemini cycle system that links her to Mon3tr, which has granted her immortality, so that PRTS will not recognize her as AMa-10. Kal'tsit willingly takes the risk and severs her connection with Mon3tr, who becomes alarmed that it can no longer sense Kal'tsit's presence.<ref name="15-13"/>

Kal'tsit directly interfaces with PRTS's core mainframe, absorbing all of its information into her brain. She wrestles to take control over the system from Priestess. Acting as a substitute for PRTS, she contacts Raidian and has her relay a message to all active operators on board the landship. Everyone must rendezvous in designated buffer zones located throughout the landship and work to protect each other. Meanwhile, the elite operators are charged with breaching the core of the landship, where the Originium entities originate from: the Abyss.<ref name="15-13"/>

In the Medical Department, Warfarin does her best to fend off the encroaching Originium entities. Warfarin notices that for some reason, they are not hostile to Chloe, and realizes her unique Oripathy strain must be the reason behind this unprecedented behaviour. At Chloe's own insistence, Warfarin conducts numerous dangerous tests on her, obtaining valuable information that is used to upgrade the Originium Arc equipped by all Operators. Regrettably, Chloe's condition rapidly deteriorates and she loses her life as a result.<ref name=11Aft/><ref name="15-13"/>

===Open Your Eyes===
Inside the Rhodes Island spaceship, Hierda has been studying and communicating with the various residents on board, such as [[Raydistorter]] and [[Character/NPC-Minor#The_Presence|The Presence]]. Yet, she knows she can never be truly part of this magnificent crew. After waking up from a brief sleep, she discovers time has somehow rewound, and nobody seemingly remembers her.<ref name="15-9"/> Hierda's panic is interrupted by Priestess, who takes her to a massive monument. Priestess states that this structure was a tombstone of every civilization that ever existed in the universe.<ref name="15-12">[[15-12/Story|15-12]]</ref>

[[File:Contact A.png|thumb|left|Having glimpsed a vestige of truth, can Hierda go back into the darkness of ignorance?]]
Hierda recognises Priestess as the one who saved her from death and thanks her. Much to her surprise, Priestess states that Hierda did indeed die, but she continues "living" as information recorded by Originium. Hierda may not understand, but she is in fact inside the Assimilated Universe. The spaceship and its residents are also just streams of data being repeated over and over, dedicated to preserving a snapshot of an ancient past.<ref name="15-12"/>

Priestess shows Hierda a glimpse of the brilliant yet fleeting magnificence of the universe's civilizations. Hierda is amazed by the sight, enticed to learn more about the universe. She decides to spend the rest of her time on the spaceship to continue learning from the ship's inhabitants.<ref name="15-12"/> Gradually, she starts to feel disconnected from Terra, which now feels light years away. Priestess once again summons Hierda, informing her that she will be visiting Terra. She offers two choices to Hierda: she can stay here and keep learning, or return to her home planet.<ref name="15-16"/>

While Hierda ponders the decision, Priestess leaves the Assimilated Universe to see Oracle once more.

===For She Has Returned===
[[File:60 i11 2M.png|thumb|Elite Operators, advance forward!]]
Command signals are now being rerouted through the newly activated ZOOT. Its advanced calculative abilities allow Operators to navigate the ever-twisting interior of the landship and accurately predict enemy movements. The Originium Arc also helps them maintain their Arts despite the high concentration of Originium in the environment. On their way to the Abyss, [[Blaze]] and [[Rosmontis]] come face-to-face with [[Lancet-2]], [[Castle-3]], [[THRM-EX]], and [[Justice Knight]]. The [[Operation Platform]]s have gone rogue due to PRTS's influence and start attacking them. Fortunately, Friston-3–who has been previously disconnected from PRTS–arrives and frees rampaging buggies from their corrupted systems using a special code synchronization system.<ref name="15-14">[[15-14/Story|15-14]]</ref>

Blaze and Rosmontis reconvene with the others and break through the formation of Originium entities. The Doctor opens the Sarcophagus and rescues Kal'tsit, who is exhausted but alive. With her modifications complete, Kal'tsit is able to open a passageway into the Abyss. The Doctor rallies the elite operators as they march into the rift.<ref name="15-15">[[15-15/Story|15-15]]</ref> Inside, the elite operators see a massive machine located in a white room that is protected by some kind of dimensional warp. It is then they realize that the Doctor has vanished. PRTS suddenly activates, identifying the elite operators as a primary threat, and begins summoning hordes of Originium entities.<ref name=17Bef>[[15-17/Story#Before operation|15-17 Before]]</ref>

[[File:60 i25.png|thumb|left|"Long time no see."]]
Meanwhile, the Doctor, Amiya, and Kal'tsit somehow find themselves on the landship's deck, separated from the Elite Operators. The skies suddenly darken and Originium dust rains from the sky, causing crystals to rapidly spread across the floor. A giant Originium crystal grows on the deck, its tip blooming into a distinct, familiar human figure... She is Kal'tsit's greatest fear, Originium's creator, and the enemy of all of Terra. Priestess.<ref name="15-16"/>

With nothing but a thought, Priestess dispels the [[Catastrophe]] clouds forming above their heads. Priestess understands that Oracle lost their memories to an unfortunate series of events, but still wishes for them to be reunited once more. She implores the Doctor to return to her side and continue the Originium project. The Doctor momentarily falters, but steels their resolve to protect the living beings of Terra which Priestess perceives as an act of betrayal. Kal'tsit is relieved by the Doctor's decision and decides to finally reveal the truth. She tells the Doctor about their past as Oracle, and how they created Originium together with Priestess. She promises to protect the Doctor and Amiya to the very end, and prepares to face her worthless creator.<ref name=17Bef/>

[[File:60 i12 2.png|thumb|She has kept her promise.]]
Kal'tsit's modifications have forced Priestess to become temporarily mortal while she is on Terra. With the combined power of Mon3tr's strength and Amiya's Arts, they obliterate Priestess into pieces. However, the progenitor of Originium suddenly appears from behind them, revealing that they had merely destroyed a substitute made using Originium. Priestess expresses her disappointment in Kal'tsit's betrayal and her insignificant beliefs. She deems her as an experimental failure and decides to destroy her once beloved creation. With a single point of her finger, the piece of Originium on Kal'tsit's shoulder rapidly expands and consumes her body whole, before disintegrating into nothing but dust.<ref name=17Aft>[[15-17/Story#After operation|15-17 After]]</ref>

The life of AMa-10, Kal'tsit, ends here.

==The Hole Left Behind==
===Loss===
Having lost its host, Mon3tr howls in pain and shrinks into a crystalline cocoon. Amiya and the Doctor are utterly shaken, but they have no time to grieve. Priestess manipulates the Originium around them in an attempt to encase Amiya and the Doctor. The Doctor then recalls the formulas they recovered from Civilight Eterna and tells Amiya to weave them into her Arts, allowing her to stall the advance of Originium. Meanwhile, the Doctor reestablishes contact with the Elite Operators thanks to Mantra and Raidian’s Arts. The Doctor urges everyone to evacuate Rhodes Island as soon as possible.<ref name="15-18">[[15-18/Story|15-18]]</ref>

[[File:PRTS Unbound.png|thumb|PRTS has assembled a killing machine made of things once familiar to them.]]
The Elite Operators are able to overcome the Originium entities and push deeper into the Abyss. They come face-to-face with a massive machine, its metal frame crudely assembled with repurposed parts of Rhodes Island’s landship.<ref name="15-18"/> The [[Mimic Machine]] proves to be a tough foe as it continuously adapts to their attacks and constantly repairs itself with Originium no matter how many times they strike it down. Mechanist discovers that the strange device in the Abyss core is a special Sarcophagus which also doubles as PRTS’s power source and data server. Using a backdoor left behind by Kal'tsit, he manages to retrieve the core data of PRTS and deletes everything else from the system, finally shutting it down.<ref name="15-20">[[15-20/Story|15-20]]</ref>

With their mission accomplished, the elite operators rush to the landship’s deck and save the Doctor and Amiya. Amiya manages to snatch Mon3tr’s cocoon just before they flee. Everyone recuperates at the isolation zone around the perimeter of the ship. Although everyone managed to survive, Rhodes Island has lost their home, and their trusted leader.<ref name="15-20"/>

===A Life Untethered===
One month has passed since the tragic incident. News of the landship’s destruction spread quickly to the branch offices, with many initially thinking it was some unverifiable rumour. Rhodes Island has been gradually moving their personnel and assets to a new nomadic plate that Kal’tsit purchased in Butchart, near the Victorian border. Everyone has been pitching in to help take care of the construction work and assemble all the plates together.<ref name="15-20"/>

[[File:"Mon3tr".png|thumb|left|Mon3tr no longer has a master to watch over her, but she will continue to carry on Kal'tsit's legacy.]]
The Doctor and Amiya check on Mon3tr’s cocoon that they hid inside a forest clearing. Just then, the cocoon blooms into a great crystal tree with innumerable branches, before collapsing into a single point. In its wake is Mon3tr, who has now taken on a human form. The Doctor and Amiya cannot help but feel a twinge of pain that it was not Kal’tsit standing there, but they nonetheless welcome her as a new Operator of Rhodes Island.<ref name="15-20"/>

Warfarin conducts a presentation about a scientific paper she wrote, with Chloe credited as its co-author. She describes that Oripathy–or rather, Originium itself–may be undergoing a drastic, unprecedented change, and that Terra must be prepared to face what’s to come. Following the conference, Mon3tr proclaims that she had discovered a way to resurrect Kal'tsit. The primary requirement is that they need an incredibly powerful life support device—a Sarcophagus. With the Sarcophagus in Rhodes Island buried under thick chunks of Originium, there is only one other usable Sarcophagus remaining...<ref name="15-21">[[15-21]]</ref>

Rhodes Island prepares to return to where it all began: where they saved the Doctor in [[Chernobog]], [[Ursus]].

===Premonition of Disaster===
In the Assimilated Universe, Hierda tells Priestess that she has decided that she wants to return to Terra. Priestess obliges, and when Hierda opens her eyes, she finds herself back on Rim Billiton, near the wreck of the Rhodes Island landship. A distraught miner sees her and brings her over to his Infected brother, who is on the verge of disintegrating. Hierda clasps the dying man’s hand to offer him some peace, but then a miracle occurs. The man’s Originium crystals begin to gleam brightly and he no longer feels any pain from his Oripathy.<ref name="15-21"/>

Out in the wilderness beyond the borders of Kazdel, Legatus [[Oren Argiolas]] has captured a Sarkaz mercenary who robbed a caravan. Touting himself as an upholder of justice, Oren points his gun at him and fires. Somehow, he misses, and the Sarkaz screams in horror; not at the near-death experience, but at the horns growing out of Oren’s head. A realization dawned upon him: he became a [[Sankta#Fallen|Fallen Sankta]] for attempting to shoot a Sarkaz.<ref name="15-21"/>
