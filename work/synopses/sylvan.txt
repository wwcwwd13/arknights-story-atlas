==Plot==

===Unexpected Arrival===
[[File:RS-Kjeragandr Watching the Snow Realm B.png|thumb|The Kjeragis thank their god's blessing by constructing a massive statue of Her.]]
Three years after the Snowcap Incident that almost tore the nation apart, [[Kjerag]] is beginning to fully embrace modernization. Under [[SilverAsh|Enciodes]]' leadership, [[Karlan Trade]] has been importing new technologies from the other nations, mainly from [[Victoria]]. Homemade heavy industries are established and luxury high-speed railway systems attract tourists from all over [[Terra]] to visit the hinterland. To thank Kjeragandr for their newfound prosperity, they erect a giant statue of her atop an island on [[Lake Silberneherze]]–a sacred icy lake believed by the locals to be the most precious treasure the goddess has ever left.<ref name=ST1>[[RS-ST-1]]</ref>

Regular tourists aren’t the only people visiting the snowy country though. The [[Duke of Caster]] from Victoria dispatches Viscount [[Harold|Harold Craigavon]]–a seasoned war veteran–along with his personal army, whose sudden arrival startles the Kjerag commonfolk. Many were initially unsettled about what motives the foreign dignitaries could have, but the soldiers do their best to mingle with and help the villagers in their daily lives. A month passes smoothly without incident, and it truly seems like they were just here for vacation.<ref name=4Aft>[[RS-4/Story#After operation|RS-4 After]]</ref>

Nevertheless, Enciodes knows that the Duke of Caster did not send Harold to Kjerag for no reason. On one hand, it is a form of political pressure, a test against the country’s sovereignty. On the other hand, Karlan Trade must also honour their long-term partnership with House Caster. Enciodes busies himself cozying up to the Viscount while ordering his messenger [[Courier]] and [[Degenbrecher]] the Black Knight to keep an eye on the envoys’ movements. In two days, the country will be hosting a huge inauguration ceremony to commemorate the Statue of Kjeragandr’s completion. At this crucial juncture, Kjerag cannot afford to make any mistakes.<ref name=ST1/>

===The Wild Ride===
[[File:RS-The Ursine and the Victorian.png|thumb|left|A humble Ursine girl and a joyful Feline noble, coincidentally travelling to the same destination.]]
Meanwhile, Rosalind Larina, a.k.a. Operator [[Leto]] of [[Rhodes Island]], is taking a trip to Kjerag as well. Along the way, Leto becomes acquainted with Harold, who shares the same train ride to Lake Silberneherze, and the two have a hearty chat in his cabin. Leto explains that she is half-Kjeragi by blood and that she is on a journey to search for the romantic site where her mother met her long-lost father. She intends to return her mother's memento to the site, and, if possible, confront the father who abandoned her.<ref name=ST1/>

On board the same train is [[Bellingham]], a [[Trilby Asher]] dispatched by the Duke of Caster to do reconnaissance work. Just then, Degenbrecher suddenly appears on the train with bloodstained hands, searching for Harold. Believing that the viscount must be in serious trouble, Bellingham attempts to stop her as Harold flees, but to no avail.<ref name=1Bef>[[RS-1/Story#Before operation|RS-1 Before]]</ref><ref name=2Aft>[[RS-2/Story#After operation|RS-2 After]]</ref>Upon catching up to him, Degenbrecher reveals that she actually needs Harold's veterinary expertise to help with the premature childbirth of a farmer's burdenbeast; her bloodied hands are the results of performing first aid. The misunderstanding is cleared, and the party splits ways.<ref name=3Bef>[[RS-3/Story#Before operation|RS-3 Before]]</ref>

Degenbrecher informs of Rosalind’s return to Enciodes, [[Gnosis]], and [[Ratatos Browntail]]. The presence of a Trilby Asher within the country’s borders signifies that Victoria has sensed something amiss. They come up with a daring plan to send the Trilby Asher on a wild goose chase: they instruct [[Sciurus Browntail|Sciurus]] to offer Leto a personal invitation to the ceremony on behalf of the Browntails. This leads Bellingham to believe that Leto is more important than she appears to be and so the spy decides to follow her.<ref name=3Bef/>

===Family Reunions===
[[File:RS-Pardon the Interruption.png|thumb|left|Enya: "You're awful!"<br/>Enciodes: "You too."]]
Leto arrives at the construction site of the Kjeragandr statue to pray when she meets an old monk resembling her. The old monk, who turns out to be the former chief of the Paleroche Clan, recognises her as his son [[Arctosz Paleroche|Arctosz]]'s long lost child.<ref name=3Aft>[[RS-3/Story#After operation|RS-3 After]]</ref> He kindly invites her to stay in the mansion of the Paleroches, hoping to reunite the girl with her father, only to find Arctosz completely drunk. Seeing the bottles of fine wine he’s drinking, Leto decides to join him. Together, Arctosz and Leto spend a gleeful, alcohol-filled evening. Although this was not how he envisioned it, the old Paleroche is grateful to see such a warm-hearted moment.<ref name=4Bef>[[RS-4/Story#Before operation|RS-4 Before]]</ref><ref name=4Aft/>

Later that night, Harold decides to hold a grand banquet to show his appreciation towards the Kjeragis' kind hospitality.<ref name=4Aft/> He invites both Enciodes and [[Pramanix|Enya]] the Karlan Saintess to the banquet, yet he did not anticipate such tension between the siblings. Enya is upset with her brother's harsh methods in running the country's economy, though Enciodes rebukes that he's only doing so for the greater good of Kjerag. Despite their dispute, the banquet is a success, with Karlan Trade’s employees bonding together with the Victorian soldiers over drinks.<ref name=ST2>[[RS-ST-2]]</ref>

===Domestic Disputes===
The next day, Leto leaves the mansion early and heads towards Mount Fugen, known to be a romantic spot that couples frequent. During the climb, she runs into Bellingham, who has secretly been stalking her ever since she stepped off the train.<ref name=5Bef>[[RS-5/Story#Before operation|RS-5 Before]]</ref> He pretends to act friendly with her, but she finds his behaviour suspicious and interrogates him. Their tense exchange is soon interrupted by both Arctosz and Degenbrecher. In an attempt to save Leto, Arctosz accidentally causes the ground to collapse, and the two fall into a forested valley.<ref name=5Aft>[[RS-5/Story#After operation|RS-5 After]]</ref>

[[File:RS-Ya tvoy otets.png|thumb|Arctosz: "I... am your father."<br/>Leto: "So what?! Screw you!"]]
Upon waking up from the fall, Leto discovers the romantic site she has been searching for–a pair of conjoined trees located in the valley. After hanging her mother's "memento" up on the trees, Arctosz reveals the truth to her: he is in fact Leto's long-lost father.<ref name=6Bef>[[RS-6/Story#Before operation|RS-6 Before]]</ref> He tells Leto of the tragic tale behind his marriage with her [[Ursus|Ursine]] mother–[[Tatyana Larina]]–which was complicated by Kjerag’s then-conservative nature, leading to Arctosz eventually having to banish her and Leto for their own safety. He asks Leto for her forgiveness, hoping that she would still consider him a father.<ref name=6Aft>[[RS-6/Story#After operation|RS-6 After]]</ref>

Leto does not buy Arctosz's sympathy and instead punches him in the face, mocking his cowardice. Even after the two manage to leave the valley, Leto still bears a grudge against Arctosz.<ref name=6Aft/>

===Faith...===
[[File:RS-Enjoy the View.png|thumb|left|Down there, Kjera is satisfied with the statue's "renovation work".<br/>Up there, it seems the Trilby Asher is enjoying some nice skydiving.]]
Meanwhile, Bellingham is forced to engage in a fierce duel with Degenbrecher. The Trilby Asher is unable to withstand the physical might of the Black Knight, and flees in panic. During his escape, he hijacks a burdenbeast ride, skates across the ice, and even clambers up the Kjeragandr statue.<ref name=6Bef/> The cat-and-mouse game ends with Degenbrecher sending the Trilby Asher crashing down into Lake Silberneherze. Fortunately, Harold and Leto arrive to pull the unfortunate man out of the icy water.<ref name=7Bef>[[RS-7/Story#Before operation|RS-7 Before]]</ref> As the Trilby Asher tries to approach the statue again, he is immediately teleported by [[Kjera]], and the spy is completely bewildered by the "existence" of the "goddess".<ref name=7Aft>[[RS-7/Story#After operation|RS-7 After]]</ref>

Despite the comical failure, the Trilby Asher’s persistence finally pays off, and he manages to gain valuable intel; Karlan Trade has been storing their excess imported metal from Victoria at an underground station beneath Lake Silberneherze. The Kjeragandr statue is most likely a front for a secret weapon factory. The Trilby Asher relays this information to Harold, saying that as per the Duke of Caster’s orders, punishment must be doled out to those who would dare to deceive Victoria. They can no longer turn a blind eye to the country’s schemes. Harold’s worst nightmare has just come true.<ref name=7Aft/>

===...or Loyalty?===
[[File:RS-One Woman Army.png|thumb|Standing menacingly, the Black Knight is ready to face the thousand Victorian soldiers who dare trample this virgin land.]]
Harold returns to the barracks and organizes a troop of two thousand armed soldiers to destroy the Kjeragandr statue during the inauguration ceremony the following day. The soldiers lose their playful and outgoing demeanor, marching out into the streets and threatening civilians to stay out of the way. They even kidnap [[Matterhorn]] and Courier to prevent them from bringing intel to Enciodes. Once they completely expose Kjerag’s secret, they will coerce the country into trading exclusively with Victoria, effectively subjugating their economy.<ref name=8Bef>[[RS-8/Story#Before operation|RS-8 Before]]</ref>

Nevertheless, Enciodes already expected the troops’ arrival, and is patiently waiting for a "special guest" to arrive at the ceremony. In order to stall for time, Degenbrecher steps forward to stop the Victorian troops on the frozen lake. A self-proclaimed fan of the Black Knight, Harold is honored to have a fight with her, but at his side are two thousand veteran soldiers. In spite of the odds, the superior manpower and weapons prove to be no match against the Black Knight's raw strength. Even as she suffers from severe wounds, she makes her last stand to halt the soldiers from trampling her beloved country.<ref name=8Bef/>

[[File:RS-Wybrales zly pokoj, kolego.png|thumb|left|Mob: "''Przepraszam,'' you need something?"<br/>Trilby Asher: "Sorry, wrong room again!"]]
Sciurus is skeptical over Enciodes' "plan B," and decides to order the railway station to speed up the train with her own guests as fast as possible. However, little does she know that Enciodes’ guests are also on that same train. Bellingham infiltrates the train, preparing to assassinate their VIPs. To his bewilderment, the "guests" turned out to be diplomatic envoys from [[Kazimierz]]. Inside the cabin is [[Mob]]–who is now a spokesman of the [[Kazimierz General Chamber of Commerce]]–accompanied by Sciurus's own guests, who are campaign knights representing the [[Adeptus Sprawiedliwi Kazimierz]]. Not only that, the [[Doctor]] of [[Rhodes Island]] and [[Muelsyse]] of [[Rhine Lab]] are also present.<ref name=8Aft>[[RS-8/Story#After operation|RS-8 After]]</ref>

The Trilby Asher realizes he cannot take any action, lest he wishes to incur the wrath of two of Kazimierz’s ruling bodies. The important guests then arrive at Lake Silberneherze safe and sound, and Enciodes has Mob publicly announce Karlan Trade’s partnership with the K.G.C.C.. Upon hearing the announcement, Harold immediately orders the troops to cease fire, fearing his military operation would offend the Kazmierzian government, which in turn would further complicate Victoria's geopolitics, especially considering that the country was still recovering from the [[Victorian Crisis]].<ref name=8Aft/>

===Preparing For the Storm===
In the aftermath, both Harold and Bellingham humbly admit defeat upon realizing that the country's potential must not be underestimated. Despite their offense, Enya forgives them and even invites the envoys to take part in the post-ceremony banquet. Kjerag covers up the incident by portraying the sudden military operation as a brief training exercise between the two nations. Some Victorian soldiers even choose to retire and settle in Kjerag.<ref name=ST3>[[RS-ST-3]]</ref>

[[File:RS-Saving For the Rainy Day.png|thumb|Kjerag is biding time for her greater good, but after the first shot rings, there's not much time left...]]
Karlan Trade also makes a few agreements with Rhine Lab. The science company is hoping to construct a space center on Mount Karlan, the highest point on Terra, so they can launch spacecrafts to penetrate the [[Starpod]]. Enya has concerns about the project since Mount Karlan is a holy ground, but Kjera is delighted at the prospect of having "awesome cannons" installed on the mountain. Enciodes' partnership with the K.G.C.C. is an act to counter-balance both Victoria and Columbia's geopolitical influences. Leto decides to let go of her bitter past with Arctosz, though she also demands from him excessive "financial compensation".<ref name=ST3/>

The dust has finally settled, and Kjerag's national secret has fortunately not been leaked. Gnosis and Ratatos quietly enter a hidden passage beneath the Kjeragandr statue, revealing the true reason behind its construction; an underground weapons factory, wherein the Kjeragis are working on their greatest national project&ndash; the construction of Kjerag's first homemade high-speed landship dubbed "''Walnut''." Harold's failed operation is a mere wake-up call, and Kjerag must prepare for the worst to come.<ref name=ST3/>
