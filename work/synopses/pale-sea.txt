==Synopsis==
===Pirates of Mar Pálido===
To hone his swordsmanship, [[Operator]] [[Thorns]], real name Isidro, returns to his homeland of [[Iberia]] in search of a legendary sword technique known as ''El Dominio del Mar''—a style said to conquer both wind and waves. His journey leads him to ''Mar Pálido'', the Pale Sea—a vast salt flat strewn with bleached bones. Almost immediately upon arrival, a massive saltfin bursts from the sand, catching him off guard and knocking him unconscious. Later, a young girl named [[Rose Salt|Pascuala]] stumbles upon his half-buried body. Mistaking him for a corpse, she tries to loot his belongings, only to be caught by [[Anastasio]], a notorious and ruthless cleric of the [[Inquisition]].

As Isidro regains consciousness and moves to protect the girl, Anastasio declares that Pascuala has stolen a valuable relic from the ''Bastión de Cánticos'': a forbidden compass that points to its bearer's deepest desires. Isidro immediately recognizes it as an alchemical artifact containing Corazonix, a rare reagent once coveted by alchemists during Iberia's [[Iberia#Golden Age of Iberia|Golden Age]]. At the mention of alchemy, Anastasio turns his blade on Isidro—alchemy is forbidden knowledge, and the cleric has spent years hunting down all who dare practice it.

Anastasio and Thorns clash, but the Inquisitor swiftly overpowers him and shatters his sword. Thinking quickly, Isidro takes advantage of the trembling ground and dives away just as the same saltfin erupts again, knocking Anastasio aside. The cleric is impaled on a nearby saltfin skeleton, giving Isidro and Pascuala a narrow chance to escape. Seizing the moment, they leap onto the saltfin's back and ride it toward Aarón, a once-thriving town now left desolate in the wake of the [[Profound Silence]].<ref name=1Bef>[[EP-1/Story#Before operation|EP-1 Before]]</ref>

[[File:57 i01.png|thumb|Sometimes, getting into trouble is simply unavoidable...]]
Upon arriving in Aarón, Isidro and Pascuala part ways. After many failed attempts at speaking with the unwelcoming locals, Isidro is directed by one to the grave of a man who once learned swordsmanship from the pirates. Under cover of night, he chooses to exhume the grave in search of the man's diary but is caught by a member of the Inquisition. He is nearly hanged at the gallows alongside Pascuala—who has also been captured for theft—when a band of pirates, Pascuala's crewmates, arrive just in time to rescue them. They take him back to their hidden camp in the salt flats. However, it quickly becomes clear that the rescue was no act of kindness. In exchange for saving him, their captain, [[Juana]], demands one thing: that he repair the broken compass.

Meanwhile, Anastasio survives his impalement and makes his way to Aarón. There, he seeks out [[Silver]], a ''calificador'' of the Inquisition who has watched over the town for decades. He enlists Silver and his guards to help track down the compass.<ref name=1Aft>[[EP-1/Story#After operation|EP-1 After]]</ref>

===The Curse of the Compass===
Two weeks pass with no word from Thorns. Concerned for his safety, Operators [[Elysium]] and [[Weedy]] travel to Aarón, his last known location. There, they consult Silver, who tells them of Isidro's alleged crimes and his subsequent abduction into the Salt Flats. Elysium fears that Isidro is being held hostage—or worse, already dead. Certain there must be more to the story, Elysium decides to venture into the Salt Flats alone to search for his best friend, while Weedy stays behind in Aarón.<ref name=2Aft>[[EP-2/Story#After operation|EP-2 After]]</ref>

[[File:57 i02.png|left|thumb|Alchemy was his lifeline once, but that was a long time ago.]]
Meanwhile, Isidro remains trapped in the pirate camp despite his numerous attempts to escape. Although many of the crew have their doubts, Captain Juana is convinced that Isidro is an alchemist. She believes if he repairs the compass, it will guide them back to the sea and give them a better life.<ref name=2Bef>[[EP-2/Story#Before operation|EP-2 Before]]</ref> Isidro vehemently denies having any knowledge of alchemy, but to no avail.<ref>[[EP-ST-1]]</ref> In truth, while he does possess an exceptional understanding of alchemy, he refuses to use it, as he credits all he knows of the craft to his old mentor and father figure, [[Aulus]], a fanatical bishop of the [[Church of the Deep]] and a man he now despises.

As their resources dwindle, the pirates take a risk and bring Isidro along to hunt a massive saltfin, larger than any they have faced before. The crew manages to wound the beast severely, but it escapes into the Skeletal Reef—a treacherous expanse marked by heat vents that can shatter a ship's hull and boil a man alive.<ref name=3Bef>[[EP-3/Story#Before operation|EP-3 Before]]</ref> Juana, refuses to let their prize slip away, anchors herself to the saltfin and vanishes into the reef. Using his equipment, Isidro then guides their ship through the Skeletal Reef and locates Juana, who has slain the saltfin. On their way back to camp, they are ambushed by the Inquisition. Several pirates are killed—Juana, still wounded from the hunt, confronts Anastasio and Silver, but Thorns intervenes and saves her. The crew escapes, but they lose the saltfin and return to camp empty-handed.<ref name=3Aft>[[EP-3/Story#After operation|EP-3 After]]</ref>

Having grown closer to the crew and seeing their desperation firsthand, Isidro changes his mind. He agrees to help Juana repair the compass and works alongside the pirates. As compensation, Juana provides Isidro with his own alchemy lab and a brand new outfit originally intended for the previous alchemist.<ref name=4Bef>[[EP-4/Story#Before operation|EP-4 Before]]</ref>

===On Salty Tides===
A group of pirates captured during the Inquisition's ambush manage to break out of their cells in Aarón. It is later revealed that Silver deliberately let them escape, a plan which Anastasio disagreed with, preferring a more violent method. Meanwhile, Elysium is found by sailors and auctioned off to Pascuala as a bodyguard. The escapees reach the pirate camp safely and share startling news: the Inquisition is willing to pardon the pirates if they hand over both Pascuala and the stolen compass. Many among the crew already resented Juana's plan to return to the sea; the promise of normal life pushed first mate [[Javier]] to plot a mutiny with other discontented crewmates. Having heard the news, Pascuala has Elysium apply to be the fleet's alchemist assistant, leading to a reunion between the two friends.<ref name=4Bef>[[EP-4/Story#Before operation|EP-4 Before]]</ref> While Juana and Isidro venture into the Salt Flats to gather special materials for the compass's repair, Javier seizes the chance to act and takes both Pascuala and Elysium hostage.<ref name=5Bef>[[EP-5/Story#Before operation|EP-5 Before]]</ref>

[[File:57 i04.png|thumb|"How could you call yourselves pirates, when you've never even seen the sea?"]]
Juana leads Isidro to a cave, where she shows him a Seaborn bristling with blades—a creature that resembles a cross between an eel and an anglerfish. It has devoured countless swordsmen, mastering their techniques and hoarding their swords, including that of Cruz, whose unique sword style Isidro seeks—the original captain of Juana's fleet. After a brutal battle, Isidro slays the Seaborn, masters Cruz's technique, and takes its sinew for material to repair the compass.<ref name=5Bef/> When they return, they are met by a mob of furious pirates with weapons drawn. Just as a clash seems inevitable, Pascuala strikes a deal with the pirates, buying Isidro a week to finish fixing the compass. Reluctantly, the crew stands down.<ref name=5Aft>[[EP-5/Story#After operation|EP-5 After]]</ref>

The week passes under heavy tension, but Isidro ultimately fails and the compass is destroyed. Upon hearing this, Javier travels to Aarón to negotiate directly with Silver. In exchange for a stable life in the town for his crew and Juana's life, he betrays the pirates by revealing the location of their camp.<ref name=5Aft/> Juana lets Isidro, Pascuala, and Elysium go, thanking them for their effort. She knows an attack is coming but refuses to flee. Isidro gives her the compass, revealing that he had spent the week attempting to replicate it and that the destroyed compass was a failed replica. He apologizes and hopes that she will be able to find a better alchemist. Ignoring Silver's warnings, Anastasio rallies the town's warrior priests and launches an assault on the pirate camp, setting fire to their ships and tents. Many pirates surrender to the Inquisition, while those who resist are cut down.<ref name=6Bef>[[EP-6/Story#Before operation|EP-6 Before]]</ref>

===Dead Man's Revenge===
After parting ways with Pascuala and Elysium, Isidro sees the fire and rushes back to help. He reunites with Teach, Elysium, and Pascuala, and together they rescue Juana, who had been drugged by Javier. To escape, they work as one to seize control of one of their old ships, ''La Conquista''—an antique vessel long considered obsolete. However, Anastasio manages to sneak aboard and tries to kill Isidro while he's alone in the alchemy lab. To end the fight quickly, Isidro blows up the lab, blasting Anastasio out of the cabin and off the ship. With the threat gone, the crew sails into the Salt Flats, free, for now.<ref name=6Aft>[[EP-6/Story#After operation|EP-6 After]]</ref>

[[File:57 i06 2 a.png|left|thumb|None may escape the Alguacil's judgement.]]
Silver was certain he had seen the last of Anastasio after the explosion and decided against sending help. Quietly relieved, he begins making plans to evacuate the townspeople from Aarón using the ships and blueprints obtained from the pirates. But he is soon proven wrong when the mad Inquisitor appears before him in the dead of night. Anastasio declares Silver's desire for his death a sin—and kills the ''calificador''.<ref name=ST2>[[EP-ST-2]]</ref>

The next day, Anastasio demands that Javier and the other pirates help him capture their former captain and the compass. Although Javier remains suspicious, realizing that Anastasio is a lunatic who killed Silver, the others see no other alternatives and agree to the deal. They believe they will be rewarded for their efforts, unaware that Anastasio has no intention of sparing them once they've outlived their usefulness.<ref name=7Bef>[[EP-7/Story#Before operation|EP-7 Before]]</ref>

===Conquering the Conquest===
The crew spends an uneasy night aboard ''La Conquista'' as they sail deeper into the flats. Knowing the Inquisition will catch up sooner or later, Isidro devotes every moment to repairing the compass. Unfortunately, Anastasio and Javier remain in close pursuit, and their salt ships are far faster. Just as Isidro completes the repairs, Anastasio fires his handcannon from his vessel, striking the makeshift alchemy lab, and shattering the compass and his probes. The handcannon's Arts energy interferes with the process and the Corazonix begins to destabilize. With no other choice, Isidro uses his right arm to reach for the Corazonix, and it becomes one with his arm.<ref name=7Bef/>

[[File:57 i07 4.png|thumb|left|If there is no compass, then ''he'' will be the compass.]]
Under Javier's command, the pirates attempt to cut off ''La Conquista'', but Juana sees through their plan and steers the ship into the Skeletal Reef. Both vessels enter the narrow passage as scalding steam geysers shred their sails and jagged rocks batter their hulls. One of the pursuing salt ships is destroyed, but the other closes the distance. Pirates fire grappling hooks into ''La Conquista'' while the Inquisition's warrior priests storm aboard. Weedy and Elysium hold the helm, while Pascuala and Teach struggle to repel the warrior priests flooding the lower deck.<ref name=7Aft>[[EP-7/Story#After operation|EP-7 After]]</ref>

Pascuala and Teach manage to fight off the pursuing warrior priests, but Teach faints from her injuries. Meanwhile, Isidro faces Anastasio alone. The Corazonix within his arm absorbs information from the environment, allowing him to predict the timing of the reef's steam bursts. He uses this to gain an advantage in their fierce duel, but the Inquisitor remains a formidable foe. Isidro then makes a desperate gamble and leaps off the ship into a steam vent.<ref name=8Bef>[[EP-8/Story#Before operation|EP-8 Before]]</ref> As Anastasio savors his supposed victory and briefly contemplates ending his own life, Isidro bursts from the steam, catches him off guard, and slices through his neck. Anastasio chokes on his own blood and tumbles into the jagged rocks below, bringing an end to the mad Inquisitor.<ref name=8Aft>[[EP-8/Story#After operation|EP-8 After]]</ref>

===At Reef's Edge===
[[File:57 i13.png|thumb|"Anchors aweigh, hoist the sails—We sail out to sea!"]]
At the same time, Juana boards the pursuing vessel to confront her old crew. One by one, the pirates challenge her to a duel, a tradition she once taught them, but none can match their former captain. Finally, Javier steps forward, determined to prove himself worthy of leading them. Although he is ultimately defeated, he earns the respect of his crewmates, who at last acknowledge him as a true captain and Juana declares him as the winner of their duel. With their leader fallen, the Inquisition soldiers choose to retreat while Isidro steers ''La Conquista'' out of the Skeletal Reef.<ref name=8Aft/>

Those still brave enough to venture out to sea board ''La Conquista'', following Juana and Isidro as they prepare to sail onward. Weedy stays behind with them to help maintain the ship, while Elysium returns to Rhodes Island and bids everyone farewell. As the two ships sail in opposite directions, Juana dances on top of the mast of ''La Conquista''.<ref name=8Aft/> Javier and the remaining pirates head back to Aarón to sell their salt ships before going their separate ways in search of better lives. Pascuala goes even further, setting out for [[Columbia]] in the hope of striking it rich.<ref name=ST3>[[EP-ST-3]]</ref>

As for Anastasio, his will to live proves so strong that his corpse drags itself out of the Skeletal Reef despite his shattered bones. There, he encounters a Seaborn with an equally strong desire to live—likely the same one Isidro felled in the cave—and the two devour each other. Together, they live, and rise again as one.<ref name=ST3/>
