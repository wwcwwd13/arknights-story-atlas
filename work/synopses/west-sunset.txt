==Rule, Victoria==

===Reclaiming Londinium===
[[File:GO-Victoria's Sword.png|thumb|"Together, we shall revive Victoria!"]]
The year is late September 1098, and the [[Victoria]]n crisis is quickly approaching its end. With much of the Military Commission's forces dismantled, the [[Exemplars]] prepare to march into the heart of [[Londinium]] to defeat the Nachzehrer King, [[Nezzsalem]]. Despite the odds turning in their favour, the remaining [[Victoria]]n dukes choose to siege the city instead of commencing a frontal assault. The dukes appear more interested in salvaging [[The Shard]] than winning the war, and rumours circulate that they are prepared to abandon Londinium if needed.<ref name=":4Bef">[[GO-4/Story#Before operation|GO-4 Before]]</ref> They have even set up a blockade to prevent the Exmeplars from marching any further.

Just then, factories all across Londinium roar to life, a sign of rebellion from Londinium's citizens. Moved by the sight, the ducal alliance's soldiers decide to disobey their orders and help the Exemplars retake Londinium.<ref name=":2Bef">[[GO-2/Story#Before operation|GO-2 Before]]</ref> Around the same time, [[Millscar]] was tasked with destroying the remaining [[Sarkaz]] Witchcraft altars by a [[Entelechia|mysterious]] [[Vampire]]. Aided by [[Wiš'adel]], the [[Londinium Citizens' Self-Salvation Corps|Self-Salvation Corps]] and [[Charles Lynch]], the Exemplars charge towards the Palace of Westhaleg. In the final battle, Nezzsalem was pierced by the Sighs of Kings, finally marking the death of the Nachzehrer King. No one knows who dealt the final blow to him, but what is certain is that Victoria has defeated their ultimate enemy with their own hands.<ref name=":8Bef">[[GO-8/Story#Before operation|GO-8 Before]]</ref>

[[File:GO-Farewell to the Steam Knight B.png|thumb|left|The despicable meeting her end at the hands of the hero, that is how it should have happened. Alas, it is not the end of their story yet.]]
After the battle, Vina goes into the Palace of Westhaleg and hears the voice of her father, [[Alistair II]] and confronts him in the throne room. After laying his echo to rest, she places the Victorian crown on the throne, but before leaving the palace, [[Clovisia|a certain]] [[Unicorn]] comes out and faces her. She reveals how she's an [[Ageless]] who's been watching over Victoria for hundreds of years, and how she was responsible for the downfall of the [[Draco]] Dynasty, along with playing a major part in Alistair II's execution. She then offers Vina gifts befit of a hero, but Vina simply laughs, denouncing her so-called "gifts", saying she has no right to make her choose something she's already been doing this whole time. The Unicorn calls her a fool like her father then tells her to inform the Self-Salvation Corps of her "death."<ref name=":9Aft">[[GO-9/Story#After operation|GO-9 After]]</ref> Vina then leaves the palace to face her people. Upon hearing Vina announcing their victory, Charles Lynch finally allows himself to die. Millscar then takes his core so he can witness Victoria's future.<ref name=":8Aft">[[GO-8/Story#After operation|GO-8 After]]</ref>

A week passes since Nezzsalem was defeated. The remaining active dukes, [[Duke of Caster|Caster]], [[Duke of Gododdin|Gododdin]] and the Duke of Normandy (represented by the [[Earl of March]]) approach Vina with a proposal. They suggest that she become the Speaker of Parliament, proclaiming that she is the perfect figurehead for a new Victorian era. However, Vina knows the dukes have their own agenda, hoping to manipulate her from behind the scenes. She initially refuses, but after receiving some encouragement from the [[Doctor]], Vina decides to accept the position.<ref name=":1Aft">[[GO-1/Story#After operation|GO-1 After]]</ref>

===Everything for the People===
After negotiations facilitated by [[Rhodes Island]], the Sarkaz troops officially withdraw from Londinium. Since then, Vina has improved the city's state significantly, but there are a few thorny issues left, the most notable being the leftover Sarkaz in Londinium and the severe lack of [[Oripathy]] suppressants. Between all this, Vina's attention is drawn towards Allerdale, who has taken up the identity of the notorious mercenary boss [[Millscar]]. Millscar has been secretly smuggling Sarkaz out of the city and selling underground Oripathy suppressants at unreasonable prices. Those who are unable to pay will be beaten or outright killed. Despite this, many citizens support Millscar and her activities, posing a conundrum for Vina.<ref name=":1Bef">[[GO-1/Story#Before operation|GO-1 Before]]</ref>

[[File:GO-Parliament's Decree.png|thumb|left|"Warrant for Millscar's arrest... effective... immediately."]]
Things soon reach a boiling point and Vina is left with no choice but to put Millscar on trial for her crimes. She waits for her to show up for her defense, but all they get is another report of her mercenaries assaulting an innocent woman. Vina unwillingly has to put out an arrest warrant for her and her mercenaries' neutralization and arrest.<ref name=":3Aft">[[GO-3/Story#After operation|GO-3 After]]</ref> Still, despite days of searching, Millscar continues to elude the Defense Forces.

When tensions about lack of resources grow, Vina decides to represent parliament and announces multiple future programs that will help the people of Londinium. She announces that they will provide support and resettle the Exemplars so that they will be able to enjoy comfortable lives. The broadcast is suddenly cut short because of the dukes' interference, who are apparently displeased with her results. During Vina's tense discussion with them, a report comes in that the Victorian crown has been stolen from the throne.<ref name=":7Bef">[[GO-7/Story#Before operation|GO-7 Before]]</ref> Instead of running away with it, the culprit brings it straight to Vina's desk in her office. The culprit is revealed to be a veteran named [[Diane Weber]], who [[Morgan]] recognises as first soldier to defy the dukes' orders to block the Exemplars. She idolises Vina and tries persuading her into becoming the king, convinced that it is the best choice for Victoria's future. However, Vina chooses to leave the throne vacant, resting the crown upon it once again.<ref name=":7Aft">[[GO-7/Story#After operation|GO-7 After]]</ref>

==Never Will Be Slaves==

===Crime and Punishment===
[[File:GO-Of the People A.png|thumb|Vina promises that things will get better from here on out, with or without a king.]]
The day after Diane's arrest, some people start protesting, saying that a war hero like Diane deserves at least a lighter punishment. Vina isn't proud of what she's doing, but if she uses her authority to nullify Diane's punishment, then it will set a dangerous precedent for any future crimes. As others try to reason with her, Vina visits Diane in a detention center, where Vina put her deliberately on account of her old war friends being guards there. Vina has a drink with her and tells her the secret of what happened in the Palace of Westhaleg, which she hadn't told a soul. She assures Diane that Victoria does not need a monarch in order to succeed.<ref name=":9Aft"/>

On the day of Diane's execution, Vina goes to the parliament hall to meet with the other dukes. By the end of their discussion, Vina manages to argue back some rights and things that, in Vina's words, belong to the people of Londinium, not the dukes. After she walks out of the parliament building, she is confronted by Diane's supporters, but the protest is resolved quickly and everyone reaches at least some amount of understanding. In fact, many people arrive to voice their support for Vina, including Lieutenant [[Shearer]] and [[Catherine]].<ref name=":9Aft"/>

Vina, unwilling to sacrifice Diane, decides to stage her execution. Millscar has her mercenaries steal the "body," knowing that if anyone discovered that Diane was alive, people could use it to blackmail Vina. She hands Diane over to a [[Columbia|Columbian]] man named [[Bobbing|Eliseo]], whom she had became good friends with during his stay in Londinium. He takes her out of the city and starts trekking back to Columbia with her, but Diane's Oripathy is at such a late stage that Eliseo notes that she will most likely pass before they make it. Diane is acceptant of this outcome, requesting him to bring what's left of her to Columbia and then back to Londinium in the future.<ref name=":ST2>[[GO-ST-2]]</ref>

===Glasgow Forever===
[[File:GO-Glasgow Forever.png|thumb|"Vina!"]]
Ever since Vina became the Speaker of Parliament, she has grown more distant from the rest of the [[Glasgow]] Gang. [[Delphine]] has been assisting Vina with her duties, but there was little she could do when it came to interpersonal relationships. Vina's heavy responsibilities naturally clashed with the carefree life the Glasgow Gang are so used to.

The members of the [[Glasgow|Glasgow Gang]] decide to return to Norport Borough. The group reminisce over [[Baird]], lamenting how much has changed since they first left. After a night of heavy drinking and bemoaning that Vina can't be with them, they pass out, only to wake up to Vina being next to them, who snuck away from parliament to hang out with them. No matter what happens in the future, Vina promises she'll always be a member of Glasgow. Morgan decides to document all of their experiences together in a book titled ''The Memoir of Alexandrina''.<ref name=":ST2"/>
