==Synopsis==
===Winter is Coming===
[[File:Kjerag Homecoming.png|thumb|left|"It should be another nice holiday, right, Doctor?"]]
The Tri‑Clan Council convenes at the sacred temple atop Mount Karlan to deliberate on an incident uncovered a month earlier: it was discovered that [[Karlan Trade]]’s Chief Technical Officer, [[Gnosis|Gnosis Edelweiss]], had been conducting illicit mining operations on the holy mountain and had even gone so far as to attack an inspection team. [[SilverAsh|Enciodes Silverash]] has since fired the man from his post, but clan leaders [[Arctosz Paleroche]] and [[Ratatos Browntail]] remain unsatisfied. They accuse the Silverashes of overstepping their bounds, and demand that the clan surrender their territory and be banished from the Council. Much to everyone’s astonishment, Silverash agrees, but under one condition: his territory, along with that of the other clans, is to be handed over to the Saintess of the Vine-Bead Court. He proposes that the Karlan Saintess—his estranged sister [[Pramanix|Enya Silverash]]—should become the impartial, leading authority of the three clans. Out of respect for the Saintess, the Paleroches and Browntails unanimously agree.<ref>[[BI-1/Story#After operation|BI-1 After]]</ref>

In order to fill the position left empty by Gnosis' departure, Enciodes sends an [[Letter|invitation]] to the [[Doctor]] of [[Rhodes Island]]. After a long train ride, the Doctor arrives in Kjerag alongside Enciodes’ sister, [[Cliffheart|Ensia Silverash]], and is joined by [[Courier|Weiss]] and [[Matterhorn]] of Karlan Trade. Operator [[Aurora]] and her captain, [[Elite Op|Elite Operator]] [[Sharp]], serve as the Doctor’s bodyguards.<ref>[[BI-1/Story#Before operation|BI-1 Before]]</ref> The Doctor was initially invited to assist in the construction of a medical facility, but was soon roped into helping Enciodes survey his territory’s factories and oversee the handover procedures. However, Arctosz harbored concerns about the Doctor’s credibility, so he assigned Generals [[Gulo]] and [[Valais]] to closely monitor them during the process.<ref>[[BI-ST-1]]</ref> Although a minor scuffle occurred, caused by the locals, the Doctor was able to peacefully resolve the matter and earn the Paleroches’ trust. This incident served to alert the Rhodes Islanders that someone is trying to disrupt the unity of the Tri-Clan Council, leading them to quietly investigate.<ref>[[BI-3/Story#Before operation|BI-3 Before]]</ref>

[[File:BI-Railbridge Demolition.png|thumb|This "accident" was only the beginning of something far larger and more dangerous...]]
After he was fired by Enciodes Silverash, a frustrated Gnosis approached Ratatos Browntail. In exchange for key information on Enciodes, he wished to form an alliance with the Browntails to put a stop to Enciodes’ schemes.<ref>[[BI-2/Story#After operation|BI-2 After]]</ref> As a demonstration of his goodwill, he revealed to Ratatos the location of a secret railway bridge that the Silverashes have been using to secretly smuggle soldiers and weapons into the heartland. Gnosis then destroys the bridge with Originium explosives, derailing the train and providing an opportunity for the Browntail clan to capture the elite soldiers hiding on board. Despite this victory, the Browntails are now complicit in this crime, and have no choice but to cooperate with Gnosis' plans.<ref>[[BI-ST-2]]</ref>

===Visionary or Madman?===
On the day before the Grand Ceremony, the three clans gather to conduct the Holy Hunt, a religious festival celebrating the day of Kjeragandr’s first appearance. In accordance to Kjerag tradition, Enciodes walks towards Mount Karlan with his head bowed in prayer. Many are surprised to witness the cunning businessman seemingly convert to a pious believer overnight, and his supporters choose to join him on the ascent up the mountain. Once Enciodes arrives for the Hunt, the three clans raise their weapons and venture into the forests to hunt for wild beasts.<ref>[[BI-5/Story#Before operation|BI-5 Before]]</ref> During the hunt, an assassin named [[Monch]] attempted to kill Enciodes, but is quickly foiled. News of the assassination attempt quickly spreads, and rumors of the Tri-Clan Council’s internal disputes garner public sympathy for Enciodes.<ref>[[BI-5/Story#After operation|BI-5 After]]</ref>

[[File:Judge, Jury, and Executioner.png|thumb|Enciodes casts his judgment on Gnosis with Degenbrecher as the executioner.]]
Despite the incident, the three clans proceed with the Grand Ceremony the following day. The families pay annual tribute to the [[Great Elder]]—the leader of the Vine-Bead Court—and the Saintess. Just then, they receive news that yesterday's assassin had been captured, and they present her before the gathered crowd. [[Sciurus Browntail]], Ratatos' younger sister, immediately recognizes Monch as her own subordinate and rushes to her defense, proclaiming that she is innocent. However, her good intentions end up implicating the Browntails in the assassination plot, shocking the general public. The Great Elder orders the guards to arrest Sciurus for conspiring to murder Enciodes Silverash during the Holy Hunt.<ref name=ST3>[[BI-ST-3]]</ref>

To further worsen the situation, the Great Elder is suddenly struck with a violent coughing fit, and falls into a coma. Enya soon discovers that he had been poisoned by the wine presented as tribute by the Paleroches. Realizing what had happened, Enciodes publicly lambasts the Browntail and Paleroche clan for their shameless plot to overturn the Tri-Clan Council. Gnosis appears in the midst of the chaos and proclaims that he would stop Enciodes in the Browntails' name. Although he is immediately incapacitated by Enciodes’ bodyguard, [[Degenbrecher]], the damage is done. The two clans, knowing that public opinion has turned against them in light of the false accusations, proceed to rally their forces against Enciodes. Enciodes strikes back by summoning his "[[Tschäggättä]]s", his own special unit who received training in [[Victoria]], with the intent of capturing the two clan heads. The Doctor, however, had anticipated this move and orders Sharp to intervene. Although Ratatos and Arctosz manage to escape safely due to the Doctor's interference, Sciurus’ husband, [[Yucatan]], is captured while trying to rescue her.<ref name=ST3/>

The crowd celebrates the Silverash's victory, lauding Enciodes as the hero who dared to stand against an evil conspiracy. However, everything that had transpired had been instigated and staged by Enciodes himself; he and Gnosis had orchestrated their feud deliberately to tarnish the names of the two clans. Gnosis infiltrated the Browntail Clan so that his audacious actions at the ceremony would reflect poorly on them. Furthermore, he arranged and supplied the poisoning of the Great Elder, inciting general Valais to commit the deed by exploiting the grudge she held towards the Vine-Bead Court.<ref>[[BI-6/Story#Before operation|BI-6 Before]]</ref> By staining the names of the other two clans while boosting his own image, Enciodes had won the game of public opinion—when in reality, he was the mastermind who orchestrated the entire debacle. His ultimate goal is simple: to appoint the Silverashes as the sole ruler of Kjerag and accelerate the country’s industrialization, with himself at the helm.

===Cold War===
[[File:A Heated Discussion.png|thumb|left|"This is fine," Enciodes decrees, during his ''heated'' discussion with Ratatos.]]
In the aftermath of the ceremony’s unexpected conclusion, the Browntail clan fractured as several villages under their rule defected to the Silverash clan. Unwilling to accept her defeat, Ratatos trapped herself and Enciodes inside a building and set it on fire. Amidst the flames, Ratatos voiced her disappointment in Enciodes, believing he had no intention of working for the betterment of Kjerag. She had hoped that Karlan Trade would bring prosperity to Kjerag and unite the Tri-Clans, but only his company stood to reap the benefits. In turn, Enciodes responds by saying that, despite all the opposition he had faced, everything he had done was to prevent a potential invasion of Kjerag in the near future. The two were ultimately rescued by Degenbrecher and Sciurus, and the prideful Browntails were forced to admit their defeat.<ref>[[BI-6/Story#After operation|BI-6 After]]</ref>

[[File:A Cold War.png|thumb|Arctosz, with the Doctor on his side, takes on the Silverashes.]]
The Paleroches, however, sees Enciodes's actions as a mockery of their faith in Kjeragandr. Amidst the turmoil, they find an unlikely ally: Rhodes Island. Arctosz believes that only the Doctor could stop Enciodes from plunging the nation into further chaos. In turn, the Doctor agrees that the Silverashes are about to start an unnecessary civil war in the country and wants to save as many lives as possible. The Doctor invites the Browntails to the Paleroche estate and discusses their plan with the two clans. The only way to stop the war is to rescue the Saintess, who officially still wields the highest authority in Kjerag. Arctosz and the Doctor will command an army to Mount Karlan and save Enya while Gulo and the others divert their attention by taking over the neighbouring pass. Sharp will be in charge of making sure that the wild card Degenbrecher remains occupied. Sciurus decides to take responsibility and voluntarily joins the Browntails to conduct a rescue mission for Yucatan.<ref>[[BI-7/Story#Before operation|BI-7 Before]]</ref><ref>[[BI-8/Story#Before operation|BI-8 Before]]</ref>

In case their plan fails, the Doctor tells Aurora to secretly deliver a message to Ensia. Aurora informs Ensia that she is to be their insurance—she has to protect the Saintess from her brother, should he act against her. Upon receiving the message, Ensia leaves the Silverash manor, resolving to keep her sister safe. With the soldiers distracted by the fighting, she successfully sneaks past them and begins to climb Mount Karlan alone. The sky darkens as a fierce blizzard begins to roar, as if signifying the turbulent conflict ahead.<ref name=7Aft>[[BI-7/Story#After operation|BI-7 After]]</ref>

===A Realm Thawed===
[[File:The Saintess Descends.png|thumb|left|The Saintess’s descent is hailed as a divine blessing from Kjeragandr herself]]
Inside the temple of Mount Karlan, tensions are rising as the elders of the Vine-Bead Court debate on their next course of action. Just like her brother, Enya has been wishing to reform the nation using her status as Saintess. However, in the eyes of the dying Great Elder, faith is the only way to pacify the people, and reformation means breaking the peace. In the last moments of his life, the Great Elder proclaims that it is the Saintess's duty to uphold the centuries-old faith, imploring her to stop Enciodes from changing Kjerag irrevocably. However, Enya states that while she disagrees with her brother’s methods, she has a responsibility to improve the lives of the people, and Kjerag has stagnated for too long. The Great Elder is devastated by her answer and passes away soon after.<ref name=7Aft/>

Ensia manages to sneak into the mountain temple and tearfully reunites with her sister. With encouragement from both Ensia and the court's head maid, [[Kjera]], the Saintess gathers the courage to descend the mountain personally and confront her brother. Down at the square, the Paleroches prepare to engage in direct combat with the Silverashes. Kjera—secretly the avatar of Kjeragandr Herself—provides those gathered a small 'miracle'. The frigid blizzard abruptly ceases, and the dark clouds part to herald the Saintess’s arrival. Warriors from all three clans watch in a mixture of shock and awe before laying down their arms and bowing before her. Even Enciodes is left flabbergasted. It is like the retelling of a centuries‑old legend in which the Saintess once pacified the battlefield.<ref name=8Aft>[[BI-8/Story#After operation|BI-8 After]]</ref>

[[File:A Nice Game of Chess.png|thumb|"How about a nice game of chess, Doctor?"]]
And thus, with the Saintess's descent, the "Snowcap Incident" came to an end. Empowered by the newfound authority bestowed upon her, Enya began actively opening up the country by welcoming foreign believers. Arctosz resigned from his position as the head of the Paleroches, while the Browntail clan signed an agreement of subordination to the Silverashes. The Silverashes assumed leadership of the Tri‑Clan Council, effectively uniting the three clans and allowing the continued modernization of their country. Gnosis agreed to serve as Enciodes’s representative on Rhodes Island, and he soon learned that Monch had left his employ.<ref>[[BI-ST-4]]</ref>

Although the situation did not go entirely according to plan, Enciodes considers the outcome a victory all the same. As for the Doctor, Enciodes offers an apology for having used them in his scheme and promises to invite the Doctor on a genuine vacation next time.
