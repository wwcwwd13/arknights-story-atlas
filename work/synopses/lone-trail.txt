==One small step for man...==
{{Image list|Intertwining Helixes.png|The exploration of space will go ahead, whether we join in it or not, and it is one of the great adventures of all time, and no nation which expects to be the leader of other nations can expect to stay behind in the race for space.<br/>—John F. Kennedy}}

===A Thousand Suns===
November 1099, just one month after the [[Dorothy's Vision/Synopsis|Site 359 incident]]. [[Kristen Wright]], head of [[Rhine Lab]], has disappeared from the public eye of [[Columbia]]. [[Justin Fitzroy Jr.]] pulls strings to have [[Loken Williams]], an infamous scientist, released from prison on Kristen's orders. [[Ferdinand Clooney]] teams up with Colonel [[Blake]] of the [[Columbia]]n Department of Defense to track down Kristen. [[Muelsyse]] is ambushed by [[Ho'olheyak]] in Control's office as she searches for clues of Kristen's whereabouts. The [[Tin Man]] and [[Kal'tsit]] have a brief discussion before burning wreckage cuts through the sky, crash-landing into the Triton chemical plant located in District 13 of [[Trimounts]].<ref name="CW-ST-1">[[CW-ST-1]]</ref>

[[File:38_i07.png|thumb|Muelsyse and Ho'olheyak, Elf and K'uk'ulkan—similar, yet intrinsically different.]]

The [[Doctor]], present in Columbia, is awoken by one of Muelsyse's water clones. She shows them news coverage of the chemical plant explosion before dissipating as Kal'tsit arrives with [[Ifrit]] and [[Rosmontis]], with the Doctor questioning the Elite Operator's presence. Rosmontis made her own choice to come to Columba, however, and the group heads for the crash site where [[Saria]] is already investigating. Ho'olheyak has already recovered an important object from the wreckage on Kristen's orders, but notices [[Silence]] trying to sneak in. Ho'olheyak is amused, and provides Silence a small opening to sneak into the site. The Tin Man and Saria enter the district officially and escape their military escort to examine the crash themselves, discovering evidence of Kristen's involvement. Silence finds an instance of [[Dorothy]]'s [[Transmitter]], accomplishing a request from Dorothy to locate stolen Transmitter, which led her to Trimounts in the first place. However, the Transmitter flies away before she can catch it. Before leaving the site, Silence spots Saria and questions her involvement, but does not confront her.<ref name="CW-1 Before">[[CW-1/Story]]</ref>

[[File:38_i11.png|thumb|left|Ferdinand stands before the Department of Defense, who are anticipating Kristen's next move.]]
Elsewhere, Blake meets with Vice President [[Conrad Jackson]] on behalf of the military, and urges him to cancel his itinerary and leave Trimounts. Jackson refuses to leave in light of recent incidents and instead extends his stay with the intent to visit [[Rhine Lab]]. Afterwards, Blake and Ferdinand discuss the situation, confirming that the wreckage is the result of a prototype test flight for the Horizon Arc Project, specifically the {{Tip|H.A.M.H.R.R.|High-altitude Arrayed and Maneuverable Hyperbeam Receiver-Redirector}}. The Horizon Arc Project was a collaboration between the Columbian Department of Defense and Rhine Lab, with the goal of developing a superweapon to counter [[the Shard]] in light of the [[Victorian Crisis]]. Kristen, however, has taken the project and vanished—leaving the military and her investors in a state of panic. The military now seeks to track her down, fearing that the superweapon could fall into the hands of another country, or in the worst case scenario, turned against Columbia itself.<ref name="CW-1 After">[[CW-1/Story]]</ref>

To accomplish this goal, the military sought to recover the black box from the Triton Factory crash site in order to determine Kristen's launch site and hideout, but this mission failed due to Ho'olheyak's meddling. With this failure and V.P. Jackson's refusal to leave Trimounts, Blake decides that the {{Tip|DOD|Department of Defense}} cannot hesitate any longer. Back at the crash site, Silence tries to eavesdrop on Saria and the Tin Man, but is caught by a Columbian soldier. Before Silence could be questioned, she is rescued by [[Jara Wilson]], Rhine Lab's Director of Human Resources. Jara covers for Silence, and the two are allowed to leave without issue. The Director sends Silence ahead to her residence in district 13 and lingers to speak with the Tin Man, who witnessed the entire interaction. They discuss her past as Maylander's ''Morningstar'' and the death of Kristen's parents, the Wrights, who died when their test flight impacted the invisible ceiling of the [[Starpod]]. Ho'olheyak delivers the black box to [[Nasti Lunorey]], Rhine Lab's head of Engineering.<ref name="CW-1 After">[[CW-1/Story]]</ref>

===Explorers and Pioneers===
Nasti meets with Kristen about the test flight's crash, and Ferdinand and Blake have a heart-to-heart. Blake is certain the Kristen intends to use Arc-01 to destroy Max D.C., the Capital of Columbia, and the Triton crash was an attempt to provoke the DOD. He believes Kristen hates Columbia and seeks revenge for the death of her parents. Desperate to restore the DOD's tarnished reputation, Blake conspires to assassinate the Vice President and recover Arc-01, in order to prevent the scandal from going public.<ref name="CW-2 Before">[[CW-2/Story]]</ref>

[[File:38_i06.png|thumb|left|Although they are not entirely aligned, they walk towards the same goal.]]
Kal'tsit begins to suspect the involvement of ancient technology, as she predicts that Kristen's experiment will consume far more energy than Terra's current level of technology can sustain. The Rhodes Islanders' hideout comes under attack by remotely-controlled power armor developed by Rhine Lab. Although they repel the attack with little difficulty, Saria is angered and prepares to face Rhine Lab directly. Silence is given a new outfit alongside contacts to replace her glasses by Jara. The Director stresses the futility of Silence's actions, but she is resolute in continuing her investigation.<ref name="CW-2 Before">[[CW-2/Story]]</ref>

Meanwhile, under the watch of a concerned Ifrit, Rosmontis recalls scattered memories from her time as Loken's test subject. Ifrit shares her own experiences as Rhine Lab's test subject, and the two girls bond. Their conversation is interrupted by the arrival of the Tin Man, who has arrived to recruit Rhodes Island's help in foiling the military's plot to assassinate the Vice President, and the Tin Man leaves impressed by the Doctor. Silence escapes District 13 and follows an instance of Transmitter to District 9, eventually tracking it to [[Ahrens Parvis]]. She seeks answers on the use of Transmitter, but Parvis eventually dismisses her, citing that they are on the cusp of greatness, and she may watch. Silence feels anger once more, coming face-to-face with the reality of Rhine Lab's scheme and their lack of care for the common man. She leaves, aimlessly wandering the streets before nearly getting hit by a truck through Ho'olheyak's interference, and is saved by Muelsyse. The [[Elf]] points Silence towards the Doctor, giving Silence a goal.<ref name="CW-2 After">[[CW-2/Story]]</ref>

===Big Bang===
The next day, the Doctor—accompanied by Ifrit and Rosmontis—arrives at Rhine Lab with full access. The Maylander Foundation has mobilized personnel to protect the Vice President, and they successfully take down multiple undercover Military agents without causing a panic. Rosmontis destroys multiple suits of power armor stolen by the military with Saria's assistance, and the Vice President's tour continues without incident. Ifrit confronts two agents in an underground passageway, but is interrupted as planted explosives destroy the street above. Ifrit and the two DOD agents she saved are rescued from the rubble by Saria. The convoy caught in the blast is revealed to be a decoy, and Vice President Jackson is escorted to safety via an entirely different route by Jara.<ref>[[CW-3/Story]]</ref>

Ifrit reunites with Silence under Saria's watch, but Saria is shot in the head by a high-powered energy-based sniper rifle on Colonel Blake's orders. Jara shows up once more to save Silence, Ifrit, and the unconscious Saria. At Rhine Lab, Justin Jr. indirectly warns the Doctor that Loken is searching for Rosmontis before leaving. Muelsyse helps the Doctor and Rosmontis evade Defense Section employees, but they are found by Loken regardless. The scientist is glad to see Rosmontis again, and calls her by Narcissa. Kal'tsit continues to investigate Kristen's unknown energy source.<ref>[[CW-ST-2]]</ref>

Loken attempts to force Rosmontis to remember her time as a test subject, and the Doctor attacks him in a rage after seeing her distress. Loken escapes under the cover of multiple suits of power armor, and Muelsyse is shaken by the revelation that Kristen seems to not trust her. Jara tries to convince Silence to leave Trimounts once more with Ifrit and Saria, but is interrupted by a meeting with Ferdinand, who tells her that Saria, Rhine Lab, and Rhodes Island are suspected of assisting Kristen in stealing classified information relating to national security.<ref name="CW-4 Before">[[CW-4/Story]]</ref>

Soon after, the Tin Man is approached by Ho'olheyak in an empty alley as he awaits the Doctor. Ho'olheyak announces her betrayal, and kills the Tin Man with Nasti's help as a [[Banshee]]. The Doctor arrives soon after to discover the Tin Man's head, and is held hostage by Ho'olheyak. She intends to frame the Doctor and Rhodes Island at large for his death. Yet to Ho'olheyak's surprise and anger, the Doctor turns out to be one of Muelsyse's water clones, with the real Doctor relaying their words from far away.<ref name="CW-4 After">[[CW-4/Story]]</ref>

Silence, Saria, and Ifrit are pursued by Columbian Special Forces, and Silence sends the two of them to safety via her drone. The determined [[Liberi]] meets with Ferdinand directly, and injects her Transmitter into herself in order to strike a deal. Kristen and Parvis have assembled a new Transmitter hub, and now Silence would lead the DOD to Kristen.<ref name="CW-4 After">[[CW-4/Story]]</ref> Ifrit and Saria follow the military's trail, trusting Silence.<ref name="CW-5 Before">[[CW-5/Story]]</ref>

==...One giant leap for mankind==
===Enterprises===
The Doctor meets Muelsyse in her Vivarium, and the two share a dance as Muelsyse shares her story with the Doctor, who seeks to understand her. Nasti is approached by Justin Jr., who wishes to watch the imminent Arc-01 launch with her.<ref name="CW-5 Before">[[CW-5/Story]]</ref> Loken finishes the research Kristen freed him for, and thinks on his shock when Kristen first showed him the subject of his study, cited as knowledge from [[Predecessor|God]]. Ferdinand directly accesses the blueprints of the {{Tip|S.H.A.F.T.|Synergistic Hyperbeam Amplifying and Focusing Tube}}, and discovers that the project is secretly drawing energy from an unknown source, bypassing the Trimountian energy grid entirely. He contacts Blake and lies—declaring that Kristen cannot launch the H.A.M.H.R.R. due to a lack of propulsion, correctly implicates Nasti Lunorey as one of Kristen's supporters, and convinces the Colonel that launching Arc-01 is a cover and Kristen's real goals lies elsewhere. Afterwards, he seeks out an aircraft that can reach the H.A.M.H.R.R. during takeoff.<ref name="CW-5 After">[[CW-5/Story]]</ref>

Silence uses a neural-electricity stimulator to amplify the Transmitter's effects, allowing the military and the Maylander foundation to close in on Kristen's location quicker. Ho'olheyak is prepared, however, and uses her [[Originium Arts|Arts]] alongside unmanned power armor to delay the combined Columbian forces. As a result, the aircraft transporting Silence and Jara is forced to crash at the hand of her formidable wind-based Arts. Rosmontis, accompanied by the Doctor and Muelsyse, arrive at Loken's lab at his invitation.<ref name="CW-5 After">[[CW-5/Story]]</ref>

[[File:38_i02.png|thumb|Loken seeks judgement from his creation.]]
Returning to the lab that was her home causes Rosmontis to recall her time as a test subject, and finds herself alone with Loken Williams once more. He proclaims their meeting to be his trial, and wants Rosmontis, his greatest creation, greatest failure, to kill him. She grows more despondent and enraged as he forces her to relive her forgotten memories, until Ifrit arrives. Meanwhile, Saria nearly kills Ho'olheyak before she escapes.<ref name="CW-6 Before">[[CW-6/Story]]</ref>

The Doctor and Muelsyse find a vivarium within H.A.M.H.R.R. that, much to the Doctor's horror, contains a [[Sarcophagus]]. Muelsyse realizes that Kristen kept her promise, and finally chooses to take her side. She uses her Arts to gently remove the Doctor from the lab. Kristen and Ho'olheyak's deal has been concluded successfully, and Kristen provides her Loken's research alongside a set of coordinates. With this, she passes Muelsyse by as she leaves for good, and they pass each other by with little interaction.<ref name="CW-6 After">[[CW-6/Story]]</ref>

Silence and Jara break free of the wrecked aircraft, and Silence goes on alone, ready to see this incident through to the end. She chances upon Saria, and passes her by. With Ifrit's help, Rosmontis is able to calm down and restrain her power. She faces Loken once more, and proclaims that he is nothing to her, sentencing him to death. The Doctor, now outside of H.A.M.H.R.R., is joined by Kal'tsit and [[Mon3tr]] as the ring-shaped laboratory begins takeoff.<ref name="CW-6 After">[[CW-6/Story]]</ref>

===Voyagers and Challengers===
[[File:38_i01.png|left|thumb|The two department directors know that time is of the essence.]]
H.A.M.H.R.R. has now risen above Trimounts, leaving the city stunned with awe. Justin Jr. receives a call from a Justice of Columbia's Supreme Court, and convinces the Justice not to interfere with Kristen's plan or Rhine Lab. Colonel Blake and Vice President Jackson discuss the military's response to the launch, but are blindsided as the S.H.A.F.T., thought to be under complete military control, begins activating, receiving power from an unknowm source. Despite their efforts, the S.H.A.F.T. begins to activate.<ref name="CW-7 Before">[[CW-7/Story]]</ref>

Kal'tsit now knows the location Kristen's power source, and enlists the Doctor's help to investigate. Saria and Ferdinand forge an alliance to board H.A.M.H.R.R. as Silence infiltrates the S.H.A.F.T., following the Transmitter within her.<ref name="CW-7 After">[[CW-7/Story]]</ref>

Kal'tsit and the Doctor arrive at the source, only to be intercepted by Ho'olheyak. Due to the presence of [[Predecessor]] security systems, Kal'tsit is unable to summon Mon3tr, but the Tin Man's timely arrival leads to him easily incapacitating Ho'olheyak, allowing them to proceed. Meanwhile, Ferdinand and Saria board H.A.M.H.R.R. successfully, evading the Columbian drones attacking the structure. The Rhodes Island duo proceed into the facility, discovering hundreds of thousands of dormant Sarcophagi. The facility activates, and a voice speaking an unknown language demands answers to their presence, calling Kal'tsit by her true name—Ama-10.<ref name="CW-8 Before">[[CW-8/Story]]</ref>

[[File:Background-Hall of Stasis.png|thumb|A graveyard of the ancient past, hidden within the depths of Trimounts.]]
The voice reveals himself as the Preserver, a simulated consciousness tasked to oversee the survival of those within the Sarcophagi. However, he has now chosen to divert the remaining power within the facility to aid Kristen in piercing the Starpod, as the facility has long since been nothing more than a mass grave. Within the S.H.A.F.T., Silence is stopped by Jara as she nears the Transmitter hub, and the two fight. Jara is unable to stand by and allow Kristen's dreams to be impeded. Their battle is interrupted by the continuous charging of the S.H.A.F.T., and Silence uses the opportunity to get past Jara. As Nasti and Justin Jr. watch, Arc-01 successfully absorbs all the energy provided by the S.H.A.F.T..
<ref name="CW-8 After">[[CW-8/Story]]</ref>

Ferdinand parts ways with Saria, determined to document the scientific breakthroughs occurring aboard the H.A.M.H.R.R., only to be briefly interrupted by Colonel Blake and Columbian soldiers.<ref name="CW-9 Before">[[CW-9/Story]]</ref>

===To the New Horizons===
[[File:38_i14.png|thumb|left|Ifrit wakes Silence up from her stupor, reminding her of her principles.]]
Silence once again confronts Parvis, who has been attempting to create and artificial consciousness via Transmitter with Kristen's help. Saria is forced to battle her former Defense colleagues on her way to Kristen. Parvis continues to show the extent of his madness, spurred by his deteriorating mind, and Silence destroys the Transmitter hub responsible for maintaining Arc-01's accuracy. Parvis, however, substitutes his own consciousness via injected Transmitter to allow Kristen to achieve her goal, killing himself. Silence is shaken, but Ifrit arrives shortly after, and the two escape together.<ref name="CW-9 After">[[CW-9/Story]]</ref>

Back within the facility, the Preserver reveals that he was a last-ditch effort to preserve life. Because an advanced AI posed risks, an uploaded simulation of a human consciousness was required. In order to test the Doctor and Ama-10, the Preserver employs an ancient method—"Debate"—in which the individual engages in a debate with their own mind via a face-to-face discussion. For the Doctor, the opposition take the form of many individuals, including [[Priestess]], [[Talulah]], [[Amiya]], and a suspiciously independent [[Theresa]]. The debate shakes the Doctor, but they are resolute in their beliefs and are determined to stand with Rhodes Island.<ref name="CW-9 After">[[CW-9/Story]]</ref>

As the H.A.M.H.R.R. rotates once more, Saria draws upon what she knows of the Directors of Rhine Lab to deduce the way forward. She breaks into Muelsyse's Vivarium, and the two discuss the truth of Arc-01—Kristen Wright's Galleria Stellaria, Terra's first spacecraft. Muelsyse tries to stop Saria, but fails, and Saria instructs her to leave the craft, knowing that Kristen wishes the same. Saria moves on, and Kristen meets her in the deepest part of the flying lab: An atrium, filled with small metal planets flying individual orbits. Kristen and Saria converse, but come to blows regardless.<ref name="CW-10 Before">[[CW-10/Story]]</ref>

[[File:38_i16.png|thumb|All of Terra shall witness the truth behind their false sky.]]
Within her "Debate", Kal'tsit is met with an illusion of [[Lillia]]. Their conversation is simple, and Kal'tsit is quick to admit her powerlessness and growing impatience. Once the Debate ends, she speaks with the Preserver and reveals that the [[Civilight Eterna|Black Crown]] remains on Terra. The Doctor returns, and Kal'tsit addresses the Preserver by name—[[Friston-3|Trevor Friston]]. They discuss the situation on Terra, making note various notable areas: [[Aegir]] and the [[Abyssal Hunters]], the ancient [[Nightzmora]] Khagan who destroyed the southern [[Stargate]], and the intact Stargate beyond the [[Infy Icefield]]. Meanwhile, Kristen predicts Saria's actions to such a degree that she was able to jettison her from the Galleria via an emergency hatch. Immediately afterwards, the Galleria fires the built-up energy, ripping a hole in the Starpod and revealing the [[Twin Moons]] to Terra for the first time in thousands of years.<ref name="CW-10 Before">[[CW-10/Story]]</ref>

The Starpod ripples like the ocean's waves, as the aftereffects produced by Arc-01's firing are seen across Terra by all manner of life, leaving Terra's superpowers in disarray as they desperately seek the truth of the matter. The H.A.M.H.R.R. suffers severe damage after firing, and detaches from the Galleria Stellaria as the final crewmembers, including Muelsyse, escape via pods. However, the escape pod's emergency landing gear malfunctions, and Muelsyse stretches her hydrokinesis to the limit in order to save the crew and the unconscious Saria's life. Some time after the crash landing, Silence finds Saria and helps her, standing as equals.<ref name="CW-ST-3">[[CW-ST-3]]</ref>

==Goodnight, Terra==
The Preserver requests that Kal'tsit erases all of 4.7 million days of his emotional data, so that he can die as his original self with his original memories and emotions. Before that, the Preserver reveals that Priestess has placed restrictions on Kal'tsit, preventing her from disclosing information on the truth of [[Originium]].<ref name="CW-ST-3">[[CW-ST-3]]</ref>

[[File:38_i14.png|thumb|left|The Doctor and Kal'tsit face the ire of Columbia's president.]]
The Doctor and Kal'tsit watch in grim silence as Trevor Friston is forced to relive every day of his vigil once again at a rapid pace as his data deletes itself. They leave as the bunker begins to collapse, but Ho'olheyak manages to sneak in. Friston, however, no longer has any knowledge of Terra or its language, and Ho'olheyak is angered that the "God" she sought seemingly refuses to speak with her. In Trevor Friston's final moments, Ho'olheyak sees a vision of a father and daughter witnessing Terra from the surface of [https://endfield.wiki.gg/wiki/Talos-II another planet].

Still enraged that "God" has abandoned her, she takes the fragments of his consciousness into her Arts unit. In the cavern outside, the Tin Man and the Rhodes Island duo nearly come to blows until Ho'olheyak leverages Trevor Friston's remaining consciousness to secure a way out through the now-deserted S.H.A.F.T. Ho'olheyak would go to Rhodes Island, and they would be free to leave Columbia. Soon after, [[Mark Max]] converses with the Doctor and Kal'tsit through the screens within the empty facility. He calls Trevor Friston his father, and respects his will. In Loken's lab, Rosmontis watches as the elderly scientist passes away naturally, realizing that he simply wished to be remembered by someone personally. As her own revenge, she immediately forgets about him and moves on.<ref name="CW-ST-3">[[CW-ST-3]]</ref>

[[File:38_i12.png|thumb|In the end, Kristen succeeded in fulfilling her lifelong dream.]]
Upon returning to the surface, the Doctor seeks out a depressed Muelsyse within her Vivarium, and confide their loneliness in each other. The Doctor pulls Muelsyse from the vivarium, rescuing her from herself.<ref name="CW-ST-3">[[CW-ST-3]]</ref>

Many days later, Ferdinand and Blake share a prison cell in Max D.C., and Justin Jr. arrives, leveraging his connections to free them. Rhine Lab has been undergoing extensive reorganization, and Silence and Saria are working together to rebuild Rhine Lab alongside the remaining directors.<ref name="CW-ST-4">[[CW-ST-4]]</ref>

Aboard the Galleria Stellaria, Kristen prepares to enter a modified Sarcophagus. It is revealed that contrary to Muelsyse's beliefs, every plant aboard the ship has survived thus far. The pioneer bids goodnight to the universe, and the Galleria Stellaria is left to drift through space.<ref name="CW-ST-4">[[CW-ST-4]]</ref>
