==Synopsis==
===Together Below the Neons===
In the summer of 1099, [[Hoshiguma]] receives a letter from her estranged adoptive father, [[Tessai]], head of the [[Kinsekikai]] yakuza clan. In his letter, Tessai implores Hoshiguma to return to her home in [[Mitsukue]], [[Higashi]], in preparation for a funeral rite for Kajimachi District, which is set to be demolished soon. Hoshiguma has not returned to the city in twenty years and is unsure of how to proceed. After consulting with Lady [[Fumizuki]], Hoshiguma leaves [[Lungmen]] and makes her way towards the city.<ref name=1Bef>[[AT-1/Story#Before opreation|AT-1 Before]]</ref>

[[File:64 i01.png|thumb|left|A warm meal in a cold interrogation room. A great start.]]
Hoshiguma finds that the city has changed in many ways, its once barren streets now filled with shopping districts, towering skyscrapers, and street food vendors. Her return to Mitsukue has not gone unnoticed by the authorities, who recognize her for being the primary suspect in the murder of legislator candidate Shinjirou Koretou twenty years ago. Hoshiguma does her best to evade capture, but in the process she trespasses onto the concert stage of local celebrity [[Haruka|Momoka Hanyuu]], drawing further attention to herself. After spending an hour or so blending into the crowd, she takes refuge at an oden stall run by a shady information broker named [[Matsukiri|Tooru Moriuchi]]. By pure coincidence, Momoka was also dining there, and her shrieks instantly give away Hoshiguma's position. Hoshiguma is promptly arrested and brought to Mitsukue's police station for questioning.<ref>[[AT-ST-1]]</ref>

Hoshiguma is interrogated by Detective [[Koretou]], who questions why she has returned to Mitsukue after two decades. A tall [[Phidia]] man suddenly barges in, who Hoshiguma recognizes as [[Kouhei Mifune]], the second-in-command of Kinsekikai.<ref name=1Bef/> Mifune bails her out and brings her to Kajimachi—home turf of the yakuza clan—to see Tessai. After reuniting with Tessai at his house, Hoshiguma learns that Mifune had forged the letter to her in order to lure her back to Mitsukue, for some hitherto unknown purpose. He explains that while he is still officially chairman of Kinsekikai, Mifune has taken over virtually all of the clan's business operations, and has even expanded into the TV and talent agency sector under the name Kinseki Group. Now, he is planning to demolish Kajimachi under the pretense of urban redevelopment. Outraged by the situation, Hoshiguma vows to help Tessai.<ref name=1Aft>[[AT-1/Story#After opreation|AT-1 After]]</ref>

===Hundred Ghosts Parade===
[[File:64 i11.png|thumb|The vicious Oni went mad and killed everyone on sight; a scar on the city's past that remains to this day.]]
Hoshiguma heads back to her old family house to pay respects to her deceased biological father. Little did Hoshiguma know, her house has become the source of many terrifying ghost stories, where many prominent members of Kinsekikai died under strange circumstances. The legend of the Ao-Oni and the Haunted House has become a prominent kaidan tale, with rumours saying that the vicious [[Oni]]'s ghost still lurks within. At her producer's orders, Momoka had snuck into the Haunted House to film a special program and accidentally runs into Hoshiguma cleaning up. In her panic, she grabs a stick in an attempt to shoo her away before crashing through the floorboards and fleeing back home.<ref name=1Aft/>

The following day, Hoshiguma runs into [[Tetsuya Sorimachi]], who is Tessai's adoptive son. Tetsuya has long grown distant from his father and has switched over to Mifune's side, as the latter gives him more attention. Mifune has trusted him with the mission of investigating rumours about a spy from the Northern Court, leading him to suspect Hoshiguma. His interrogation is interrupted by [[Kichisei]], owner of the Lucky Star pachinko parlor and a huge fan of Momoka. When Hoshiguma expresses her concerns about Tetsuya's relationship with his father, the young Oni vents out his anger on her. After trying and failing to hit her, he begrudgingly limps away, leaving Hoshiguma worried about his safety.<ref name=2Bef>[[AT-2/Story#Before opreation|AT-2 Before]]</ref>

[[File:64 i02.png|thumb|left|"The whereabouts of popular singer and actress Momoka Hanyuu are currently unknown."]]
Meanwhile, Momoka's show ends up being a huge success, with multiple TV channels broadcasting it. Despite this, Momoka couldn't help but feel guilty as the show displayed Kajimachi in a bad light. She makes a post to clear Kajimachi's reputation, but immediately receives a call from Mio and her producer to delete it. As her talent agency is under the Kinseki Group, who are in charge of the Kajimachi redevelopment plan, she has no choice but to follow. In the middle of the night, while she tries recuperating from the day's stressful events, her TV suddenly starts glitching out and shows a strange recording. In it, Mifune suspects that Momoka is colluding with Tessai and states that she had taken something important from the Haunted House. He orders his second-in-command, [[Mio (Ato)|Mio]], to infect Momoka with [[Oripathy]] and force the idol into retirement. Momoka searches her belongings and finds that the "stick" she had taken was actually a broken blade. Not a few minutes later, Mio suddenly enters Momoka's room without warning, a cold and dispassionate gaze on her face.<ref name=2Aft>[[AT-2/Story#After operation|AT-2 After]]</ref>

Fearing for her life, Momoka leaps out of her window and dashes off with her scooter towards Moriuchi's oden stall, where she runs into Hoshiguma. Despite her incoherent babbling, Hoshiguma understands that Momoka is being targeted by the Kinsekikai, and helps fix her scooter. Hoshiguma uses her massive shield to cover for Momoka as they race down the alleyways and escape the yakuza. After hearing about this, Mifune himself makes an incredibly rare public appearance on TV announcing the disappearance of Momoka, imploring the public to assist in finding her. Although it seems like a missing persons plea, Mifune had effectively placed a bounty on Momoka's head, and now the whole town will be after her.<ref name=3Bef>[[AT-3/Story#Before opreation|AT-3 Before]]</ref>

===Kami, Kaidan, and Ninjutsu===
[[File:64 i05 2.png|thumb|The cool breeze drags slowly across the pond, as if all that running and chasing was merely a dream.]]
At Moriuchi's previous suggestion, Hoshiguma and Momoka drive towards Kizuna Jinja, the shrine of bonds, located at the edge of town. The two meet with [[Suzuran's Father|the head priest]], a [[Nine-Tailed Vulpo|nine-tailed Vulpo]] man. Hoshiguma asks him for help in locating a supposed kami she saw at her house, the "Wanyuudou", which apparently manifests as a unicycle with an invisible rider. By a stroke of luck, Momoka spots the unicycle roaming around shrine grounds, and works together with Hoshiguma to catch it. Momoka then receives a call from Kichisei, who informs her that Kinsekikai appears to have surrounded the shrine. The head priest guides them towards a secluded road leading away from the shrine, using his mystical [[Arts]] to destroy any nearby surveillance equipment.<ref name=4Bef>[[AT-4/Story#Before opreation|AT-4 Before]]</ref>

Hoshiguma and Momoka bring back the "Wanyuudou" to Moriuchi, who is astonished that they were able to locate an actual kami. The kami has his own questions for Moriuchi regarding the strange kaidan tales that plague Mitsukue, nearly all of which turn out to be underhanded dealings being disguised as spooky stories. While hearing their discussion, Momoka is reminded of the Haunted House, and asks Hoshiguma about the broken blade she took. Hoshiguma points out that the blade belongs to Sanoguchi, the second victim of the Haunted House. It is then that the group realize that this blade is vital piece of police evidence that could prove Mifune was responsible for killing Sanoguchi, which explains why he is so desperate to obtain it. Momoka comes to the startling realisation that all her shows and the kaidan occurrences happening around her have just been a cover for Kinsekikai's foul deeds.<ref name=4Aft>[[AT-4/Story#After opreation|AT-4 After]]</ref>

[[File:64 i09 2.png|thumb|left|The rage around Hoshiguma now spreads outwards, burning on her arms, shield, and sword.]]
Just then, the group gets surrounded by ninjas sent by Mifune. Despite Hoshiguma's best efforts to defend them, the ninjas manage to capture Moriuchi.<ref name=4Aft/> The two decide to stay low at Momoka's house for a while. Momoka is appalled to discover that her room has been thoroughly ransacked, and that someone has even cut her beloved potted plant Hasuke. Amidst tears, Momoka starts telling Hoshiguma about her past and her struggles to become an idol. After she finishes packing up, they head towards Kajimachi to seek refuge, since the Kinsekikai are prohibited from entering the area. On the way, they run into Tessai, who is protecting Kichisei from Mifune's ninjas. Tessai offers the group shelter at his home.<ref name=5Bef>[[AT-5/Story#Before opreation|AT-5 Before]]</ref>

Before leaving to rescue Moriuchi, Hoshiguma leaves her trusty shield, Hannya, in Tessai's care so that he can reforge it into a weapon that can surpass even [[Ch'en]]'s Chi Xiao. After all, Tessai was the original craftsman of Hannya, who made it specifically for her. In exchange, Tessai gives Hoshiguma her old yakuza garb and sword.<ref name=5Bef/> Hoshiguma breaks into Mifune's office by herself, giving Moriuchi a chance to escape in the chaos. Mifune reveals that he had lured Hoshiguma to Mitsukue so that he could obtain Hannya, whom many consider to be a symbol of succession within Kinsekikai. Mifune then orders Mio to stall Hoshiguma while he leaves to take care of Tessai. And while Hoshiguma is certain that her mentor is stronger; what she doesn't know is that Mifune has already identified Tessai's biggest weakness.<ref name=5Aft>[[AT-5/Story#After opreation|AT-5 After]]</ref>

===Ruin===
Mifune heads to Kajimachi and reveals his trump card to Tessai: photos showing Tetsuya opening crates containing weapons smuggled from the Northern Court. Fearing his son would be implicated as a spy, Tessai reluctantly concedes and hands over Haruka and Kichisei to him. In accordance with Mifune's orders, Tessai willingly gets himself into a debt of 200 million mon at a local auction and uses the deed to Kajimachi District as collateral to cover for the costs. Unbeknownst to Tessai, the entire process had been recorded with a secret camera and livestreamed directly to Tetsuya, who has been imprisoned in one of Kinsekikai's offices. Tetsuya can only watch in horror as Mifune proceeds to stab his father with his sword, taking both the signed contract and the reforged Hannya with him. Mio then receives direct orders from Mifune to get rid of Tetsuya in order to tidy up any loose ends. However, Mio ultimately decides to let Tetsuya go out of pity.<ref name=ST2>[[AT-ST-2]]</ref>

[[File:64 i06.png|thumb|The fireworks have not begun, yet Kajimachi could not be more "lively"...]]
Hoshiguma returns to Tessai's house only to find him mortally injured. Hoshiguma accompanies the old man in his final moments as construction equipment begins tearing down Kajimachi. At the same time, the police arrive at the scene to arrest Hoshiguma for Tessai's murder, who are revealed to be working with the Yakuza.<ref name=ST2/> Hoshiguma cuts through the army of ninjas and yakuza accompanying them, avoiding injuring any law enforcement. She finally manages to escape Kajimachi and hides herself in a narrow alleyway to pour a drink for the deceased Tessai. While she contemplates her next step, she is approached by Detective Koretou, who sits down with her for a drink. Koretou reveals that he is the nephew of the Diet candidate she killed twenty years ago, who blames her confession as the reason the case was never investigated properly.<ref name=6Bef>[[AT-6/Story#Before opreation|AT-6 Before]]</ref>

Koretou says Mifune has now allied himself with a very powerful member of the National Diet, putting the police under his thumb. Nonetheless, Koretou still wants to help Hoshiguma by smuggling her out of Mitsukue, but the latter states that nothing will be resolved that way. Just then, Hoshiguma hears Momoka's voice coming from an old TV down the alley. The TV shows a live feed of Momoka pleading for help, saying that she and Kichisei are trapped at Mifune's base. Hoshiguma rediscovers her resolve and tells Koretou that she will find a way to stop Mifune and fulfil her promise to Momoka. Koretou promises to do what he can to hold back pressure from the government.<ref name=6Bef/>

===Fulfiller of Dreams===
[[File:64 i07.png|thumb|left|"Mitsukue is under attack by the Northern Court. Repeat, Mitsukue is under attack by the Northern Court."]]
Momoka is rescued by a mysterious self-proclaimed "cyber ronin ninja" named [[Sarasa Nishigori]], one of Kinseki Group's tech employees. She tells Momoka that she should leave town, but Momoka refuses, saying that she must first stop Mifune. Thus, Sarasa coordinates a plan of attack with Moriuchi and Hoshiguma, aiming to create a distraction so that they can enter the stage where Mifune's accession ceremony will be taking place.<ref name=6Aft>[[AT-6/Story#After opreation|AT-6 After]]</ref> Using her hacking skills, she hijacks Kinseki Channel's TV network and broadcasts a citywide announcement warning that the Northern Court is invading Mitsukue. Mifune is caught off-guard and receives an urgent summons from his benefactor in the Diet.<ref name=7Bef>[[AT-7/Story#Before opreation|AT-7 Before]]</ref>

With the yakuza patriarch indisposed and the TV station in chaos, the group make their move. Moriuchi slips inside undetected into Mifune's office in order to retrieve Hannya, while Hoshiguma takes care of all the yakuza guards downstairs.<ref name=7Bef/> At the same time, Sarasa, Kichisei, and Momoka sneak into the ceremony venue. Despite their caution, the trio are stopped by Mio, who had been waiting for them. Fortunately, Hoshiguma arrives just in time, and gets into an intense duel against Mio, three swords against one. Moriuchi arrives a few minutes later and tosses Hannya to Hoshiguma, who uses it to swiftly incapacitate Mio in a single move. The Liberi finally concedes defeat, and knowing that Mifune will soon meet the same fate, she leaves to prepare Kinsekikai for the aftermath of his penultimate downfall.<ref name=7Aft>[[AT-7/Story#After opreation|AT-7 After]]</ref>

[[File:64 i08 2.png|thumb|Mifune's crimes are exposed by his talent agency's star idol, on his own television network.]]
With the venue cleared, Momoka steps on stage. Thousands of televisions across Mitsukue are now showing her face. Mifune does not have time to stop her before Tetsuya enters his office and fights him, hellbent on taking revenge for his father's death. With brave tears in her eyes, Momoka reveals the truth of how Kinsekikai and Kinseki Group have been manipulating her as well as how they have been fabricating the kaidan tales in order to cover up their dirty deeds. Furthermore, she formally announces that she will be cutting ties with her talent agency. Any chance for Mifune to turn the tide has been completed destroyed, together with his lofty ambitions. Upon seeing that Mifune is pretty much done for, Tetsuya leaves, his rage satisfied.<ref name=8Bef>[[AT-8/Story#Before opreation|AT-8 Before]]</ref>

Hoshiguma arrives at Mifune's office to find the man waiting for him. Hoshiguma states that she will be cutting her last ties with Higashi and requests that he write a formal letter of excommunication to declare that she no longer has anything to do with Mitsukue or the Kinsekikai. Mifune is amused by her proposal and instead proposes to settle things the old-fashioned way: a duel.<ref name=8Bef/>

===''Gokudō'': Like an Oni===
[[File:64 i18.png|thumb|A fight to sever ties with the past and to live for the future.]]
Hoshiguma and Mifune stop at an empty lot by the riverbank, near the remnants of Kajimachi, now razed to the ground. Hoshiguma asks Mifune where his hate comes from and why he started all this. Mifune confesses that it was because Tessai was a weakling; he boasted of the how the Kinsekikai were all family, yet when push came to shove, he cut ties with Hoshiguma. In Mifune's eyes, Tessai was nothing more than a hypocrite—a pawn who thought himself a king—so it was only natural for Mifune to take over and clean up his messes. In the end, he was the one who saved Kinsekikai from dissolution. However, it seems that Hoshiguma doesn't truly believe him, thinking that he's just putting on an act to make himself appear more honourable.<ref name=8Aft>[[AT-8/Story#After opreation|AT-8 After]]</ref>

With that said, the two take their stance and, end their twenty-year long feud in a single strike. Hoshiguma takes only a cut to her face, but Mifune takes a much deeper cut, though not enough to be life-threatening. Hoshiguma states that he will be judged for what he did to Tessai and Momoka. Yet, in spite of her victory, Hoshiguma feels only that she lost, because she can no longer see this city as her home. As she walks back towards the city, past the buildings and bright lights, she reminisces on her past in this city before meeting up with Momoka, who gives her a "fireworks" show with sparklers in an effort to cheer her up. As she celebrates with her newfound friends, Hoshiguma spots a Perro-shaped Kami-sama nearby; a symbol of good luck in love or relationships.<ref name=8Aft/>

[[File:64 i13.png|thumb|left|The tiny fireworks in front of her seem brighter than the rest of the city combined.]]
In the aftermath, Mifune is arrested for his crimes and brought to trial. Hoshiguma returns to Lungmen, bringing back an ''omiyage'' for Lady Fumizuki. Tetsuya has left Mitsukue to pursue the life of a wandering crime-fighting ''kyoukaku''. Detective Koretou is dismissed from his post, though is now planning to run for the Diet office. Sarasa, who is actually the heir to the Nishigori Clan of the Northern Court, is reprimanded for sneaking away from home. Momoka, no longer having to worry about being exploited by Kinsekikai, starts making plans to start her own talent agency. She mentions that she’d like to travel to [[Rhodes Island]]; even Moriuchi seems interested in a company that Hoshiguma praises so highly.<ref name=ST3>[[AT-ST-3]]</ref>

Somewhere high up in a luxury apartment, Mio meets with Mifune's benefactor from the Diet. She delivers the news that she is now managing Kinsekikai and has done her best to deal with the aftermath of the incident. After she leaves, the Diet member enters a small room in the back, where an old pachinko machine is standing. It turns out that the entire reason for Kajimachi's forced takeover plan was just a pitiful excuse by a dull-witted man to procure his lucky pachinko machine. He thanks the Kami of Happy Returns for blessing this machine, for giving him success in life, and for the prosperous development of the entire Higashinese Southern Court. However, with property prices rapidly climbing, these happy days may soon come to an end...<ref name=ST3/>

==Trivia==
Ato's plot is reminiscent to that of the ''Yakuza''/''Like a Dragon'' series.
*The relationship between Hoshiguma, Mifune, and Tessai is similar to that between [[fandom:yakuza:Kazuma Kiryu|Kazuma Kiryu]], [[fandom:yakuza:Akira Nishikiyama|Akira Nishikiyama]], and [[fandom:yakuza:Shintaro Kazama|Shintaro Kazama]], specifically in regards to how Mifune sees Tessai as always favoring Hoshiguma.
*Haruka shares her given name with [[fandom:yakuza:Haruka Sawamura|Haruka Sawamura]], who is also an idol in the fifth game. Both also had to make grand speeches on-stage in regards to their career near the end of the story, and both of them also have ties to the yakuza.
**The title of the movie Haruka stars in, ''Like A Yakuza: Birth of the Vicious Oni'', is a nod to the series.
