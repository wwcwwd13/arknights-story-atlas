==Boiling Point==
The story starts with [[Alanna]] and [[Warmy]] (teasingly referred to as Potlid) inside of a transport truck. The two of them basically live in the truck and have even set up a makeshift (illegal) kitchen. While Warmy is cooking tomato braised cystbeast, Alanna brings up that they need to get some supplies before they head to Iron Carrot City and gives Warmy a list of items to buy. She tells Warmy that she can make sell the scrap pots and cutlery to make some money.

Warmy loads all of her items onto a handcart and pushes it through the city, but then loses control and slips down the slope. A magenta-haired [[Cautus]] named [[Ray]] halts the cart in its tracks before it crashes into a recycling facility. Though embarrassed, Warmy toughs it out and gathers back all her items. She brings them to a shopkeeper to sell, but the shopkeeper is reluctant due to the poor quality of the items. Fortunately, upon mentioning "Alanna of the Barrenlands," the shopkeeper changes his mind and offers to take the items, saying that Alanna owes him a meal now. Although Warmy manages to sell off the items, she doesn't find a particular valve and decides to look elsewhere.

Warmy ends up spending a long time searching but is only able to get a somewhat similar valve. Alanna mentions that it wasn't likely that she could find such an old model in the market anyway. The truck is getting old and is no longer "standardised", so their going to Iron Carrot City to get a replacement. Alanna just needs the items to ensure that the truck is able to make that last trip. However, Warmy has grown rather attached to the truck and is hesitant about letting it go.

The next day, Warmy goes to the union office to hand over their truck's decommisioning report. [[Savage]] happens to be there and offers to help Warmy take care of it. With all the paperwork done, Warmy returns to the truck and hides in the toolroom. Only two people enter: a [[Zalak]] by the name of [[Jerry]] and the magenta-haired [[Cautus]] that Warmy saw yesterday. In the middle of the ride, a PA announcement suddenly echoes through the vehicle: the truck has been hijacked and will instead go to the Grinning Valley.

==One-way Tracks==
Ray has a crossbow aimed at Alanna's neck. Alanna negotiates with her to lower the crossbow and promises that she'll get the truck straight to the Grinning Valley. Ray firmly says that she'll let Alanna and the passengers go once they arrive, and that she'll even pay her. Hearing this, Alanna comes up with a plan. She decides to take a route that goes through a security checkpoint. She's hoping that she'll be able to contact the officers for help to deal with the truckjacker. However, the checkpoint is more congested than usual, and Alanna finds out why: an [[Originium]] vein exploded at a nearby mine, prompting a search for [[Infected]].

Alanna panics because she knows that Warmy is actually infected. Although she tries to negotiate with the officers, they forcibly enter her vehicle. They find Warmy (and Jerry) hiding in the toolroom and drag them both out. They slap infection monitors onto everyone's wrist, which beeps when it detects [[Oripathy]]. As Alanna had feared, Warmy's device starts beeping. Alanna pleads with the officers, explaining that she's just helping Warmy find her missing father, but the officers remain unfazed. They take Warmy out of the truck when another device starts beeping. This time, it was Ray's infection monitor, and this time she's prepared. She uses her crossbow to incapacitate all the security officers and rescues Warmy. Ray brings Warmy back inside the truck and Alanna steps on the gas, ramming through the security checkpoint.

Now that they're away from the authorities, Alanna thanks Ray for saving Warmy. She decides to ask why Ray wants to go to the Grinning Valley. The area is a hostile place rampant with [[Catastrophe]]s and is nothing but wasteland ruins. Ray reveals that she is searching for a "huge, talking shadow" that can shoot "rays of light through the fog."

==Running Bottletree==
The group stop near some bottletrees in order to collect water from them. Alanna says that they'll use transporting bottletree water as an excuse in the city and patch the truck up, so that nobody will know that it's a vehicle that's gone off-route. Jerry, being an insurance marketer, is nervous and talks about all the risks of doing even the slightest dangerous activity.

Alanna later tasks Ray and Jerry to go buy some supplies. While the two walk around the city, Jerry can't stop fidgeting and keeps worrying that security checkpoint officers might come after them. He frantically checks newspapers to see if his picture has appeared in any missing persons list. Ray asks him about it and Jerry reveals that he was on his way to his wedding ceremony when Ray hijacked the transport vehicle. Ironically, he was actually pretty happy about it because he didn't know the other woman at all since it was arranged by his family.

Jerry then spots some uniformed people nearby and gets paranoid, so he decides to bail. In an effort to return to the truck undetected by crawling underneath the vehicles, he ends up boarding the wrong car and bumps into a Cautus woman named Keira. Jerry and Keira flee into a sewage tunnel in a mad escapade of trying to escape some invisible threat. The two work together to navigate the tunnels and escape through a factory waste disposal system. In the process, the two form a close bond, and Jerry asks Keira to be the beneficiary of his insurance plan. Surprisingly, she agrees. As the two of them start talking, Keira reveals she had also run away from a marriage, and that the man he was supposed to be married to sounds strangely like Jerry. The two suddenly realise that they may not be complete strangers after all.

Meanwhile, Alanna is asking around for the best route to the Grinning Valley. She contacts [[Asbestos]], a seasoned explorer. Alanna also asks for some information about the huge shadow that Ray is looking for. Asbestos explains that they might be searching for something known as a [[Feranmut]], but she doesn't know much more than that. At the same time, the [[Doctor]], [[Amiya]], and Savage are wandering around the city. Amiya reminiscenses about her time in [[Rim Billiton]] and how she had spent her childhood here.

With the info in hand, Alanna and Ray get into the truck and begin their long journey to the Grinning Valley. As she is driving, Alanna suddenly realises that Warmy is missing.

==Hammer Down==
[[File:46 i01.png|thumb|Though there have been some unexpected twists, nothing's better than sitting under the stars in the outback.]]
Alanna finds that Warmy has been in the power room all this time, using her Arts to heat up the engine to keep the ride smooth. However, the extended use of her ability has caused Warmy to lose control of her Arts. Alanna tries to give Warmy some folk remedies but to no avail. Her subconscious casting is increasing the temperature inside the vehicle, causing the pipes to smoke and fuses to break. Ray thinks about knocking Warmy out in order to prevent her from bringing more harm to the others. Hearing this, Warmy begins talking to Ray about her family and how she laments being an Infected.

Fortunately, the Doctor, Amiya, and Savage had been driving nearby when they notice the smoking vehicle. Savage knocks on the door of the transport and Alanna answers. Alanna insists that she doesn't need help when a small explosions rocks the vehicle. Sensing an emergency, Savage enters the vehicle and sees Warmy's condition flaring up. Amiya and the Doctor then come into the vehicle. Amiya uses her Arts to put her to sleep, stopping her subconscious casting. The Rhodes Island crew decide to stay with Alanna and the others to monitor her condition.

Later that night, the group set up a campfire under the stars. Alanna explains that they were heading to the Grinning Valley to look for Ray's Feranmut. Warmy is back up on her feet and is cooking potato soup for everyone. Amiya asks Alanna regarding the emotion she had sensed in Warmy earlier; a deep sorrow that contradicts her smiling face. Alanna explains that it was because of what Warmy's father told her before he went missing: "I want you to grow up happily, just as you are now."

Hearing about Warmy's past reminds Amiya of her own. She starts wondering what would have happened if she had never met the Doctor. Though the journey has been difficult, Amiya is grateful that for being able to help people like Warmy. If she could meet her parents again, Amiya would like to say to them, "I survived." In that moment, the Doctor suddenly remembers about Rhodes Island's predecessor, "[[Babel]]," and a bloody mission suffered by one of its mercenary teams to procure a Rim Billiton mining module.

Savage herself feels grateful to the Doctor for letting her join Rhodes Island. Though she had some initial doubts about the Doctor and [[Kal'tsit]]'s relationship with Amiya, she admires the company's creed of protecting the weak and promises that her hammer will always guard the Doctor.

==Grinning Mineshaft==
[[File:46 i02 1.png|thumb|"Why is it holding on to the Doctor's hood so tightly?!"]]
[[File:46 i03.png|thumb|She got the meeting she asked for, yet also not.]]
Ray is having a flashback from back when she worked as a pitsinker, inspecting the safety of [[Originium]] mines before the other workers head in. She goes down a mineshaft together with her Sandbeast. During the inspection of the deeper, darker areas of the mineshaft, Ray finds several signs point to active Originium. This includes a toxic gas leak, which causes her Sandbeast's death.

The memory then turns into a hallucination that blends with reality, with Ray believing that the Sandbeast she is currently following is actually talking to her. Unbeknownst to her, Amiya, the Doctor and Warmy follow her. One Sandbeast grabs onto the Doctor's hood and Amiya tries to get it back off, while Warmy is feeding some others and getting along with them quite well. Amiya's shouting snaps Ray back to reality entirely, allowing her to step in and easily gets the Sandbeast to let go of the Doctor. Ray then sends the Sandbeasts along, but one remains at her side.

Alanna then comes running in, asking the Doctor about weird readings from the survey equipment. The Doctor immediately realises what is going on, and tells everyone to get away from an impending [[Catastrophe]]. The group runs back to the truck as fast as they can to escape direct impact, except for Ray who stays behind in an attempt to get all the Sandbeasts to safety.

In a twist of fate, the Sandbeasts take action and carry Ray away instead. They bring her into a cavern where they are sheltered from the wind outside, where Ray hops off. Ray notes that the cavern clearly runs deeper than the Sandbeasts had brought her, but the path has been blocked off. Still determined to help, she starts to chisel at the rocks forming the blockade.

A loud voice stops Ray by complaining about being taken out of its slumber once more. Ray questions whether she is hallucinating again, until the "blockade" opens up to reveal a giant illuminating eye from the [[Feranmut]] she was looking for for so long. Ray starts to explain how the Feranmut had saved her life when she was young, and wanted to thank It for that. The Feranmut replies that it could not have been It, because It had been trapped underground in the outback for longer than Ray has been alive. There aren't even any others like It left as far as the Feranmut is aware. This news leaves Ray wondering what exactly she saw instead. The Feranmut tells her she should best leave again, but regardless still allows her to make a request from It.

==Stepped-on Shadow==
With the Feranmut's answer, Ray's journey comes to an unsatisfying end. Nevertheless, she quietly returns to the group and follows them back to Iron Carrot City. Alanna later runs into Jerry, who was on a honeymoon with his newlywed Keira. It turns out the two were supposed to be married to each other, but it all turned fine in the end.

Meanwhile, Savage had offered to bring Warmy to Rhodes Island in order to treat her Oripathy. She was enlisted as an operator and given a caster's staff as well as a new set of clothes. Although Warmy is delighted to have a place to learn Arts, she is upset that it meant leaving Alanna and hugs her in tears. Ray is later also enlisted as a Rhodes Island Operator, bringing along a sandbeast cub with her.

Back on the landship, Amiya gifts Doctor a small vivarium. She hopes that it will serve as a small slice of Rim Billiton life and help the Doctor feel less lonely. Later, Ray comes into the office and asks Amiya about the Feranmut that she saw. As Ray talks about the Feranmut, Amiya "sees" into her memories. She saw Ray helping out [[Sarkaz]] mercenaries as they were navigating a fierce sandstorm, bringing with them a freshly excavated Rhodes Island landship. In those memories, Amiya sees the "light" Ray had witnessed over ten years ago: the founder of [[Babel]], [[Theresa]].

Amiya suddenly recalls a conversation she once had with Theresa when she was younger. Theresa had said that one day, Amiya would be the one to decide what Rhodes Island should mean. The virtous path is difficult and unending, one that cannot be walked alone. However, as long as Amiya believes in the people she trusts, like the Doctor, then the journey might be a little less scary.
