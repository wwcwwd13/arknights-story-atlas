==Synopsis==
===The Red Wine Murder===
Under the New Urban Management Act, [[Nuova Volsinii]] has seen an influx of foreign capital and businesses entering the city from all across [[Terra]]. The city is currently hosting the Red Wine Championship, hosted by Czarek Radoslaw, owner of the renowned Golden Prairie Winery from [[Kazimierz]]. Mayor [[Leontuzzo]] steps onto the stage with him, thanking him for having such faith in their city's transparent bureaucracy. As the two step off the stage, they are approached by [[Demetri Certaldo]], owner of the promising winery Saluzzo Vino. He asks Czarek about the judging criteria, to which the latter proclaims that the victor will be crowned based solely on its flavor, regardless of the wine's origins.

After their stint with [[Antonio Venezia|Antonio]] from Famiglia Venezia, [[Capone]] and [[Gambino]] now work as Czarek's hired security, serving as guards in charge of protecting the venue. As they patrol the cellar, they notice a suspicious [[Caprinae]] leaving the area. Not long afterwards, a blonde Caprinae follows suit. Upstairs, the wine tasting competition is entering the final stage. Czarek presents the judge, Paola Memminger, conductor and musician of the Schwarzervorhang Orchestra, who performed the opening act for the competition. Paola carefully tastes the three wines presented for the finals before selecting the third glass. Czarek is giddy to hear her choice, believing that it was his winery's vintage, but much to his surprise, the wine in question in fact belongs to Saluzzo Vino. After nearly embarrassing himself, he announces Saluzzo Vino as the competition's winner.

[[File:071 mini01.png|thumb|As Demetri passes by Leontuzzo, their gazes meet for the shortest of instants, before they are separated.]]
After the competition concludes, the attendees continue to chat and dine as usual, tasting the wine that was presented during the competition. All of a sudden, Paola begins coughing violently, and she collapses to the floor. The guests around her quickly realise that she has been poisoned. Thanks to Leontuzzo and the municipal staff, the situation is quickly handled, but Czarek accuses Demetri of committing the crime. Multiple eyewitnesses claimed to have seen Paola drink a glass of Saluzzo Vino's red wine before dying. Furthermore, Demetri is also the current don of Famiglia Bellone, which Czarek says makes him even more untrustworthy. Demetri remains calm and collected in spite of the accusation, stating that he will fully cooperate with the investigation.

Gambino and Capone haul Demetri into a vehicle, which takes him away to the police station. Afterwards, Gambino falls deep into thought, saying that in the end, Nuova Volsinii is still a Siracusan city. A lot of things have changed, but at the same time, nothing has changed. And maybe, the problems in Nuova Volsinii aren't just limited to the famiglie anymore.<ref>[[The Red Wine Murder]]</ref>

===Business Acumen===
Backed by the [[Kazimierz General Chamber of Commerce]] (K.G.C.C.), Golden Prairie Winery has been aggressively expanding its influence in Siracusa. One week before the Red Wine Championship, Czarek's niece, [[Tosia]], moved to Nuova Volsinii after a corruption scandal related to the [[Silverlance Pegasi]] caused some unrest in [[Kawalerielki]]. Tosia contacts stores all across the city, asking them to sign a special shelf display agreement with Golden Prairie. Although the rewards are generous, it has an exclusivity clause that ensures only Golden Prairie's wines are allowed to be displayed. As more shops sign the agreement, local Siracusan wines will gradually find themselves pushed out of the market.

After having dinner with Czarek, Tosia walks around the city and runs into a young girl named Bettina, selling wines belonging to a local winery named Verdi, where her grandfather works. However, she hasn't sold a single bottle since morning. Bettina asks whether Tosia could teach her some tips, to which the latter brazenly states that the two are business rivals and refuses to help her. Tosia starts to leave, but gets worried she may have been too harsh. She goes back to check on Bettina, who is preparing to jump over a railing. Thinking she was about to commit suicide, Tosia changes her mind and dives to save her, promising to teach her. It turns out that Bettina was just trying to rescue someone's pet.

Tosia teaches Bettina a bunch of popular buzzwords and market lingo to help promote her product better. However, Bettina thinks that such advertising is swindling the customer. Tosia explains that the wine market in Nuova Volsinii has shifted dramatically with the entry of large industry players like [[Victoria]], [[Columbia]], and even [[Bolívar]]. Foreign competitors can offer better wines at a cheaper price, while local Siracusan wineries are getting suppressed due to their outdated production facilities and old-fashioned marketing. In true Kazimierzian fashion, she states that to compete in such a cutthroat market means having to sacrifice some sincerity for the sake of profit.

Tosia gives Bettina an example of how to properly package and present her wine. By pure coincidence, a wealthy man hears her presentation and pays a huge sum for a bottle of Verdi before hurrying off to a last-minute meeting. However, Bettina is worried that Verdi's cheap wine is inappropriate for the customer's purposes and the two chase down the customer to apologise. It turns out the man had specifically bought Verdi as his family frequently ate dinner with it. Bettina later receives a purchase offer from a foreign businessman who heard about her wines from the woman whose pet she saved. Although Tosia knows that one small deal won't save Verdi, she is delighted to see Bettina making a proper sale using her own methods.<ref>[[Business Acumen]]</ref>

===Witness===
For years, Verdi Winery has been struggling to make a profit. By the time the family-run business was inherited by [[Lupina]], it was already operating at a loss. So, when Golden Prairie made an offer to acquire Verdi, Lupina couldn't refuse. One of her workers, Niccolo—Bettina's grandfather—was upset that she would choose to sacrifice her grandfather's legacy. He asserts that while the Kazimierzians' harvesting methods save time in the long run, they do not respect the time and craft behind tending the grape vines. Lupina believes that it's better for the winery to die with dignity now, than to continue to try to scrape by in Nuova Volsinii.

Lupina goes to visit Demetri at his private bar. Demetri had heard about Verdi Winery's situation from one of his staff, Elio Fabri. Lupina asks Demetri whether he could take in her remaining workers, and the latter agrees. Although Lupina had already signed a letter of intent to sell, Niccolo and Elio refuse to sit down and do nothing. They decide to sabotage the wine-making equipment and the water pipes in hopes that the shoddy equipment would deter the Kazimierzians from buying the winery.

The auditor arrives the next day to survey the area. As expected, he had noticed the poor quality of the equipment, but he reveals that Golden Prairie did not include them in their original assessment of the property. Golden Prairie viewed traditional Siracusan winemaking equipment as outdated and incompatible with modern production lines. They were, in fact, more interested in the land which the winery sat on. Lupina is quite appalled by this revelation, but there is nothing she can do except wait for the acquisition. Niccolo later apologises for his sabotage, saddened that his descendants will never see Verdi again. As one last favour, he asks that Lupina comes to her granddaughter Bettina's tenth birthday, with a bottle of wine as old as her age, as per Siracusan tradition.

Lupina is unsure how she would be able to find such an old vintage, but she receives a surprise letter from Demetri asking her to come to his bar. Upon arriving, Lupina finds a gift box sitting near the drum kit she often played. Inside the box was a precious wine bottle with the label 'Verdi 1092'.<ref>[[Witness]]</ref>

===Crash===
After [[I Portatori dei Velluti/Synopsis|the incident at Carnevale]], the Truckers' Union was integrated into the city's municipal system to better protect its workers. With more Kazimierzian enterprises entering Nuova Volsinii, the Agenzia per la Sicurezza dei Trasporti (AST) enters a cooperation with the K.G.C.C.. Initially, many AST drivers were happy with the higher pay, but they are also heavily fined if they fail to meet their deadlines, and their pay is docked for every sick day. [[Eirene Lavazza]], now a commissioner of the AST, gets into a debate with the Kazimierzians when a driver gets accused of breaching the terms of their contract.

After reexamining the contract, Eirene begins to realise that although the individual terms appear reasonable, combined together they serve to put pressure on the lowest rung of the ladder. She meets with the K.G.C.C. representative to readjust the terms of the contract and requests more flexibility when dealing with edge cases. The representative agrees to not scrutinise the penalty records of individual drivers and will set aside the recent records. Eirene believed that everything would go well afterwards, but one day before the Red Wine Championship, a truck driver crashed while doing a last-minute delivery of Golden Prairie's wine. Thankfully, the K.G.C.C. kept their promise, as they will not pursue individual liability for the incident, and will write off the goods as a failed contract.

Unfortunately, things changed after the Red Wine Championship ended with the murder of Paola. The AST vehicle that delivered the samples under suspicion happened to be the one that caused the accident involving the truck delivering Golden Prairie's wine. Armed with this fact, the K.G.C.C. demands an enormous compensation fee for their losses or they will terminate the contract. The driver being sued says that if the AST doesn't find a solution, he will talk to the Bellones instead. Eirene finds herself stuck between a rock and a hard place, unable to provide her workers with the safety she had promised. Left with no other choice, Eirene decides to call [[Lavinia]] for help.<ref>[[Crash]]</ref>

===Unheard Sound===
After the unexpected death of Paola, the Schwarzervorhang Orchestra has fallen into despair. Paola's adopted daughter, [[Ripresa|Liesel Memminger]], is left to negotiate with Czarek about the terms and conditions of their contract. With star performer Paola gone, Czarek decides to not renew their contract and cancels all future performances with them. Additionally, due to ''force majeure'' clauses embedded in the contract, the orchestra may not receive their corresponding performance fees. When Liesel informs her fellow performers about this, they become enraged, blaming the Siracusan famiglie for killing Paola. One of them even threatens to attack the Bellones if he ever saw one.

Later that night, Liesel rummages around Paola's room, sorting out her old things. As she looks out at the bright lights of the city outside the window, she is suddenly reminded of the strange Caprinae man she saw in the cellar, and how he was handling a wine glass. Liesel's chest tightens as she starts to think that one of her fellow Leithanian performers is the culprit. Her fears are amplified when she receives a call from the police stating that two of the orchestra's performers got into a conflict on Bellone property.

When Liesel arrives at the holding cell, she discovers that the situation was not what she anticipated. Apparently, her orchestra performers were actually trying to stop a couple of [[Columbia]]n businessmen from harassing hotel staff and ended up in a brawl. Liesel cannot help but laugh and reprimands herself for ever suspecting them. Liesel takes the opportunity to apologise to the Columbian businessmen and offers a free and exclusive performance at their brand new mall. Seeing that as a valuable business opportunity for both sides, the Columbians agree.

Some days later, Schwarzervorhang Orchestra receives a standing ovation for their performance at the Piazza Parasota mall. As they gather in the dressing room backstage, Liesel tells her fellow performers that she had seen Paola's murderer—a Caprinae—and vows to bring him to justice.<ref>[[Unheard Sound]]</ref>

===Ordine Nuovo===
Since the incident at the Red Wine Championship, Czarek has been constantly putting pressure on Mayor Leontuzzo. Leontuzzo and Lavinia find themselves in a difficult position, as they understand that Czarek is targetting Demetri not simply because he is a business rival, but because of his mafia background. Most local merchants in Siracusa have ties to the famiglie, and if such ties become valid grounds for accusations of wrongdoing, the local industry will come under pressure. It is not only the wine industry under threat, but also logistics, service, and manufacturing. Even with more foreign companies opening businesses in the city, they prefer to employ people from their own countries, resulting in a shrinking job market for locals.

Leontuzzo had envisioned a city free of famiglie, but this has instead only made them more vulnerable to greedy foreign capitalists who are eager to exploit the loopholes in their laws and suck up as much profit as possible. Lavinia presents a revision to the New Urban Management Act that could potentially mitigate their issues. The new act proposes tariffs on foreign goods, anti-monopoly laws on key sectors, and issuing franchise permits to famiglia-controlled corporations. Leontuzzo is concerned that these rules would make them appear as though they were regressing to their old Siracusan methods, but Lavinia argues that as it stands right now, the only force capable of uniting everyone is still the famiglie. Leontuzzo leaves the draft on his desk, contemplating on whether to sign it.

Two weeks after the murder, the trial for Demetri Bellone commences. Lavinia calls various witnesses to the stand, including Liesel Memminger. The testimony provided by the witnesses indicates that Demetri was not the culprit. Lavinia then calls forward a surprise witness: Elio Fabri. Lavinia questions why Elio sabotaged Verdi Winery, causing Golden Prairie to incur substantial maintenance costs after their acquisition. Elio publicly decries Golden Prairie's unfair business practices, including how they've been using underhanded tactics to monopolise the market and acquire struggling wineries like Verdi, only to shut them down later. Demetri then steps in, saying that he understands Elio's motives but is still willing to claim responsibility for his actions. This proclamation steers the trial into a heated debate, and  Lavinia announces that the verdict will be given at a later date.

[[File:071 mini02.png|thumb|"Your established rules will keep the famiglie in Nuova Volsinii from running amok, while my ways will gather all the power and spirit of Siracusa."]]
Leontuzzo privately visits Elio in his cell and asks why he is still working with Famiglia Bellone when Nuova Volsinii offers so many other job opportunities. Elio contends that he is working for Demetri not merely to benefit the Bellones, but to protect Nuova Volsinii from greedy businesses like Golden Prairie. When Leontuzzo goes to visit Demetri, he reiterates the same sentiment. Just as Leontuzzo cannot afford to lose ground to foreign countries, Demetri cannot allow the famiglia and those who depend on it to lose their home. They are like a pack of whelps grouping up to keep themselves from being devoured by a bigger beast. They are two sides of the same coin, working towards Siracusa's future. Leontuzzo finally makes up his mind and contacts Lavinia, saying that he is ready to sign the amended act.<ref>[[Ordine Nuovo]]</ref>

Perhaps there really will come a day when Nuova Volsinii is completely free of the famiglie's influence, but until then, they will have to wait a little longer.
