==Synopsis==
===The Land of a Thousand Poleis===
[[File:69_i01.png|thumb|If heroic epics are the rain that nourishes Minos, then these temples are the mushrooms that grow afterwards.]]
The year is currently 1100, and the turn of the century is bringing great change to the small country of [[Minos]]. Once colonised by [[Sargon]], Minos has since gained independence and is slowly rebuilding its former glory. It has been 5 years since Minos and Sargon signed a non-aggression pact, putting an end to the skirmishes on their nations' borders.

As part of efforts to improve diplomatic relations, the two nations have agreed to conduct a cultural exchange program. Sargon has promised to repatriate [[Kereseira]]'s harp—a national treasure belonging to the founding hero of Minos—which had been recently found in a Padishah's collection. In return, Minos will exchange engineering blueprints and technical data left behind by Sargon during the construction of the Three Poleis, Athenius, Corinia, and Lachedamon.<ref name="ST1">[[ME-ST-1]]</ref>

As the current curator of the [[Varjavandabad Museum]] and a descendant of a noble family, [[Titi|Medjehtiqedti Nasutu Mashayer]] (a.k.a. Titi) was chosen to represent Sargon for the exchange. Accompanying her is deputy chief of mission, [[Behnui Enshi-Pah]], a haughty man whose family is well known for their military exploits in Minos. Titi is also accompanied by [[Mio]], a [[Feline]] [[Beast Lord]] who has been with her since the aftermath of [[Adventure That Cannot Wait for the Sun/Synopsis|the Menat-Ha'mait incident]]. They are scheduled to meet with [[Lydia]], the Astynomos of Athenius, who is in charge of handling the city's diplomatic affairs.<ref name="ST1"/>

Titi strays away from the group and uses the ancient route once used by Kereseira, giving her an impressive view of [[Athenius]], the First Polis. She makes her way towards the Delphi Canal, where she meets the dockmaster [[Varkáris|Xanthos]] and his apprentice [[Lykeion]]. Xanthos once lost his best friend Ithakos in the war against Sargon, and has despised the Sargonians ever since. To avoid his wrath, Titi introduces herself as a traveler intending to sell [[Wrankwood]] merchandise in Minos, and they believe her to be [[Columbia]]n. Although Titi gets seasick, she is safely ferried across the river without incident.<ref name="ST1"/>

===Sightseeing in Minos===
After a long nap at the hotel, Titi meets with the Grammateús [[Hekádemos]], aide to the Astynomos. He begins to guide her to Kereseira's First Temple, where Kereseira first received divine revelation in the mountains. Though the original temple was destroyed when Sargon invaded Minos, the Minoans are determined to rebuild the temple with their own hands to venerate the heroic spirit. Hekádemos bails halfway the second his shift is over, so Titi and Mio walk on their own to the temple.<ref name="1Bef">[[ME-1/Story#Before operation|ME-1 Before]]</ref>

[[File:69_i03.png|thumb|left|"The only way to solve a problem is to face it directly."]]
At the temple, Titi and Mio meet with a worried craftsman. He explains that the craftsman and fruit farmers have been at odds for the past several days due to their conflicting schedules and interests. Fortunately, Titi was able to provide a solution that satisfies both parties after calming them via an arm wrestling match, and they thank her for her assistance.<ref name="1Aft">[[ME-1/Story#After operation|ME-1 After]]</ref> Titi later meets [[Pallas]]—the original proponent of the First Temple's reconstruction—who is impressed by Titi's quick and efficient thinking. The two have a hearty conversation about history and culture over bottles of wine.<ref name="2Bef">[[ME-2/Story#Before operation|ME-2 Before]]</ref>

At the graveyard, Xanthos puts flowers on the grave of Ithakos. He is approached by [[Kassandra]], a close friend of Ithakos and the Head Priestess of Endymion Temple. She attempts to console him, but Xanthos is unwilling to let go of his rage. After Ithakos disappeared during the conflict with Sargon, Xanthos had joined the Minoan guerrillas in search of him. He spent two years travelling and fighting, coming across the weapons and gear he left behind in the hands of others. Finally, he confronted a Sargonian Padishah, whose army was responsible for wiping out Ithakos and his squad. Xanthos wanted to execute the padishah, but he was stopped by his comrades. Minos and Sargon had been planning to sign a ceasefire, and his comrades were afraid his actions would break the truce. In the end, he had nowhere to release his anger.<ref name="1Aft"/><ref name="6Aft">[[ME-6/Story#After operation|ME-6 After]]</ref>

Meanwhile, [[Rhodes Island]] [[Operator]] [[Perfumer the Distilled|Lena Athopoulou]] has recently returned to her hometown to establish a branch office in Athenius. Lena has been busy handling all sorts of administrative work to set up a proper [[Oripathy]] medical system for the local [[Infected]].<ref name="2Bef"/> She visits Lykeion's house for an Oripathy checkup and asks him why he's been visiting the local Rhodes office so many times. Lykeion begrudgingly reveals that he has been obtaining medication on behalf of his master, Xanthos, who is an unregistered Infected. Lykeion refrained from telling anyone as Minoans believed that Oripathy sapped the heroic spirit and didn't want people to look down on his master. His Oripathy condition is particularly severe, yet Xanthos rarely rests.<ref name="2Aft">[[ME-2/Story#After operation|ME-2 After]]</ref>

===Democratic Vote===
Titi later asks Astynomos Lydia whether they could host the exchange ceremony in the First Temple's square, to be displayed in public instead of behind closed doors. Lydia accepts her proposal on the condition that Titi becomes the supervisor of the First Temple's rebuilding. Her background and expertise would help to speed up reconstruction and avoid further delays. However, Titi is concerned that getting involved would be problematic considering her role as the Sargonian envoy. Just then, a man named [[Períandros]], an ambassador invited from Corinia, suggests that they hold a vote to decide.<ref name="3Bef">[[ME-3/Story#Before operation|ME-3 Before]]</ref>

[[File:69_i04_a.png|thumb|She wants to smile, that famous bright, carefree smile. But she cannot find the words, or her smile.]]
That night, dozens of citizens gathered in the amphitheatre to hear Titi explain how she would help with the project. Many of the craftsmen and farmers she befriended chose to vote for her, but one man loudly objects to Titi's involvement. His attitude reminds Titi that for many people, she is still a Sargonian, a child of the nation who trampled upon Minoan soil. After the voting period ends, Titi remains at the amphitheatre alone. Tears flow down her face while Mio tries to make her feel better.<ref name="3Aft">[[ME-3/Story#After operation|ME-3 After]]</ref>

Xanthos is enraged upon learning that the Sargonian emissaries were planning to use Kereseira's harp as a "political bargaining chip" when it was a Minoan treasure in the first place.<ref name="3Bef"/> He devises a scheme to take back the harp; he instructs Lykeion to ram his boat into the hotel kitchen to create a distraction. The resulting fire forces Titi and Pallas to evacuate from the hotel, leaving the box with the harp behind.<ref name="4Bef">[[ME-4/Story#Before operation|ME-4 Before]]</ref> Xanthos then enters the hotel and makes his way to Titi's room. Unfortunately, his plot is discovered by Titi and Pallas, who quickly arrive to stop him. Xanthos takes the harp out of the box and leaps out the window to escape, with Titi in hot pursuit.<ref name="4Aft">[[ME-4/Story#After operation|ME-4 After]]</ref>

Xanthos runs through the city streets and heads towards the river, where he has prepared an escape boat. Titi uses her Arts to catch up to Xanthos and tries to take the harp from him. Their struggle causes the boat to tip sideways and the harp flies out of their hands. The two watch in horror as the priceless Minoan relic crashes onto the floor. Titi examines the harp to discover that its pedestal has cracked. Peace officers quickly arrive at the scene and arrest both Xanthos and Lykeion for theft and damaging a national treasure. Behnui suggests that they take this chance to punish Minos and establish Sargon's superiority, but Titi adamantly rejects his idea.<ref name="4Aft"/>

===Questions Without Answers===
[[File:69 i14.png|thumb|Xanthos thought of many ends for himself, but never this one.]]
Titi tries to repair the harp, but quickly discovers something strange about the wood chips that fell from the cracked part. Although it appeared to be made of authentic Minoan wood, lab testing revealed traces of Sargonian resin, dating back around a hundred years. It was undeniable proof that the harp was actually a replica made by Sargonians using Minoan wood. Titi is appalled by this, but Behnui does not particularly care, as their duty is simply to conduct the exchange ceremony, in accordance with the Padishah's orders. In Behnui's eyes, he is the real ambassador of the Sargonian mission, and thus decides to take matters into his own hands.<ref name="5Bef">[[ME-5/Story#Before operation|ME-5 Before]]</ref>

Behnui uses the crisis to exert pressure on Lydia, threatening her with a reminder that the delicate peace between their two nations can be broken at any time. To satisfy his demands, Lydia has no choice but to punish Xanthos and Lykeion in the name of treason and places them on death row.<ref name="5Bef"/> The news shocks Lena, as Lydia is her adoptive sister. Lena is deeply concerned over her patients' health, worried that Xanthos might get an acute Oripathy attack. Hekádemos runs into her at the garden and the two find they share a similar goal. Kassandra later meets the two at Hekádemos's house; as a friend of Xanthos, Kassandra decides to help them devise a plot to break the two Infected out of jail.<ref name="6Bef">[[ME-6/Story#Before operation|ME-6 Before]]</ref>

At the temple square, Titi confides to Pallas about the replica harp. On one hand, she is unwilling to give Minos a fake relic; on the other hand, revealing the truth could potentially cause a diplomatic crisis. Without the real harp to hand over, she can only think of one solution: to steal the fake harp and flee the country. Titi is willing to take all the blame for herself and sacrifice everything to preserve Minos's integrity. Pallas advises against it, worried for her future, but Titi insists. Her companion Mio cannot help but joke that it's "the most Medjehtiqedti way" of solving the problem.<ref name="5Aft">[[ME-5/Story#After operation|ME-5 After]]</ref><ref name="6Bef"/>

===Miracles of Fate===
In prison, Xanthos and Lykeion are approached by a strange old man, who reveals himself as Ithakos's father. He reveals a surprising depth of knowledge regarding the Sargonian occupation of Minos, as his own grandfather had been one of the slaves made to move the Three Poleis to nomadic cities. Contrary to what is commonly written, Padishah Malla had used Minoan workers during the construction process. Xanthos then realises that what Sargon is after are not the construction blueprints, but engineering notes containing records of their activities in Minos. With those notes, the Sargonians will be free to whitewash their colonial history and dictate the truth as they please.<ref name="5Bef"/><ref name="6Aft"/>

On her way back to the hotel, Titi runs into Kassandra. Kassandra brings Titi to a [[Burdenbeast|burdenbeast]] ranch located at the outskirts of town. She reveals a harp built into the pasture's fence, which Kassandra claims is the true harp of Kereseira. Titi is shocked by this revelation and asks why she had never brought it out. Kassandra explains it was because the harp could not produce music anymore, unfit to use as a centerpiece for celebration. According to legends, Kereseira's harp fell silent after Sargon's invasion. Kassandra however adds that if Titi could create a miracle and make it sing again, she may use the harp for the exchange ceremony.<ref name="ST2">[[ME-ST-2]]</ref>

[[File:69_i06_2.png|thumb|left|The gentle light of the twin moons falls upon the pasture, and upon the girl.]]
Titi tries playing the strings but the harp only produces incredibly dull sounds. She rants to Mio about how her father always told her many amazing stories about Minos, and remembered the harsh words people said when he passed away. She wanted to prove that she could be a "hero" like the ones she's heard from her father's stories. As she tries to figure out a solution, drowsiness overtakes her and she falls asleep on the pasture.<ref name="ST2"/>

Morning dawns, and the day of the exchange ceremony has finally arrived. Lena hands some Oripathy suppressants and pain medication to Hekádemos, who will sneak into the prison to free Xanthos and Lykeion. Unbeknownst to them, there is already someone else interested in helping them escape. Proud to see that Ithakos had found such a good friend in Xanthos, Ithakos's father frees him and Lykeion from their shackles. The two make their escape and run into Hekádemos in the prison. Hekádemos quickly escorts them outside before the jailers return.<ref name="7Bef">[[ME-7/Story#Before operation|ME-7 Before]]</ref>

[[File:69 i07.png|thumb|Dozens of burdenbeasts charge through the streets with unstoppable momentum.]]
At the ranch, Mio decides to help Titi out with a little miracle. Mio frightens the nearby burdenbeasts, causing the terrified creatures to barge through the fence and stampede through the city, causing chaos everywhere. The ranch owner, [[Melitta]], desperately chases after them. Fortunately, the ambassador from Lachedamon, [[Cynisca]], was able to calm them down by using a single finger to hold them in place. She notices that one of the burdenbeasts has a harp stuck to its horns and hands it over to Kassandra. Kassandra then places the real harp on the hands of the statue at the First Temple's square. The true harp of Kereseira has returned at last.<ref name="7Aft">[[ME-7/Story#After operation|ME-7 After]]</ref>

===Heroic Spirit===
The problem of the ceremonial play for the exchange ceremony is still at hand. Titi makes a bold suggestion: ''Kereseira and the Outlander'', a famous classical drama that was often used in temple ceremonies honoring the heroes. The play was banned during the Sargonian occupation of Minos, but even after the ban was lifted, it has not been performed in over a century because nobody wanted to play the role of the outlander character, who became a metaphor for the Sargonian invaders. Titi insists on playing the role of the outlander, believing that this was her responsibility as a Sargonian.<ref name="7Aft"/>

[[File:69_i11.png|thumb|left|"What makes you think you can shamelessly come to Athenius, acting like nothing ever happened?"]]
That afternoon, the ceremonial play begins. Everyone watches with bated breath as the play reaches its climatic scene; Kereseira–played by Pallas–picks up the blade and prepares to stab Titi the "outlander". Just before the climax, Xanthos suddenly stomps into the amphitheater and stops the play. He kicks one of the actors, who had secretly swapped the rubber knife for a real one. Xanthos decries this trickery, but it is his countrymen's attitude towards the Sargonians which enrages him even more. For over a century they have been at war, yet now they are acting as though nothing had happened. However, Xanthos refuses to forget the pain, burying hatred deep within his heart. Titi understands where his anger comes from and respects his heroic spirit; she invites him to a duel, so that he may finally find the solace he seeks.<ref name="8Bef">[[ME-8/Story#Before operation|ME-8 Before]]</ref>

During the battle, Titi injures Xanthos's shoulder, causing blood to spray across the statue holding Kereseira's harp. To everyone's surprise, the long-silent harp begins to play music, inducing the trio on the stage into a trance. Xanthos, Titi, and Pallas seem to see visions of each others' pasts. They see a vision of Titi's father writing down ancient legends from a Minoan prisoner and secretly releasing him. They see a vision of Pallas talking with a fan of [[Dikaiopolis|Dikaiopolis the Blood Knight]], a Minoan Infected who became famous in [[Kazimierz]]. They see Xanthos and Ithakos together on a clean river, yearning for the end of the war, and the beautiful spring that will come afterwards.<ref name="8Aft">[[ME-8/Story#After operation|ME-8 After]]</ref>

Xanthos finally remembers what he said to Ithakos all those years ago. His hatred diminished, his strength spent, the dockmaster kneels onto the ground. Those present begin to realize that they have witnessed a miracle. A new chapter has been written in the stories of the heroes.<ref name="8Aft"/>

===Katharsis===
[[File:69 i08.png|thumb|Whatever the result of the referendum, Xanthos will accept it with grace.]]
The exchange ceremony proved to be a resounding success. In the aftermath, Astynomos Lydia lifted the detention order on Xanthos and Lykeion. The people of Athenius will host a referendum determining whether or not to banish him from Minos. Hekádemos resigns from his poistion as Grammatéus and leaves Athenius with Lykeion so that they can see more of the world. Lena gives them a new batch of medicine before bidding them farewell.<ref name="ST3">[[ME-ST-3]]</ref>

Kassandra is relieved with how everything turned out, yet she cannot shake the unease from her heart. Kassandra privately discusses with Pallas regarding her "vision", where Pallas claimed to see the beginnings of Minos in her dreams, and witnessed Kereseira and her First Temple. She expressed concern when Pallas suddenly returned six months ago and requested an interpretation to her dreams. Kassandra could tell hers was an incredibly powerful premonition, and the events of today have only corroborated her interpretation that it was a foreboding omen. Nevertheless, Pallas promises to remain and continue overseeing Minos, wishing to visit the other poleis.<ref name="ST3"/>

Despite the public accusation by Xanthos, Lydia was able to provide a satisfactory explanation to the Athenian Council and retains her position as Astynomos. Períandros and Cynisca pay her a visit, and the former makes a business proposal: to set aside the differences between the Three Poleis and revive an ancient festival. A grand arena is being built in Lachedamon, one that will host competitors from far and wide. With great changes happening across Terra, this old tradition will hopefully unite Minos once more.<ref name="ST3"/>

As a symbol of gratitude for her contributions, Astynomos Lydia grants the title of "Honorary Minoan" to Titi, and wants her to write the foreword for a journal about Minoan history that will be published in Columbia. Upon returning to Menat-Ha'mait, Titi arranges a special exhibit at the Varjavandabad Museum, showcasing all the material that she received from the exchange, including those that revealed the dark sides of the Minoan occupation. While her work received numerous accolades from across [[Terra]], the Sargonian Padishahs were not particularly pleased, and Behnui had to call in countless favours to smooth things over with them.<ref name="ST3"/>

Titi's work has even attracted the attention of the Sargonian Shahanshah himself, who sends an envoy to invite her to the capital city of [[Shar-Agade]]. An excited Titi thus prepares for her journey to the silent City of Gold...<ref name="ST3"/>
