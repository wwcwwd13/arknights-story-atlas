==Synopsis==
===Plant the Seeds===
[[File:47 i02.png|thumb|Although they're here on official work, getting to meet their hard-working sibling is always a joyous occasion.]]

Far in northern [[Yan]] lies [[Dahuang]], the nation's largest producer of grain. For thousands of years, the people of Dahuang have greatly venerated [[Shennong]], the Divine Farmer, carrying her dream to cultivate a rice plant that could grow in [[Originium]]-polluted areas. But now, Dahuang finds itself at the frontline in the battle against [[Sui]] the [[Feranmut]].

As part of the preparation against Sui's revival, the imperial court has ordered siblings [[Nian]] and [[Dusk]] to help construct the "Twelve Pagodas and Five Cities," a mega construction meant to withstand the consequences of a potential war with bestial Sui. Should the Sui be killed, the Pagodas will function as alternate vessels for Sui's fragmented avatars and prevent them from vanishing, for their disappearance would spell the end of Yan's prosperity. Nian and Dusk accept the deal, which also provides an opportunity to visit [[Shu]], their sixth sibling who has been teaching the farmers there for over a millennia.<ref name="ST1">[[HS-ST-1]]</ref>

The sheer scale of the Twelve Pagodas project spells the end of Dahuang's agrarian lifestyle as it undergoes conversion into a [[nomadic city]]. [[Zuo Le]] arrives at Dahuang under the order of his father, General [[Zuo Xuanliao]], to supervise the project. Yet deep down, he knows that this is just punishment for his carelessness during the [[Yumen]] unrest. Upon arrival, he is warmly welcomed by Shu, who requests Zuo Le to help the local farmers. Although this greatly deviates from his regular duties as a [[Sui Regulator]] Candleholder, he willingly accepts this tedious job as a form of atonement. He befriends Tianshi apprentice [[Wanqing|He Sheng]] and livestock herder [[Grain Buds]] who helps him integrate into their lifestyle.<ref name="1Bef">[[HS-1/Story#Before operation|HS-1 Before]]</ref>

===Cleanse the Dirt===
[[File:47 i15.png|thumb|left|It begins with just a small mistake, but everything will soon be out of control.]]
The time has arrived for the annual Shennong Festival, an important celebration to give thanks to the eponymous Divine Farmer, and everyone takes a break to enjoy the opera performance retelling her legacy. Far away, however, the Tumu Tianshi notice an abnormal increase of Originium granules in the river. The dam suddenly bursts and a large amount of polluted water gushes out.<ref name="2Bef">[[HS-2/Story#Before operation|HS-2 Before]]</ref> Everyone quickly returns to protect their farmland, and the Tianshi work together to repair the dam. Nian and Dusk intervene to mitigate some damage, while He Sheng rushes towards the control tower to separate the mobile farm plates to drain the polluted water.<ref name="2Aft">[[HS-2/Story#After operation|HS-2 After]]</ref> 

Unfortunately, the damage done is irreversible, and several fields have been polluted by Originium. A meeting is quickly convened at the town hall to evaluate the damages and discuss any possible solutions. The city subprefect, [[Rong Wanqing]], says that they have to delay the Twelve Pagodas project so they can save the remaining crops. However, Assistant Minister of Engineering [[Wan Qincheng]] believes that as a national defense project, the Twelve Pagodas is more important. Assistant Minister of Rites, [[Ning Ciqiu]], decides to interject, saying that preventing the people from going hungry is top priority, and that she will personally consult with the higher-ups regarding the matter.<ref name="2Aft"/>

[[File:47 i06.png|thumb|"Long time no see, my beloved sister."]]
The farmers and the ''Nongye Tianshi'' lament another year of poor harvest as they are forced to remove the contaminated crops. He Sheng and Grain Buds feel at loss. Just then, they encounter a man wearing a fabulous robe who claims to be a travelling merchant. He says he can sell them anything they wish for–even a prosperous future. Zuo Le immediately interrupts as he knows who this man is: [[Ji]], the seventh avatar of Sui, a conscientious businessman who can weave anything through his silk embroideries. Ji proclaims that he has simply returned to his hometown to visit Shu, his older sister, yet Zuo Le has his doubts.<ref name="3Bef">[[HS-3/Story#Before operation|HS-3 Before]]</ref><ref name="3Aft">[[HS-3/Story#After operation|HS-3 After]]</ref> 

Meanwhile, strange things begin to happen around Dahuang. While searching for her stockbeasts, Grain Buds encounters the mute Old Lumberjack, an acquaintance of hers. She follows him to the river and witnesses a vision of a blood-red ricefield, and she almost jumps into the water after seeing "shadows" of her livestock reflected on the surface. Fortunately, [[Old Tianshi|a mysterious young girl]] with a hot temper pulls her away from the edge.<ref name="3Aft"/> The girl then brings her around on patrol in search of unusual red stalks of rice, whilst also ordering her to keep this a secret.<ref name="4Bef">[[HS-4/Story#Before operation|HS-4 Before]]</ref>

On He Sheng's side, he sees a vision of Shennong conversing with him, asking him to plow the field. As he tries to plow the field, he begins to age rapidly, tilling until he collapses from old age.<ref name="4Bef"/> Zuo Le eventually finds him and wakes him up, becoming concerned with the strange happenings. The Candleholder presses Grain Buds, who tells him about the presence of a red rice plant. Zuo Le becomes alarmed and urges He Sheng to check the crops inside the granary. He opens the door and witnesses a horrifying sight–the golden grains of rice have turned into disgusting reddish-black husks.<ref name="4Aft">[[HS-4/Story#After operation|HS-4 After]]</ref>

===Water the Fields===
[[File:47 i14 1.png|thumb|In a blink of an eye, Shu summons a rain of forgiveness...]][[File:47 i14 2.png|thumb|...And ascends to the heavens in the whims of the wind without regrets.]]
He Sheng immediately reports the situation to Subprefect Rong, suspecting that the polluted water was not caused by Originium, but from something beneath the soil. Rong has known about this for a long time: across the river is one of the sources of the northern [[Collapsal]]s, a leftover shard from the Tianshi attempted to use it during the [[Yan#The Great Hunt|Great Hunt]] against Sui. The spread of demonic taint has alerted the Chamber of Heaven’s Designs, which has ordered everyone to abandon the farm plots they have worked so hard to cultivate and retreat to the core city. Even Nian and Dusk are shocked by this decision.<ref name="5Aft">[[HS-5/Story#After operation|HS-5 After]]</ref>

Ji continues wandering around Dahuang in spite of receiving warnings from Shu, even making covert deals with Subprefect Rong and Assistant Minister Wan.<ref name="6Bef">[[HS-6/Story#Before operation|HS-6 Before]]</ref> Zuo Le catches up to him, and with a scroll provided by Dusk, traps Ji inside a painting scroll. Zuo Le interrogates him, suspecting that he is somehow responsible for the demonic pollution of Dahuang's crops. Ji casually admits to the allegations. When questioned about his motives, he instead responds by asking Zuo Le as to why the Yanese imperial court continues to force the his siblings to serve the dynasty with no reasonable benefits. All he wants is to see his dearest sister Shu to be released from her burdens.<ref name="5Bef">[[HS-5/Story#Before operation|HS-5 Before]]</ref>

For a thousand years, Shu has been single-handedly suppressing the demonic influence creeping beneath Dahuang with her divine powers. However, recent events have started to push things spiralling outside her control. In order to save the land she cherishes, she decides to sacrifice herself by disintegrating her physical body, ascending into the heavens and fading into soft, drizzling rain. Although this successfully cleanses the pollution, her “death” results in her existence being erased from the people’s memories. Ji had expected this, but is nonetheless saddened by her decision. After escaping from the painting scroll, he intends to test the mortals' resolve and judge whether they are worthy enough to bring Shu back.<ref name="5Aft"/>

===Protect the Harvest===
[[File:47 i08.png|thumb|left|Without Shu's control, Dahuang's fertile land is tainted by the demonic power of the north.]]
Ji proceeds to the Twelve Pagodas' energy core to finish his mission, but he is immediately intercepted by Nian and Dusk. The seventh brother easily repeals their attack and even traps them inside the makeshift Sui vessel. He then inserts a bundle of contaminated rice into the energy core.<ref name="ST2">[[HS-ST-2]]</ref> Upon that moment, the living "heart" of the vessels activates, causing disastrous phenomena across the farmland. Without Shu's protection, the demonic pollution beneath the soil rampantly spreads; the Sky Poles used to measure contamination levels go haywire and start flying around like swarms of locusts. Eerie fabric beasts manifest out of thin air and begin to attack the terrified farmers.<ref name="6Bef"/>

Zuo Le and He Sheng rally the farmers, explaining that these beasts are fuelled by their fear. The farmers strengthen their resolve and bravely fend off the fabric monsters with whatever tool they can find even as the city plates start breaking apart. Assistant Minister Wan and Subprefect Rong also decide to take responsibility and face the beasts, becoming severely injured in the process.<ref name="6Bef"/> Grain Buds nearly loses her life trying to save the old lumberjack when she is rescued by the hot-tempered Tianshi apprentice, who reveals herself to be the [[Ageless]] Tianshi grandmaster who has been stationed in the north for centuries.<ref name="7Aft">[[HS-7/Story#After operation|HS-7 After]]</ref>

[[File:47 i09.png|thumb|"Heaven is everlasting and earth is enduring. The reason that they are everlasting is because they do not exist for themselves. "<br/>&mdash;Chapter 7, ''Tao Te Ching'']]
Nian, Dusk, and Shu find themselves inside a chaotic void, once again confronted with the question, “Who am I?” Nian and Dusk see various memories of her past that are filled with regrets, yet the two sisters bravely face them with their long-given answer.<ref name="8Bef">[[HS-8/Story#Before operation|HS-8 Before]]</ref> Shu meets with the Feranmut side of herself, frightened and cowardly, representing Sui's consciousness. Remembering the land that she dearly loves, she resists returning to Its consciousness and vanquishes her inner demons. Her enlightenment not only allows herself to return to the mortal realm, but also stabilizes the rampaging Twelve Pagodas’ energy core and eradicates all the demonic pollution across Dahuang. The farmers rejoice as the fabric beasts disappear and the sky returns to normal.<ref name="8Aft">[[HS-8/Story#After operation|HS-8 After]]</ref>

===Sow the Cause===
[[File:47 i11.png|thumb|left|"Time is like a loom, weaving Yan's majestic mountains and rivers." Such is the testimony of Yan's destiny woven in a majestic silk.]]
Though the crisis appears to be over, Zuo Le suspects that Ji must be planning something else. He follows him and discovers him weaving a majestic robe. It was an unimaginable tapestry—a long silk robe made of the ''guozuo'', or fate of the nation, which he has named the <i>Hundred Scenic Portraits of Great Yan</i>. In order to challenge Yan's ability to defeat Sui, Ji has woven the empire's mountains and rivers into the robe's threads, a piece of clothing that represents Yan's destiny; Dahuang was the last spool to finish his <i>magnum opus</i>.<ref name="8Aft"/>

Nian and Dusk break out of the Sui vessel to settle their "family business."<ref>[[HS-9/Story#Before operation|HS-9 Before]]</ref> In the midst of a heated fight, Shu traps the siblings in her realm before things escalate and confronts Ji about the real mastermind behind this incident. At that moment, a familiar dark figure appears, and the sisters are surprised that it is none other than [[Wang]], the evil second brother. Wang invites Shu to join his grand mission by revealing his real intention: he will first reawaken Sui, then kill Its real consciousness and substitute it with his own — becoming the new Sui — to ensure their "siblings'" survival, Yan's future prosperity, and finally revive [[Jie]], their deceased 5th sibling. In response to his query, Shu decides to give him a handful of seeds, saying that they will help him when the time is right. Their conversation is then interrupted by the angry Old Tianshi who forces her way into the realm. Before disappearing, Wang and Ji declare that they will someday meet again.<ref name="9Aft">[[HS-9/Story#After operation|HS-9 After]]</ref>

Shu finally returns to the mortal realm, but she knows that her temporary disappearance has erased the people's precious memories of her. He Sheng feels a sense of ''déjà vu'' upon meeting her, yet is unable to remember. Nevertheless, Shu is amazed by He Sheng's good news: he finally discovered a single sample of an Originium-resisting rice plant amidst the spoiled plow in the aftermath of the calamity. Now that there is new hope for the world, Shu could finally free herself from her burdens and wishes good luck to her beloved people.<ref name="9Aft"/>

===Reap the Consequence===
[[File:47 i12.png|thumb|"We have no master, so we have to appoint you as our Son of Heaven."<br/>&mdash;"First Annual of Taizu", ''History of Song'']]
The Dahuang pollution crisis throws the imperial court into chaos. Zuo Le receives news from [[Leizi]] to return to his position, but first he needs to investigate the presence of Sui's avatars on [[Rhodes Island]]. Far away at Mount T'ien, the [[Grand Tutor]] gets the chance to interrogate the [[Grand Commandant]] who has long been on the hawkish side in the Feranmut problem. He confesses to abetting the [[Shanhaizhong]] during the [[Yumen]] crisis and allowing Wang to escape from the capital. However, he has grown concerned over Wang's increasingly unpredictable actions, asking the Grand Tutor what they should do next.<ref name="ST3">[[HS-ST-3]]</ref>

With everything prepared, Wang is waiting for the right moment to execute his plan. Ji congratulates his brother by helping him wearing the silk robe of Yan's fate. While both brothers still hold strong ties with the mortal humans, they have to end their business with both the imperial court and finally Sui Itself. Wang declares that Sui will eventually have to face Its ancient humiliation against the mortals, and victory needs to be achieved to free both the people and his "siblings" from Its shadow.<ref name="ST3"/>
