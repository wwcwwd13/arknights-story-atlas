A crossover with the award-winning, slice-of-life adventure fantasy manga and anime ''[[fandom:delicious-in-dungeon:Delicious in Dungeon Wiki|Delicious in Dungeon]]'', Delicious On Terra features [[Laios|Laios Touden]], [[Marcille|Marcille Donato]], [[Chilchuck|Chilchuck Tims]], and [[Senshi|Senshi of Izganda]] of [[Laios's Party]] who have been somehow transported to [[Terra]] while exploring an unmapped area within [[fandom:delicious-in-dungeon:Dungeon|the Dungeon]]. As they travel across Terra to reach [[Rhodes Island]], Laios and co. do their usual business of making delicious food out of... you guessed it, Terra's myriad bestiary they come across in their journey, from the cute to the creepy. Along the way, they stumble into R.I.'s resident big eater [[Ceobe]], resulting in a fun and comedic road trip story.

==Leaving the Dungeon==
===The Orc and his Slugs===
[[File:Scary Orc.png|thumb|First time in a new world and already in danger!]]
[[Laios]] and his party venture into the dungeon in search of his sister Falin, who was eaten by a nefarious Red Dragon. Together with agile [[Chilchuck]], magic-wielding [[Marcille]], and their amazing chef [[Senshi]], the four were unstoppable… until they wound up in another world entirely. They somehow traversed into Terra, which possesses a very different ecosystem compared to the dungeon they were familiar with.<ref>[[DT-1/Story#Before operation|DT-1 Before]]</ref>

While trying to figure out where they are, the party discovers a strange hut with many acid-spewing slugs. An angry metal orc suddenly steps outside with a chainsaw in hand. Panicking, the group flee and hide in a nearby cave. Laios &mdash; either out of stupidity or bravery &mdash; introduces himself to the orc. Marcille was ready to defend Laios when the “orc” started explaining himself: his name was Big Bob and that they were trespassing on his Originium Slug Ranch.

After resolving their differences, [[Big Bob]] teaches them how to decontaminate and cook [[Originium Slug]]s. Together with Senshi, they make a spectacular feast. Big Bob, seeing their culinary interest, hands them a handbook regarding the edibles (and non-edibles) of Terra. He advises them to seek out Rhodes Island, believing that they are most qualified to help with their problems. Little did they know that the journey would prove to be long and arduous, with the group finding no shortage of trouble.<ref>[[DT-1/Story#After operation|DT-1 After]]</ref>

===Jungle Fever===
[[File:Jungle Fatty Bird Banquet.png|thumb|Is this soup or bathwater now?]]
In the jungles of Sargon, the party discover [[The Big Ugly Thing|a giant mechanical machine]] that kept firing explosives. While fleeing, they run into a [[Tiacauh Warrior]] who brings them to an [[Acahualla]]n village. Along the way, Marcille accidentally eats a poisonous plant, mistaking it for an ordinary raspberry.<ref>[[DT-2/Story#Before operation|DT-2 Before]]</ref> She begins hallucinating the Red Dragon and starts casting her magic all over the place. The local Tiacauh urge the party to find the High Priest, who knows the cure to her condition. Laios works on calming down Marcille while Chilchuck and Senshi prepare to cook a feast in order to lure the High Priest.

While gathering ingredients, Senshi mistakenly the High Priest for a common bird and knocks him out. Together with some vegetables and mushrooms, they cook up a fantastic stew accompanied by fish skewers. The High Priest then wakes up to find himself inside a “flavourful bath.” The party were completely unaware that the High Priest looked like a normal bird! He says that to cure Marcille, she must drink the stew they just made. Thankfully, it works, and the party are able to continue on their journey to find Rhodes Island.<ref>[[DT-2/Story#After operation|DT-2 After]]</ref>

===Golden Carrots===
[[File:D32 Steelpot Golden Carrot Feast.png|thumb|left|It may not be made of real gold, but the taste is just as amazing.]]
After escaping the jungle, Laios' party eventually crossed the continent to reach the outbacks of [[Rim Billiton]]. Marcille purchases a [[D32 Steel]] flashlight, having been told that the material can withstand incredible amounts of energy. Much to her dismay, it doesn't seem very compatible with her magic, and she heads back to complain to the factory owner. The owner is plagued by financial issues, lacking the funds to even pay his own workers. That's when the party hears about a traditional dish made with golden carrots that they used to eat. Hearing the workers' tale, Senshi is inspired to cook that dish for them.<ref>[[DT-3/Story#Before operation|DT-3 Before]]</ref>

The workers inform them that to create these golden carrots, they need to obtain special secretions from Goldglitter Originium slugs. The party go into the outback to hunt some slugs, but accidentally tumble inside a mine. They discover dozens of slugs inside but are unable to identify the correct one. Marcille ends up using her flashlight to channel her magic and defeat the slugs. Surprisingly, the flashlight makes the mucus the slugs leave behind into something edible. Senshi thus reforges Marcille's flashlight into a cooking pot (much to her dismay) and is able to cook a delicious stew to give to the factory workers. In return, the owner allows the party to ride in their truck for free.

==Peculiar Treats==
===A Void in the Icefields===
[[File:Otherworldly Aftertaste A.png|thumb|Incorporating an incomprehensible entity into a dish will likely cause some scary side effects...]]
The group then heads north, following the advice of some “tour guides” to find Snowpriests in [[Sami]]. They spend days lost in the snowy woodlands until they encounter an apprentice named [[Oakcup]], who explains they were likely cheated. While they are talking, two [[Foldartal]]s suddenly drop out of a nearby tree. After deciphering the Foldartals, Oakcup says that the prophecies of Sami lead them further north, towards the endless [[Infy Icefield]]s.<ref>[[DT-4/Story#Before operation|DT-4 Before]]</ref>

The group then encounters a corrupted Burdenbeast being chased by a young [[Perro]] girl named [[Ceobe|Kay]]. During the chase, Kay accidentally causes an avalanche that separates Chilchuck from the rest of the group. Fortunately, a passing Cyclops named [[Valarqvin|Arges]] saves Chilchuck, having foreseen that something terrible would happen. Arges warns Chilchuck about the existence of “[[Collapsal|demons]]”, and Chilchuck hurries to save his friends. Unfortunately, he is too late; the others have already eaten the strange Burdenbeast. Possessed by Collapsals, the trio rushes into the icefields at terrifying speeds, with Chilchuck scrambling to chase after them.<ref>[[DT-4/Story#After operation|DT-4 After]]</ref>

===Lost in Brushstrokes===
[[File:Ink Spirit Platter.png|thumb|left|Ink Spirits are actually pretty chill when you become friends with them.]]
After Chilchuck miraculously gets the party sane again, the group winds up inside a strange [[Yan]]ese village named [[P'o-shan]] where day and night do not exist. Laios’ party saves the villagers from the continuous ravages of strange [[Ink Spirit]]s. Between the fights, the party hangs out with [[Dawn]] and the other villagers. They spend so long in the village that they begin to lose track of time, with Marcille seemingly the only one aware of their strange circumstances.<ref>[[DT-5/Story#Before operation|DT-5 Before]]</ref>

One day, Laios disappears together with the Ink Spirits, causing Marcille to worry. She finds Senshi and Chilchuck, who have been completely stupefied by the idyllic countryside life. She manages to snap them back to reality by reminding them of Falin and they hurry to find Laios. They discover him taming the Ink Spirits at the edge of a mountain, even learning how to speak their language. After they bring up their findings with him, Laios deduces that this place feels more like an illusory realm. Suddenly, the [[Dusk|master of the painted world]] descends from the sky, furious that they are ruining her painting due to Laois' antics changing the Ink Spirits. She instantly kicks them out, teleporting them into the wilderness.<ref>[[DT-5/Story#After operation|DT-5 After]]</ref>

===Call of the Ocean===
[[File:Salty Aftertaste.png|thumb|"Come and join We Many..."]]
The party then reaches the sluggish seaside town of [[Sal Viento]] in [[Iberia]]. They find the locals starving, so they decide to cook a feast to cheer them up. A robed priest approaches them, claiming to be from a church that protects the town. He points towards a peculiar cave further down the coast where they may be able to find the ingredients they need. Upon entering, the group find the floor covered with [[Nethersea Brand]] and innumerable other [[Sea Terror]]s, along with the remains of [[Sal Viento Bishop Quintus|a humongous sea monster]].<ref>[[DT-6/Story#Before operation|DT-6 Before]]</ref>

Chilchuck is dispatched outside to set up traps and stand guard. While doing so, he spots the priest from earlier and eavesdrops on him. Realising that the priest had intended to send the party to their deaths, Chilchuck lures the unsuspecting priest into the jaws of a mimic, incapacitating him. Laios and the others return, having successfully defeated the Seaborn in the cave. With that, the group is able to cook up a “nutritious” feast for the locals, unknowingly alarming [[Anita]] to attempt to stop them, though she was too late. In an attempt to demonstrate how edible the dishes were, Laios’ party ends up partially assimilating with We Many and wanders into the ocean in high spirits in search for more ingredients, to the shock and horror of the locals.<ref>[[DT-6/Story#After operation|DT-6 After]]</ref>

==The Moving Island==
===Who Wants Popcorn?===
[[File:Drone Afternoon Tea.png|thumb|left|Turning a farming disaster into a delicious snack is Senshi's specialty.]]
After their near miss at Iberia, the party travels into the plains of [[Columbia]]. A nearby [[Catastrophe]] causes some pioneers' drones to go haywire and start randomly attacking the party. Marcille uses her magic to send the bombs back to their drones, which crash into the pioneers' camp. Believing that someone was trying to rob them, the pioneers storm out to confront the party. Fortunately, a Rhodes Island Operator named Ryan intervenes. Both sides unanimously agree to turn back without fighting.<ref>[[DT-7/Story#Before operation|DT-7 Before]]</ref>

Later that night, the two groups meet again in the forest while camping. They end up sharing some gloompincer meals and agree to collaborate on dealing with the malfunctioning drones. The next morning, Ryan tries to fix the drone relay system but needs someone to distract the bomber drones. Senshi realises that the drones were attacking based on hostile movement and gives Marcille an idea. She conjures a familiar in the form of a scary-faced Laios. The familiar lures the drones away and gets them to drop all their bombs, giving Ryan enough time to fix the relay. However, the bombs drop into the pioneers' cornfield, and the intense heat cooks the raw corn into popcorn. Ryan finds Kay gorging on the mountain of popcorn and takes her back to Rhodes Island. Upon hearing that Rhodes Island is nearby, the party quickly gives chase.<ref>[[DT-7/Story#After operation|DT-7 After]]</ref>

===Taming a Hungry Beast===
[[File:Rhodes Island Dinner.png|thumb|After spending years searching, the party can celebrate at Rhodes Island with a huge feast!]]
After following Ryan’s trail, the party finally catches sight of the massive Rhodes Island landship. They board the landship and encounter Mudrock, a friend of Big Bob, who welcomes them onboard. She tells them to wait by the elevator, but they accidentally head to another floor while she is looking away. Chilchuck then runs into Arges again, who warns that they will encounter flames and a great beast. Following her instructions, they go to the training room, where they meet the High Priest again, along with [[Eunectes]]. The cunning bird tricks them into getting into the Big Ugly, which blows and and catapults them into one of Dusk’s paintings. The four fall through the scenery of P’o-Shan Village before going through a portal and crashing into the dining hall.<ref>[[DT-8/Story#Before operation|DT-8 Before]]</ref>

Before the party have a chance to explain themselves to the shocked Operators, a strange rumbling sound comes from Kay’s room. Upon entering, they discover that the girl has turned into [[Tri-Maw|a massive three-headed beast]]. The party temporarily teams up with Rhodes Island to tame Kay and successfully turn her back to normal with the help of [[Matterhorn]] and [[Amiya]]'s [[Arts]]. Amiya then introduces herself as Rhodes Island’s representative and promises to do the best they can to assist the party with returning home.

The group decides to rest on Rhodes Island, each of them doing their own thing. Marcille gets cleaned up at the dorms and receives some hair cream from [[Beeswax]] while exchanging haircare tips. Laios causes a scene by asking extremely uncomfortable questions to the RI Operators, even incurring [[Kal'tsit]]'s wrath. Senshi tries (and fails) to cook chestnuts with [[Skyfire]]’s flames, angering her in the process, and [[Mint]] attempts to calm her down while frantically calling for [[Shaw]]. Chilchuck drinks at the bar with [[Mountain]], [[SilverAsh]], and [[Hellagur]], and [[Hoshiguma]] joins in, mistaking Chilchuck for a child. After a long day, the party gathers in the kitchen to cook a grand feast, inspired by all the dishes they’ve cooked on their journey. Laios and the others express their gratitude to the Doctor, eager to continue experiencing new things on Terra in the future.<ref>[[DT-8/Story#After operation|DT-8 After]]</ref>
